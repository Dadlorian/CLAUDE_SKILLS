### Overall Objective
[What will this specific project accomplish?]

"The objective of this proposal is to identify and validate metabolic pathways that drive chemotherapy resistance in CRC, and to test novel combination therapies targeting these pathways."

### Central Hypothesis
[Testable prediction based on preliminary data]

Example:
"We hypothesize that CRC cells resistant to 5-fluorouracil (5-FU) undergo metabolic reprogramming toward increased dependence on glutamine metabolism, creating a therapeutic vulnerability that can be exploited with glutaminase inhibitors."

### Rationale
[Why is the hypothesis reasonable? Based on what preliminary data?]

Example:
"Our preliminary data show that 5-FU-resistant CRC cells exhibit 3-fold higher glutaminase expression and increased glutamine consumption compared to sensitive cells (Preliminary Data, Fig. 1). Importantly, glutaminase inhibition resensitized resistant cells to 5-FU in vitro (Fig. 2)."

### Specific Aims

**Specific Aim 1:** [Action verb] + [what] + [how]

Example:
**Specific Aim 1: Identify metabolic pathways altered in 5-FU-resistant CRC cells.**

*Hypothesis:* 5-FU resistance is associated with metabolic reprogramming toward glutamine dependence.

*Approach:* We will perform comprehensive metabolomic profiling of paired 5-FU-sensitive and -resistant CRC cell lines (n=3 pairs) using LC-MS/MS. Metabolic flux analysis with 13C-labeled glutamine will quantify pathway activities.

*Expected Outcome:* Identification of 10-20 metabolites significantly altered in resistant cells, with glutamine metabolism as a top enriched pathway.

**Specific Aim 2:** [Validate findings in vivo]

Example:
**Specific Aim 2: Validate glutamine dependence as a therapeutic target in preclinical models.**

*Hypothesis:* Glutaminase inhibition will enhance 5-FU efficacy in resistant CRC xenografts.

*Approach:* We will test the glutaminase inhibitor CB-839 + 5-FU combination in 5-FU-resistant patient-derived xenograft (PDX) models (n=3 PDX lines, n=10 mice/group).

*Expected Outcome:* Combination therapy will reduce tumor growth by ≥50% compared to 5-FU alone (p<0.05).

**Specific Aim 3:** [Translate to clinical relevance]

Example:
**Specific Aim 3: Determine clinical relevance of glutamine metabolism signature in patient samples.**

*Hypothesis:* High glutaminase expression predicts poor response to 5-FU-based chemotherapy in CRC patients.

*Approach:* We will perform immunohistochemistry for glutaminase on tumor biopsies from 100 CRC patients treated with 5-FU chemotherapy (existing cohort with outcome data).

*Expected Outcome:* High glutaminase expression (top quartile) will correlate with decreased progression-free survival (HR>1.5, p<0.05).

Example:

If successful, this study will provide a rationale for clinical trials combining glutaminase inhibitors with standard chemotherapy, potentially improving outcomes for thousands of CRC patients."

#### Preliminary Data (2-3 pages)
**Critical for R01 success - shows feasibility**

Example:

**Figure 1. 5-FU-resistant CRC cells exhibit altered metabolism.**

[Insert figure: Bar graph showing metabolite levels in sensitive vs resistant cells]

We generated 5-FU-resistant CRC cell lines (HCT116-R, HT29-R) by chronic exposure to escalating 5-FU doses (IC50 increased 10-fold, Fig. 1A). Preliminary metabolomic profiling revealed significant upregulation of glutamine metabolism (Fig. 1B). Key findings:

[Insert figure: Cell viability curves]

#### Specific Aim 1: Detailed Methods

**1.1 Cell Culture & Resistance Models**

*Rationale:* Need paired sensitive/resistant models to identify resistance-specific changes.

*Approach:*
- Cell lines: HCT116, HT29, SW480 (ATCC, authenticated by STR profiling)
- Generate resistant lines: 6-month escalating 5-FU exposure
- Validation: Growth curves, IC50 determination, cross-resistance testing

*Quality Controls:*
- Mycoplasma testing (monthly)
- Regular re-authentication (every 6 months)
- Confirm resistance stability (test IC50 every passage)

**1.2 Metabolomic Profiling**

*Rationale:* Unbiased approach to identify metabolic alterations.

*Approach:*
- Platform: LC-MS/MS at Metabolomics Core Facility
- Sample prep: Cells cultured in triplicate, metabolites extracted in 80% methanol
- Coverage: 300+ metabolites across major pathways
- Analysis: Principal component analysis (PCA), pathway enrichment (MetaboAnalyst)

*Sample Size:*
- N=3 cell line pairs × 3 biological replicates = 18 samples
- Power analysis: n=3 replicates provides 90% power to detect 1.5-fold changes (α=0.05)

*Timeline:* Months 1-6

**1.3 Metabolic Flux Analysis**

*Rationale:* Confirm pathway activity, not just metabolite levels.

*Approach:*
- Tracer: U-13C-glutamine (Cambridge Isotope Laboratories)
- Incubation: 6 hours (validated time point)
- Detection: Mass isotopomer distribution by LC-MS/MS
- Analysis: Flux calculation using INCA software (Dr. Young, Vanderbilt)

*Expected Results:*
- Increased 13C-labeling in TCA cycle intermediates (α-KG, succinate, malate)
- Confirms glutamine → TCA flux is elevated in resistant cells

*Alternative Approaches:*
- If glutamine flux is not elevated: Test alternative nutrients (glucose, palmitate)
- If technical issues: Validate key findings with Seahorse metabolic flux analyzer

**Aim 1 Expected Outcomes & Interpretation:**
- Outcome 1: Glutamine metabolism is top enriched pathway → Proceed to Aim 2
- Outcome 2: Another pathway is enriched → Adapt Aim 2 to test that pathway
- Outcome 3: No clear pathway emerges → Focus on individual metabolites, test in small molecule screen

#### Specific Aim 2: Detailed Methods
[Similar detailed breakdown for each aim...]

**2.1 In Vivo Xenograft Models**

*Approach:*
- PDX models: 3 lines from 5-FU-resistant CRC patients (existing collection, IRB approved)
- Engraftment: NOD/SCID mice, 106 cells subcutaneous
- Treatment groups (n=10 mice/group):
  1. Vehicle control
  2. 5-FU alone (25 mg/kg, once weekly)
  3. CB-839 alone (200 mg/kg, daily oral gavage)
  4. Combination (5-FU + CB-839)

*Power Analysis:*
- Previous PDX studies: Tumor growth SD = 150 mm³
- Expect 50% reduction in combination arm
- N=10/group provides >90% power (α=0.05, two-sided t-test)

*Primary Endpoint:* Tumor volume at day 28 (measured twice weekly by caliper)

*Secondary Endpoints:*
- Time to tumor doubling
- Survival (humane endpoint: tumor >2000 mm³)
- Toxicity: Body weight, clinical observations

*Timeline:* Months 12-30

Mice are the most appropriate model to test drug combinations in vivo. NOD/SCID mice enable PDX engraftment, modeling human CRC biology.

Animals housed in AAALAC-accredited facility with 12hr light/dark cycles, ad libitum food/water. Veterinary staff monitor daily.

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Start with Specific Aims** - Get this perfect first, as it drives everything else

2. **Preliminary Data is Critical** - R01 requires strong preliminary data showing feasibility

3. **Be Specific** - Vague aims like "characterize" or "explore" are weak. Use precise, measurable outcomes.

4. **Address Rigor & Reproducibility**:
   - Biological replicates (n≥3)
   - Blinding where possible
   - Power analyses
   - Authentication of cell lines, validation of antibodies

5. **Alternative Approaches** - Always discuss "Plan B" if experiments fail

6. **Logical Flow** - Each aim should build on the previous, with contingencies

7. **Realistic Timeline** - Use a Gantt chart showing milestones

8. **Strong Team** - Include collaborators with complementary expertise

9. **Get Feedback** - Internal review by colleagues, institutional mock study section

10. **Follow Instructions** - Page limits, formatting, required sections
