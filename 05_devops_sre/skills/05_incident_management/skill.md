# Incident Management - Elite Professional Practices

**Structured response, mitigation, and learning from production incidents**

---

## Overview

Incident Management is the process of responding to unplanned interruptions or reductions in quality of IT services. This covers practices from Google SRE, PagerDuty, and other incident response leaders.

## Incident Response Framework

**Roles**:
- **Incident Commander**: Coordinates response, makes decisions
- **Communications Lead**: Stakeholder updates, status page
- **Technical Lead**: Investigation and mitigation
- **Scribe**: Documents timeline and actions

**Process**:
1. Detection & Triage (minutes)
2. Response & Mitigation (minutes to hours)
3. Recovery & Verification (hours)
4. Post-Incident Review (48-72 hours)

## Technology Stack

**PagerDuty** - On-call scheduling and alerting
**Opsgenie** - Incident management
**Statuspage** - External communication
**Slack/Teams** - War room coordination

## Best Practices

- Blameless post-mortems focus on systems, not people
- Track action items to completion
- Share learnings widely
- Measure MTTR and incident frequency
- Sustainable on-call rotations
