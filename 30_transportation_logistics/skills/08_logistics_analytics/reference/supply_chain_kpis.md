# Supply Chain KPIs: Comprehensive Reference

## Overview

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are quantifiable metrics used to evaluate the success and efficiency of supply chain operations. This reference provides a comprehensive catalog of essential logistics and supply chain KPIs organized by functional area.

## Transportation KPIs

### Cost Metrics

**Transportation Cost per Unit**
- **Formula**: Total Transportation Costs / Total Units Shipped
- **Target**: Industry benchmark: $2-8 per unit (varies by product)
- **Purpose**: Measure efficiency of transportation spending

**Transportation Cost as % of Sales**
- **Formula**: (Total Transportation Costs / Total Sales Revenue) × 100
- **Target**: 4-10% depending on industry
- **Purpose**: Benchmark transportation efficiency relative to revenue

**Cost per Mile**
- **Formula**: Total Transportation Costs / Total Miles Traveled
- **Target**: $1.50-3.00 per mile for full truckload
- **Purpose**: Evaluate carrier efficiency and rate competitiveness

**Freight Cost per Hundredweight (CWT)**
- **Formula**: Total Freight Costs / (Total Weight Shipped / 100)
- **Target**: Varies by mode and distance
- **Purpose**: Standardized cost comparison across shipments

### Efficiency Metrics

**On-Time Delivery Rate**
- **Formula**: (On-Time Deliveries / Total Deliveries) × 100
- **Target**: 95-98%
- **Purpose**: Measure reliability and customer satisfaction

**On-Time Pickup Rate**
- **Formula**: (On-Time Pickups / Total Pickups) × 100
- **Target**: 95-98%
- **Purpose**: Track carrier reliability at origin

**Average Transit Time**
- **Formula**: Sum of (Delivery Date - Ship Date) / Number of Shipments
- **Target**: Varies by lane and mode
- **Purpose**: Monitor delivery speed and consistency

**Transit Time Variance**
- **Formula**: Standard deviation of transit times
- **Target**: Minimize variance (< 10% of average)
- **Purpose**: Measure consistency and reliability

**Load Factor**
- **Formula**: (Actual Weight or Volume / Vehicle Capacity) × 100
- **Target**: 85-95%
- **Purpose**: Optimize vehicle utilization

**Empty Miles Percentage**
- **Formula**: (Empty Miles / Total Miles) × 100
- **Target**: < 15%
- **Purpose**: Reduce waste and improve network efficiency

### Quality Metrics

**Claims Rate**
- **Formula**: (Number of Claims / Total Shipments) × 100
- **Target**: < 1%
- **Purpose**: Track damage and loss incidents

**Perfect Order Percentage**
- **Formula**: (Orders delivered complete, on-time, damage-free, with correct documentation / Total Orders) × 100
- **Target**: 90-95%
- **Purpose**: Overall measure of delivery quality

**Damage Rate**
- **Formula**: (Damaged Shipments / Total Shipments) × 100
- **Target**: < 0.5%
- **Purpose**: Monitor handling quality

## Warehouse & Inventory KPIs

### Inventory Management

**Inventory Turnover**
- **Formula**: Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value
- **Target**: 4-12 turns per year (industry dependent)
- **Purpose**: Measure inventory efficiency and freshness

**Days of Inventory Outstanding (DIO)**
- **Formula**: (Average Inventory / COGS) × 365
- **Target**: 30-90 days depending on industry
- **Purpose**: Measure how long inventory sits before sale

**Inventory Accuracy**
- **Formula**: (Accurate Inventory Records / Total Inventory Records) × 100
- **Target**: 95-99%
- **Purpose**: Data quality for planning and fulfillment

**Stock-Out Rate**
- **Formula**: (Number of Stock-Outs / Total SKUs) × 100
- **Target**: < 2%
- **Purpose**: Measure availability and customer service

**Fill Rate**
- **Formula**: (Units Shipped / Units Ordered) × 100
- **Target**: 95-98%
- **Purpose**: Track order fulfillment capability

**Safety Stock Adequacy**
- **Formula**: (Actual Safety Stock / Required Safety Stock) × 100
- **Target**: 95-105%
- **Purpose**: Ensure buffer inventory is appropriate

**Dead Stock Percentage**
- **Formula**: (Value of Dead Stock / Total Inventory Value) × 100
- **Target**: < 5%
- **Purpose**: Identify obsolete inventory for disposal

### Warehouse Operations

**Order Picking Accuracy**
- **Formula**: (Orders Picked Correctly / Total Orders Picked) × 100
- **Target**: 99.5%+
- **Purpose**: Quality control for order fulfillment

**Orders Picked per Hour**
- **Formula**: Total Orders Picked / Total Labor Hours
- **Target**: 60-150+ depending on automation
- **Purpose**: Labor productivity measurement

**Lines Picked per Hour**
- **Formula**: Total Lines Picked / Total Labor Hours
- **Target**: 100-300 depending on operation
- **Purpose**: Detailed productivity metric

**Units Picked per Hour**
- **Formula**: Total Units Picked / Total Labor Hours
- **Target**: Varies widely by SKU size
- **Purpose**: Volume-based productivity

**Warehouse Capacity Utilization**
- **Formula**: (Used Storage Space / Total Available Space) × 100
- **Target**: 80-90%
- **Purpose**: Optimize space usage

**Dock-to-Stock Time**
- **Formula**: Average time from receipt to inventory availability
- **Target**: < 24 hours for most operations
- **Purpose**: Receiving efficiency

**Order Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: Average time from order receipt to shipment
- **Target**: Same-day to 24 hours
- **Purpose**: Overall fulfillment speed

**Putaway Accuracy**
- **Formula**: (Items Put Away Correctly / Total Items Put Away) × 100
- **Target**: 99%+
- **Purpose**: Receiving quality control

**Receiving Productivity**
- **Formula**: Units Received / Labor Hours
- **Target**: 200-500 units per hour
- **Purpose**: Inbound labor efficiency

## Demand Planning & Forecasting KPIs

**Forecast Accuracy**
- **Formula**: 100% - |Actual - Forecast| / Actual × 100
- **Target**: 80-90% depending on product stability
- **Purpose**: Measure prediction quality

**Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)**
- **Formula**: (1/n) × Σ |(Actual - Forecast) / Actual| × 100
- **Target**: < 20% for stable products
- **Purpose**: Standard forecast error metric

**Bias**
- **Formula**: Σ(Forecast - Actual) / Σ(Actual) × 100
- **Target**: -5% to +5% (near zero)
- **Purpose**: Detect systematic over/under forecasting

**Forecast Value Added (FVA)**
- **Formula**: Improvement in accuracy vs. naive forecast
- **Target**: Positive FVA for all process steps
- **Purpose**: Justify forecasting process complexity

**Demand Planning Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: Time from data collection to finalized forecast
- **Target**: Weekly or monthly cycle completion
- **Purpose**: Process efficiency measurement

## Order Management KPIs

**Order Accuracy Rate**
- **Formula**: (Accurate Orders / Total Orders) × 100
- **Target**: 99%+
- **Purpose**: Quality of order processing

**Order Entry Time**
- **Formula**: Average time to enter order into system
- **Target**: < 5 minutes for standard orders
- **Purpose**: Process efficiency

**Cash-to-Cash Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: DIO + DSO - DPO
- **Target**: Minimize (< 30 days ideal)
- **Purpose**: Working capital efficiency

**Order-to-Cash Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: Time from order receipt to payment receipt
- **Target**: 30-45 days
- **Purpose**: Revenue cycle efficiency

**Backorder Rate**
- **Formula**: (Backordered Items / Total Ordered Items) × 100
- **Target**: < 2%
- **Purpose**: Measure inventory availability

## Supplier & Procurement KPIs

**Supplier On-Time Delivery**
- **Formula**: (On-Time Deliveries from Supplier / Total Deliveries) × 100
- **Target**: 95%+
- **Purpose**: Supplier reliability measurement

**Supplier Quality Rate**
- **Formula**: (Accepted Receipts / Total Receipts) × 100
- **Target**: 98%+
- **Purpose**: Inbound quality control

**Purchase Order Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: Average time from requisition to PO issuance
- **Target**: 1-3 days
- **Purpose**: Procurement efficiency

**Cost Savings from Sourcing**
- **Formula**: (Previous Cost - New Cost) / Previous Cost × 100
- **Target**: 3-10% annually
- **Purpose**: Procurement value contribution

**Supplier Lead Time**
- **Formula**: Average time from PO to delivery
- **Target**: Varies by supplier and product
- **Purpose**: Planning and safety stock calculations

## Network & Optimization KPIs

**Network Optimization Score**
- **Formula**: Actual Total Cost / Theoretical Minimum Cost
- **Target**: 1.05-1.15 (5-15% above theoretical minimum)
- **Purpose**: Measure network efficiency vs. optimal

**Cross-Dock Percentage**
- **Formula**: (Cross-Docked Units / Total Units) × 100
- **Target**: 30-70% depending on network design
- **Purpose**: Measure direct shipping efficiency

**Multi-Stop Route Efficiency**
- **Formula**: Actual Miles / Theoretical Shortest Path Miles
- **Target**: 1.05-1.10
- **Purpose**: Route optimization effectiveness

**Cube Utilization**
- **Formula**: (Used Cubic Feet / Available Cubic Feet) × 100
- **Target**: 85-92%
- **Purpose**: Volumetric loading efficiency

**Network Flow Balance**
- **Formula**: Standard deviation of facility utilization rates
- **Target**: Minimize variance
- **Purpose**: Balanced network loading

## Financial KPIs

**Total Logistics Cost as % of Revenue**
- **Formula**: (Total Logistics Costs / Total Revenue) × 100
- **Target**: 8-15% depending on industry
- **Purpose**: Overall logistics efficiency benchmark

**Warehousing Cost per Unit**
- **Formula**: Total Warehousing Costs / Units Handled
- **Target**: $0.50-3.00 depending on product
- **Purpose**: Warehousing efficiency metric

**Inventory Carrying Cost**
- **Formula**: (Average Inventory Value × Carrying Cost Rate)
- **Typical Rate**: 20-30% of inventory value annually
- **Purpose**: True cost of holding inventory

**Supply Chain ROI**
- **Formula**: (Supply Chain Savings - Investment) / Investment × 100
- **Target**: 15-25%+ ROI
- **Purpose**: Investment justification

## Customer Service KPIs

**Perfect Order Rate**
- **Formula**: Orders meeting all criteria (complete, on-time, accurate, undamaged) / Total Orders × 100
- **Target**: 90-95%
- **Purpose**: Comprehensive customer satisfaction metric

**Order Cycle Time**
- **Formula**: Average time from order placement to delivery
- **Target**: 1-3 days for e-commerce
- **Purpose**: Customer experience metric

**Customer Order Fill Rate**
- **Formula**: (Complete Orders / Total Orders) × 100
- **Target**: 95%+
- **Purpose**: Service level measurement

**Return Rate**
- **Formula**: (Returned Units / Total Units Shipped) × 100
- **Target**: < 5% (< 10% for e-commerce)
- **Purpose**: Quality and customer satisfaction

## Sustainability KPIs

**Carbon Emissions per Shipment**
- **Formula**: Total CO2 Emissions / Number of Shipments
- **Target**: Continuous reduction year-over-year
- **Purpose**: Environmental impact measurement

**Fuel Efficiency**
- **Formula**: Miles per Gallon for fleet
- **Target**: 6-8 MPG for trucks
- **Purpose**: Environmental and cost efficiency

**Packaging Waste Ratio**
- **Formula**: Packaging Weight / Product Weight
- **Target**: < 0.15 for most products
- **Purpose**: Sustainability and cost reduction

## KPI Dashboard Best Practices

### Selection Criteria
- Align KPIs with strategic business objectives
- Ensure data availability and accuracy
- Focus on actionable metrics
- Balance leading and lagging indicators
- Include efficiency, quality, and cost metrics

### Reporting Guidelines
- Establish clear ownership for each KPI
- Define measurement frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Set realistic but challenging targets
- Trend over time rather than point-in-time snapshots
- Segment by product, region, or customer where relevant

### Visualization Recommendations
- Use traffic light indicators (red/yellow/green) for status
- Show trends with line charts
- Compare to targets with bullet charts
- Display distributions with histograms
- Use heat maps for multi-dimensional analysis

### Continuous Improvement
- Review and update KPIs quarterly
- Retire metrics that don't drive action
- Add new metrics as business evolves
- Benchmark against industry standards
- Link KPIs to compensation where appropriate
