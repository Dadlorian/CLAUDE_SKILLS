# Freight Management

## Overview
Freight Management is the comprehensive discipline of planning, executing, and optimizing the movement of goods across global supply chains. This skill encompasses freight mode selection, carrier management, rate negotiation, international shipping compliance, freight classification, cost optimization, and end-to-end visibility of shipments across all transportation modes.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Freight Mode Selection & Optimization
- Multi-modal transportation analysis (LTL, FTL, parcel, air, ocean, rail, intermodal)
- Mode selection criteria: cost, transit time, reliability, product characteristics
- Total cost of ownership calculations including inventory carrying costs
- Service level requirements and capacity constraints
- Environmental impact assessment and carbon footprint optimization

### 2. Carrier Management & Procurement
- Carrier qualification, evaluation, and performance monitoring
- RFP/RFQ processes for freight procurement
- Contract negotiation: rates, accessorials, service commitments, liability
- Carrier scorecarding: on-time delivery, damage rates, customer service
- Diversification strategies and contingency planning

### 3. International Shipping & Trade Compliance
- Incoterms selection and application (FCA, FOB, CIF, DDP, etc.)
- Customs documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, certificates of origin
- Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) classification
- Import/export regulations, trade agreements, and restricted party screening
- Customs clearance coordination and duty optimization strategies

### 4. Freight Classification & Rating
- NMFC (National Motor Freight Classification) understanding and application
- Freight class determination: density, stowability, handling, liability
- Dimensional weight calculations for air and parcel shipments
- Tariff interpretation and accessorial charge management
- Density-based pricing vs. class-based pricing strategies

### 5. Rate Management & Cost Optimization
- Rate shopping across multiple carriers and service levels
- Freight audit and payment validation
- Identification and dispute of incorrect charges and accessorials
- Load consolidation and pooling strategies
- Network optimization and continuous improvement initiatives

### 6. Documentation & Compliance
- Bill of Lading (BOL) preparation and management
- Proof of delivery (POD) collection and archival
- Freight claims processing for loss and damage
- Hazardous materials shipping documentation (DG declarations)
- Electronic data interchange (EDI) for transportation documents

### 7. Technology & Systems Integration
- Transportation Management Systems (TMS) utilization
- API integration with carriers for rating, booking, and tracking
- Real-time shipment visibility and exception management
- Analytics and reporting: spend analysis, lane analysis, carrier performance
- Automation of routing guides and carrier selection logic

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Cost Metrics
- **Freight Cost per Unit**: Total freight spend divided by units shipped
- **Freight Cost as % of Revenue**: Freight spend relative to total sales
- **Cost per Mile/Kilometer**: Transportation costs normalized by distance
- **Accessorial Cost Ratio**: Accessorial charges as percentage of base freight
- **Savings from Optimization**: Quantified improvements from consolidation and mode shifts

### Service Metrics
- **On-Time Delivery Rate**: Percentage of shipments delivered within promised timeframe
- **Transit Time Variance**: Difference between actual and planned transit times
- **Damage Rate**: Percentage of shipments with damage claims
- **Claims Ratio**: Value of claims as percentage of freight spend
- **Perfect Order Rate**: Shipments delivered complete, on-time, and damage-free

### Operational Metrics
- **Load Factor/Utilization**: Percentage of vehicle capacity utilized
- **Tender Acceptance Rate**: Percentage of loads accepted by carriers on first tender
- **Routing Guide Compliance**: Adherence to preferred carrier selection logic
- **Invoice Accuracy Rate**: Percentage of freight invoices without errors
- **Freight Audit Recovery**: Overcharges identified and recovered through audit process

## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: Freight Cost Volatility
**Problem**: Unpredictable rate fluctuations due to capacity, fuel, and market dynamics
**Solutions**:
- Long-term contract agreements with escalation clauses
- Multi-carrier diversification to maintain competitive tension
- Mode shifting and network optimization to reduce dependence on spot market
- Fuel surcharge indexing to transparent benchmark indices
- Scenario planning and budget contingencies

### Challenge 2: Capacity Constraints
**Problem**: Inability to secure transportation capacity during peak seasons
**Solutions**:
- Strategic carrier partnerships with volume commitments
- Earlier booking and scheduling to secure capacity in advance
- Flexible pickup/delivery windows to increase carrier efficiency
- Private fleet or dedicated contract carriage for critical lanes
- Drop trailer programs to provide operational flexibility to carriers

### Challenge 3: International Shipping Complexity
**Problem**: Navigating customs, documentation, and compliance across multiple countries
**Solutions**:
- Partnership with experienced freight forwarders and customs brokers
- Standardized documentation templates and validation processes
- Proactive HTS classification and landed cost modeling
- Free Trade Agreement utilization for duty reduction
- Country-specific playbooks for common destination countries

### Challenge 4: Lack of Visibility
**Problem**: Limited real-time tracking and exception notification across carriers
**Solutions**:
- TMS implementation with multi-carrier tracking integration
- Control tower approach with dedicated monitoring and exception management
- Carrier requirements for GPS tracking and milestone updates
- API integration for automated status updates
- Proactive customer communication on delays and exceptions

### Challenge 5: Invoice Accuracy & Audit
**Problem**: High error rates in freight invoices and difficulty validating charges
**Solutions**:
- Automated freight audit software matching invoices to contracts
- Pre-audit validation rules to flag anomalies before payment
- Systematic dispute process with carriers for overcharges
- Root cause analysis to address recurring billing errors
- Self-billing arrangements with trusted carrier partners

## Best Practices

### Strategic Planning
1. **Conduct comprehensive network analysis** to understand lane volumes, costs, and service requirements
2. **Develop routing guides** with primary and backup carriers for all major lanes
3. **Establish clear KPIs and SLAs** aligned with business objectives and customer requirements
4. **Perform annual freight procurement** with competitive bidding across qualified carriers
5. **Model total cost scenarios** including inventory, warehouse, and transportation trade-offs

### Operational Excellence
1. **Optimize load building** to maximize trailer/container utilization and reduce shipments
2. **Consolidate shipments** across business units, facilities, and time periods where feasible
3. **Standardize packaging** to improve density and reduce dimensional weight charges
4. **Provide accurate information** to carriers: weight, dimensions, freight class, special requirements
5. **Schedule consistently** to enable carrier route optimization and improve reliability

### Continuous Improvement
1. **Analyze freight spend regularly** to identify optimization opportunities by lane, mode, and carrier
2. **Review carrier performance** quarterly and adjust routing guides based on data
3. **Benchmark rates and service** against industry standards and peer companies
4. **Pilot new technologies** and service offerings to stay ahead of industry trends
5. **Invest in staff development** through industry certifications and training programs

### Risk Management
1. **Diversify carrier portfolio** to avoid over-dependence on single providers
2. **Validate insurance coverage** for cargo, liability, and contingent auto coverage
3. **Screen carriers** for safety ratings, financial stability, and compliance
4. **Document everything** to support claims and dispute resolution
5. **Establish contingency plans** for carrier failures, capacity shortages, and disruptions
