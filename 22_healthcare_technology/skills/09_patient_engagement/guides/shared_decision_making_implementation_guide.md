# Shared Decision-Making Implementation Guide

## Organizational Readiness

### Assessment Checklist
- [ ] Leadership commitment and funding
- [ ] Clinical champion identified
- [ ] Decision aids available
- [ ] EHR capability to support SDM
- [ ] Time allocated in visits for SDM
- [ ] Staff training program planned
- [ ] Success metrics defined

### Staff Training Program

**Provider Training (4 hours):**
- Communication skills for SDM
- Eliciting and exploring values
- Presenting evidence clearly
- Supporting informed choice
- Documenting shared decisions
- Role-playing practice

**Clinical Staff Training (2 hours):**
- Supporting SDM process
- Helping patients prepare
- Managing questions
- Providing resources

**Administrative Support (1 hour):**
- Scheduling and workflow
- Documentation requirements
- Patient communication

### Workflow Integration

**Visit Structure (16-18 minutes):**
1. Establish rapport (2 min)
2. Assess understanding (2 min)
3. Present evidence (5 min)
4. Elicit preferences (3 min)
5. Reach decision (2 min)

**Supporting Tools:**
- Decision aids (printed and digital)
- Preference assessment worksheets
- Documentation templates
- Patient summaries

### Implementation Timeline
- Months 1-2: Readiness assessment, staff training
- Months 3-4: Pilot in select clinics
- Months 5-6: Expand to all relevant clinics
- Months 7-12: Evaluate and optimize

### Measurement and Monitoring
- Provider adherence to SDM process
- Patient activation and involvement
- Decision conflict and satisfaction
- Clinical outcomes
- Implementation barriers and solutions
