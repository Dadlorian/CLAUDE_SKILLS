# Patient Engagement Strategy Guide

## Strategic Planning Framework

### Vision and Goals

**Vision Statement Example:**
"Empower patients to actively engage in their healthcare through accessible, personalized digital solutions that improve health outcomes and patient satisfaction."

**SMART Goals:**
- Achieve 60% portal registration within 12 months
- Increase monthly active users by 40% year-over-year
- Improve medication adherence by 15%
- Reduce appointment no-shows by 10%
- Achieve 4.2/5 patient satisfaction rating

### Stakeholder Analysis
- Clinical leadership
- IT and operations
- Patient advisory council
- Providers and clinical staff
- Administrative and billing
- Patients and families

### Engagement Roadmap

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Foundation**
- Portal launch and promotion
- Basic medication management
- Appointment scheduling
- Patient education library
- Target: 40% registration

**Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Expansion**
- Mobile app development
- Health metrics tracking
- Wearable integration
- Advanced education
- Target: 50% active users

**Phase 3 (Year 2): Optimization**
- Personalization engine
- Predictive analytics
- SDM implementation
- Advanced coaching
- Target: Measurable outcome improvements

### Implementation Success Factors
- Executive sponsorship
- Adequate resourcing
- Change management
- Staff engagement
- Patient involvement
- Continuous improvement culture
- Measurement and accountability

### Risk Mitigation
- Address technology barriers
- Build patient trust through privacy
- Ensure clinical integration
- Manage change resistance
- Plan for contingencies
- Monitor adoption trends
