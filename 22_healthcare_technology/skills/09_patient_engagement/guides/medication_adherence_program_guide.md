# Medication Adherence Program Guide

## Program Framework

### Assessment Phase
- Baseline adherence measurement (MPR, pharmacy refills)
- Identify high-risk non-adherers
- Root cause analysis (intentional vs unintentional)
- Patient goals and motivations

### Intervention Strategies
```
Low-Risk Group (>80% adherence):
├─ Maintenance support
├─ Quarterly check-ins
├─ Education reinforcement
└─ Recognition/incentives

Moderate-Risk (60-80% adherence):
├─ Monthly phone coaching
├─ Reminder systems
├─ Medication synchronization
├─ Barrier problem-solving
└─ Provider communication

High-Risk (<60% adherence):
├─ Intensive case management
├─ Weekly contact
├─ Smart medication devices
├─ Pharmacy collaboration
├─ Mental health support if needed
└─ Possible medication simplification
```

### Implementation Components

**1. Simplification Strategies**
- Once-daily dosing preferred
- Combination pills to reduce pill burden
- Pre-filled medication packs
- Blister packs from pharmacy

**2. Reminder Systems**
- SMS text reminders
- Mobile app notifications  
- Pill bottle alarms
- Phone calls from care team
- Calendar/habit tracking

**3. Patient Education**
- Why each medication matters
- How to take medications correctly
- Side effect management
- Drug interaction awareness
- Importance of consistency

**4. Monitoring and Support**
- Regular check-ins (phone, visit, app)
- Medication refill tracking
- Adherence feedback
- Performance incentives
- Provider notification of non-adherence

### Measurement and Outcomes

**Adherence Metrics:**
- Medication Possession Ratio (target >80%)
- Proportion of Days Covered (target >80%)
- Refill compliance
- Patient self-report adherence

**Clinical Outcomes:**
- Disease-specific metrics (A1C, BP, INR)
- Complication rates
- Healthcare utilization
- Patient satisfaction

**Program Impact:**
- Adherence improvement rate
- Cost avoidance (prevented ED visits, hospitalizations)
- Return on investment
- Sustainability of improvements

### Success Factors
- Patient engagement and motivation
- Provider support and involvement
- Pharmacy collaboration
- Technology accessibility
- Organizational commitment
- Adequate staffing and resources
