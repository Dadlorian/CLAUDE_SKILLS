# Patient Engagement Metrics Reference

## Defining Patient Engagement

### Engagement vs. Compliance

**Patient Compliance:**
- Patient follows provider instructions
- Passive acceptance of treatment
- Focus on adherence
- Short-term behavior change

**Patient Engagement:**
- Patient actively participates in healthcare
- Partnership with providers
- Sustained behavior change
- Shared decision-making
- Long-term health management

## Categories of Engagement Metrics

**Technology Adoption:**
- Portal registration rate (target: >60%)
- Portal login frequency
  - Monthly active users (target: >30%)
  - Average logins per user per month (target: >2)
- Time spent on portal (target: >5 minutes/session)

**Feature Utilization:**

```
│  ├─ Average views per user: X

│  ├─ Online booking rate: X%
│  ├─ Cancellations rate: X%
│  └─ No-show rate: X%
├─ Secure Messaging
│  ├─ Active users: X%
```

**Content Engagement:**

### 2. Clinical Engagement Metrics

**Appointment and Visit Engagement:**
- Appointment attendance rate (target: >95%)
- No-show rate (target: <5%)
- Cancellation rate (target: <10%)
- Same-day appointment requests (indicator of engagement)

**Medication Management:**
- Medication Possession Ratio (target: >80%)
- Proportion of Days Covered (target: >80%)
- Refill request timeliness
- Pharmacy communication participation

**Preventive Care:**
- Recommended screening completion rate
- Vaccination rate
- Annual wellness exam participation
- Preventive service utilization

**Chronic Disease Management:**

### 3. Experience and Satisfaction Metrics

**Patient Satisfaction:**
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
  - Target: >50
  - Formula: % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)

- Patient Satisfaction Scores (HCAHPS-like)
  - Communication with staff
  - Clarity of information
  - Coordination of care
  - Respect for preferences
  - Target: >4/5 rating

**Perceived Involvement in Care:**
- CollaboRATE scale (perceived shared decision-making)
  - 3 questions, 0-9 scale
  - Target: >6/9 (high collaboration)

- SDM domains:
  - Information provided
  - Values and preferences discussed
  - Decision support given

### 4. Knowledge and Activation Metrics

**Health Literacy Improvement:**
- Knowledge assessment scores
  - Pre-test vs. post-test improvement
  - Target: >20% knowledge gain

- Understanding of condition
  - Disease mechanism comprehension
  - Treatment understanding
  - Warning sign recognition

**Patient Activation Measure (PAM):**
```
PAM Scale (13-item version):
├─ Level 1 (Passive): 13-47 score
│  └─ Lacks confidence, limited knowledge
├─ Level 2 (Aware): 47.1-55.1 score
│  └─ Understands importance but limited skills
├─ Level 3 (Taking Action): 55.2-67 score
│  └─ Starting to take action
└─ Level 4 (Maintaining): 67.1+ score
   └─ Engaged in ongoing management

Target:
- Increase proportion in Levels 3-4 by 10% per year
- Baseline assessment and tracking
- Longitudinal improvement assessment
```

```
Depression/Mental Health:
├─ PHQ-9 score improvement
├─ Medication adherence
├─ Follow-up visit completion

```

**Utilization Outcomes:**
- Emergency department visits (30% reduction target)
- Hospital readmissions (20% reduction target)
- Preventable hospital admissions
- Length of hospital stay
- Primary care visit frequency

**Quality of Life Outcomes:**
- Functional status improvement (SF-36 scores)
- Symptom burden reduction
- Activity level improvement
- Mental health improvement
- Work or school absences reduction

## Patient Engagement Scoring Systems

### Composite Engagement Index

**Multi-Dimensional Scoring:**
```
Engagement Score = (Portal Use × 20%) + (Medication Adherence × 25%) +
                   (Visit Attendance × 20%) + (Health Outcomes × 20%) +
                   (Patient Satisfaction × 15%)

Scoring Scale:
0-20: Low engagement (urgent intervention needed)
21-40: Below average (additional support recommended)
41-60: Average (continue current approach)
61-80: Above average (consider for peer ambassador role)
81-100: High engagement (exemplary)
```

### Engagement Stratification

**Risk-Based Engagement Tiers:**
```
Tier 1: High-Risk Disengagement (Score 0-30)
├─ No portal login in 6+ months
├─ <50% medication adherence
├─ Multiple missed appointments
├─ Declining health metrics
└─ Intervention: Intensive outreach, care management

Tier 2: Moderate-Risk (Score 31-60)
├─ Sporadic portal use
├─ 50-80% medication adherence
├─ Occasional missed appointments
└─ Intervention: Regular check-ins, automated reminders

Tier 3: Engaged (Score 61-90)
├─ Regular portal use
├─ >80% medication adherence
├─ Attending appointments
└─ Intervention: Maintain engagement, consider programs

Tier 4: Highly Engaged (Score 91-100)
├─ Active portal usage
├─ >95% medication adherence
├─ All appointments attended
└─ Intervention: Peer support, advanced programs
```

## Engagement Program Metrics

### Program-Specific KPIs

**Medication Adherence Program:**
- Enrollment rate (target: >50%)
- Active participation rate (target: >70%)
- Medication Possession Ratio improvement (target: +10%)
- Cost per patient engaged: $X
- Cost per hospitalization avoided: $Y
- ROI (target: 3:1 or better)

**Patient Education Program:**
- Enrollment rate (target: >40%)
- Module completion rate (target: >75%)
- Knowledge gain assessment (target: >20%)
- Behavior change: Self-reported (target: >50%)
- Sustainability at 6 months (target: >60% sustained)

**Chronic Disease Management Program:**
- Enrollment (% of eligible patients)
- Active participation (% of enrolled)
- Clinical outcomes improvement
- Quality of life improvement
- Healthcare utilization reduction

### Return on Investment (ROI) Tracking

**Engagement Program Economics:**
```
Cost Calculation:
├─ Software/technology: $X/year
├─ Staff (coordinator, nurse): $X/year
├─ Incentives/rewards: $X/year
├─ Training and support: $X
└─ Administrative overhead: $X/year
Total Annual Cost: $X

Benefit Calculation:
├─ ED visit reduction: X visits × $1,200 cost avoided
├─ Hospital admission reduction: X admits × $10,000 avoided
├─ Readmission reduction: X readmits × $8,000 avoided
├─ Improved productivity: X employees × $500 value
└─ Quality improvement bonus/savings: $X
Total Annual Benefit: $X

ROI = (Benefit - Cost) / Cost × 100%
Payback period: X months
```

### Data Collection Methods

**Passive Data (No Patient Effort):**
- EHR and claims data extraction
- Portal usage analytics
- Appointment and medication systems
- Wearable device data
- Smart device transmissions

**Active Data (Patient Input):**
- Patient surveys (web, phone, paper)
- Patient interviews
- Focus groups
- Diary or journaling
- Interactive assessments

**Hybrid Approaches:**
- Surveys triggered by portal interactions
- Passive data drives survey timing
- Patient-reported outcomes (PROs) in EHR
- Automated data aggregation with periodic surveys

## Continuous Quality Improvement

### Engagement Improvement Cycle

```
1. Measure & Monitor
   └─ Collect engagement data consistently

2. Analyze & Identify
   ├─ Stratify patients by engagement level
   ├─ Identify barriers and gaps
   └─ Determine root causes

3. Plan Interventions
   ├─ Design engagement strategies
   ├─ Allocate resources
   └─ Set improvement targets

4. Implement & Execute
   ├─ Deploy interventions
   ├─ Train staff
   └─ Monitor adherence to protocol

5. Monitor & Evaluate
   ├─ Track process measures
   ├─ Assess outcome changes
   └─ Collect feedback

6. Sustain & Share
   ├─ Spread successful changes
   ├─ Update protocols
   └─ Celebrate improvements
```

## Challenges in Engagement Measurement

### Data Integration Challenges
- Multiple data sources (EHR, portal, claims, devices)
- Lack of interoperability standards
- Data quality and completeness issues
- Privacy and security in data aggregation

### Measurement Validity Issues
- Causality vs. correlation (engagement causes outcomes?)
- Time lag between intervention and outcome
- Confounding variables and attribution
- Short-term vs. long-term measurement

### Patient Privacy Considerations
- HIPAA compliance in data collection
- Consent for data use
- De-identification requirements
- Secure data transmission and storage
