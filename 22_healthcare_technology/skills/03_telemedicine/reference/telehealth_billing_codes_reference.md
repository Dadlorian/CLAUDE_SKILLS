# Telehealth Billing Codes Reference

## Overview
Comprehensive guide to CPT, HCPCS, and modifier codes for telehealth services, including Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial payer requirements.

---

## Key Modifiers for Telehealth

### Modifier 95
**Description**: Synchronous telemedicine service rendered via real-time interactive audio and video telecommunications system

**Usage**:
- Append to E/M codes and other services delivered via synchronous video
- Required by most payers to identify telehealth services
- Medicare accepts modifier 95 for telehealth services
- Use instead of GT modifier (older modifier being phased out)

**Example**: 99214-95 (Established patient office visit via telehealth)

---

### Modifier GT (Legacy)
**Description**: Via interactive audio and video telecommunications system

**Usage**:
- Older modifier for telehealth services
- Being replaced by modifier 95 in most jurisdictions
- Some payers still require GT
- Medicare prefers modifier 95

**Status**: Phasing out, check payer requirements

---

### Modifier GQ
**Description**: Via asynchronous telecommunications system

**Usage**:
- Store-and-forward telehealth services
- Not widely covered by Medicare (except Alaska, Hawaii federal telemedicine demonstration)
- Some Medicaid programs cover asynchronous services
- Dermatology, ophthalmology common use cases

**Example**: 99214-GQ (Asynchronous evaluation)

---

### Place of Service (POS) Codes

**POS 02 - Telehealth Provided Other Than in Patient's Home**:
- Patient at a clinical site (e.g., rural clinic) receiving telehealth from distant provider
- Traditional telehealth model
- Required for some payer claims

**POS 10 - Telehealth Provided in Patient's Home**:
- Patient located at home during telehealth visit
- Allowed by Medicare and most payers post-COVID-19
- More commonly used in current environment
- Some payers require specific documentation of home location

**POS 11 - Office**:
- Some payers want POS 11 with modifier 95 instead of POS 02/10
- Check payer-specific requirements
- Coding guidance varies by state and payer

## Evaluation and Management (E/M) Codes

### Office/Outpatient Visits - New Patient (99201-99205)

**99202**: Office visit, new patient, straightforward
- **Typical Time**: 15-29 minutes
- **Complexity**: Straightforward medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~1.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$76

**99203**: Office visit, new patient, low complexity
- **Typical Time**: 30-44 minutes
- **Complexity**: Low complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~2.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$113

**99204**: Office visit, new patient, moderate complexity
- **Typical Time**: 45-59 minutes
- **Complexity**: Moderate complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~3.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$169

**99205**: Office visit, new patient, high complexity
- **Typical Time**: 60-74 minutes
- **Complexity**: High complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~4.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$224

**Telehealth Usage**: Append modifier 95, use POS 10 (home) or POS 02

---

### Office/Outpatient Visits - Established Patient (99211-99215)

**99211**: Office visit, established patient, minimal (typically nurse visit)
- **Typical Time**: N/A
- **Complexity**: Minimal
- **Telehealth**: Rarely used via telehealth
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$25

**99212**: Office visit, established patient, straightforward
- **Typical Time**: 10-19 minutes
- **Complexity**: Straightforward medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~0.7 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$56

**99213**: Office visit, established patient, low complexity
- **Typical Time**: 20-29 minutes
- **Complexity**: Low complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~1.3 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$93
- **Most Common**: Frequently used telehealth code

**99214**: Office visit, established patient, moderate complexity
- **Typical Time**: 30-39 minutes
- **Complexity**: Moderate complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~2.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$132

**99215**: Office visit, established patient, high complexity
- **Typical Time**: 40-54 minutes
- **Complexity**: High complexity medical decision making
- **RVUs**: ~3.0 work RVUs
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$186

**Telehealth Usage**: Append modifier 95, use POS 10 or POS 02

### Telehealth-Specific E/M Codes

**G2012**: Brief communication technology-based service, 5-10 minutes
- **Description**: Virtual check-in with established patient
- **Requirements**: 5-10 minutes of communication, patient initiates, not related to visit in past 7 days
- **Medicare**: Covered
- **Reimbursement**: ~$14-25 depending on payer
- **Use Case**: Quick follow-up, symptom check, medication question

**G2010**: Remote evaluation of patient video/images
- **Description**: Review of recorded video/images submitted by patient
- **Requirements**: Established patient, not related to visit in past 7 days
- **Medicare**: Covered
- **Reimbursement**: ~$12-22
- **Use Case**: Dermatology rash photos, wound checks

**99421-99423**: Online digital E/M service, cumulative time during 7 days
- **99421**: 5-10 minutes
- **99422**: 11-20 minutes
- **99423**: 21+ minutes
- **Description**: Asynchronous patient portal communication
- **Requirements**: Established patient, cumulative time over 7-day period
- **Medicare**: Covered (as of 2024)
- **Use Case**: Patient portal messages requiring evaluation and management

- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$65
- **Telehealth**: Append modifier 95

**90834**: Psychotherapy, 45 minutes
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$97
- **Telehealth**: Most common therapy session code
- **Modifier**: 95

**90837**: Psychotherapy, 60 minutes
- **Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$130
- **Telehealth**: Append modifier 95

**90839/90840**: Psychotherapy for crisis

**90863**: Pharmacologic management

- **Add-on**: Billed with psychotherapy codes

## Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) Codes

### 99453 - Device Setup and Patient Education
**Description**: Remote monitoring of physiologic parameter(s), initial setup and patient education
**Frequency**: Once per device per episode of care
**Time Requirement**: None specified
**Requirements**:
- Education on device use
- Initial device transmission verified
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$19
**Cannot Bill With**: N/A
**Telehealth Modifier**: Not required (distinct service)

---

### 99454 - Device Supply with Daily Recording
**Description**: Remote monitoring of physiologic parameter(s), device supply with daily recording(s) or programmed alert(s) transmission
**Frequency**: Once per 30-day period per device
**Time Requirement**: None
**Requirements**:
- At least 16 days of data transmission in a 30-day period
- Automatic upload/transmission from device
- Medical device as defined by FDA
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$64
**Cannot Bill With**: CPT codes for devices providing <16 days of data
**Multiple Devices**: Can bill 99454 for each device (e.g., BP monitor and scale)

---

### 99457 - Treatment Management Services (First 20 Minutes)
**Description**: Remote physiologic monitoring treatment management services, first 20 minutes
**Frequency**: Once per calendar month
**Time Requirement**: At least 20 minutes of interactive communication
**Personnel**: Clinical staff (RN, pharmacist, etc.) under physician/QHP supervision
**Requirements**:
- Review of transmitted data
- Interactive communication with patient (phone, video, or secure messaging)
- Communication must be interactive (two-way)
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$51
**Cannot Bill With**: CCM codes for same time/activities
**Documentation**: Time, data reviewed, patient interaction, interventions

---

### 99458 - Additional Treatment Management Time
**Description**: Each additional 20 minutes of remote physiologic monitoring treatment management services
**Frequency**: Can bill multiple times per month
**Time Requirement**: Each additional 20-minute increment
**Requirements**: Same as 99457
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$42
**Usage**: Bill after 99457, for each 20-minute increment beyond initial 20 minutes
**Example**: 45 minutes total = 99457 + 99458

---

### 99091 - Physician/QHP Data Review
**Description**: Collection and interpretation of physiologic data stored and transmitted by patient
**Frequency**: Once per 30-day period
**Time Requirement**: At least 30 minutes of physician/QHP time
**Requirements**:
- Minimum 30 minutes of physician/QHP time (not clinical staff)
- Review and interpretation of data
- Data collection over minimum 30-day period
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$56
**Cannot Bill With**: 99457/99458 in same month by same provider
**Use Case**: Physician review when not billing 99457/99458

---

## Chronic Care Management (CCM) Codes

### 99490 - CCM (First 20 Minutes)
**Description**: Chronic care management services, first 20 minutes of clinical staff time
**Frequency**: Once per calendar month
**Time Requirement**: At least 20 minutes of clinical staff time
**Requirements**:
- Two or more chronic conditions expected to last 12+ months
- Comprehensive care plan
- 24/7 access to care
- Enhanced communication with patient/caregivers
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$43
**Cannot Bill With**: RPM codes for same time, TCM codes in first 30 days

---

### 99439 - Additional CCM Time
**Description**: Each additional 20 minutes of CCM
**Frequency**: Multiple times per month
**Time Requirement**: 20-minute increments
**Requirements**: Same as 99490
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$38

---

### 99487 - Complex CCM (First 60 Minutes)
**Description**: Complex chronic care management, first 60 minutes
**Requirements**:
- Same as 99490 PLUS
- Moderate to high complexity medical decision making
- Minimum 60 minutes of clinical staff time
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$94

---

### 99489 - Additional Complex CCM Time
**Description**: Each additional 30 minutes of complex CCM
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$47

---

## Principal Care Management (PCM) Codes

### 99424 - PCM (First 30 Minutes)
**Description**: Principal care management for single high-risk disease, first 30 minutes
**Frequency**: Once per calendar month
**Requirements**:
- Single complex chronic condition expected to last 12+ months
- Comprehensive care plan focused on single condition
- 30 minutes of clinical staff time
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$75
**Use Case**: Advanced heart failure, advanced COPD, advanced diabetes

---

### 99425 - Additional PCM Time
**Description**: Each additional 30 minutes of PCM
**Reimbursement** (Medicare 2024): ~$50

## Telephone and Online Services

### 98966-98968 - Telephone E/M by Qualified Healthcare Professional

**98966**: 5-10 minutes
**98967**: 11-20 minutes
**98968**: 21-30 minutes

**Requirements**:
- Qualified healthcare professional (physician, NP, PA, etc.)
- Patient or legally authorized person
- Not related to E/M service in previous 7 days
- Does not lead to E/M service within 24 hours or soonest available appointment

**Reimbursement**: Variable by payer, ~$14-50
**Medicare**: Covered for telephone visits
**Modifier**: Not typically required (telephone-specific codes)

---

### 99441-99443 - Telephone E/M by Physician

**99441**: 5-10 minutes
**99442**: 11-20 minutes
**99443**: 21-30 minutes

**Requirements**: Same as 98966-98968 but physician only
**Reimbursement**: Similar to 98966-98968
**Usage**: Physician telephone visits not otherwise billable as E/M

## Audio-Only Telehealth

### State Variations
Some states and payers cover audio-only (telephone) visits using standard E/M codes with specific modifiers:

**Common Approaches**:
1. E/M code + modifier 95 + modifier FQ (audio-only)
2. E/M code + modifier 93 (audio-only in some states)
3. Telephone E/M codes (98966-98968, 99441-99443)

**Payers Covering Audio-Only**:
- Medicare: Limited coverage for behavioral health
- Medicaid: Many state programs cover audio-only
- Commercial: Varies widely, check payer policy

## Billing Best Practices

### Documentation Requirements

**Essential Elements**:
1. **Location**: Document patient's location (city, state) at time of service
2. **Consent**: Document patient consent for telehealth
3. **Provider Location**: Document provider's location
4. **Technology Used**: Note platform/modality (video, audio, etc.)
5. **Clinical Content**: Same documentation as in-person visit
6. **Time**: For time-based codes, document start and stop times
7. **Medical Necessity**: Justification for telehealth vs in-person

**Time Documentation** (for RPM and CCM):
```
Example:
Date: 01/15/2025
Start Time: 10:00 AM
End Time: 10:23 AM
Total Time: 23 minutes
Activity: Reviewed BP and weight data from past 30 days. Spoke with patient via phone regarding elevated BP readings. Discussed medication adherence, lifestyle modifications. Adjusted lisinopril dosage per protocol. Patient verbalized understanding. Plan: Continue daily monitoring, follow-up in 2 weeks.
Personnel: Jane Doe, RN
```

---

### Common Billing Errors to Avoid

**Error 1**: Billing both RPM and CCM for same time
- **Solution**: Keep separate time logs, ensure no overlap

**Error 2**: Insufficient days of data for 99454
- **Solution**: Verify 16+ days of transmission before billing

**Error 3**: Not documenting interactive communication for 99457
- **Solution**: Document all patient interactions with time and content

**Error 4**: Using wrong Place of Service code
- **Solution**: Verify payer requirements for POS 02 vs POS 10 vs POS 11

**Error 5**: Missing modifier 95
- **Solution**: Add modifier 95 to all synchronous telehealth E/M codes

**Error 6**: Billing new patient visit for established patient
- **Solution**: Verify 3-year rule (new patient if no visit in 3+ years)

**Error 7**: Incorrect time calculation for time-based codes
- **Solution**: Use start/stop times, round per CPT guidelines

---

### Payer-Specific Requirements

**Medicare**:
- Modifier 95 for synchronous video
- POS 10 for patient's home
- Check Medicare Telehealth Services List for covered codes
- Two-way interactive audio-video required (except behavioral health audio-only)

**Medicaid**:
- Requirements vary by state
- Some states require prior authorization
- Check state Medicaid telehealth policy
- May have different modifier requirements

**Commercial Payers**:
- Verify coverage before visit
- Check if prior authorization needed
- Confirm modifier requirements (95, GT, GQ)
- Understand parity law applicability in state

## Compliance and Auditing

### Red Flags for Auditors

1. **Unusually high volume** of telehealth visits
2. **Very short or very long** visit times
3. **Consistent use of high-level codes** (99214, 99215)
4. **Billing RPM without qualifying diagnoses**
5. **Billing CCM and RPM for same time**
6. **Missing or inadequate documentation**
7. **Billing for non-covered services**

### Audit Preparation

**Maintain**:
- Complete medical records with all required elements
- Time logs for time-based services
- Consent forms
- Platform usage logs (date, time, duration)
- Communication logs
- Device transmission reports

**Regular Reviews**:
- Internal audits quarterly
- Provider education on documentation
- Coding compliance reviews
- Payer policy updates
