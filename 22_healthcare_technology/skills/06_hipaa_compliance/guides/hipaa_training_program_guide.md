# HIPAA Training Program Guide

## Training Requirements

**Who:** All workforce members (employees, volunteers, contractors)
**When:** Before PHI access, annually thereafter
**Content:** Privacy and security practices

## Training Program Structure

### 1. New Hire Training (Before PHI Access)

**General HIPAA Training (60 minutes):**
- HIPAA overview and importance
- Privacy Rule basics
- Security Rule basics
- Breach Notification Rule
- PHI definition and examples
- Permitted uses and disclosures
- Minimum necessary
- Individual rights
- Authorization requirements
- Incidental disclosures
- Sanction policy

**Security Awareness (30 minutes):**
- Password management
- Physical security
- Clean desk/clear screen
- Workstation security
- Email security
- Phishing awareness
- Social engineering
- Incident reporting
- Mobile device security
- Removable media handling

**Role-Specific Training (30-60 minutes):**
Based on job function

**Assessment:**
- Quiz (80% passing score)
- Certificate of completion
- Acknowledgment of policies

### 2. Annual Refresher Training

**All Workforce (45 minutes):**
- HIPAA updates and changes
- Policy reminders
- Recent incidents (lessons learned)
- Emerging threats
- Best practices review

**Assessment:**
- Updated quiz
- Certificate of completion

### 3. Role-Specific Training

**Clinical Staff:**
- Treatment, payment, healthcare operations
- Minimum necessary in clinical setting
- Patient rights requests
- Authorizations for research
- Psychotherapy notes
- Verbal disclosures

**Front Desk/Administrative:**
- Facility directory opt-out
- Sign-in sheets (alternatives)
- Confidential communications
- Calling patients in waiting room
- Leaving messages
- Verifying identity

**Billing/Claims:**
- Billing disclosures
- Collection agencies
- Minimum necessary
- Worker's compensation
- Subpoenas and court orders

**IT Staff:**
- Technical safeguards in depth
- Access control administration
- Audit logging
- Encryption
- Incident response
- Vulnerability management

**Management:**
- Oversight responsibilities
- Sanction policy application
- Breach reporting
- Individual rights requests handling
- Complaint procedures

### 4. Specialized Training

**Phishing Awareness:**
- Quarterly phishing simulations
- Immediate education on click
- Reporting suspicious emails
- Recent examples

**Incident Response:**
- Breach response team
- Detailed procedures
- Tabletop exercises
- Real scenarios

**Privacy Officer/Security Officer:**
- Advanced HIPAA training
- OCR guidance updates
- Incident investigation
- Audit preparation
- External courses/conferences

## Training Delivery Methods

**Online Training:**
- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Self-paced modules
- Automatic tracking
- Built-in assessments
- Certificate generation

**In-Person Training:**
- Instructor-led sessions
- Group discussions
- Q&A opportunities
- Role-playing scenarios
- Manual attendance tracking

**Hybrid Approach:**
- Online for general content
- In-person for role-specific
- Live webinars for updates

## Training Content Development

**Effective Training Includes:**
- Real-world examples
- Organization-specific scenarios
- Interactive elements
- Visual aids
- Case studies
- Practical exercises
- Clear takeaways

**Avoid:**
- Legal jargon without explanation
- Overly long modules (>60 min)
- Boring slide readings
- No practical application
- Irrelevant content

## Best Practices

1. Train BEFORE granting PHI access
2. Make training engaging and relevant
3. Use real examples from your organization
4. Keep modules under 60 minutes
5. Include knowledge checks throughout
6. Provide job aids and quick references
7. Offer training in multiple formats
8. Track completion diligently
9. Follow up on overdue training
10. Apply sanctions for non-compliance
11. Update training annually
12. Gather feedback and improve
13. Include training in onboarding checklist
14. Make training easily accessible
15. Recognize training completion

## Common Training Mistakes

1. **One-and-done approach**
   - Need annual refreshers

2. **Generic training only**
   - Need role-specific content

3. **No tracking system**
   - Cannot prove compliance

4. **Training after PHI access granted**
   - Must train first

5. **Boring, ineffective content**
   - Low retention, failed purpose

6. **No consequences for non-completion**
   - Training becomes optional

7. **Outdated content**
   - Regulatory changes not included

## Sample Training Outline

**Module 1: HIPAA Foundations (15 min)**
1.1 What is HIPAA?
1.2 Why HIPAA matters
1.3 Penalties for violations
1.4 Your role in compliance

**Module 2: Privacy Rule (20 min)**
2.1 What is PHI?
2.2 Permitted uses and disclosures
2.3 Minimum necessary
2.4 Individual rights
2.5 Notice of Privacy Practices

**Module 3: Security Rule (15 min)**
3.1 ePHI protection
3.2 Access controls
3.3 Audit logging
3.4 Physical security
3.5 Workstation use

**Module 4: Breach Notification (10 min)**
4.1 What is a breach?
4.2 Reporting incidents
4.3 Breach examples
4.4 Your responsibilities

**Module 5: Practical Application (10 min)**
5.1 Daily scenarios
5.2 What to do/not do
5.3 How to report concerns
5.4 Q&A

**Assessment (10 min)**
- 20-question quiz
- 80% passing score
- Certificate upon completion

Total: 80 minutes
