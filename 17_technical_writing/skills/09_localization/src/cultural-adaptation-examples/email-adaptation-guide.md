## Key Adaptation Principles

### Language Formality

| Market | Formality | Greeting | Closing |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------|
| US | Casual | "Hi", "Hey" | "Cheers", emoji |
| Germany | Very Formal | "Sehr geehrte/r" | "Mit freundlichen Grüßen" |
| Japan | Formal | "[Name] 様" | "CloudSync Pro チーム" |
| Middle East | Formal | "السيد/السيدة" | "مع أطيب التحيات" |
| Brazil | Semi-formal | "Prezado/a" | "Abraços" |

### Information Density

| Market | Style | Detail Level | Structure |
|--------|-------|--------------|-----------|
| US | Concise | Low-Medium | Bullet points, short |
| Germany | Comprehensive | High | Numbered, detailed |
| Japan | Very detailed | Very High | Numbered, explanations |
| Middle East | Formal | Medium-High | Structured sections |
| Brazil | Warm | Medium | Natural, flowing |

### Security and Privacy Emphasis

| Market | Emphasis | Approach |
|--------|----------|----------|
| US | Moderate | Brief mention, link to policy |
| Germany | Very High | Detailed, GDPR compliance prominent |
| Japan | Very High | Extensive explanation, certifications |
| Middle East | High | Data protection details |
| Brazil | Moderate | Privacy policy link |

### Call-to-Action Style

| Market | CTA Style | Button Style | Multiple CTAs? |
|--------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| US | Urgent, FOMO | Bright color | Yes (multiple) |
| Germany | Professional | Neutral color | Single strong CTA |
| Japan | Polite, option | Subtle color | Multiple (optional) |
| Middle East | Respectful | Professional | Single |
| Brazil | Friendly | Warm color | Yes |

## Compliance Considerations

### Email Headers and Footers

All emails must include:
- Sender identification
- Unsubscribe option
- Physical address (varies by region)
- Privacy policy link
- Terms of service link

### Region-Specific Requirements

**EU (GDPR)**:
- Explicit consent for each email type
- Easy unsubscribe in every email
- Privacy policy in local language
- Data processing terms

**US (CAN-SPAM)**:
- Subject line accuracy
- Clear sender identification
- Physical mailing address
- Unsubscribe link
- Honor opt-out within 10 days

**Japan**:
- Company information required
- Unsubscribe option mandatory
- Language must be Japanese (not English)
- Contact information required

**Middle East**:
- Respect business hours and holidays
- Islamic calendar awareness
- Local language required
- Formal tone expected
