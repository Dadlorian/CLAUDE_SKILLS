## Section 1: Advanced Principles (8 questions)

### Question 1.1
**Type**: Multiple Choice
**Weight**: 3%

Which of the following best describes "cognitive load" in the context of tutorial design?

A) The mental effort required to complete the tutorial
B) The total number of concepts presented
C) The file size of tutorial resources
D) The reading level required

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Cognitive load refers to mental effort. Effective tutorials manage cognitive load by presenting information appropriately.

---

### Question 1.2
**Type**: Scenario Analysis
**Weight**: 3%

**Scenario**: You're writing a tutorial on API authentication. You have three complex concepts to teach: OAuth 2.0, tokens, and scopes. One learner complained the tutorial was overwhelming.

What should you do?

A) Remove one of the concepts to simplify
B) Break the content into smaller modules with progression
C) Extend the tutorial significantly to explain everything
D) Add more code examples without reorganizing

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Breaking content into manageable modules reduces cognitive load while maintaining completeness.

---

### Question 1.3
**Type**: True/False
**Weight**: 2%

"Learning objects" are independent, reusable chunks of content designed to teach specific concepts.

**Correct Answer**: True
**Explanation**: Learning objects are modular, allowing flexible reuse and combination.

---

### Question 1.4
**Type**: Multiple Choice
**Weight**: 2%

What is "scaffolding fade" in advanced tutorial design?

A) Removing all support at the end
B) Gradually reducing guidance as learner competence increases
C) Replacing structured content with unguided exploration
D) Simplifying content inappropriately

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Scaffolding fade gradually reduces support, developing learner independence.

---

### Question 1.5
**Type**: True/False
**Weight**: 3%

Intrinsic motivation (internal drive to learn) should be prioritized over extrinsic motivation (external rewards) in tutorial design.

**Correct Answer**: True
**Explanation**: Intrinsic motivation promotes deeper learning and long-term retention.

---

### Question 1.6
**Type**: Multiple Choice
**Weight**: 3%

The "zone of proximal development" (ZPD) in tutorial context means:

A) The area next to the computer setup
B) The gap between independent performance and performance with guidance
C) The geographic region where tutorials are popular
D) The physical workspace for learning

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: ZPD is the learning space where guidance is most effective.

---

### Question 1.7
**Type**: True/False
**Weight**: 2%

Interleaving (mixing different concepts during practice) is less effective than blocked practice for learning.

**Correct Answer**: False
**Explanation**: Research shows interleaving improves long-term retention over blocked practice.

---

### Question 1.8
**Type**: Scenario Analysis
**Weight**: 3%

**Scenario**: Your tutorial data shows 40% of learners stop at step 5 of a 10-step process. The step itself is simple.

What's the most likely issue?

A) The step is too easy
B) Cumulative cognitive load has overwhelmed learners
C) The next steps are more interesting
D) Learners are distracted

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: By step 5, accumulated complexity may overwhelm learners. Revisit organization and scaffolding.

## Section 3: Content Strategy & Mapping (9 questions)

### Question 3.1
**Type**: Multiple Choice
**Weight**: 3%

What is a "learning objective" and why is it important?

A) A marketing goal for the tutorial
B) A specific, measurable outcome that learners should achieve
C) A topic to be covered
D) A deadline for completion

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Learning objectives clarify what learners should be able to do after completion.

---

### Question 3.2
**Type**: Short Answer
**Weight**: 3%

Write a well-formed learning objective using the format: "After completing this tutorial, learners will be able to [action verb] [specific behavior]."

**Sample Acceptable Answer**: "After completing this tutorial, learners will be able to implement OAuth 2.0 authentication in a Node.js application."

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Uses measurable action verb (implement, create, configure, deploy)
- Specifies clear, observable outcome
- Includes context (Node.js application)

---

### Question 3.3
**Type**: True/False
**Weight**: 2%

Content mapping involves organizing learning objectives hierarchically from foundational to advanced concepts.

**Correct Answer**: True
**Explanation**: Proper sequencing supports learning by building on prior knowledge.

---

### Question 6.2
**Type**: Short Answer
**Weight**: 3%

List three accessibility best practices for tutorials.

**Sample Acceptable Answers**:
1. Provide alt text for all images
2. Use semantic HTML/proper heading hierarchy
3. Ensure sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 for text)
4. Include transcripts for videos
5. Use clear, simple language

*Any three valid practices accepted*
