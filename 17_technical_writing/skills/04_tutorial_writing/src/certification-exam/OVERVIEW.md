# Tutorial Writing Certification Program

## Certification Levels

This comprehensive certification program validates expertise in tutorial writing across three progressive levels. Each level builds upon the previous, ensuring well-rounded competency.

### Level 1: Fundamentals (Beginner)
**Focus**: Core tutorial writing principles and basic structure
- **Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Passing Score**: 70%
- **Questions**: 40 multiple choice
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Target Audience**: New tutorial writers

### Level 2: Practitioner (Intermediate)
**Focus**: Advanced techniques, audience targeting, and quality standards
- **Duration**: 90 minutes
- **Passing Score**: 75%
- **Questions**: 50 mixed format
- **Prerequisites**: Level 1 certification
- **Target Audience**: Working tutorial writers with 3+ months experience

### Level 3: Expert (Advanced)
**Focus**: Leadership, mentoring, and system-level design
- **Duration**: 120 minutes
- **Passing Score**: 80%
- **Questions**: 60 mixed format + practical project
- **Prerequisites**: Level 2 certification
- **Target Audience**: Senior technical writers and educators

## Exam Policies

### Registration
- Valid email address required
- Payment of exam fee
- Acceptance of exam terms
- System requirements verification

### Attempt Rules
- Level 1: Unlimited attempts (7 day waiting period between failures)
- Level 2: 3 attempts within 12 months
- Level 3: 2 attempts within 12 months

### Scoring
- Weighted scoring based on question difficulty
- Partial credit for some question types
- Automated grading for objective questions
- Expert review for subjective components

### Retakes
- Candidates may retake after waiting period
- Previous scores kept in record
- Highest score used for certification
- Detailed feedback provided after each attempt
