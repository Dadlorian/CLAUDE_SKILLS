## Part I: Examination (60 minutes, 40% weight)

### Section 1: Strategic Leadership (8 questions)

#### 1.1: Strategic Vision
**Type**: Essay
**Weight**: 3%

You are appointed to lead tutorial writing initiatives for a growing technology company. The company currently has 200+ fragmented tutorials with no consistent standards, varying quality levels, and outdated content.

Write a brief strategic plan (300-400 words) that addresses:
1. How you would assess current state
2. Immediate priorities (first 3 months)
3. Long-term vision (12-24 months)
4. Success metrics
5. Stakeholder management approach

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Demonstrates understanding of organizational change
- Prioritizes strategically (quick wins + long-term value)
- Includes measurable goals
- Shows leadership awareness
- Realistic and feasible within constraints

---

#### 1.2: Scenario Analysis
**Type**: Scenario-based Short Answer
**Weight**: 3%

**Scenario**: Your organization writes tutorials for enterprise customers with varying technical expertise (from business users to data scientists). Marketing wants a single tutorial covering all use cases. Your team lacks capacity for multiple versions.

What approach would you recommend?

**Quality Indicators**:
- Acknowledges trade-offs
- Proposes innovative solutions (modular content, personas)
- Addresses capacity constraints
- Considers customer satisfaction
- Explains reasoning

---

#### 1.3: Portfolio Strategy
**Type**: Short Answer
**Weight**: 2%

Define your criteria for evaluating whether an idea warrants a new tutorial versus an update to existing content.

---

#### 1.4: Market Analysis
**Type**: Multiple Choice with Justification
**Weight**: 2%

Competitor analysis shows a rival company offers 5x more tutorials than your organization. What's your strategic response?

A) Rapidly create tutorials to match volume
B) Focus on tutorial quality, user satisfaction, and outcomes
C) Analyze competitor tutorials for best practices
D) B and C

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Quality and smart benchmarking beat quantity; matching volume without strategy is wasteful.

## Part II: Practical Project (60 minutes, 60% weight)

### Project Brief

You are tasked with creating a comprehensive tutorial system for a scenario below. This is a real-world project simulating your expert-level responsibilities.

---

### Scenario: Choose One (or Custom)

#### Option A: Enterprise SaaS Platform
A mature SaaS analytics platform needs a complete tutorial overhaul. Current state:
- 150+ outdated tutorials
- Users spanning business analysts to data engineers
- Multiple deployment models (cloud, on-premise, hybrid)
- Languages: English primary, expanding to 5 additional languages
- Compliance requirements: HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2
- High support ticket volume indicates tutorial gaps

#### Option B: Developer Tool/SDK
A developer-focused infrastructure tool is in rapid evolution. Current state:
- Fast-moving product with monthly updates
- Community contributions to documentation
- Highly technical audience with diverse experience levels
- Open-source ecosystem with ecosystem partners
- Need for integration tutorials with 3rd-party services

#### Option C: Consumer Technology
A consumer-facing mobile application needs better onboarding and feature discovery. Current state:
- 5M+ monthly active users
- High user churn, especially for advanced features
- Limited internal resources
- AI/ML-powered recommendations
- Accessibility critical for diverse user base

---

### Project Deliverables

Submit a comprehensive proposal addressing:

#### 1. Assessment Report (15 minutes, 10 points)
Analyze the current state:
- User research findings (personas, needs, pain points)
- Content audit (what exists, quality, gaps)
- Technology assessment (platforms, tools, integrations)
- Competitive analysis
- Key opportunities and risks

#### 2. Strategic Roadmap (15 minutes, 15 points)
Outline 12-month plan:
- Phase 1 (0-3 months): Foundation
- Phase 2 (4-6 months): Expansion
- Phase 3 (7-9 months): Optimization
- Phase 4 (10-12 months): Scale & Sustain
- Resource requirements
- Budget estimation
- Risk mitigation

#### 3. System Design (15 minutes, 15 points)
Define the tutorial ecosystem:
- Architecture and technology stack
- Content management approach
- Versioning and localization strategies
- Analytics and measurement framework
- Quality assurance processes
- Scalability plan

#### 4. Sample Tutorial (10 minutes, 12 points)
Create one high-quality tutorial demonstrating:
- Your strategic approach
- Target audience understanding
- Quality standards
- Accessibility compliance
- Innovativeness

#### 5. Team & Execution Plan (5 minutes, 8 points)
Specify:
- Team composition and roles
- Training/onboarding approach
- Communication and governance
- Success metrics and KPIs
