# Comprehensive Outro Template

## Purpose
High-converting outro that encourages subscriptions, drive engagement, and guide viewers to next content.

**Duration:** 45-90 seconds

---

## STANDARD OUTRO SCRIPT (VERSION A - Casual)

### Visual Sequence
1. **0:00-10 seconds:** Summary of video content
2. **10-30 seconds:** Call-to-action for subscription and engagement
3. **30-60 seconds:** Suggested next videos to watch
4. **60-90 seconds:** Final thank you and sign-off

### Voiceover Script

**[SECTION 1: SUMMARY - 10 seconds]**
"So there you have it—[brief 1-2 sentence summary of key learning].

This knowledge will [benefit statement for implementing it]."

**[SECTION 2: CTA - 20 seconds]**
"If you found this helpful, I'd really appreciate it if you could:

One—Like this video so YouTube knows it's valuable
Two—Subscribe to the channel so you don't miss future videos like this
Three—Drop a comment below with your biggest takeaway or question

Your engagement helps me know what content you want to see more of."

**[SECTION 3: NEXT STEPS - 30 seconds]**
"If you're interested in [related topic], check out this video where I cover [topic], or watch this playlist on [related series].

Both are linked in the description and on screen right now. They're great next steps in your [subject area] journey."

**[SECTION 4: CLOSING - 10 seconds]**
"Thanks so much for watching. I'll see you in the next video. Keep [inspiring message related to channel theme]!"

See you in the next one!"

## VISUAL ELEMENTS FOR OUTROS

### On-Screen Elements
```
SUBSCRIBE BUTTON:
- Position: Lower right corner
- Size: 200x60 pixels
- Animation: Fade in at 0:30, stay until end
- Design: Branded color, clear text

VIDEO RECOMMENDATIONS (Cards):
- Position: Center or lower right
- Number: 2-3 cards maximum
- Animation: Slide in from sides
- Duration: Visible for 15-20 seconds
- Include: Thumbnail, title, duration

LINKS/RESOURCES TEXT:
- Position: Lower third of screen
- Font: Sans-serif, 32-48pt
- Color: High contrast with background
- Duration: Show each link for 5-7 seconds
- Style: Clickable boxes or bullet points

CALL-TO-ACTION BUTTONS:
- Subscribe button
- Play next video button
- Visit link button
- Comment prompt button
```

### Design Specifications
```
BACKGROUND:
- Option 1: Host on simple background
- Option 2: Compilation of video clips
- Option 3: Branded template or studio set
- Option 4: Animated pattern or motion graphics

BRANDING:
- Logo in corner (small, non-intrusive)
- Consistent color scheme
- Branded fonts throughout
- Channel name visible

MUSIC:
- Background music: Uplifting, positive tone
- Volume: -18 to -16 dB under voiceover
- Duration: Extends past voiceover with 1-second fade
- Tempo: 120-130 BPM (matches energy of content)

TRANSITIONS:
- Fade between sections
- Subtle whoosh sounds between cuts
- Keep moving but not jarring
```

## CALL-TO-ACTION STRATEGY

### Primary CTAs (Choose 1-2)
1. **Subscribe:** "Subscribe for weekly videos on [topic]"
2. **Watch Next:** "Watch the next video to continue learning"
3. **Download:** "Download the free [resource] linked below"
4. **Join Community:** "Join our Discord/community at [link]"
5. **Share:** "Share this with someone who needs it"

### Secondary CTAs
1. **Like:** "Like this video if it was helpful"
2. **Comment:** "Comment with your biggest takeaway"
3. **Visit Website:** "Learn more at [website]"
4. **Follow Social:** "Follow me on [platform] for daily tips"
5. **Read Blog:** "Read the full article at [link]"

### CTA Timing
- **First CTA:** 20-30 seconds into outro
- **Second CTA:** 50-60 seconds into outro
- **Third CTA:** 70-80 seconds into outro
- **Final CTA:** 80-90 seconds (closing)

### CTA Buttons (YouTube)
- Place buttons strategically (not covering important content)
- Use at least one subscription button
- Include suggested next video
- Make buttons large enough to click easily

## BEST PRACTICES FOR OUTROS

1. **Be authentic:** Don't sound desperate or over-the-top
2. **Keep energy:** Match energy from rest of video
3. **Be specific:** Say exactly what to do, not generic CTAs
4. **Add value:** Provide real resources, not just self-promotion
5. **Make it scannable:** Viewers should understand immediately
6. **Test variations:** See what drives most engagement
7. **Keep it brief:** Don't let outro feel like it drags
8. **Use graphics:** Reinforce CTAs with clear on-screen elements
9. **Repeat key points:** Remind viewers of main takeaway
10. **Thank viewers:** Express genuine gratitude

---

## COMMON OUTRO MISTAKES

- [ ] Outro too long (watching metrics shows viewers drop off)
- [ ] No clear CTAs (viewers don't know what to do)
- [ ] Too many CTAs (overwhelming, viewers do nothing)
- [ ] Mismatched energy (sad outro after enthusiastic video)
- [ ] Generic message (same outro for every video)
- [ ] Poor sound quality (drops in outro quality)
- [ ] Distracting visuals (eye-catching but unrelated to content)
- [ ] No suggested next video (missed opportunity for watch time)
- [ ] Thanking without context (generic 'thanks for watching')
- [ ] No branding (doesn't reinforce channel identity)

## AUDIENCE PSYCHOLOGY

### Why People Stay for Outros
- Curiosity about next video
- Desire to support creator
- Habit/pattern recognition
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Social proof (others are subscribing)

### How to Increase Engagement
- Create anticipation for next video
- Make subscription feel like joining community
- Offer exclusive benefits for subscribers
- Use pattern recognition (same time, same place)
- Show appreciation for engagement
- Make commenting easy (specific question to answer)

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC OUTRO STRATEGIES

### YouTube
- Use end screens and cards for CTAs
- Include subscription button
- Suggest 2-3 videos
- Add playlist link
- Include community post link

### TikTok/Instagram Reels
- Keep outro under 15 seconds
- Use trending sounds
- Include obvious call-to-action
- Direct to profile/link in bio
- Use stickers for engagement

### LinkedIn
- Keep outro brief (5-10 seconds)
- Encourage connection/follow
- Direct to company website
- Ask for commentary/insights
- Professional tone

### Podcast
- Audio-only, no screen elements
- Mention links verbally and repeat
- Invite feedback/reviews
- Promote next episode
- Include show name and host

## ANALYTICS TO TRACK

### Engagement Metrics
- Click-through rate on subscribe button
- Suggested video click rate
- Average view duration (especially outro segment)
- If viewers drop off before outro
- Comment rate
- Share rate

### Use Data to Improve
- If viewers leave before outro, make it more engaging
- If few click next video, suggest better content
- If no comments, make question more specific
- If few subscriptions, strengthen CTA
- If CTA doesn't match audience, adjust messaging
