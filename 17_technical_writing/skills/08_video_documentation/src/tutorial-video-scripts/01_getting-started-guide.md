## VIDEO SCRIPT

### SEGMENT 1: INTRODUCTION (0:00 - 1:00)
**Duration:** ~1 minute | **Voiceover Speed:** Normal

**VISUAL ELEMENTS:**
- Animated title slide with product logo
- Fade to workspace/dashboard

**VOICEOVER:**
"Welcome to [Product Name]! I'm [Your Name], and in this getting started guide, I'll walk you through everything you need to know to get up and running in the next 10 minutes.

Whether you're completely new to [Product Name] or looking to refresh your knowledge, this video covers the essentials: setting up your account, navigating the interface, and completing your first workflow.

Let's get started!"

**PACING NOTES:**
- Allow 2-second pause after title card
- Use enthusiastic but professional tone
- Speak clearly, avoiding filler words

---

### SEGMENT 2: ACCOUNT SETUP (1:00 - 3:00)
**Duration:** ~2 minutes | **Visual:** Screen recording of setup process

**VISUAL ELEMENTS:**
- Screen recording: Visit [product-url]
- Highlight sign-up button
- Walk through registration form
- Email verification process
- First login to dashboard

**VOICEOVER:**
"First, let's set up your account. Navigate to [product-url] in your browser. You'll see the sign-up button prominently displayed on the homepage.

Click 'Sign Up' and fill in your basic information: your email address and a secure password. Make sure to use a password with at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Next, verify your email by checking your inbox for the confirmation link. Click that link, and you're ready to log in to your new account!

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard for the first time."

**CURSOR ACTIONS:**
- Move slowly (not jerky)
- Pause for 1 second on important fields
- Click with clear intention
- Highlight text fields being filled

**PACING NOTES:**
- Slow down during form filling (2 seconds per field)
- Speed up between form sections
- Allow pauses for user comprehension

**PACING NOTES:**

### SEGMENT 4: FIRST WORKFLOW (5:30 - 10:00)
**Duration:** ~4.5 minutes | **Visual:** Step-by-step workflow demonstration

**VISUAL ELEMENTS:**
- Creating a new project
- Adding basic configuration
- Inviting a team member
- Completing first action
- Viewing results

**VOICEOVER:**
"Now, let's walk through your first workflow. This will take just a few minutes and shows the core capabilities of [Product Name].

Step one: Create a new project. Click the 'Create' button in your sidebar and select 'New Project.'

You'll see a modal appear. Give your project a name—something descriptive, like 'My First Project.' Add an optional description explaining the project's purpose.

Next, choose your project type. For beginners, I recommend starting with the 'Standard' template. This gives you a solid foundation without overwhelming options.

Click 'Create Project,' and within seconds, your new project appears in your dashboard.

Step two: Configure basic settings. Click on your project to open it. You'll notice a settings panel on the right side. Set your project visibility—most users start with 'Private,' which means only you can see it initially.

Step three: Invite a collaborator. Even if you're working solo, inviting a team member is a valuable exercise. Click the 'Team' tab in your project, then 'Invite Member.' Enter your colleague's email address and select their role—'Contributor' is a good starting point.

Your colleague will receive an email invitation they can accept to join the project.

Step four: Complete your first action. This varies by your specific [Product Name] type, but generally, you'll create, upload, or configure your first item. This might be a task, a document, a file, or a setting, depending on your use case.

Step five: Review your work. Take a moment to view what you've created. Explore the preview, output, or results. Notice how your actions are reflected in the interface and any generated outputs or dashboards."

**INTERACTION TECHNIQUES:**
- Pause 2 seconds between major steps
- Mouse over UI elements before clicking
- Speak slowly during navigation
- Use phrases like "Notice how..." to draw attention
- Celebrate completion with positive reinforcement

**PACING NOTES:**
- This is the most important segment; don't rush
- Allow 1-2 seconds after each completed action
- Build momentum gradually
- End on a success note

## Speaker Notes & Tips

### Tone & Delivery
- Friendly but professional
- Speak slightly slower than normal conversation
- Pause frequently for understanding
- Avoid "um," "uh," and other filler words
- Use varied intonation to maintain engagement

### Visual Pacing
- Don't move the mouse constantly
- Give viewers time to read text
- Pause before clicking to show intent
- Highlight important elements
- Use zoom for small text

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Speaking too quickly
- Clicking too rapidly without pausing
- Cluttered screen with too many open tabs
- Poor microphone audio quality
- Text too small to read
- Cursor disappearing or moving erratically
- Not explaining why you're doing something

### Recovery Techniques
- If you misspeak, pause and repeat correctly
- If you make a mistake, stop and restart that section
- Keep a sip of water nearby for throat clearing
- Do a few test runs before recording
- Record in short segments rather than one long take

---

## Accessibility Considerations
- Provide detailed captions for all dialogue
- Describe important visual elements for screen readers
- Use high-contrast colors for text
- Avoid rapid flashing or strobing effects
- Provide alternative text descriptions for images
- Consider providing transcripts for users who prefer reading
