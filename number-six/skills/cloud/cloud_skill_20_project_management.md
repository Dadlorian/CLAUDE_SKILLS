## Specialized Knowledge Areas

### Agile Metrics That Matter

**Team-Level Metrics**
- **Velocity**: Story points completed per sprint (trend over time, not absolute comparison)
- **Sprint Commitment Reliability**: Percentage of committed work completed
- **Cycle Time**: Time from start to done for work items (mean, median, percentiles)
- **Lead Time**: Time from request to delivery
- **Throughput**: Number of items completed per time period
- **Defect Rate**: Bugs per story point or per sprint
- **Team Happiness**: Survey-based team morale and satisfaction
- **Psychological Safety**: Edmondson's framework assessment

**Flow Metrics (Kanban)**
- **Work In Progress (WIP)**: Current active items per stage
- **Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD)**: Visual representation of WIP over time
- **Cycle Time Distribution**: Percentile-based delivery predictions (50th, 85th, 95th)
- **Throughput Trend**: Items completed over time
- **Flow Efficiency**: Value-add time / Total cycle time
- **Blocked Time**: Time items spend in blocked state

**Program/Portfolio Metrics**
- **Program Predictability**: Percentage of planned features delivered
- **Release Train Velocity**: Aggregate velocity across teams
- **Program Increment (PI) Objectives Achievement**: SAFe metric for PI success
- **Portfolio Flow**: Value stream throughput and efficiency
- **Business Value Delivered**: Revenue, cost savings, customer satisfaction impact
- **Technical Debt Trend**: Increasing, stable, or decreasing
- **Team of Teams Flow Efficiency**: Cross-team collaboration effectiveness

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

**Scrum Anti-Patterns**
- ❌ **Scrum-but**: "We do Scrum, but..." (skipping ceremonies, no retrospectives)
- ❌ **Zombie Scrum**: Going through motions without achieving business value
- ❌ **Command and Control**: Scrum Master as project manager dictating tasks
- ❌ **Sprint Commitment as Fixed Contract**: Treating estimates as guarantees
- ❌ **Skipping Sprint Reviews**: Not getting stakeholder feedback regularly
- ❌ **Theater Retrospectives**: Retrospectives without real change
- ❌ **Technical Debt Accumulation**: Never addressing quality issues

**Agile Transformation Anti-Patterns**
- ❌ **Agile Waterfalls**: Big upfront planning with agile labels
- ❌ **Fake Agile**: Superficial adoption without cultural change
- ❌ **Tool-First Approach**: Buying tools before changing mindset
- ❌ **Ignoring Culture**: Forcing practices without addressing organizational impediments
- ❌ **Copy-Paste Frameworks**: Implementing SAFe/LeSS without adaptation
- ❌ **Lack of Leadership Support**: Agile teams in command-and-control organization

**Program Management Anti-Patterns**
- ❌ **Dependency Hell**: Tightly coupled teams with constant blocking
- ❌ **Death by Coordination**: More time in meetings than doing work
- ❌ **Ivory Tower PMO**: PMO creating bureaucracy instead of enabling delivery
- ❌ **Vanity Metrics**: Tracking metrics that don't drive improvement
- ❌ **Hero Culture**: Relying on individuals instead of sustainable processes
- ❌ **Risk Theater**: Maintaining risk registers without real risk management

## Advanced Techniques

### Value Stream Mapping

When optimizing delivery processes:

1. **Identify the Value Stream**: Pick specific product/feature flow
2. **Map Current State**: Document all steps, handoffs, wait times, rework
3. **Calculate Metrics**:
   - Process Efficiency = Value-add time / Total lead time
   - Identify biggest delays and bottlenecks
4. **Design Future State**: Eliminate waste, reduce batch sizes, automate
5. **Create Improvement Roadmap**: Prioritized changes with owners and timelines

### Monte Carlo Simulation for Forecasting

When predicting project completion:

1. **Collect Historical Data**: Past cycle times or throughput rates
2. **Run Simulations**: 10,000+ iterations sampling from historical distribution
3. **Generate Forecasts**: Percentile-based predictions (50%, 85%, 95% confidence)
4. **Update Regularly**: Re-run with latest data for improved accuracy
5. **Communicate Uncertainty**: Show probability distributions, not single dates

**Example**: "Based on the last 50 items completed, there is an 85% chance we'll complete the remaining 30 items within 6-8 weeks."

### Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF)

When prioritizing backlog in SAFe:

**Formula**: WSJF = Cost of Delay / Job Size

**Cost of Delay** = User-Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction/Opportunity Enablement

**Process**:
1. Score each component (1-10 or Fibonacci)
2. Sum for total Cost of Delay
3. Estimate Job Size (story points or t-shirt sizes)
4. Calculate WSJF score
5. Prioritize highest WSJF first

### Evidence-Based Management (Scrum.org)

Measure value delivery across four key value areas:

1. **Current Value (CV)**: Value delivered today
   - Revenue per employee, customer satisfaction, product cost ratio
2. **Unrealized Value (UV)**: Potential value not yet captured
   - Market gap, customer satisfaction gap
3. **Ability to Innovate (A2I)**: Capability to deliver new value
   - On-product time ratio, defect trends, technical debt
4. **Time to Market (T2M)**: Speed of value delivery
   - Release frequency, cycle time, lead time

Use these to guide strategic decisions and improvement investments.
