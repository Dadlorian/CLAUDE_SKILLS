## Section 4: Feedback Collection

### Feedback Methods

**In-App Feedback**:
- [ ] Feedback button in product
- [ ] Hotspot surveys at key moments
- [ ] Session recording tools (Clarity, Fullstory)
- [ ] Heat maps (Hotjar, Crazy Egg)

**Surveys**:

**Survey 1: Initial Impressions (Day 1-2)**
```markdown
1. How easy was it to get started? (1-5 scale)
2. What's your first impression? (Open-ended)
3. Any immediate issues or questions? (Open-ended)
4. One word to describe your experience (Open-ended)
```

**Survey 2: Feature Feedback (Week 1)**
```markdown
1. Which features have you tried? (Checklist)
2. For each feature: [Rate usefulness 1-5, any issues?]
3. What's missing? (Open-ended)
4. Any bugs or problems? (Open-ended)
5. Would you recommend to a colleague? (Yes/No/Maybe)
```

**Survey 3: Deeper Usage (Week 2)**
```markdown
1. How often are you using? (Daily/Weekly/Few times/Once)
2. Has this solved your [problem]? (Explain how)
3. What's the most valuable feature? (Open-ended)
4. What needs improvement? (Open-ended)
5. Net Promoter Score: [0-10 scale]
```

**Survey 4: Launch Readiness (Week 4)**
```markdown
1. Would you use this if available today? (Yes/No/Maybe)
2. What would increase your adoption? (Open-ended)
3. Would you be a reference customer? (Yes/No/Maybe)
4. Any final feedback or concerns? (Open-ended)
5. NPS: [0-10 scale]
```

**Customer Interviews**:

**Interview Guide** (30-45 min):

```markdown
### Opening (5 min)
1. What's your role and organization?
2. What problem are you trying to solve?

### Feature Testing (15-20 min)
3. What features have you tried so far?
4. What's worked well?
5. What's been confusing or difficult?
6. Have you achieved your goal yet?

### Comparison (5 min)
7. How does this compare to [alternatives]?
8. What's your single biggest reason to use this?

### Feedback (5 min)
9. What one thing would make biggest difference?
10. Would you recommend to a colleague?

### Closing (5 min)
11. Any final thoughts or concerns?
12. Would you be willing to be a reference?
```

**Interview Recruitment**:
- [ ] Target: [X] interviews during beta
- [ ] Mix of: Heavy users, casual users, non-adopters
- [ ] Timing: [When to schedule interviews]
- [ ] Incentive: [Free upgrade, gift, etc.]

### Feedback Organization

**Feedback Log Template**:

| Date | Participant | Feedback Type | Category | Item | Priority | Action |
|------|-------------|---------------|----------|------|----------|--------|
| [Date] | [Name] | [Survey/Interview/In-app] | [Theme] | [Feedback] | [Critical/High/Med/Low] | [What to do] |

**Feedback Categories**:
- Usability: [Issues with interface or workflow]
- Performance: [Speed, responsiveness issues]
- Features: [Missing features or limitations]
- Value: [Questions about value delivery]
- Documentation: [Help docs unclear]
- Bugs: [Functional issues]
- Positive: [What's working well]
- Other: [Miscellaneous]

**Feedback Prioritization**:
- **Critical**: Blocking core use or causing negative experience (fix immediately)
- **High**: Important for value delivery or user satisfaction (ship week 2-3)
- **Medium**: Nice-to-have improvements (consider for roadmap)
- **Low**: Edge cases or suggestions (document for future)

## Section 7: Customer Champions & Advocacy

### Identifying Beta Champions

**Champion Criteria**:
- High engagement (weekly+ usage)
- Positive sentiment (NPS 9-10)
- Willing to advocate (answered yes to "recommend")
- Relevant segment (good reference customer)
- Articulate (can communicate value)

**Champion Identification Process**:
- [ ] Week 1-2: Monitor usage and feedback for potential champions
- [ ] Week 2: Identify top 5-10 candidates
- [ ] Week 3: Conduct deeper interviews with champions
- [ ] Week 4: Formalize champion relationships

### Champion Programs

**Reference Customer Program**:
- **Benefit to champion**: Free service, dedicated support, recognition
- **Benefit to you**: Sales references, testimonials, case studies
- **Commitment**: Be willing to speak with 2-4 sales prospects per month
- **Duration**: [Ongoing/Year 1]

**Case Study Program**:
- **Benefit to champion**: Free service, publicity, thought leadership
- **Benefit to you**: Detailed customer success story for marketing
- **Commitment**: 3-4 hour interview, review and approval of write-up
- **Duration**: [Timing]

**Public Testimonial**:
- **Benefit**: Recognition, quote visibility
- **Commitment**: Provide 1-2 sentence quote or quote
- **Usage**: Website, marketing materials, case studies

**Beta Ambassador Program** (if applicable):
- **Role**: Help recruit additional customers, spread word
- **Benefits**: Special pricing, priority support
- **Commitment**: [What you're asking them to do]

### Champion Communication

**Initial Champion Outreach**:
- Personal message from CEO or product leader
- Thank them for being great partner
- Explain importance and ask about deeper involvement
- Offer specific program (reference, case study, ambassador)
- Next steps

**Ongoing Champion Communication**:
- Monthly check-ins
- Beta updates and sneak peeks
- Exclusive webinars or training
- Recognition in company communications
- Special events or customer advisory board
