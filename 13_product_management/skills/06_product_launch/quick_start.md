# Product Launch: 30-Minute Quick Start Guide

Plan and execute launches that create momentum and drive adoption.

---

## Your 30-Minute Roadmap

- **Minutes 1-5**: Understand launch tiers (major vs. feature vs. minor)
- **Minutes 6-15**: Build your 8-week launch timeline
- **Minutes 16-25**: Create launch messaging and go-to-market plan
- **Minutes 26-30**: Set success metrics and communication channels

---

## 5 Essential Concepts Every PM Needs

### 1. Launch Tier Framework: Different Launches Need Different Energy
Not every launch requires press releases and marketing blitzes. Match your effort to importance.

**Tier 1 (Major Launch)**: New product, major pivot, market expansion
- 8-12 weeks prep, executive involvement, press release, sales training
- Example: Slack launch, Figma launch, Stripe launch

**Tier 2 (Significant Feature)**: Major new capability, major expansion to existing product
- 4-8 weeks prep, marketing focus, sales enablement, customer briefings
- Example: Slack threading, Notion database updates

**Tier 3 (Minor Update)**: Incremental features, improvements, bug fixes
- 1-2 weeks prep, release notes, in-app announcement
- Example: UI improvements, performance updates

**Actionable**: Classify your upcoming launch. What tier is it? That determines your timeline and resource allocation.

### 2. Launch Timeline: 8 Weeks Before Launch
A well-executed 8-week timeline prevents last-minute chaos and ensures cross-functional alignment.

**Weeks 1-2**: Strategy & Planning
**Weeks 3-4**: Messaging & Content Creation
**Weeks 5-6**: Marketing Setup & Sales Enablement
**Weeks 7-8**: Final Testing & Communication

**Actionable**: Identify your launch date. Count back 8 weeks. Block that time for launch planning across team.

### 3. Go-to-Market Strategy: Who, What, When, Where, Why
GTM answers: Who's the target customer? What's the core message? When/where do we reach them? Why should they care?

**Actionable**: Write 1-page GTM plan: target customer + value prop + key messages + channels (email, blog, webinar, social, sales) + timeline.

### 4. Beta Programs Build Champions and Validate
Before wide launch, run a structured beta with selected customers. They validate product-market fit and become advocates.

**Actionable**: Identify 20-30 power users willing to test 1-2 weeks before launch. Get their feedback. Make final improvements.

### 5. Launch Success Metrics Aren't Vanity
Don't just track "downloads" or "signups." Track engagement, retention, and business metrics that correlate to long-term success.

**Actionable**: For your launch, define 3 success metrics (adoption, retention, revenue impact). Set targets for 30/60/90 days post-launch.

## First Steps Checklist

### Week 1: Launch Strategy
- [ ] Confirm launch date
- [ ] Identify launch tier (Major/Feature/Minor)
- [ ] Define target customer for this launch
- [ ] Identify 1-2 customer use cases (stories to tell)
- [ ] List success metrics: What does success look like?

### Week 2: Messaging & Positioning
- [ ] Core value prop: "This enables customers to..."
- [ ] Key messages (3-5 talking points)
- [ ] Customer quote or story (social proof)
- [ ] Competitive differentiation: "Unlike [competitor], we..."
- [ ] Success criteria for launch: (adoption target, retention %, revenue impact)

### Week 3-4: Content Creation
- [ ] Blog post (500-800 words, clear why customers should care)
- [ ] Email copy (subject line, body, CTA)
- [ ] Social media posts (3-5 posts, different angles)
- [ ] Sales deck update (new features, use cases, objection handling)
- [ ] Support documentation (help articles, FAQs, how-to guides)
- [ ] In-app announcement (tooltip, banner, release notes)

### Week 5-6: Launch Setup
- [ ] Marketing calendar: Email send times, social posting schedule
- [ ] Sales enablement: Team training, demo environment, talking points
- [ ] Customer success brief: Who are early adopters? Onboarding plan?
- [ ] Support prep: Are support docs ready? Team trained on new feature?
- [ ] Performance testing: Will site/app handle traffic spike?
- [ ] Analytics setup: Are tracking events configured? Dashboards created?

### Week 7: Beta Testing (Optional but Recommended)
- [ ] Select 20-30 power users for beta
- [ ] Brief them: "Here's what we're testing, feedback deadline is X"
- [ ] Gather feedback: Bugs, clarity, missing features
- [ ] Fix critical issues
- [ ] Get testimonial or quote from beta users

### Week 8: Launch!
- [ ] Final checklist: All content ready? Analytics set up? Team briefed?
- [ ] Send to power users first (24 hours early if possible, get testimonials)
- [ ] Announce via email, blog, social, in-app, sales
- [ ] Sales team reaches out to target accounts
- [ ] Monitor: Feedback, bugs, adoption, engagement
- [ ] Share progress: Daily launch report first week

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Launching Without Beta Testing
You announce and immediately get "This is broken" feedback.

**Fix**: Run 1-2 week beta with 20-30 power users. Fix obvious issues before broad launch.

### Mistake 2: Poor Communication Leads to Confusion
Customers miss the launch. They don't understand what's new or why they should care.

**Fix**: Create communication plan. Email, blog, social, in-app announcement. Repeat message across channels.

### Mistake 3: Sales Team Unprepared
Sales doesn't know about the launch or how to position it. Opportunity lost.

**Fix**: Sales training 2 weeks before launch. Demo environment ready. Sales deck updated. Role-play objection handling.

### Mistake 4: No Metric Tracking
Launch happens, but you don't know if it worked. Can't learn for next launch.

**Fix**: Define 3 success metrics before launch. Track daily first week, weekly for 90 days. Review learnings.

### Mistake 5: Launch Fatigue (Announcing Too Much)
You announce the same feature across 10 channels and overwhelm everyone.

**Fix**: Pick 3-4 primary channels based on where target customers hang out. Quality over quantity.

## Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy Template

```
# [Feature] Go-to-Market Plan

## Target Customer
- Primary: [Role, company size, pain point]
- Secondary: [Additional segment]

## Customer Problem
- [Problem being solved]
- [How significant is this problem?]
- [What do they do today?]

## Solution & Value Prop
- [How our feature solves the problem]
- [Quantified benefit if possible: saves time, increases efficiency, etc.]

## Key Messages
1. [Message 1]
2. [Message 2]
3. [Message 3]

## Launch Channels & Timeline
- Email: [When, target list]
- Blog: [When, link]
- Social: [When, 3 posts planned]
- Sales: [When training, who reaches out]
- In-app: [When, format]

## Success Metrics (30/60/90 days)
- Adoption: X% of users activate feature
- Retention: X% still using in week 4
- Revenue impact: $X expanded revenue

## Competitive Positioning
- Unique vs. [Competitor]: [Differentiation]

## Timeline
- Beta: [Dates]
- Announcement: [Date]
- Full availability: [Date]
```

## Launch Day Checklist

**24 hours before:**
- [ ] All content approved and scheduled
- [ ] Analytics tracking verified
- [ ] Beta feedback incorporated
- [ ] Support team trained
- [ ] Sales team ready

**Launch day (Hour by Hour):**
- [ ] Hour 1: Email goes to power users (4-6 hours before broad announcement)
- [ ] Hour 6: Blog published
- [ ] Hour 6.5: Social posts go live
- [ ] Hour 7: In-app announcement live
- [ ] Hour 8: Sales outreach begins
- [ ] Hour 8+: Monitor customer feedback, respond to questions
- [ ] End of day: Launch report (adoption, feedback, issues)

**First week:**
- [ ] Daily standup: Feedback, bugs, adoption metrics
- [ ] Fix critical bugs same day
- [ ] Respond to customer questions within 4 hours
- [ ] Gather testimonials from early users
- [ ] Adjust messaging if needed based on feedback

## Success Indicators

- [ ] Launch messaging is clear and resonates with target customers
- [ ] Adoption rate meets or exceeds targets in first 30 days
- [ ] Support team isn't overwhelmed (not more than 10x normal volume)
- [ ] Early users are engaged and seeing value
- [ ] You've gathered testimonials and success stories for future marketing
- [ ] Sales team closed deals influenced by launch
- [ ] You documented learnings for next launch
