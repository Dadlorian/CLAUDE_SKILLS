# Executive Update Template

## Overview

Use this template for regular executive updates (weekly, monthly, or quarterly). Adapt the frequency and content based on your stakeholder's preference.

## General Writing Guidelines

### Tone & Style

- **Professional but Not Stiff:** Be clear and direct without jargon
- **Honest:** Acknowledge problems, don't sugarcoat
- **Action-Oriented:** Focus on what you've done and will do
- **Data-Driven:** Use numbers, metrics, evidence
- **Executive-Friendly:** Respect their time, get to the point

### Structure

- **Lead with Headlines:** Executives skim, so put key info first
- **Use Sections:** Break content into clear sections
- **Highlight Key Numbers:** Use bold or callout boxes
- **Provide Context:** Explain what numbers mean, not just what they are
- **Call Out Decisions Needed:** Make asks crystal clear

### Format

- **One-Page Maximum for Weekly:** More length = less likely to be read
- **Use Bullets:** Not paragraphs
- **Use Visual Hierarchy:** Headers, bold, white space
- **Consistent Format:** Same format every time makes updates easier to skim
- **Appendices for Detail:** Put supporting materials in appendices, not the main text

### What NOT to Do

- ❌ Bury the headline
- ❌ Include information they don't need
- ❌ Use jargon or acronyms without defining
- ❌ Passive language ("mistakes were made")
- ❌ Lengthy explanations when short ones work
- ❌ Multiple pages when one would do
- ❌ Unclear asks or decisions

### What TO Do

- ✅ Start with status and most important update
- ✅ Be concise
- ✅ Use clear, active language
- ✅ Own the narrative (we did/will do)
- ✅ Include metrics and data
- ✅ Make asks explicit
- ✅ Respect their time

---

## Using These Templates

1. **Choose the Right Template:** Weekly, monthly, quarterly, or crisis
2. **Customize for Your Stakeholder:** Adjust level of detail, frequency, focus areas
3. **Keep It Current:** Update before sending
4. **Be Honest:** Include challenges, not just wins
5. **Make Asks Clear:** Don't assume they know what you need
6. **Follow Up in Writing:** If discussing in meeting, send written summary
7. **Use Consistently:** Same format every time makes scanning easier

---

## Questions to Answer in Every Update

- What is the current status?
- What was accomplished since last update?
- What are we working on next?
- What decisions do you need to make?
- What resources do we need?
- What could go wrong?
- Am I on track?

If you answer these, your updates will be valuable.
