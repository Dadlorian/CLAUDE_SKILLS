## How to Use This Template

1. **Fill in current state** (Section 2): Establish baseline metrics
2. **Document loops** (Section 3): Map how growth happens
3. **Set targets** (Section 4): Define stage-specific goals
4. **Test assumptions** (Section 5): Validate or refute
5. **Plan experiments** (Section 7): Design tests
6. **Review monthly**: Track against targets
7. **Update quarterly**: Adjust based on learnings

This is a living document. Update it as you learn more about your growth levers.
