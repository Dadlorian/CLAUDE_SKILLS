# Annual Planning Calendar Reference

## 12-Month Roadmap Planning Rhythm

This calendar outlines the optimal timing for roadmap planning activities across a full year. Adapt based on your fiscal calendar (calendar year vs. fiscal year).

---

## Q4 (Oct-Dec): Annual Strategy & Planning

### September (Pre-Planning Phase)

**Week 1-2: Data Gathering**
- Compile annual performance metrics
  - Revenue/growth targets vs. actuals
  - Product engagement metrics
  - Customer satisfaction (NPS, retention)
  - Engineering velocity and quality
- Competitive intelligence update
- Market trend analysis
- Email customer feedback synthesis request

**Week 3-4: Team Input Collection**
- Engineering: Ask for tech debt opportunities
- Sales: Top customer requests and churn reasons
- Support: Most common user issues
- Design: UX/usability improvements needed
- Marketing: Positioning and messaging feedback
- Customer Success: Health of key accounts

**Deliverables**:
- Annual performance summary document
- Competitive landscape update
- Consolidated feedback from all functions
- Tech debt and infrastructure assessment

---

### October (Strategy Development)

**Week 1-2: Executive Alignment**
- CEO/Founder one-on-ones (20 min each, 3-5 conversations)
- Sales/Customer Success leadership review
- Engineering leadership discussion
- Finance: Budget and resource constraints
- Document current thinking on next year

**Week 3-4: Strategy Workshop**
- 2-day off-site or working sessions
- Participants: CEO, VP Product, VP Eng, VP Sales, VP Marketing, Head of Design
- Agenda:
  - Annual review presentation (40 min)
  - Problem identification (60 min)
  - Strategic theme ideation (60 min)
  - Theme prioritization and refinement (60 min)
  - Success metric definition (40 min)

**Outputs from Workshop**:
- 3-5 strategic themes for coming year
- Estimated business impact for each
- Initial OKR drafts
- Rough timeline and sequencing
- Identified risks and dependencies

**Sample Output**:
```
STRATEGIC THEMES FOR 2025

1. Enterprise GTM Acceleration
   - Build security/compliance features
   - Multi-org administration
   - Advanced reporting
   - Impact: $2M+ ARR from enterprise customers

2. AI-Powered Personalization
   - Recommendation engine
   - Smart notifications
   - Predictive analytics
   - Impact: 25% engagement increase

3. Developer Ecosystem
   - Public API (GraphQL)
   - Documentation and SDKs
   - Integrations marketplace
   - Impact: 10% new customer acquisition via integrations

4. Product Reliability & Scale
   - Infrastructure upgrade
   - Performance optimization
   - Reliability engineering
   - Impact: 99.99% uptime SLA

5. Customer Education Platform
   - In-app learning and tutorials
   - Academy/certification program
   - Knowledge base improvements
   - Impact: 20% faster time-to-value
```

---

### November (Detailed Roadmap Development)

**Week 1-2: Deep Dive Planning**
- For each strategic theme:
  - Brainstorm 8-12 potential initiatives
  - Estimate effort and timeline
  - Identify dependencies and sequencing
  - Map to quarterly buckets (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)

**Week 3: OKR Refinement**
- Define Company OKRs (CEO + leadership)
- Each function defines departmental OKRs aligned to company
- Product defines Product OKRs mapped to themes
- CFO confirms budget allocation

**Week 4: Roadmap Review & Adjustment**
- Early review with board advisors (if applicable)
- Customer advisory board input (if available)
- Final strategic theme and roadmap adjustments
- Finalize Now/Next/Later roadmap document

**Key Outputs**:
- Detailed strategic roadmap (Now/Next/Later format)
- Company OKRs and Product OKRs
- Quarterly breakdowns with theme focus
- Risk and dependency map
- Resource allocation by theme/quarter

---

### December (Communication & Alignment)

**Week 1-2: All-Hands Presentation & Cascade**
- Full company kickoff with CEO
  - Strategy narrative (30 min)
  - Key themes and OKRs (30 min)
  - Q&A (30 min)
- Department-specific presentations (VP Product, VP Eng, VP Sales)
- Cross-functional planning sessions begin

**Week 3: Holiday Buffer (Limited Activity)**
- Document Q1 roadmap in detail
- Finalize presentations for January
- Minimal planning activities

**Week 4: Preparation for Q1 Kickoff**
- Prepare quarterly kickoff materials
- Finalize Q1 sprint planning
- Schedule January planning sessions

---

## Q1 (Jan-Mar): Q1 Planning & Kickoff

### January (Q1 Kickoff & Execution Begins)

**Week 1: Organizational Kickoff**
- Company-wide all-hands
  - Strategy narrative (refresher)
  - Q1 priorities and outcomes
  - Resource allocation and team assignments
- Department kickoffs

**Week 2-3: Deep Dive Planning**
- Cross-functional working sessions
  - Weekly: Design + Engineering + Product alignment on Now items
  - Technical architecture review
  - Design/UX spec review
  - Identify and resolve blockers
- Create sprint roadmaps

**Week 4: Execution Begins**
- First sprint of year kicks off
- Weekly standup cadence established
- Progress tracking systems live

**Standing Meetings Established**:
- Weekly Product + Design sync (30 min)
- Weekly Product + Engineering sync (30 min)
- Weekly cross-functional standup (15 min)
- Biweekly Product Review (60 min)

---

### February-March (Q1 Execution)

**Ongoing Activities**:
- Weekly progress tracking
- Customer feedback loops (interviews, surveys)
- Competitive monitoring
- Technical debt assessment
- Weekly team syncs and planning

**Mid-Quarter Checkpoint** (Mid-Feb):
- Review Q1 progress against OKRs
- Course corrections as needed
- Identify blockers and risks
- Adjust sprint roadmap for remaining 6 weeks

**Q1 Planning for Next Quarter Begins** (Late March):
- Start light Q2 planning
- Validate Q2 theme priorities
- Engineering estimates for Q2 Now items
- Sales/CS input on customer needs for Q2

---

## Q2 (Apr-Jun): Mid-Year Adjustments & Planning

### April (Q2 Kickoff & Adjustments)

**Week 1-2: Q1 Closure & Learning**
- Q1 retrospective with full product team
  - What went well?
  - What did we learn?
  - How does it impact Q2/annual strategy?
- Executive review of Q1 results vs. OKRs
- Market and competitive updates
- Customer health review

**Week 3-4: Q2 Kickoff**
- Refine Q2 detailed roadmap
- Finalize Q2 OKRs based on Q1 learning
- Announce Q2 priorities to organization
- Engineering detailed planning for Q2 Now items

---

### May-June (Q2 Execution & Mid-Year Planning)

**Ongoing**: Same cadence as Q1 (weekly syncs, biweekly reviews)

**Mid-Year Business Review** (Late May):
- Executive summary of year-to-date progress
- H2 strategy adjustment (if needed)
- Board update/investor update
- Half-year customer health review

**H2 Planning Begins** (Late June):
- Gather H2 feedback from all functions
- Validate H2 theme priorities
- Early estimates for Q3/Q4 work
- Sales/CS input on H2 priorities

---

## Q3 (Jul-Sep): H2 Planning & Execution

### July-August (Q3 Execution)

**Q3 Kickoff**: Similar to Q1/Q2 (week 1-2)

**Ongoing**: Same cadence (weekly syncs, biweekly reviews)

**Back-to-School/August Slump**: Plan light activity (team holidays, focus on execution)

---

### September (Annual Review Begins)

**Week 1-2: Planning Begins**
- This kicks off the Q4 strategy and planning cycle
- Data gathering for annual review (see Q4 section above)
- Set up strategy workshop for October

**Note**: This overlaps with Q3 execution. Keep Q3 team focused on delivery while PM leadership shifts attention to Q4 planning.

## Meeting Cadences Throughout the Year

### Weekly Meetings (Every Week, All Year)

**Product + Design Alignment (30 min)**
- Review design specs, UX decisions
- Problem-solve design challenges
- Discuss user feedback and insights
- Owner: Head of Product Design

**Product + Engineering Sync (30 min)**
- Technical progress updates
- Blockers and dependencies
- Scope/timeline discussions
- Tech debt and reliability items
- Owner: VP Engineering

**Cross-Functional Standup (15 min)**
- Quick status across all functions
- Highlight blockers needing cross-team help
- Week's priorities
- Owner: VP Product

### Bi-Weekly Meetings (Every 2 Weeks, All Year)

**Product Review (60 min)**
- Progress on current quarter initiatives
- Data and metrics review
- Customer feedback highlights
- Decisions and approvals needed
- Participants: Core product team + leadership

**Quarterly Business Review (60 min, 4x per year)**
- Full performance review vs. OKRs
- Strategic priorities adjustment
- Market and competitive updates
- Planning for next quarter
- Participants: Executive team + VPs

### Monthly Meetings (Monthly, All Year)

**Customer Advisory Board Sync** (30 min, if applicable)
- Share upcoming features with key customers
- Gather feedback on direction
- Build advocates

**Executive Stakeholder Update** (45 min)
- CEO + VP Product + VP Eng + VP Sales + VP Marketing
- Month progress vs. roadmap
- Key metrics and KPIs
- Issues and decisions

**Product Team Retrospective** (60 min, first week of month)
- What went well this month?
- What didn't go well?
- Process improvements
- Team morale and well-being

### Quarterly Meetings (4x per year)

**Strategic Planning Session** (4-6 hours)
- Review last quarter
- Adjust strategy based on learning
- Detailed planning for next quarter
- OKR refinement
- Participants: Product, Engineering, Design, Sales, Marketing, Leadership

**Board Update** (30-60 min, if applicable)
- Quarterly business review for board
- Strategic updates
- Financial performance
- Risks and opportunities

## Tips for Effective Annual Planning

1. **Start Early**: October planning seems early but gives time for refinement
2. **Involve the Right People**: CEO, VPs, customer voices, not just PM
3. **Use Data**: Back every theme with customer and business data
4. **Create Artifacts**: Documented strategy is shared strategy
5. **Communicate Widely**: Share roadmap beyond just exec team
6. **Build Flexibility**: Budget 20-30% capacity for adjustments
7. **Iterate Quarterly**: Annual plan is guide, not gospel
8. **Reflect Regularly**: Monthly and quarterly retrospectives improve planning
9. **Respect Cadences**: Consistent timing builds culture and trust
10. **Over-Communicate**: Share more than you think you need to
