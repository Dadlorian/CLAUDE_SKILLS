# Quarterly Planning Guide

A step-by-step guide for executing quarterly planning cycles. This enables you to maintain strategic direction while staying flexible to learning and market changes.

---

## Why Quarterly Planning Works

**Annual plans are too rigid**:
- Too much uncertainty 12 months out
- Market changes require adjustment
- Learning from execution should inform future priorities
- Teams need clarity on near-term work

**Monthly plans are too granular**:
- Not enough strategic thinking
- Constant context switching
- Hard to complete meaningful work

**Quarterly planning is the sweet spot**:
- ✅ Detailed enough for execution clarity
- ✅ Flexible enough to adjust based on learning
- ✅ Aligned with business cycles and team rhythms
- ✅ Allows strategic work to surface and be prioritized
- ✅ Creates natural review moments

---

## Quarterly Planning Cadence

### Timeline

```
QUARTER EXECUTION

Week 1-2   → Kickoff & Launch
Week 3-12  → Execution
Week 12    → Checkpoint (optional mid-quarter)
Week 13    → Closure, Learning, & Planning

TRANSITION

Week 14 → Final adjustments, next quarter prep
Week 15 → Soft launch of next quarter
Week 16 → Full kickoff of next quarter
```

## Phase 1: Preparation (Week 13 of Current Quarter)

### Goals
- Gather learnings from current quarter
- Understand performance vs. goals
- Identify inputs for next quarter
- Prepare materials for planning session

### Activities

**Week 13: Gather Data**

**1. Quarterly Performance Review**
```markdown
## Q1 2025 Performance Summary

### Roadmap Delivery
- Initiatives completed: 5/6 (83%)
- On-time delivery: 5/6 (83%)
- Status of delayed items: [details]

### Success Metrics Review
Strategic Theme: Improve Onboarding
- Target: Increase D30 retention to 75%
- Actual: 72% (3 points short, but up from 65%)
- Learning: [What drove the improvement?]

Strategic Theme: Enable Collaboration
- Target: 30% of teams using collab features
- Actual: 22% (below target)
- Learning: Adoption slower than expected; maybe need better onboarding for this feature?

### Business Metrics
- Revenue: $1.3M ARR (on track to $3M for year)
- Customer Growth: 95 new customers (on track)
- Churn: 5% (target was 4%, slightly above target)
- NPS: 46 (target 45, achieved)

### Wins & Celebrations
- [Feature A] shipped early and exceeded usage expectations
- [Feature B] received excellent customer feedback
- Team delivered at high quality with no major incidents

### Learning & Insight
- Collaboration features need better onboarding
- Video tutorial strategy is working (users watch 80% of time)
- User segmentation matters - advanced users adopt different features
- [Other key learning]

### Issues & Blockers
- [Issue 1] delayed delivery by 2 weeks (how will you prevent?)
- [Issue 2] customer feedback contradicted our assumptions (how will you adjust?)

### What We'd Do Differently
- [Improvement 1]
- [Improvement 2]

### Recommendation
We achieved 2/3 primary objectives. Onboarding/activation progress was good.
Collaboration adoption needs attention. Recommend doubling down on collaboration
education in Q2 and/or adjusting go-to-market strategy.
```

**2. Customer Feedback Synthesis**
- Review support tickets and top issues
- Analyze feature requests (volume, frequency, sources)
- Digest customer interviews from quarter
- Identify emerging customer needs

**3. Competitive Landscape Update**
- What did competitors ship in Q1?
- Did it change competitive dynamics?
- Are there new threats?
- Did we gain or lose ground?

**4. Cross-Functional Input**
Send input request to:
- Sales: Deal blockers, win/loss analysis, top customer needs
- Support: Top issues, common complaints, pain points
- Engineering: Tech debt assessment, infrastructure needs, quality concerns
- Design: UX opportunities, capability gaps
- Marketing: GTM challenges, positioning gaps

**Example Input Request**:
```markdown
## Q2 Planning Input Request

We're planning Q2 (April-June) and want your input. Please respond by [DATE] with:

### Top 3 Items for Next Quarter
What 3 things should we prioritize for Q2?

### Top Problem You Hear
What's the #1 problem customers/users mention to you?

### Unexpected Learnings
What surprised you in Q1 that we should know about?

### Process Feedback
What should we do differently in our planning/execution?

Please keep to 1 page max.
```

## Phase 2: Planning Session (Week 14)

### Goal
Refine annual roadmap for next quarter and finalize specific priorities.

### Timing
Full day or two half-days

### Participants
- VP Product + Product Managers
- VP Engineering + Engineering leadership
- VP Sales / Head of Customer Success
- Head of Design
- CEO or leadership stakeholder
- Data/Analytics lead (if available)

### Agenda

**Morning (3-4 hours): Learning & Strategy**

```
Time: 8:00-8:15   Opening (15 min)
- Why we're here
- Goal for the day
- Any urgent issues to discuss first?

Time: 8:15-8:45   Q1 Performance Review (30 min)
- Summary of Q1 results vs. goals
- What went well?
- What didn't?
- Key learnings

Time: 8:45-9:30   Customer & Market Input (45 min)
- Sales perspective (top customer needs, competitive threats)
- Support perspective (top issues and requests)
- Customer feedback themes
- Competitive landscape update

Time: 9:30-10:00  Break

Time: 10:00-11:00 Strategic Adjustment (60 min)
- Are our annual themes still valid?
- Do we need to adjust based on Q1 learning?
- Any new opportunities we should address?
- Pressure test: Did anything change the strategy?

Time: 11:00-12:00 Q2 Priorities (60 min)
- Review Next from annual roadmap (what was planned for Q2)
- Discuss: Is it still right? Should we adjust?
- Identify Q2 focal areas (3-5 key outcomes)
```

**Afternoon (2-3 hours): Detailed Planning**

```
Time: 1:00-1:45   Capability Assessment (45 min)
- Engineering: Team capacity, constraints, estimates
- Design: Scope and feasibility review
- Any dependencies between initiatives?

Time: 1:45-2:30   Q2 Detailed Roadmap (45 min)
- List all Q2 initiatives (what are we building?)
- Sequence by quarter (early Q2, mid Q2, late Q2)
- Assign owners
- Identify key success metrics

Time: 2:30-3:00   Dependencies & Risks (30 min)
- Cross-team dependencies
- External dependencies (customers, vendors, partners)
- Risks that could impact delivery
- Mitigation plans

Time: 3:00-3:30   Next Steps & Alignment (30 min)
- Confirm deliverables
- Communication plan
- Team-specific planning next week
- Assign who will own creating final roadmap document
```

### Expected Outputs from Planning Session

- [ ] Q2 focal areas / outcomes (3-5 key areas)
- [ ] List of initiatives for Q2 (with owners and timeline)
- [ ] Confirmed sequencing (early/mid/late Q2)
- [ ] Success metrics for each initiative
- [ ] Identified risks and mitigation plans
- [ ] Cross-team dependencies documented
- [ ] Decision on any strategic adjustments

### Facilitation Tips

**Make Good Decisions**:
- Start with data (Q1 performance, customer feedback)
- Distinguish between what we want vs. what's feasible
- Have capacity planning done (don't assume infinite resources)
- Make explicit trade-offs: "If we do X, what gets delayed?"

**Build Alignment**:
- Invite perspective from all functions
- Resolve disagreements with data (not hierarchy)
- Call out unspoken concerns
- Get explicit agreement before leaving

**Keep Energy High**:
- Take breaks every 60-90 minutes
- Vary activities (discussion, writing, presenting)
- Celebrate Q1 wins
- End on positive note with clear path forward

## Phase 3: Finalize & Document (Week 14-15)

### Goal
Create finalized Q2 roadmap document and OKRs.

### Activities

**1. Write Quarterly Roadmap Document**

```markdown
# Q2 2025 Product Roadmap

## Quarterly Theme
[If applicable: Is there one overarching theme for Q2?]

## Learning from Q1
[Brief summary of learnings that shaped Q2 priorities]

## Q2 Strategic Focus
[3-5 key outcomes we're pursuing]

## Q2 Roadmap

### [Initiative Name 1]
**Owner**: [Name]
**Timeline**: [Early Q2 / Mid Q2 / Late Q2]
**What**: [Customer-focused description]
**Why**: [Problem being solved]
**Success Metric**: [How we'll measure]
**Dependencies**: [What needs to happen first?]
**Risk**: [What could go wrong?]

### [Initiative Name 2]
[Same format]

## What We're Not Doing
[What have we explicitly decided NOT to do, and why?]

## Success Metrics
[Quantified targets for each strategic area]

## Risks & Mitigations
[Key risks and how we'll address them]
```

**2. Develop OKRs**

```markdown
## Q2 2025 OKRs

### Product OKRs
[Aligned to annual OKRs, specific targets for Q2]

**Objective: Improve Collaboration Adoption**
- KR1: Reach 30% of teams using collaboration features
- KR2: Increase average team size to 1.4 users (from 1.2)

**Objective: Maintain Revenue Growth**
- KR1: Acquire 30 new customers
- KR2: Expand to $1.5M ARR (on track to $3M annually)

### Engineering OKRs
[What engineering team will achieve]

### Sales OKRs
[Sales-specific goals]

### Design OKRs
[Design-specific goals]

## How We'll Track OKRs
- Weekly check-ins on progress
- Monthly deep-dive reviews
- Quarterly assessment of final achievement
```

**3. Create Communication Plan**

```markdown
## Q2 Communication Plan

### All-Hands Announcement
- When: [Date]
- Who: CEO or VP Product
- Format: 30-min presentation + Q&A
- Key message: [One sentence summary]

### Department Kickoffs
- Engineering: [Specific date/time]
- Sales: [Specific date/time]
- Design: [Specific date/time]

### Stakeholder Briefings
- Customers: Roadmap preview (what will be new?)
- Board: Q2 strategy and expectations
- Partners: Any partnership opportunities in Q2

### Documents to Share
- Full Q2 roadmap document
- Q2 OKRs
- Success metrics dashboard
- FAQ addressing common questions

### Timeline
- Week 15: Internal kickoff
- Week 16: Full team launch
- Week 17: External communication (if applicable)
```

## Phase 4: Kickoff & Launch (Week 15-16)

### All-Hands Presentation

**Format**: 30-45 minutes

**Agenda**:
```
Opening (5 min)
- Q1 celebration and wins

Performance Review (5 min)
- How we did vs. goals
- Key learnings

Q2 Strategy (10 min)
- Q2 focus areas
- Why these priorities?
- How they connect to annual strategy

Q2 Roadmap (10 min)
- Key initiatives
- Timeline
- What customers/users will see

Success Metrics (5 min)
- How we'll measure Q2
- OKRs and targets

Q&A (10 min)
- Questions from team
- Address concerns
```

### Department-Specific Kickoffs

Schedule 60-90 min sessions with each team:

**Engineering Kickoff**:
- Deep technical dive on Q2 work
- Architecture and design decisions
- Capacity and timeline
- Blockers and risks
- Sprint planning

**Sales Kickoff**:
- Features to sell
- Customer value propositions
- Launch dates and GTM support
- Competitive messaging
- Deal enablement

**Design Kickoff**:
- User experience direction
- Design challenges
- Design system updates
- Research needs
- Collaboration points with product/eng

## Phase 5: Execution (Weeks 16-26)

### Weekly Cadence

**Weekly Product Sync** (30 min):
- What's shipping this week?
- Any blockers?
- Customer feedback updates
- Roadmap status

**Weekly Cross-Functional Standup** (15 min):
- Quick status across all functions
- Blockers needing cross-team help
- This week's priorities

### Monthly Rhythm

**Monthly Roadmap Review** (60 min):
```markdown
## Monthly Roadmap Review - May

### Headlines
- What did we complete?
- What's on track?
- What's at risk?

### Metrics Update
- Progress on OKRs
- Leading indicators
- Trends to watch

### Deep Dive: One Initiative
- How is [Initiative X] going?
- Customer feedback
- Quality and performance
- Lessons learned

### Roadmap Adjustment Requests
- Any changes to Q2 roadmap needed?
- Impact assessment
- Decision

### Looking Ahead
- Adjustments to Later initiatives?
- Early planning for Q3?
```

### Mid-Quarter Checkpoint (Optional - Week 20)

If tracking significantly behind or ahead, schedule a checkpoint:

```markdown
## Mid-Quarter Checkpoint

### Performance vs. Plan
- Will we hit Q2 goals?
- What's tracking ahead? Behind?
- What's the risk level?

### Course Correction
- Do we need to adjust plan?
- What's slowing us down?
- How will we get back on track?

### Reforecast
- Will we hit end-of-quarter targets?
- Any surprises to communicate to leadership?
```

## Common Quarterly Planning Challenges

### Challenge 1: Planning Takes Too Long

**Problem**: Spending 3-4 weeks on planning feels excessive

**Solution**:
- Compress planning session to 2-3 hours if possible
- Come prepared (pre-work done)
- Make decisions quickly (don't revisit)
- Use asynchronous work where possible

### Challenge 2: Plan Changes Immediately

**Problem**: By the time Q2 starts, something changes and plan is disrupted

**Solution**:
- Build flexibility into plan (don't pack to 100% capacity)
- Have escalation process for changes
- Distinguish between adjustments and full plan rewrites
- Review quarterly commitments at monthly reviews

### Challenge 3: Team Doesn't Feel "Heard"

**Problem**: Team feels roadmap is decided without their input

**Solution**:
- Include engineering leadership in planning session
- Do team-specific kickoffs to explain rationale
- Invite feedback on execution approach
- Show how team input shaped decisions

### Challenge 4: Metrics Don't Align with Reality

**Problem**: OKRs set in planning session but don't reflect actual progress

**Solution**:
- Set realistic targets (based on historical data)
- Include confidence levels ("70% confident we'll hit this")
- Monthly review to adjust if needed
- Distinguish between committed vs. aspirational goals

### Challenge 5: Planning Feels Disconnected from Execution

**Problem**: Plan gets written but doesn't guide day-to-day work

**Solution**:
- Use OKRs as reference in weekly meetings
- Monthly review tied to OKR progress
- Dashboard showing real-time status
- Weekly standup includes "How does this move our OKRs?"

---

## Tips for Effective Quarterly Planning

1. **Use Data**: Base decisions on Q1 results, customer feedback, metrics
2. **Get Input Early**: Don't surprise teams with roadmap in kickoff
3. **Be Realistic**: Build in buffer time and capacity
4. **Get Clear Approval**: Don't assume silence = approval
5. **Communicate Widely**: Share roadmap beyond just exec team
6. **Balance**: Mix of innovation, maintenance, customer work
7. **Track Progress**: Monthly reviews keep it real
8. **Stay Flexible**: 20% capacity for adjustments
9. **Celebrate**: Acknowledge team effort in planning and execution
10. **Document Learning**: Each quarter should inform the next

Quarterly planning is how you translate annual strategy into executable work while staying flexible to learning and market changes.
