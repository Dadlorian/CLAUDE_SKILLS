# Roadmap Planning: 30-Minute Quick Start Guide

Create a strategic roadmap that aligns teams and guides execution without micromanaging.

---

## Your 30-Minute Roadmap

- **Minutes 1-5**: Understand roadmap purpose (strategy communication, not timeline)
- **Minutes 6-15**: Build your Now/Next/Later roadmap structure
- **Minutes 16-25**: Align roadmap with company strategy and OKRs
- **Minutes 26-30**: Plan stakeholder communication

---

## 5 Essential Concepts Every PM Needs

### 1. Roadmap is Strategic Communication, Not Project Timeline
Your roadmap should show **themes and outcomes**, not detailed task lists. It answers: "Why are we building this? What are we trying to achieve?"

**Bad roadmap**: "Build feature X. Build feature Y. Build feature Z." (Tasks, not outcomes)
**Good roadmap**: "Enable SMB customers to scale operations 10x faster. Drive expansion revenue to existing customers." (Outcomes)

**Actionable**: Start each roadmap item with an outcome or problem you're solving, not a feature name.

### 2. Now/Next/Later Structure Provides Flexibility
Avoid rigid quarterly plans that lock you into decisions made months ago.

**Now** (0-3 months): Committed, specific, measured weekly
**Next** (3-6 months): Directional, themes, less detail
**Later** (6-12+ months): Vision and big bets, subject to change

This structure signals "we're committed to Now, but willing to pivot on Next/Later."

**Actionable**: Organize your roadmap into Now (specific initiatives), Next (themes), Later (big vision).

### 3. Outcomes > Outputs
Every roadmap item should be tied to measurable change, not just "build this feature."

**Output**: "Build real-time collaboration feature"
**Outcome**: "Increase feature adoption rate from 30% to 60% within 3 months. Enable users to create content together in 1/3 the time."

**Actionable**: For each roadmap item, define 2-3 success metrics. How will we know if this worked?

### 4. Strategic Themes Organize Multiple Initiatives
Don't list 15 random features. Group them into 3-5 strategic themes that connect to business goals.

**Example themes**:
- Accelerate new user adoption (related initiatives: improved onboarding, better search, templates)
- Drive expansion revenue (related initiatives: premium tier, analytics dashboard, API access)
- Build network effects (related initiatives: sharing, collaboration, notifications)

**Actionable**: Take your initiative list and group into 3-5 themes that each serve a clear strategic goal.

### 5. Roadmap is Living Document, Not Set-in-Stone Plan
Update your roadmap monthly (Now), quarterly (Next), and annually (Later). Share learnings that cause pivots.

**Actionable**: Schedule monthly roadmap review. Update based on metrics, competitive moves, and customer feedback. Share changes with stakeholders.

## First Steps Checklist

### Week 1: Foundation
- [ ] Clarify strategic context: What are company OKRs this year?
- [ ] Meet with leadership: What are top 3 business priorities?
- [ ] Talk to customers: What problems matter most?
- [ ] Review current product performance: What's working? What's broken?
- [ ] List all potential initiatives (50+ ideas is normal)

### Week 2: Prioritization
- [ ] Score initiatives against:
  - Strategic alignment (maps to company OKRs? 1-5 scale)
  - Customer impact (solves significant problem? 1-5 scale)
  - Business impact (revenue, retention, growth? 1-5 scale)
  - Effort (engineering complexity? 1-5 scale)
- [ ] Top 15-20 initiatives bubble up (highest score)
- [ ] Discuss with leadership: Anything misaligned?

### Week 3: Build Now/Next/Later
- [ ] **Now** (Next 3 months):
  - 3-5 initiatives max
  - Define specific outcomes and success metrics
  - Identify key milestones and dependencies
  - Assign owners

- [ ] **Next** (3-6 months):
  - 5-8 themes or initiatives
  - Outcomes defined, but less detail
  - Dependencies noted

- [ ] **Later** (6-12+ months):
  - 3-5 big bets or vision items
  - Why they matter, not how to execute
  - Subject to change based on learnings

### Week 4: Stakeholder Communication
- [ ] Create one-page summary: Vision + Now/Next/Later + Key OKRs
- [ ] Present to leadership: Explain reasoning, trade-offs, timeline
- [ ] Share with extended team: Why each initiative matters
- [ ] Create FAQ: Answer common questions
- [ ] Establish review cadence: Monthly updates on Now, quarterly on Next/Later

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Too Much Detail in Roadmap
Roadmaps with 50+ items, exact dates, and task-level detail become outdated immediately.

**Fix**: Now (specific), Next (directional), Later (vision). Avoid dates unless absolutely necessary.

### Mistake 2: Roadmap Disconnected from Strategy
"We're shipping this because X feature request, then Y feature request, then Z..."

**Fix**: Every item should trace back to a strategic outcome or OKR. If not, question whether it belongs.

### Mistake 3: No Stakeholder Alignment Before Roadmap
You build perfect roadmap, then leadership says "Wait, we're pivoting to X"

**Fix**: Align on strategic context and OKRs BEFORE building roadmap. This prevents rework.

### Mistake 4: Roadmap Treated as Promise, Not Hypothesis
"You committed to feature X in March, where is it?" (Even though you learned it's wrong)

**Fix**: Be transparent about Now vs. Next vs. Later. Share what you're learning that changes priorities.

### Mistake 5: Not Communicating Progress
Roadmap sits in a doc. No one knows what's happening. Trust erodes.

**Fix**: Weekly updates on Now initiatives (team syncs). Monthly updates on overall progress. Quarterly stakeholder review.

## Roadmap Template

```markdown
# [Product Name] Roadmap - [Year]

## Strategic Context
- Company OKRs: [List 3 key company goals]
- Product Vision: [1-2 sentence north star]
- Key Constraints: [Capacity, partnerships, dependencies]

## NOW (Next 3 months) - Committed
### Initiative 1: [Problem Outcome]
- Outcome: [Measurable change we're trying to create]
- Key Results: [Metric 1, Metric 2, Metric 3]
- Timeline: Months 1-3
- Owner: [Name]
- Dependencies: [List any dependencies]
- Status: On track / At risk / Completed

### Initiative 2: [Problem Outcome]
- Outcome: [Measurable change]
- Key Results: [Metrics]
- Timeline: Months 1-3
- Owner: [Name]

## NEXT (3-6 months) - Directional
### Theme 1: [Strategic Goal]
- Goal: [Why this theme matters]
- Key Initiatives: [Related initiatives]
- High-level approach: [How we'll tackle this]

### Theme 2: [Strategic Goal]
- Goal: [Why this theme matters]
- Key Initiatives: [Related initiatives]

## LATER (6-12+ months) - Vision
### Big Bet 1: [Transformative Outcome]
- Vision: [What becomes possible?]
- Why now: [Market/customer/capability factors]
- Success looks like: [2-3 year vision]

### Big Bet 2: [Vision Item]
- Vision: [What becomes possible?]
- Why now: [Market factors]

## Updated: [Date]
## Next Review: [Date]
```

## Prioritization Framework for Initiatives

### Scoring Each Initiative

| Initiative | Strategic Alignment (1-5) | Customer Impact (1-5) | Business Impact (1-5) | Effort (1-5, lower better) | Total Score |
|------------|---------------------------|---------------------|---------------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| Improved Onboarding | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 17 |
| Real-time Collab | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 18 |
| Advanced Analytics | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 14 |
| Mobile App | 2 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 14 |

Top scorers → Now. Medium scorers → Next. Low scorers → Later or discard.

## Communication Plan for Roadmap Launch

1. **Executive Briefing**: 30-min presentation on strategy, priorities, trade-offs
2. **Team Kickoff**: 60-min session explaining "why," answering questions
3. **Sales/Marketing Brief**: How roadmap supports go-to-market strategy
4. **Customer Comms** (if appropriate): What customers can expect, timelines
5. **Weekly Updates**: Share progress on Now initiatives
6. **Monthly Sync**: Update leadership on progress, blockers, learnings
7. **Quarterly Review**: Revisit strategy, update Next/Later based on learnings

## Success Metrics

- [ ] Team can articulate why each roadmap item matters (not just "we're building X")
- [ ] Roadmap connects to company OKRs explicitly
- [ ] You update roadmap monthly, not annually
- [ ] Stakeholders understand Now vs. Next vs. Later difference
- [ ] You've communicated trade-offs ("We're not doing X to focus on Y")
- [ ] Each Now item has clear success metrics
- [ ] You track progress weekly and share regularly
