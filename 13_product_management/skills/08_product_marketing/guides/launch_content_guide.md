# Creating Launch Content Guide

## Overview

Launch content is the coordinated package of marketing and communications materials that support a product launch. This guide covers what launch content to create, how to plan it, and how to execute effectively.

## Why Launch Content Matters

A strong launch requires:
- **Awareness**: Building visibility in market
- **Education**: Helping customers understand the value
- **Differentiation**: Showing why you're different
- **Urgency**: Creating momentum and motivation to act
- **Sales Enablement**: Giving sales team the tools to sell

## Types of Launch Content

### 1. Announcement & Press Content

**Press Release**

**Purpose**: Formal announcement to media and industry

**Structure**:
- Headline: Lead with major news/benefit
- Opening paragraph: Who, what, where, when, why (5Ws)
- Company statement: Quote from executive (benefits, vision)
- Product details: Key capabilities and benefits
- Customer validation: Quote from customer or analyst
- Availability: When/where customers can access
- About company: Standard company boilerplate
- Contact information: PR contact

**Example Structure**:

---

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

Figma Launches Multiplayer Prototyping to Accelerate Design-to-Development

Real-time prototyping collaboration brings design teams and engineers together

San Francisco, CA – [Date] – Figma, the browser-based design tool for modern design teams, today announced Multiplayer Prototyping, bringing real-time collaboration to interactive design. Building on Figma's multiplayer foundation, teams can now prototype together simultaneously, getting instant feedback from engineers and stakeholders without exporting files or scheduling async review meetings.

"Design teams waste weeks on review cycles and implementation handoff," said [CEO Name], CEO of Figma. "Multiplayer Prototyping collapses that timeline, bringing engineering and design together earlier to ship faster."

Multiplayer Prototyping allows:
- Real-time collaboration on interactive flows
- Live feedback from stakeholders during prototyping
- Instant implementation handoff from design to engineering
- Integration with developer tools for seamless handoff

"Implementing designs takes half the time with Figma because prototyping specs are already there," said [Customer CTO Name], CTO at [Company Name], which reduced design-to-development cycle by 40%.

Multiplayer Prototyping is available to all Figma users today at [link].

---

**Blog Announcement**

**Purpose**: Detailed announcement for your audience

**Structure**:
- Compelling headline highlighting the benefit
- Problem context (why this matters)
- The solution (what you're launching)
- Key capabilities and how they work
- Customer validation (quotes, case studies)
- How to get started
- CTA to try it out

**Tone**: Friendly, conversational, exciting. Speak to your audience directly.

**Example**:
```
The multiplayer design tool just got better: Live prototyping for teams

Tired of "Can you export and send me that prototype?" Design and engineering spend
weeks going back and forth on interactive flows. Today, we're launching something
that changes that: Multiplayer Prototyping.

Here's what changed...
```

**Purpose**: Drive awareness and engagement on social channels

**Platforms**:
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership angle, business impact
- Twitter: Quick announcement, conversation starter
- Product Hunt: Community-focused, engagement-focused
- Slack/Discord: Community conversation

**Content for Each**:

**LinkedIn**:
- Headline with benefit/outcome
- 2-3 paragraphs explaining why it matters
- Visual of the product/feature
- Relevant hashtags
- CTA to visit blog or try product

**Example**:

**Twitter**:
- Short, punchy, engaging
- Lead with benefit or interesting stat
- Use visuals (GIFs, screenshots)
- Ask questions or start conversation
- Include link to full announcement

**Example**:
"Just launched: Multiplayer Prototyping. Your entire team can prototype together in real-time.

No more "can you export that?" No more async feedback loops. Just real-time collaboration.

Give it a try: [link]"

**Discord/Slack Communities**:
- More casual, community-focused

### 2. Educational & Explanation Content

**Feature Deep Dive Blog Post**

**Purpose**: Explain the feature, how to use it, and why it matters

**Structure**:
- Problem statement (what problem does this solve?)
- How it works (explanation with visuals)
- Use cases (specific ways to use it)
- Tips and best practices
- Integration with other features
- CTA to try it out

**Example**:
```
# Multiplayer Prototyping: Collaborate on Interactive Designs in Real-Time

Design teams spend weeks going back and forth on interactive flows. Engineer asks for
clarification. Designer exports and sends file. Engineer reads specs. Days pass.

What if your entire team could prototype together?

## How Multiplayer Prototyping Works

Just like Figma's design canvas, prototyping is now collaborative. When you're
prototyping an interactive flow:

- See teammates' cursors and what they're working on
- Make changes in real-time and see impact immediately
- Leave comments with feedback in-context
- Get instant validation from stakeholders

## Use Cases

**Use Case 1: Design & Engineering Alignment**
[Explanation with screenshots/video]

**Use Case 2: Cross-Functional Review**
[Explanation with screenshots/video]

## Best Practices

1. [Best practice 1 with explanation]
2. [Best practice 2 with explanation]
```

**Tutorial & How-To Content**

**Purpose**: Help users get value from the new feature

**Formats**:

**Step-by-Step Guide**:
- Take user from A to B
- Clear numbered steps
- Screenshots or videos at each step
- Tips for common mistakes
- Link to more advanced content

**Video Tutorial**:
- Screen recording of feature in action
- Voiceover explanation
- Length: 2-5 minutes
- Clear sections with timestamps
- Transcript for accessibility

**Interactive Demo**:
- Let users try feature in safe environment
- Guided experience showing key workflows
- Minimal clicking to get to value
- Quick (5-10 minute) first-time experience

**Webinar**

**Purpose**: Educate audience, answer questions, build excitement

**Structure**:
- Introduction and context (5 min)
- Problem statement (5 min)
- How you solve it (10 min)
- Live demonstration (10 min)
- Use cases and examples (5 min)
- Q&A (10 min)

**Promotion**:
- Announce 1-2 weeks before
- Send email reminders
- Promote on social media
- Drive attendance with bonus offer
- Record for on-demand access

**Comparison Content**

**Purpose**: Help prospects understand your product vs. alternatives

**Before/After Comparison**:
- Situation before (old way, competitor, doing nothing)
- Situation after (with your product)
- Specific improvements and outcomes
- Time and cost savings
- Team impact

**Detailed Comparison Guide**:
- Feature comparison table
- Experience/usability comparison
- Customer success comparison
- Pricing comparison
- Migration and implementation comparison

**Versus Competitor Content**:
- Focus on key differences
- Avoid negativity
- Emphasize your strengths, not their weaknesses
- Provide objective comparisons
- Address each competitor's strengths while showing your differentiation

### 3. Social Proof & Customer Content

**Case Study**

**Purpose**: Show how customer achieved results with your product

**Structure**:
- Customer background (company, industry, team size)
- Challenge/problem they faced
- How they evaluated solutions
- Why they chose you
- Implementation and adoption
- Results and outcomes (quantified)
- Customer quotes
- Key learnings
- Visual format (PDF, web page, video)

**Example Structure**:
```
CASE STUDY: How [Company Name] Reduced Design Cycles by 40%

Challenge
[Company Name] is a design-forward B2B SaaS company with designers, product managers,
and engineers distributed across 4 countries. Their old process:
- Designers created in one tool
- Exported designs and sent to Figma
- Stakeholders provided feedback async
- Engineers requested specs multiple times

This created a 3-week cycle from design to implementation.

Solution
They switched to Figma for their entire design workflow, including prototyping.
Using Multiplayer Prototyping, their new process:
- Designers prototype in Figma
- Engineers and PMs see prototypes in real-time
- Feedback happens synchronously
- Implementation specs generated automatically

Results
- 40% reduction in design-to-implementation timeline (3 weeks → 10 days)
- 50% reduction in back-and-forth communication
- 25% improvement in on-time feature delivery
- 30% improvement in design consistency

Quote
"Figma doesn't just make design collaboration better - it makes product development
better. Our engineers don't have to dig for specs. Our designers get instant feedback.
Everyone ships faster." - [Customer Name], [Title] at [Company]
```

**Customer Testimonial**

**Purpose**: Short validation of value proposition

**Formats**:

**Video Testimonial**:
- Customer on camera (ideally)
- 30-60 second video
- One customer talking about:
  - Problem they had
  - How you helped
  - Results they achieved
  - Why they recommend you
- High quality video/audio

**Written Testimonial**:
- Quote from customer
- Their name, title, company
- Photo of customer
- Link to their company
- Should mention:
  - Specific benefit or outcome
  - Why it matters to them
  - Recommendation

**User-Generated Content**

**Purpose**: Authentic customer feedback and creativity

**Strategies**:
- Hashtag campaigns (customers tag content related to product)
- Customer design showcases (feature best customer work)
- Interview customers (short clips of how they use product)
- Ask for success stories
- Showcase unique use cases

### 4. Sales Enablement Content

**Battle Cards**

**Purpose**: Equip sales with talking points for competitive situations

**Structure for Each Competitor**:
- Competitor overview (positioning, target market)
- Strengths they have
- Weaknesses they have
- How to position against them
- If prospect says X about competitor, respond with Y

**One-Pager**

**Purpose**: Quick sales reference for key messaging

**Content**:
- Core value proposition (benefit)
- Key differentiators
- Customer proof (quotes, metrics)
- Pricing overview
- Next steps in sales process
- Contact information

**ROI Calculator**

**Purpose**: Help prospects quantify value of your product

**Components**:
- Identify key metrics that matter to customer
- Show baseline (current state)
- Project improvement with your product
- Calculate financial impact
- Show payback period
- Break down ROI by component

**Example**:
```
ROI CALCULATOR: Design Efficiency

How much faster can your team move with Figma?

Current Design-to-Implementation Cycle: 21 days
With Figma: 12 days
Time Saved Per Project: 9 days

Annual Benefit Calculation:
- Projects per year: 24
- Time saved: 216 days (9 days × 24)
- FTE hours saved: 1,728 hours (216 × 8)
- Cost per FTE hour: $75
- Annual savings: $129,600

Plus additional benefits:
- Reduced design rework: $50,000
- Faster time to revenue: $100,000

Total annual benefit: $279,600
Product cost: $48,000
Net ROI: 482%
Payback period: 1.7 months
```

**Sales Deck**

**Purpose**: Comprehensive presentation for sales conversations

**Deck Structure**:
1. Introduction & Agenda (1 slide)
2. Customer situation & challenge (2-3 slides)
3. Your positioning and solution (3-4 slides)
4. Key capabilities (3-5 slides)
5. Customer proof & validation (2-3 slides)
6. Implementation & timeline (1-2 slides)
7. Pricing & next steps (1-2 slides)
8. Q&A (ongoing)

**Key Principles**:
- Lead with customer problem, not your solution
- Each slide should have 1 key message
- Heavy use of visuals and minimal text
- Proof points on every claim
- Next steps are always clear

### 5. Advertising & Promotional Content

**Paid Search Advertising**

**Messaging Structure**:
- Headline: Benefit or outcome (search keyword)
- Description: What you offer + proof point
- CTA: Clear action
- Landing page: Matches ad promise

**Example**:
- Headline: "Cut Design Time In Half"
- Description: "Multiplayer Prototyping lets your whole team collaborate in real-time. Try free."
- CTA: "Start Free"

**Display/Programmatic Ads**

**Creative Variations**:
- Visual advertisement with product screenshot
- Emotional angle (excitement, relief, possibility)
- Social proof (customer logos, ratings)
- Clear CTA
- Simple, clear messaging

**Social Media Advertising**

**Platform-Specific Ads**:

**LinkedIn** (B2B focus):
- Lead with business outcome
- Highlight ROI and efficiency
- Professional tone
- Call-to-action to learn more

**Facebook/Instagram** (B2C or creative focus):
- Visual-focused
- Emotional or aspirational
- Clear product benefit
- CTA to sign up or try

**Email Campaign**

**Launch Email Sequence**:

**Email 1: Announcement** (Launch day)
- Subject: Exciting news about [feature/product]
- Lead with benefit
- Brief explanation
- CTA to read blog or try

**Email 2: What This Means for You** (Day 2-3)
- Subject: How [feature] changes your workflow
- Explain benefit relative to their situation
- Show specific use case relevant to them
- CTA to try or learn more

**Email 3: How to Get Started** (Day 4-5)
- Subject: 3 ways to use [feature] today
- Step-by-step how to use
- Best practices
- CTA to start using

**Email 4: Customer Success** (Day 6-7)
- Subject: See how [Customer] uses [feature]
- Case study or customer quote
- Results and outcomes
- CTA to try

**Email 5: Special Offer** (Day 8-10, if applicable)
- Subject: Limited offer on [feature/product]
- Special offer or bonus
- Time limitation
- CTA to take action

## Launch Content Planning Process

### Step 1: Define Launch Goals

Before creating content, clarify:
- What are we launching?
- Who is the target audience?
- What action do we want them to take?
- How will we measure success?
- What's the timeline?

### Step 2: Identify Key Messages

- What's the core value proposition?
- What are 3-5 key messages?
- How does this differentiate from alternatives?
- What proof points do we have?

### Step 3: Map Content to Audience & Stage

**By Audience**:
- Existing customers (emphasize new capabilities)
- Prospect customers (emphasize competitive advantage)
- Media and influencers (emphasize news value)
- Partners and integrations (emphasize opportunity)

**By Stage of Awareness**:
- Awareness stage: Announcement, explanation content
- Consideration stage: Comparison, use case content
- Decision stage: Social proof, ROI content

### Step 4: Create Content Production Plan

For each content piece:
- Content type and format
- Key message and outline
- Owner and timeline
- Approval process
- Promotion plan
- Success metrics

### Step 5: Execution & Coordination

- Assign owners for each piece
- Create review process
- Coordinate timing
- Train teams on new positioning
- Brief sales and customer success
- Execute promotions

### Step 6: Measure & Optimize

Track metrics:
- Content engagement (views, clicks, shares)
- Awareness (mentions, reach, impressions)
- Lead generation (leads, trials, signups)
- Sales impact (deals influenced, win rates)
- Customer impact (adoption, satisfaction)

## Common Launch Content Mistakes

### 1. **Creating Content Without Clear Strategy**

**Problem**: Creating content without understanding goals and target audience
**Solution**: Define goals, audiences, and key messages first

### 2. **Too Much Content, Poor Quality**

**Problem**: Creating many pieces of content none of which are excellent
**Solution**: Focus on fewer, higher quality pieces

### 3. **Announcement Without Education**

**Problem**: Announcing feature without explaining why it matters
**Solution**: Create educational content explaining benefit and how to use

### 4. **No Sales Enablement**

**Problem**: Sales team unprepared to sell the new thing
**Solution**: Create battle cards, one-pagers, and training before launch

### 5. **Inconsistent Messaging**

**Problem**: Different messages in press release, blog, social media
**Solution**: Create messaging framework and use consistently

### 6. **Timing Misalignment**

**Problem**: Content published out of sequence or too far apart
**Solution**: Create content calendar with coordinated timing

### 7. **No Measurement**

**Problem**: Creating content but not measuring effectiveness
**Solution**: Define metrics and track performance

## Launch Content Checklist

**Before Launch**:
- [ ] Positioning statement aligned across team
- [ ] Key messages defined and documented
- [ ] Press release drafted and approved
- [ ] Blog post written and scheduled
- [ ] Social media content created and scheduled
- [ ] Sales enablement materials ready
- [ ] Email campaign scheduled
- [ ] Demo/tutorial video ready
- [ ] Case studies and customer quotes collected
- [ ] Sales training completed
- [ ] Launch day checklist prepared

**Launch Day**:
- [ ] Press release distributed
- [ ] Blog post published
- [ ] Social media posts go live
- [ ] Email announcement sent
- [ ] Sales team briefed
- [ ] Customer success team notified
- [ ] Website/product pages updated
- [ ] Support team prepared for questions
- [ ] Monitoring setup for mentions and engagement

**Post-Launch**:
- [ ] Track engagement and reach
- [ ] Collect customer feedback
- [ ] Monitor win/loss impact
- [ ] Update strategy based on learnings
- [ ] Plan follow-up content
- [ ] Share launch results with team

## Key Takeaways

1. **Strategy First**: Define goals and messages before creating content
2. **Audience-Focused**: Tailor content for different audiences and stages
3. **Quality Over Quantity**: Better to have 5 great pieces than 20 mediocre ones
4. **Sales Enablement**: Don't forget to equip sales team
5. **Consistent Messaging**: Use framework across all content
6. **Timing Matters**: Coordinate content for maximum impact
7. **Measure Everything**: Track effectiveness and learn from results
8. **Coordinate Across Teams**: Align marketing, sales, product, communications
