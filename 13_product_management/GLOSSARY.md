## A

### AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
A framework for measuring user engagement across five key stages: Acquisition, Activation, Revenue, Retention, and Referral. Also known as pirate metrics, this model helps PMs track the complete user lifecycle. **See also:** [Funnel Analysis](#funnel-analysis), [Metrics Framework](#metrics-framework)

### Acquisition
The process of gaining new users or customers through marketing and distribution channels. Measured by cost per acquisition (CPA) and customer acquisition cost (CAC). A primary metric tracked in AARRR framework. **See also:** [CAC](#cac-customer-acquisition-cost), [Marketing Funnel](#marketing-funnel)

### Active Users
Users who have engaged with a product within a specific time period (daily, weekly, monthly). Commonly tracked as DAU (Daily Active Users), WAU (Weekly Active Users), or MAU (Monthly Active Users). Key metric for measuring product engagement. **See also:** [DAU](#dau-daily-active-users), [MAU](#mau-monthly-active-users), [Engagement](#engagement)

### Agile
An iterative product development methodology emphasizing continuous improvement, frequent releases, and customer feedback. Includes frameworks like Scrum and Kanban. **See also:** [Scrum](#scrum), [Sprint](#sprint), [Kanban](#kanban)

### Agile Roadmap
A flexible product roadmap updated regularly based on market feedback and business priorities. Less fixed than traditional roadmaps, allowing for pivots and adjustments. **See also:** [Product Roadmap](#product-roadmap), [Iteration](#iteration)

### AI/ML Integration
The incorporation of artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities into products to enhance functionality, personalization, and user experience. **See also:** [Personalization](#personalization), [Feature](#feature)

### Alpha Release
An early version of a product released to a limited internal audience for initial testing and feedback. Comes before beta release. **See also:** [Beta Release](#beta-release), [MVP](#mvp-minimum-viable-product)

### Analytics
The collection, measurement, and analysis of user behavior data to inform product decisions. Includes tools for tracking metrics, user flows, and engagement patterns. **See also:** [Data-Driven Decision Making](#data-driven-decision-making), [Metrics](#metrics)

### Anchor Bias
A cognitive bias where users rely heavily on the first piece of information presented when making decisions. Important consideration in pricing strategy and feature presentation. **See also:** [Pricing Strategy](#pricing-strategy), [UX Design](#ux-design)

### Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
Total revenue expected from annual subscription contracts. Key metric for SaaS companies. Calculated as: (Monthly Recurring Revenue × 12). **See also:** [MRR](#mrr-monthly-recurring-revenue), [SaaS](#saas-software-as-a-service)

### API (Application Programming Interface)
A set of protocols enabling different software components to communicate and exchange data. Critical for product integration and extensibility. **See also:** [Integration](#integration), [Developer Experience](#developer-experience)

### Assumption Testing
The process of validating product assumptions through experiments, user research, and data analysis. A key component of lean product development. **See also:** [Hypothesis Testing](#hypothesis-testing), [Validation](#validation)

### Activation
The point where users take a specific action indicating they understand and are using key product features. Measured by activation rate; critical metric in AARRR framework. **See also:** [AARRR](#aarrr-pirate-metrics), [Onboarding](#onboarding)

### Attribution
The assignment of user conversions and actions to specific marketing channels or touchpoints. Models include first-touch, last-touch, and multi-touch attribution. **See also:** [Conversion](#conversion), [Marketing Mix](#marketing-mix)

## B

### Backlog
A prioritized list of features, improvements, and fixes to be completed. Includes product backlog and sprint backlog in Agile methodology. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [Sprint Planning](#sprint-planning)

### Backward Compatibility
The ability of new versions to work with systems and data from older versions. Important for maintaining user trust and reducing migration friction. **See also:** [Technical Debt](#technical-debt), [Deprecation](#deprecation)

### Baseline
The initial measurement against which progress is compared. Establishes reference point for understanding change and improvement. **See also:** [Metrics](#metrics), [KPI](#kpi-key-performance-indicator)

### Beta Release
A version released to external users for testing before general availability. Allows for real-world feedback and bug identification. **See also:** [Alpha Release](#alpha-release), [General Availability](#general-availability)

### Bleed Features
Features that cannibalize or compete with existing revenue streams. Requires careful management in product strategy. **See also:** [Feature Trade-offs](#feature-trade-offs), [Cannibalization](#cannibalization)

### Blue Ocean
A market strategy describing creating uncontested market space rather than competing in existing markets. Opposite of red ocean strategy. **See also:** [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning), [Market Strategy](#market-strategy)

### Bounce Rate
Percentage of users who leave after viewing only one page or session without taking action. High bounce rates may indicate poor user experience or message-market fit. **See also:** [User Engagement](#user-engagement), [Session](#session)

### Business Model
The way a company creates, delivers, and captures value. Includes pricing models, revenue streams, and customer relationships. **See also:** [Revenue Model](#revenue-model), [Monetization](#monetization)

### Breadth-First Approach
Product development strategy prioritizing breadth of features over depth of any single feature. Contrasts with depth-first approach. **See also:** [Product Strategy](#product-strategy), [Feature Prioritization](#feature-prioritization)

## C

### CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Total marketing and sales costs divided by number of new customers acquired in a period. Critical metric for understanding unit economics. Formula: Total Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired. **See also:** [LTV](#ltv-lifetime-value), [Unit Economics](#unit-economics), [CLTV](#cltv-customer-lifetime-value)

### CAC Payback Period
Time required for profit from a customer to equal their acquisition cost. Important for assessing sustainability of acquisition spending. Formula: CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin). **See also:** [CAC](#cac-customer-acquisition-cost), [Profitability](#profitability)

### Cannibalization
When a new product or feature reduces sales of existing products. Requires strategic evaluation during product roadmap planning. **See also:** [Market Share](#market-share), [Product Portfolio](#product-portfolio)

### Churn Rate
Percentage of customers or users lost in a given period. Critical retention metric for subscription businesses. Inverse of retention rate. **See also:** [Retention](#retention), [Net Revenue Retention](#net-revenue-retention), [Customer Lifetime Value](#customer-lifetime-value)

### Cohort Analysis
Grouping users by shared characteristics or behavior patterns over time. Enables understanding of how different user groups perform and engage. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [User Research](#user-research)

### Competitive Analysis
Systematic evaluation of competitors' products, positioning, pricing, and strategies. Informs product strategy and positioning. **See also:** [Market Research](#market-research), [Positioning](#positioning)

### Competitive Positioning
How a product is differentiated against competitors in customers' minds. Based on value proposition and unique benefits. **See also:** [Value Proposition](#value-proposition), [Differentiation](#differentiation)

### Concept Testing
Validation of product ideas through user feedback before full development. Includes surveys, focus groups, and mockup testing. **See also:** [User Testing](#user-testing), [Validation](#validation)

### Configuration Management
Managing product settings and options allowing customization for different user needs or market segments. **See also:** [Personalization](#personalization), [Feature Flags](#feature-flags)

### Conversion
An action taken by a user aligned with product goals (purchase, signup, feature adoption). Conversion rate = (Conversions / Total Users) × 100. **See also:** [Conversion Rate](#conversion-rate), [Funnel](#funnel-analysis)

### Conversion Rate (CVR)
Percentage of users who complete a desired action. Standard metrics include: signup conversion, purchase conversion, feature adoption. Key indicator of product-market fit. **See also:** [Conversion](#conversion), [User Engagement](#user-engagement)

### Core Product
The fundamental, essential features that define the product's basic value proposition. Must be stable and reliable. **See also:** [Feature](#feature), [MVP](#mvp-minimum-viable-product)

### Cost Per Install (CPI)
Cost of acquiring a user who installs an app through marketing campaigns. Important metric for mobile app growth. **See also:** [CAC](#cac-customer-acquisition-cost), [Mobile Growth](#mobile-growth)

### Critical Path
Sequence of essential features and milestones required to validate or launch a product successfully. **See also:** [Project Management](#project-management), [Launch Planning](#launch-planning)

### Cross-Functional Alignment
Ensuring agreement and coordinated effort across product, engineering, design, marketing, and sales teams. Essential for product success. **See also:** [Stakeholder Management](#stakeholder-management), [Collaboration](#collaboration)

### CTA (Call-to-Action)
Prompt guiding users toward desired action (e.g., "Sign up now", "Learn more"). Critical element of user flows and conversion optimization. **See also:** [Conversion](#conversion), [UX Copy](#ux-copy)

### Customer Advisory Board (CAB)
Group of key customers providing feedback and insights on product direction. Valuable for enterprise product management. **See also:** [Customer Research](#customer-research), [User Testing](#user-testing)

### Customer Discovery
Process of understanding customer problems, needs, and behaviors through interviews and observation. Foundation of problem validation. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Problem Validation](#problem-validation)

### Customer Health Score
Metric combining usage data, support interactions, and expansion metrics to predict customer satisfaction and churn risk. **See also:** [Customer Success](#customer-success), [Churn Rate](#churn-rate)

### Customer Journey Mapping
Visualization of all touchpoints and interactions from initial awareness through post-purchase. Identifies friction points and opportunities. **See also:** [User Experience](#user-experience), [Touchpoint](#touchpoint)

### CLTV (Customer Lifetime Value)
Also known as LTV; total revenue expected from a customer relationship over time. Key metric for assessing customer profitability. **See also:** [LTV](#ltv-lifetime-value), [Unit Economics](#unit-economics)

## D

### DAU (Daily Active Users)
Number of unique users engaging with product within a 24-hour period. Primary engagement metric for consumer products. **See also:** [Active Users](#active-users), [MAU](#mau-monthly-active-users), [WAU](#wau-weekly-active-users)

### Data-Driven Decision Making
Basing product decisions on quantitative data and metrics rather than intuition. Core principle of modern product management. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [Metrics](#metrics), [A/B Testing](#ab-testing)

### Dayparting
Strategy of analyzing and optimizing user experience based on time of day. Used for notification timing, feature visibility, and resource allocation. **See also:** [Personalization](#personalization), [User Behavior](#user-behavior)

### Deep Linking
Technology enabling direct navigation to specific content within an app from external sources. Improves user experience and attribution. **See also:** [User Experience](#user-experience), [Navigation](#navigation)

### Deprecation
Process of removing or discontinuing features with advance notice to users. Requires change management planning. **See also:** [Backward Compatibility](#backward-compatibility), [Product Evolution](#product-evolution)

### Depth-First Approach
Product development strategy prioritizing perfection and depth of individual features over breadth. Opposite of breadth-first approach. **See also:** [Product Strategy](#product-strategy), [Feature Prioritization](#feature-prioritization)

### Design System
Standardized set of components, patterns, and guidelines enabling consistent UX across product. Improves development velocity and user experience. **See also:** [UX Design](#ux-design), [Component Library](#component-library)

### Developer Experience (DX)
Quality of experience for developers using APIs, SDKs, and documentation. Critical for platforms and B2B developer products. **See also:** [API](#api-application-programming-interface), [Documentation](#documentation)

### Differentiation
Distinct advantages or characteristics setting product apart from competitors. Based on features, quality, price, or brand. **See also:** [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning), [Value Proposition](#value-proposition)

### Digital Asset Management
Organization and distribution of digital content (images, videos, documents) used in product and marketing. **See also:** [Content Management](#content-management), [Product Content](#product-content)

### Discovery
Research and exploration phase to understand user needs, market opportunities, and competitive landscape before development. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Problem Validation](#problem-validation)

### Disruptive Innovation
Innovation fundamentally changing how markets operate or creating entirely new markets. Often starts at lower end serving underserved segments. **See also:** [Innovation](#innovation), [Blue Ocean](#blue-ocean)

## E

### Engagement
Extent to which users interact with product features and content. Measured through activity frequency, session duration, and feature adoption. **See also:** [Active Users](#active-users), [User Behavior](#user-behavior), [Retention](#retention)

### Engagement Metrics
Quantitative measures of user interaction including DAU, MAU, session duration, and feature adoption rates. **See also:** [Metrics](#metrics), [Analytics](#analytics), [Engagement](#engagement)

### Enhancement
Improvement to existing features increasing value or usability without fundamental change. Distinct from new features. **See also:** [Feature](#feature), [Roadmap](#roadmap)

### Enterprise Product Management
Product management focused on large organizations with complex buying processes, implementation, and ongoing support. Emphasizes relationship management and customization. **See also:** [B2B](#b2b-business-to-business), [Sales Enablement](#sales-enablement)

### Entry Barriers
Factors making it difficult for competitors to enter a market. Include switching costs, capital requirements, brand strength, and regulations. **See also:** [Competitive Advantage](#competitive-advantage), [Market Strategy](#market-strategy)

### Expansion Revenue
Additional revenue from existing customers through upsells, cross-sells, and increased usage. Key component of SaaS revenue growth. **See also:** [Upsell](#upsell), [Net Revenue Retention](#net-revenue-retention)

## F

### Feature
Distinct functionality or capability within a product delivering specific user value. Building blocks of product roadmaps. **See also:** [Core Product](#core-product), [MVP](#mvp-minimum-viable-product), [Feature Flag](#feature-flags)

### Feature Adoption
Percentage of users who discover and use a specific feature. Critical metric for assessing feature success and UX effectiveness. **See also:** [Engagement](#engagement), [Feature Discovery](#feature-discovery)

### Feature Discovery
Process of users finding and understanding available features. Improved through onboarding, tooltips, and contextual help. **See also:** [Feature Adoption](#feature-adoption), [Onboarding](#onboarding)

### Feature Flags
Technical implementation enabling features to be toggled on/off without deployment. Enables safe experimentation and gradual rollouts. **See also:** [A/B Testing](#ab-testing), [Experimentation](#experimentation), [Continuous Deployment](#continuous-deployment)

### Feature Parity
Matching capabilities and features of competitors. Often necessary baseline but insufficient for differentiation. **See also:** [Differentiation](#differentiation), [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning)

### Feature Prioritization
Process of ranking features by value and effort to determine development order. Core PM responsibility. **See also:** [Prioritization Frameworks](#prioritization-frameworks), [RICE](#rice), [MoSCoW](#moscow)

### Feature Spec
Detailed written specification describing feature behavior, requirements, and acceptance criteria. Foundation for development. **See also:** [Requirements](#requirements), [Documentation](#documentation)

### Feature Sustainability
Long-term viability of features considering maintenance burden, user value, and strategic alignment. **See also:** [Technical Debt](#technical-debt), [Product Maintenance](#product-maintenance)

### Feature Trade-offs
Decision to implement one feature at expense of another due to resource constraints. Requires strategic thinking and stakeholder communication. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [Resource Allocation](#resource-allocation)

### Feedback Loop
Systematic collection and integration of user feedback into product development. Enables continuous improvement and validation. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Customer Discovery](#customer-discovery)

### Fidelity
Level of detail and functionality in mockups or prototypes. Ranges from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity interactive prototypes. **See also:** [Prototyping](#prototyping), [Wireframe](#wireframe)

### First-Mover Advantage
Competitive advantage gained by being first to market with a product or feature. Benefits include market share, brand positioning, and customer lock-in. **See also:** [Competitive Advantage](#competitive-advantage), [Market Entry](#market-entry)

### Five Forces Framework
Porter's analysis of competitive landscape considering: supplier power, buyer power, competitive rivalry, threat of substitutes, threat of new entrants. **See also:** [Competitive Analysis](#competitive-analysis), [Market Analysis](#market-analysis)

### Flywheel
Self-reinforcing cycle where output drives input creating exponential growth. Example: Customer referrals drive acquisition. **See also:** [Network Effects](#network-effects), [Growth Model](#growth-model)

### Focus Group
Qualitative research method using group discussion to understand attitudes, motivations, and perceptions. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Qualitative Research](#qualitative-research)

### Freemium Model
Business model offering free basic product with paid premium features or tiers. Common in SaaS and mobile apps. **See also:** [Pricing Model](#pricing-model), [Monetization](#monetization)

### Friction
Any factor creating difficulty or resistance in user workflows. Friction audit identifies and prioritizes friction points for removal. **See also:** [User Experience](#user-experience), [Usability](#usability)

### Funnel Analysis
Tracking user progression through sequential steps (awareness → consideration → purchase). Identifies drop-off points for optimization. **See also:** [Conversion Funnel](#conversion-funnel), [AARRR](#aarrr-pirate-metrics)

## G

### Gamification
Integration of game mechanics (points, badges, leaderboards) into products to increase engagement and motivation. **See also:** [Engagement](#engagement), [User Retention](#user-retention)

### General Availability (GA)
Product or feature officially released to all customers. Indicates production-ready status with support. **See also:** [Beta Release](#beta-release), [Launch](#launch)

### Growth Hacking
Creative, low-cost strategies for rapid user acquisition and engagement. Emphasizes experimentation and viral mechanics. **See also:** [Growth Marketing](#growth-marketing), [Virality](#virality)

### Growth Loops
Repeating cycles driving user acquisition and engagement. Examples: referral loops, content loops, usage loops. **See also:** [Flywheel](#flywheel), [Network Effects](#network-effects)

### Growth Metrics
KPIs measuring product growth including: new users, revenue growth, expansion revenue, and retention rates. **See also:** [KPI](#kpi-key-performance-indicator), [Metrics](#metrics)

### Growth Model
Framework explaining mechanisms driving product growth including unit economics, acquisition, retention, and virality. **See also:** [Growth Loops](#growth-loops), [Unit Economics](#unit-economics)

### GTM (Go-To-Market) Strategy
Comprehensive plan for bringing product to market including: positioning, pricing, distribution, messaging, and sales approach. **See also:** [Launch Planning](#launch-planning), [Positioning](#positioning)

## H

### Habituation
Process where repeated product usage becomes routine behavior. Goal of many consumer products seeking sustained engagement. **See also:** [Engagement](#engagement), [Retention](#retention), [User Behavior](#user-behavior)

### Heat Map
Visualization showing where users click, hover, and scroll on page or screen. Reveals engagement patterns and friction points. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [User Behavior](#user-behavior)

### Horizontal Scaling
Expanding market by selling same product to different customer segments or geographies. **See also:** [Market Expansion](#market-expansion), [Product Strategy](#product-strategy)

### Hypothesis Testing
Formulating predictions about user behavior or product impact and validating through experimentation. Core of scientific product management. **See also:** [A/B Testing](#ab-testing), [Experimentation](#experimentation), [Assumption Testing](#assumption-testing)

## I

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Detailed description of product's best-fit customer including: company size, industry, budget, and use cases. Guides sales and marketing. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [Targeting](#targeting), [Customer Research](#customer-research)

### Impact Mapping
Technique connecting product outcomes to business objectives through user impacts. Helps prioritize features. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [OKR](#okr-objectives-and-key-results)

### Implementation Cost
Resources (time, budget, personnel) required to build a feature or product. Critical for prioritization decisions. **See also:** [Effort Estimation](#effort-estimation), [RICE](#rice)

### In-App Messaging
Communications shown within product to users (notifications, banners, modals). Used for announcements, guidance, and engagement. **See also:** [User Communication](#user-communication), [Onboarding](#onboarding)

### Integration
Connection of product with other tools and services to extend functionality. Important for enterprise adoption. **See also:** [API](#api-application-programming-interface), [Ecosystem](#ecosystem)

### International Expansion
Scaling product to new geographic markets with different languages, currencies, regulations, and cultures. **See also:** [Localization](#localization), [Market Expansion](#market-expansion)

### Innovation
Introduction of new ideas, features, or business models creating customer value. Requires balancing innovation with reliability. **See also:** [Disruptive Innovation](#disruptive-innovation), [Product Development](#product-development)

### Insight
Deep understanding of user behavior, market trends, or business opportunities derived from data and research. Informs strategic decisions. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [User Research](#user-research), [Data](#data)

### Iteration
Cycle of building, testing, and refining based on feedback. Core of agile and lean product development. **See also:** [Agile](#agile), [Sprint](#sprint), [Feedback Loop](#feedback-loop)

### Iterative Design
Refining product design through multiple cycles of prototyping, testing, and feedback. Produces superior UX than single-shot design. **See also:** [Design Thinking](#design-thinking), [User Testing](#user-testing), [Prototyping](#prototyping)

## J

### Job to be Done (JTBD)
Framework viewing products as tools helping customers accomplish specific jobs. Shifts focus from features to customer outcomes. **See also:** [Jobs Theory](#jobs-theory), [Value Proposition](#value-proposition), [Customer Research](#customer-research)

### Jobs Theory
Theoretical framework explaining that customers "hire" products to do specific jobs. Enables deeper understanding of user motivation. **See also:** [Job to be Done](#job-to-be-done-jtbd), [Customer Insight](#customer-insight)

## K

### Kanban
Agile methodology visualizing workflow with columns representing stages (To Do, In Progress, Done). Emphasizes continuous flow over time-boxed sprints. **See also:** [Agile](#agile), [Sprint](#sprint), [Scrum](#scrum)

### Key Performance Indicator (KPI)
Measurable metric reflecting progress toward strategic objectives. Examples: customer acquisition cost, churn rate, revenue. **See also:** [Metrics](#metrics), [OKR](#okr-objectives-and-key-results), [Analytics](#analytics)

### Key Features
Core functionalities delivering primary value proposition. Distinguished from nice-to-have features. **See also:** [Feature](#feature), [Value Proposition](#value-proposition), [Core Product](#core-product)

## L

### Launch
Official release of product or feature to users. Requires coordination across product, engineering, marketing, and support. **See also:** [General Availability](#general-availability), [Launch Planning](#launch-planning), [GTM](#gtm-go-to-market-strategy)

### Launch Planning
Comprehensive preparation for product release including timing, messaging, support readiness, and success metrics. **See also:** [Launch](#launch), [GTM](#gtm-go-to-market-strategy), [Critical Path](#critical-path)

### Lean Product Development
Methodology minimizing waste through rapid iteration, customer feedback, and data-driven decisions. Emphasizes building MVP quickly. **See also:** [MVP](#mvp-minimum-viable-product), [Iteration](#iteration), [Agile](#agile)

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
Total revenue expected from customer relationship over time. Key profitability metric calculated as: (ARPU × Customer Lifespan). **See also:** [CAC](#cac-customer-acquisition-cost), [Unit Economics](#unit-economics), [CLTV](#cltv-customer-lifetime-value)

### Localization
Adapting product for specific languages, cultures, and markets including translation and cultural customization. **See also:** [Internationalization](#internationalization), [Market Expansion](#market-expansion)

## M

### MAU (Monthly Active Users)
Number of unique users engaging with product in a calendar month. Standard engagement metric for consumer and SaaS products. **See also:** [Active Users](#active-users), [DAU](#dau-daily-active-users), [WAU](#wau-weekly-active-users)

### Market Analysis
Systematic evaluation of market size, growth rate, competition, trends, and opportunities. Informs strategic planning. **See also:** [Competitive Analysis](#competitive-analysis), [Market Research](#market-research)

### Market Entry Strategy
Plan for entering new market including positioning, distribution, pricing, and timeline. **See also:** [GTM](#gtm-go-to-market-strategy), [Market Expansion](#market-expansion)

### Market Research
Systematic investigation of market characteristics, customer needs, and competitive landscape. Foundation for product strategy. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Competitive Analysis](#competitive-analysis)

### Market Segmentation
Dividing market into distinct groups with different needs and characteristics. Enables targeted product and marketing approaches. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [Targeting](#targeting), [ICP](#ideal-customer-profile-icp)

### Market Size
Total addressable revenue in a market. Components include: TAM (total), SAM (serviceable), and SOM (serviceable obtainable). **See also:** [TAM](#tam-total-addressable-market), [SAM](#sam-serviceable-addressable-market), [SOM](#som-serviceable-obtainable-market)

### Marketing Mix
Components of marketing strategy: Product, Price, Place, Promotion (4Ps) or expanded frameworks. **See also:** [Pricing Strategy](#pricing-strategy), [Distribution](#distribution)

### Metrics
Quantifiable measurements of product performance and user behavior. Foundation of data-driven product management. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [KPI](#kpi-key-performance-indicator), [Data](#data)

### Metrics Framework
System for organizing and tracking metrics aligned with business objectives. Examples: AARRR, NORTH STAR. **See also:** [AARRR](#aarrr-pirate-metrics), [North Star Metric](#north-star-metric)

### Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
Smallest product version validating core value proposition with minimum resources. Enables fast learning and iteration. **See also:** [Lean Product Development](#lean-product-development), [Iteration](#iteration), [Launch](#launch)

### MoSCoW
Prioritization framework categorizing requirements as: Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have. Provides clear priority structure. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [RICE](#rice), [Kano Model](#kano-model)

### Mobile-First Design
Design approach prioritizing mobile experience before desktop. Reflects shift to mobile-dominant user behavior. **See also:** [Responsive Design](#responsive-design), [UX Design](#ux-design)

### Monetization
Strategy for generating revenue from product. Includes subscriptions, one-time purchases, advertising, freemium, and data monetization. **See also:** [Pricing Model](#pricing-model), [Business Model](#business-model)

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
Predictable monthly revenue from subscriptions. Key metric for SaaS. Calculated as: (Number of Subscribers × Monthly Price). **See also:** [ARR](#annual-recurring-revenue-arr), [Recurring Revenue](#recurring-revenue), [SaaS Metrics](#saas-metrics)

### MRR Churn
Monthly recurring revenue lost due to customer cancellations or downgrades. **See also:** [Churn Rate](#churn-rate), [MRR](#monthly-recurring-revenue-mrr)

### Mockup
Static visual representation of product design showing layout and visual hierarchy. Differs from prototype which adds interactivity. **See also:** [Wireframe](#wireframe), [Prototype](#prototyping), [Fidelity](#fidelity)

## N

### Native App
Application developed specifically for platform (iOS/Android) using platform-specific languages and APIs. Typically faster and more capable than web apps. **See also:** [Cross-Platform](#cross-platform), [Web App](#web-app)

### Navigation
Information architecture and system enabling users to find and access content. Critical UX component. **See also:** [Information Architecture](#information-architecture), [UX Design](#ux-design)

### Net Promoter Score (NPS)
Metric measuring customer loyalty (0-100) based on single question: "How likely to recommend?" Score = Promoters - Detractors. **See also:** [Customer Satisfaction](#customer-satisfaction), [CSAT](#csat-customer-satisfaction-score)

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
Percentage revenue retained from existing customers including expansion and churn. Metric crucial for SaaS sustainability; >100% indicates growth from existing customers. Formula: (Beginning ARR + Expansion - Churn) / Beginning ARR. **See also:** [Retention](#retention), [Churn Rate](#churn-rate), [Expansion Revenue](#expansion-revenue)

### Network Effects
Phenomenon where product value increases as more users join. Creates powerful competitive moat. Examples: social networks, marketplaces. **See also:** [Flywheel](#flywheel), [Virality](#virality), [Growth Loops](#growth-loops)

### North Star Metric
Single primary metric best representing progress toward ultimate business objective. Aligns entire organization. Examples: user engagement, revenue, customer outcomes. **See also:** [Metrics Framework](#metrics-framework), [OKR](#okr-objectives-and-key-results)

## O

### Objective
Qualitative goal describing what the organization wants to achieve. Foundation of OKR framework. **See also:** [OKR](#okr-objectives-and-key-results), [Key Result](#key-result)

### OKR (Objectives and Key Results)
Goal-setting framework combining qualitative objectives with measurable key results (0-1.0 scale). Aligns teams and focus effort. **See also:** [Goal Setting](#goal-setting), [Objective](#objective), [Key Result](#key-result)

### Onboarding
Process of guiding new users to experience product value quickly. Critical for conversion and activation. Includes tutorials, walkthroughs, and contextual help. **See also:** [User Experience](#user-experience), [Activation](#activation), [Feature Discovery](#feature-discovery)

### Opportunity Cost
Value sacrificed by choosing one option over another. Critical consideration in feature prioritization. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [Resource Allocation](#resource-allocation)

### Opt-in
Requiring users to explicitly choose to receive communications (emails, notifications). Preferred approach respecting user choice. **See also:** [Opt-out](#opt-out), [User Preferences](#user-preferences)

### Opt-out
Default inclusion in communications with ability to unsubscribe. Less user-friendly than opt-in. **See also:** [Opt-in](#opt-in), [GDPR](#gdpr-general-data-protection-regulation)

### Optimization
Incremental improvement to existing features or processes to increase performance. Distinct from innovation. **See also:** [A/B Testing](#ab-testing), [Experimentation](#experimentation), [Continuous Improvement](#continuous-improvement)

## P

### P2P Marketplace
Platform facilitating peer-to-peer transactions between users. Requires managing both supply and demand. **See also:** [Marketplace](#marketplace), [Network Effects](#network-effects)

### Paid Acquisition
User acquisition through paid marketing channels (ads, sponsorships). Measured by CAC. **See also:** [CAC](#cac-customer-acquisition-cost), [Organic Acquisition](#organic-acquisition)

### Partial Monetization
Only monetizing subset of product functionality or users. Alternative to blanket monetization. **See also:** [Monetization](#monetization), [Freemium Model](#freemium-model)

### Personas
Detailed fictional representations of target customer types. Created from research and used to guide design and messaging. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Segmentation](#segmentation), [ICP](#ideal-customer-profile-icp)

### Personalization
Tailoring product experience to individual users based on behavior, preferences, or characteristics. Increases engagement and conversion. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [Configuration Management](#configuration-management), [AI/ML Integration](#aiml-integration)

### Platform
Product category providing infrastructure and tools enabling others to build on top. Examples: Android, Shopify, AWS. **See also:** [Ecosystem](#ecosystem), [API](#api-application-programming-interface)

### Positioning
How product is differentiated and perceived in customers' minds relative to alternatives. Foundation of marketing and product strategy. **See also:** [Value Proposition](#value-proposition), [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning), [Messaging](#messaging)

### Post-Launch Metrics
KPIs tracked after product release including: adoption, retention, revenue, and customer satisfaction. **See also:** [Launch](#launch), [Metrics](#metrics)

### Power Users
Segment of users who use product frequently with high engagement and advanced features. Critical for feedback and advocacy. **See also:** [User Segmentation](#user-segmentation), [Early Adopters](#early-adopters)

### Predictive Analytics
Using historical data and machine learning to forecast future user behavior and outcomes. Enables proactive product decisions. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [Data](#data), [Machine Learning](#machine-learning)

### Primary Research
Gathering original data directly from users through interviews, surveys, and observation. Contrasts with secondary research. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Secondary Research](#secondary-research)

### Pricing Model
Structure for charging customers including: subscriptions, freemium, usage-based, tiered, or one-time purchase. **See also:** [Monetization](#monetization), [Pricing Strategy](#pricing-strategy)

### Pricing Strategy
Comprehensive approach to pricing including: positioning, value communication, and customer willingness to pay. Critical revenue lever. **See also:** [Pricing Model](#pricing-model), [Value-Based Pricing](#value-based-pricing), [Competitive Pricing](#competitive-pricing)

### Prioritization
Process of ranking and sequencing features, projects, and initiatives. Core PM responsibility requiring trade-off analysis. **See also:** [Prioritization Frameworks](#prioritization-frameworks), [RICE](#rice), [MoSCoW](#moscow)

### Prioritization Frameworks
Structured methods for ranking work including: RICE, MoSCoW, Kano Model, Impact-Effort Matrix. **See also:** [RICE](#rice), [MoSCoW](#moscow), [Kano Model](#kano-model)

### Problem Statement
Clear articulation of user problem or opportunity being addressed. Foundation of product development. **See also:** [Problem Validation](#problem-validation), [Customer Discovery](#customer-discovery)

### Problem Validation
Confirming through user research that identified problem actually matters to target users. Prerequisite for solution development. **See also:** [Discovery](#discovery), [User Research](#user-research), [Customer Discovery](#customer-discovery)

### Product-Market Fit
State where product satisfies strong market demand. Indicates product is solving real customer problems. Critical milestone before scaling. **See also:** [Market Research](#market-research), [Validation](#validation), [PMF](#pmf-product-market-fit)

### Product Adoption
Extent to which customers understand and use product features. Measured through adoption rate and breadth of feature usage. **See also:** [Feature Adoption](#feature-adoption), [User Onboarding](#user-onboarding)

### Product Analytics
Analysis of product usage data including: user flows, feature adoption, retention, and engagement patterns. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [Data](#data), [Metrics](#metrics)

### Product Backlog
Prioritized list of features and improvements to be developed. Maintained by product manager and refined with team. **See also:** [Backlog](#backlog), [Sprint Planning](#sprint-planning)

### Product Definition
Clear documentation of what product is, who it's for, and what value it provides. Foundation for alignment. **See also:** [Product Specification](#product-specification), [Value Proposition](#value-proposition)

### Product Development
End-to-end process of creating and launching products from discovery through post-launch optimization. **See also:** [Product Management](#product-management), [Agile](#agile)

### Product Differentiation
Characteristics making product distinct from competitors. Based on features, quality, service, or price. **See also:** [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning), [Value Proposition](#value-proposition)

### Product Discovery
Research and exploration to identify user needs, market opportunities, and optimal solutions. Precedes product development. **See also:** [Discovery](#discovery), [User Research](#user-research)

### Product Evolution
Continuous improvement and adaptation of product over time. Balances innovation with stability. **See also:** [Roadmap](#roadmap), [Iteration](#iteration), [Deprecation](#deprecation)

### Product Feedback
User-provided information about product experience, satisfaction, and improvement ideas. Essential input for development decisions. **See also:** [Feedback Loop](#feedback-loop), [User Research](#user-research)

### Product Launch
Bringing product or feature to market with coordinated go-to-market effort. Critical milestone. **See also:** [Launch](#launch), [GTM](#gtm-go-to-market-strategy)

### Product Lifecycle
Stages product moves through: introduction, growth, maturity, decline. Different strategies apply at each stage. **See also:** [Product Evolution](#product-evolution), [Market Maturity](#market-maturity)

### Product Management
Discipline of guiding product through its lifecycle from conception through launch and optimization. Sits at intersection of technology, business, and user experience. **See also:** [Product Manager](#product-manager), [Product Strategy](#product-strategy)

### Product Manager (PM)
Professional responsible for defining product strategy, roadmap, and features. Acts as "mini-CEO" of product. **See also:** [Product Management](#product-management), [Leadership](#leadership)

### Product Portfolio
Complete set of products offered by organization. Requires strategic management of cannibalization and synergies. **See also:** [Portfolio Management](#portfolio-management), [Cannibalization](#cannibalization)

### Product Roadmap
Documented plan showing features and releases over time. Communicates vision and strategy. Can be time-based or outcome-based. **See also:** [Agile Roadmap](#agile-roadmap), [Vision](#vision)

### Product Specification
Detailed documentation describing product requirements, behavior, and implementation details. Guide for development. **See also:** [Product Definition](#product-definition), [Feature Spec](#feature-spec)

### Product Strategy
Long-term plan describing how product will compete and win in market. Encompasses positioning, features, pricing, and growth strategy. **See also:** [Strategy](#strategy), [Product Management](#product-management), [Competitive Positioning](#competitive-positioning)

### Product Thinking
Mindset and approach viewing problems systemically and considering full product implications. Core skill for PMs. **See also:** [Product Management](#product-management), [Systems Thinking](#systems-thinking)

### Profitability
Revenue minus total costs resulting in net income. Essential metric for sustainable business. **See also:** [Unit Economics](#unit-economics), [Revenue](#revenue)

### Prototype
Interactive or simulation-based representation of product enabling user testing and feedback. More functional than mockup. **See also:** [Mockup](#mockup), [Wireframe](#wireframe), [User Testing](#user-testing)

### Prototyping
Process of creating prototype to test ideas and concepts. Enables rapid learning before full development. **See also:** [Prototype](#prototyping), [Iterative Design](#iterative-design), [MVP](#mvp-minimum-viable-product)

### Quantitative Research

### Quick Wins

## R

### Recurring Revenue
Revenue from subscriptions or recurring transactions. Predictable and valuable in SaaS models. **See also:** [MRR](#monthly-recurring-revenue-mrr), [ARR](#annual-recurring-revenue-arr), [SaaS](#saas-software-as-a-service)

### Referral
User acquisition through existing customer recommendations. Often indicates product value and satisfaction. **See also:** [Acquisition](#acquisition), [Network Effects](#network-effects), [Virality](#virality)

### Regulatory Requirements
Legal and compliance standards products must meet including: GDPR, CCPA, industry-specific regulations. **See also:** [Compliance](#compliance), [Data Privacy](#data-privacy)

### Requirements
Specifications of what product must do to satisfy user needs and business objectives. Foundation for development. **See also:** [Product Specification](#product-specification), [Feature Spec](#feature-spec)

### Retention
Percentage of users/customers continuing to use product over time. Often measured as inverse of churn rate. Critical for sustainable growth. **See also:** [Churn Rate](#churn-rate), [Customer Lifetime Value](#customer-lifetime-value)

### Retention Rate
Percentage of users from cohort still active after specific period (weekly, monthly, annual). Formula: (Users in Cohort End / Users in Cohort Start). **See also:** [Retention](#retention), [Churn Rate](#churn-rate), [Cohort Analysis](#cohort-analysis)

### Return on Investment (ROI)
Profit generated from investment divided by investment cost. Used to evaluate feature value. Formula: (Gains - Costs) / Costs. **See also:** [Unit Economics](#unit-economics), [Profitability](#profitability)

### Reverse Trial
Giving existing customers access to higher tier for free before charging. Increases conversion to paid. **See also:** [Freemium Model](#freemium-model), [Upsell](#upsell)

### RICE
Prioritization framework scoring features on: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort. Provides objective ranking. Formula: (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort. **See also:** [Prioritization](#prioritization), [Prioritization Frameworks](#prioritization-frameworks)

### Roadmap
Visual representation of planned features and releases over time. Communicates strategy and build priorities. **See also:** [Product Roadmap](#product-roadmap), [Agile Roadmap](#agile-roadmap)

## S

### SaaS (Software-as-a-Service)
Software delivery model where application is hosted and accessed via internet. Typical monetization through subscription. **See also:** [Subscription](#subscription), [Cloud Computing](#cloud-computing), [Recurring Revenue](#recurring-revenue)

### Sales Enablement
Providing sales team with tools, content, and information to sell more effectively. Includes competitive positioning, case studies, and training. **See also:** [GTM](#gtm-go-to-market-strategy), [Enterprise Product Management](#enterprise-product-management)

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
Portion of TAM a company can realistically serve. Accounts for geographic, industry, or capability constraints. **See also:** [TAM](#tam-total-addressable-market), [SOM](#som-serviceable-obtainable-market), [Market Size](#market-size)

### Scalability
Ability of product to handle growth in users, data, and transactions without degradation. Critical technical requirement. **See also:** [Infrastructure](#infrastructure), [Performance](#performance)

### Scenario Planning
Developing strategies for different potential futures based on market variables. Prepares product for uncertainty. **See also:** [Strategy](#strategy), [Risk Management](#risk-management)

### Scrum
Agile framework using time-boxed sprints, daily standups, and sprint retrospectives for iterative development. **See also:** [Agile](#agile), [Sprint](#sprint), [Kanban](#kanban)

### Secondary Research
Using existing data and research from third parties. Faster and cheaper than primary research but less customized. **See also:** [User Research](#user-research), [Primary Research](#primary-research)

### Segmentation
Dividing users or customers into groups with similar characteristics or behaviors. Enables targeted product development and marketing. **See also:** [Market Segmentation](#market-segmentation), [Personas](#personas)

### Sensitivity Analysis
Testing how changes to variables affect outcomes. Used in pricing, feature impact, and financial modeling. **See also:** [Scenario Planning](#scenario-planning), [Forecasting](#forecasting)

### Session
Single continuous interaction by user with product. Session duration and frequency are engagement metrics. **See also:** [Active Users](#active-users), [Engagement](#engagement)

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
Realistic portion of SAM company can capture given current resources and strategy. Most conservative market size measure. **See also:** [TAM](#tam-total-addressable-market), [SAM](#sam-serviceable-addressable-market)

### Specification
Detailed written requirements describing what product should do. Foundation for development and QA. **See also:** [Product Specification](#product-specification), [Requirements](#requirements)

### Spending
Resources allocated to initiatives including: money, time, and personnel. Critical constraint in prioritization. **See also:** [Budget](#budget), [Resource Allocation](#resource-allocation)

### Spillover Effects
Unintended positive or negative consequences of product changes affecting other metrics or user segments. **See also:** [Secondary Effects](#secondary-effects), [Experimentation](#experimentation)

### Sprint
Time-boxed iteration (typically 2 weeks) in Agile development for completing specific work. Includes planning, execution, review, and retrospective. **See also:** [Agile](#agile), [Scrum](#scrum), [Sprint Planning](#sprint-planning)

### Sprint Planning
Meeting at sprint start to define work to complete in upcoming sprint. Team estimates effort and commits to deliverables. **See also:** [Sprint](#sprint), [Backlog](#backlog)

### Stickiness
Measure of how strongly product retains users through habit and value. High stickiness indicates strong engagement. **See also:** [Engagement](#engagement), [Habituation](#habituation), [Retention](#retention)

### Strangler Pattern
Migration strategy gradually replacing legacy system functionality with new components. Reduces risk during modernization. **See also:** [Technical Debt](#technical-debt), [Product Evolution](#product-evolution)

### Strategy
Long-term plan for achieving competitive advantage and business objectives. Informs all product decisions. **See also:** [Product Strategy](#product-strategy), [Competitive Strategy](#competitive-strategy)

### Subscription Model
Business model where customers pay recurring fee for ongoing product access. Primary SaaS monetization approach. **See also:** [SaaS](#saas-software-as-a-service), [Monetization](#monetization)

### Success Metrics
KPIs indicating product is achieving objectives. Defined during planning and monitored post-launch. **See also:** [Metrics](#metrics), [KPI](#kpi-key-performance-indicator)

## T

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
Maximum market size if product captured every potential customer. Largest market size estimate. **See also:** [SAM](#sam-serviceable-addressable-market), [SOM](#som-serviceable-obtainable-market), [Market Size](#market-size)

### Targeting
Selection of specific customer segments to pursue with focused product and marketing efforts. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [Market Segmentation](#market-segmentation), [ICP](#ideal-customer-profile-icp)

### Teardown
Detailed analysis of competitor product deconstructing features, UX, and design decisions. Provides competitive insights. **See also:** [Competitive Analysis](#competitive-analysis), [Market Research](#market-research)

### Technical Debt
Accumulated cost of shortcuts and suboptimal decisions made during development. Requires ongoing paydown to maintain velocity. **See also:** [Product Maintenance](#product-maintenance), [Backward Compatibility](#backward-compatibility)

### Testing
Process of evaluating product to identify issues and validate expected behavior. Includes unit, integration, and user testing. **See also:** [Quality Assurance](#quality-assurance), [User Testing](#user-testing)

### Textbook Case
Example or scenario illustrating best practices or lessons in product management. Used for learning and discussion. **See also:** [Case Study](#case-study)

### Time-to-Value (TTV)
Time from user signup to experiencing meaningful product value. Shorter TTV drives better activation and retention. **See also:** [Onboarding](#onboarding), [Activation](#activation)

### Toggle
Feature flag enabling/disabling functionality. Used for experimentation and phased rollouts. **See also:** [Feature Flags](#feature-flags), [A/B Testing](#ab-testing)

### Touchpoint
Any interaction point between customer and brand/product. Customer journey includes all touchpoints. **See also:** [Customer Journey Mapping](#customer-journey-mapping), [User Experience](#user-experience)

### Trend Analysis
Identifying patterns and directions in metrics over time. Enables forecasting and early issue detection. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [Metrics](#metrics), [Forecasting](#forecasting)

### Trial Period
Fixed duration offering product access free or at reduced cost before requiring payment. Reduces purchase friction. **See also:** [Freemium Model](#freemium-model), [Monetization](#monetization)

### User Behavior
Patterns of how users interact with product including: feature usage, navigation paths, and engagement frequency. **See also:** [Analytics](#analytics), [Engagement](#engagement)

### User Experience (UX)

### User Flow

### User Interface (UI)

### User Journey

### User Research
Systematic investigation of user needs, behaviors, and preferences through interviews, surveys, and observation. **See also:** [Primary Research](#primary-research), [Qualitative Research](#qualitative-research), [Discovery](#discovery)

### User Segmentation
Dividing user base into groups with similar characteristics for targeted analysis and product experiences. **See also:** [Segmentation](#segmentation), [Personas](#personas)

### User Testing
Structured evaluation of product with target users through observation and feedback. Validates design and identifies issues. **See also:** [Usability Testing](#usability-testing), [Concept Testing](#concept-testing)

## V

### Validation
Process of confirming product ideas and assumptions through testing and user feedback. Essential before major investment. **See also:** [Hypothesis Testing](#hypothesis-testing), [Assumption Testing](#assumption-testing), [User Testing](#user-testing)

### Value Proposition
Clear statement of unique benefits product provides to target customers. Core messaging element. **See also:** [Positioning](#positioning), [Differentiation](#differentiation), [Brand Promise](#brand-promise)

### Value-Based Pricing
Pricing strategy where price reflects customer value received rather than cost or competitor pricing. Maximizes revenue from willing customers. **See also:** [Pricing Strategy](#pricing-strategy), [Pricing Model](#pricing-model)

### Velocity
Measure of work completed per sprint in Agile development. Enables sprint capacity planning. **See also:** [Agile](#agile), [Sprint](#sprint), [Capacity Planning](#capacity-planning)

### Vertical Integration
Company controlling multiple levels of production/distribution chain. Strategic consideration for platform strategy. **See also:** [Strategy](#strategy), [Competitive Advantage](#competitive-advantage)

### Viability
Economic sustainability of product considering costs, pricing, and market demand. Assesses whether product can be profitable. **See also:** [Business Model](#business-model), [Unit Economics](#unit-economics), [Profitability](#profitability)

### Viral Coefficient
Metric measuring how many users each customer recruits. Viral loop when >1. **See also:** [Virality](#virality), [Network Effects](#network-effects), [Growth Loops](#growth-loops)

### Virality
Degree to which product spreads through user referral and organic sharing. Network effects drive virality. **See also:** [Network Effects](#network-effects), [Viral Coefficient](#viral-coefficient), [Word of Mouth](#word-of-mouth)

### Vision
Long-term aspirational description of what product aims to achieve. Inspires and guides organization. **See also:** [Product Strategy](#product-strategy), [Roadmap](#roadmap)

## Framework Quick Reference

### Prioritization Frameworks
- **RICE**: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort
- **MoSCoW**: Must, Should, Could, Won't
- **Kano Model**: Must-haves, Performance, Delighters
- **Impact-Effort Matrix**: Quadrant-based prioritization
- **Value vs. Effort**: Simple two-factor analysis

### Goal-Setting Frameworks
- **OKR**: Objectives and Key Results (0-1.0 scale)
- **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
- **North Star Metric**: Single primary success metric
- **KPI Dashboards**: Multiple tracked metrics

### Customer Understanding Frameworks
- **Personas**: Fictional user archetypes
- **Jobs to be Done**: Problem and outcome focused
- **Segmentation**: User grouping and targeting
- **User Journey Mapping**: Touchpoint visualization
- **5 Why's**: Root cause analysis

### Analysis Frameworks
- **Five Forces**: Competitive landscape analysis
- **SWOT**: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- **Porter's Value Chain**: Value creation analysis
- **Blue Ocean**: Uncontested market strategy
- **TAM/SAM/SOM**: Market sizing methodology

### Growth and Metrics Frameworks
- **AARRR (Pirate Metrics)**: Acquisition, Activation, Revenue, Retention, Referral
- **Funnel Analysis**: Step-by-step conversion tracking
- **Cohort Analysis**: Grouped user performance over time
- **Unit Economics**: CAC, LTV, payback period
- **Metrics Framework**: Organized KPI structure
