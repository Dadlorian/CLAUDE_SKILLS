# SaaS Metrics Benchmarks by Stage

## Executive Summary

This document provides comprehensive industry benchmarks for SaaS metrics across different stages of company maturity. Data compiled from OpenView's SaaS Benchmarking Study (2023-2024), Pacific Crest Securities SaaS Survey (2023), and Tomás Tunguz's analysis of 150+ SaaS companies.

## 1. Early Stage (Series A-B) Benchmarks

### Growth Metrics

**Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) Growth Rate**
- Median: 80-100% YoY
- Top 25% performers: 150%+ YoY
- Target minimum: 60% YoY for sustainable growth
- Source: OpenView's 2023 SaaS Benchmarking Study

**Magic Number (New ARR / Prior Quarter Sales & Marketing Spend)**
- Strong: 0.75+
- Acceptable: 0.50-0.75
- Needs improvement: <0.50
- Top performers: 1.0+
- Reference: Pacific Crest Securities SaaS Survey (2023)

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
- Average: $0.50-$1.50 per dollar of ARR
- Strong: <$0.75 per ARR dollar
- Benchmarks by segment:
  - SMB: $0.40-$0.70 per ARR
  - Mid-market: $0.80-$1.20 per ARR
  - Enterprise: $1.50-$3.00 per ARR
- Reference: SaaS Capital (2023)

**CAC Payback Period**
- Acceptable: 12 months or less
- Excellent: 6-9 months
- Target for 100% growth: <12 months
- Industry median: 10-12 months
- Source: Tomás Tunguz analysis

### Retention Metrics

**Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**
- Excellent: >125%
- Good: 110-125%
- Acceptable: 100-110%
- Needs improvement: <100%
- Enterprise companies: Average 130-140%
- SMB products: Average 100-120%
- Reference: OpenView's 2023 SaaS Benchmarking Study

**Customer Churn Rate (Annual)**
- Target for growth: <5% annually
- Acceptable: 5-10% annually
- SMB SaaS: 5-7% (higher acceptable)
- Mid-market: 2-4%
- Enterprise: 1-2%
- Reference: SaaS Metrics Guide (2023)

**Logo Retention (Annual)**
- Excellent: >95%
- Good: 90-95%
- Acceptable: 85-90%
- Source: Tomás Tunguz

**Revenue Retention (Annual)**
- Excellent: >95% (accounting for downgrades)
- Good: 90-95%
- Acceptable: 85-90%
- Reference: OpenView

### Unit Economics Benchmarks

**Gross Margin**
- Early stage median: 65-75%
- Target minimum: 60%
- By business model:
  - Pure SaaS: 70-85%
  - SaaS with Services: 50-70%
  - PLG SaaS: 75-90%
- Source: Pacific Crest Securities (2023)

**Operating Margin at Series A**
- Typical: -80% to -40%
- Companies targeting IPO: -30% to -10%
- Reference: OpenView

**LTV:CAC Ratio**
- Minimum viable: 3:1
- Excellent: 5:1+
- Calculation: (ARPU × Gross Margin × Customer Lifetime in months) / (CAC)
- Source: SaaS Metrics Guide

## 2. Growth Stage (Series C-D) Benchmarks

### Growth Metrics

**Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) Growth Rate**
- Median: 40-60% YoY
- Top 25% performers: 75%+ YoY
- Decline from Series A expected
- Source: OpenView SaaS Benchmarking Study (2023)

**Magic Number**
- Median: 0.5-0.75
- Strong: >0.75
- These companies typically optimize more
- Source: Pacific Crest Securities

**Annual Contract Value (ACV)**
- Growth stage typical: $10,000-$100,000+
- Indicates focus on mid-market
- Increasing enterprise penetration

### Retention Metrics

**Net Revenue Retention**
- Median: 115-120%
- Top performers: 130%+
- Expansion revenue becomes key focus
- Source: OpenView (2023)

**Customer Churn**
- Median: 2-5% annually
- Improvement from Series A critical
- Enterprise churn: <2%
- Source: SaaS Metrics Guide

**Enterprise NRR vs SMB NRR**
- Enterprise can sustain 130-150% NRR
- SMB typically 100-120% NRR
- Mix matters significantly

### Efficiency Metrics

**CAC Payback**
- Median: 8-10 months
- Target: <12 months
- Improvement from Series A expected
- Reference: Tomás Tunguz

**CAC as % of Revenue**
- Acceptable: 30-50% of annual revenue
- Strong: <40%
- Benchmarks by segment:
  - SMB-focused: 30-40%
  - Enterprise-focused: 40-50%
- Source: OpenView

**Operating Margin**
- Median: -50% to -20%
- Path to profitability clarifying
- Reference: OpenView (2023)

## 3. Late Stage / Pre-IPO (Series E+) Benchmarks

### Growth Metrics

**Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) Growth Rate**
- Median: 25-40% YoY
- Top performers: 50%+ YoY
- Continued strong growth expected
- Reference: OpenView SaaS Benchmarking Study (2023)

**Rule of 40 Score**
- Formula: Revenue Growth Rate (%) + Operating Margin (%)
- Target: 40+
- Companies meeting Rule of 40: 15% higher valuations
- Strong performers: 50+
- Source: Pacific Crest Securities (2023)

### Market Expansion

**Total Addressable Market (TAM) Capture**
- Late stage target: 1-5% of TAM
- IPO-ready: 1-2% of TAM
- Reference: Venture Capital benchmarking

**Geographic Expansion**
- US revenue: 60-70% (for US-founded companies)
- International: 30-40%
- Target: 50/50 split for scale

### Profitability Path

**Operating Margin**
- Median: -20% to 0%
- Path to 15-20% profitability evident
- Reference: OpenView (2023)

**Adjusted EBITDA Margin**
- Pre-IPO target: 5-15%
- Roadmap to 20%+ visible
- Source: SaaS Capital

**Free Cash Flow**
- Target: Positive or near break-even
- Rule of 40 component critical

## 5. Category-Specific Benchmarks

### Vertical SaaS (Highly Specialized)

**Characteristics:**
- Higher CAC per dollar of ARR: $1.00-$1.50
- Slower initial growth: 40-60% Series A growth
- Higher NRR: 120-140% (specialized needs = expansion)
- Lower churn: 2-4%
- Reference: OpenView SaaS Benchmarking Study (2023)

**Examples:** Veeva (life sciences), Toast (restaurants)

### Horizontal SaaS (Broad Market)

**Characteristics:**
- Lower CAC per dollar: $0.40-$0.80
- Faster growth: 100%+ Series A growth
- Lower NRR: 100-115% (commodity risk)
- Higher churn: 5-10%
- Reference: SaaS Metrics Guide (2023)

**Examples:** Slack, Notion, Figma

### PLG (Product-Led Growth) SaaS

**Characteristics:**
- Very low CAC: $0.10-$0.40 per ARR
- Lower CAC but slower payback: 12-18 months
- Higher churn: 5-15% (freemium model)
- High viral coefficient: 1.2-2.0+
- NRR: Highly variable, 80-150%
- Reference: OpenView PLG SaaS Study (2023)

**Examples:** Figma, Slack, Notion (early days)

### Enterprise SaaS

**Characteristics:**
- Higher CAC: $1.50-$3.00+ per ARR
- Longer sales cycle: 6-12 months
- Higher ACV: $50,000-$500,000+
- Very low churn: <2%
- High NRR: 120-150%
- Reference: SaaS Capital (2023)

**Examples:** Salesforce, Workday, Adobe Creative Cloud

## 9. Benchmarking Methodologies

### OpenView Approach
- Survey: 400+ SaaS companies annually
- Segments: By ARR range, geography, industry
- Methodology: Weighted averages
- Frequency: Annual reports (2022, 2023, 2024)
- Strengths: Large sample, consistent methodology
- Reference: OpenView SaaS Benchmarking Study

### Pacific Crest Securities Approach
- Survey: 200+ companies, institutional investors
- Methodology: Institutional quality data
- Focus: Capital efficiency, growth sustainability
- Frequency: Annual
- Strengths: Investor perspective, quality data
- Reference: Pacific Crest Securities SaaS Survey (2023)

### Benchmarking Caveats
1. **Survivorship Bias:** Dead companies not included
2. **Timing Issues:** Survey conducted at different points in calendar
3. **Segment Mix:** Averages hide significant variation
4. **Accounting Differences:** Different revenue recognition standards
5. **Selection Bias:** VC-backed companies over-represented
6. **Currency Effects:** USD vs. international variation

## 10. Red Flags vs. Green Flags

### Green Flags

**Strong Unit Economics:**
- CAC payback: <9 months
- LTV:CAC ratio: >5:1
- NRR: >115%
- Gross margin: >70%
- Reference: OpenView SaaS Benchmarking Study (2023)

**Sustainable Growth:**
- Rule of 40 score: 40+
- Growth deceleration: <10% quarter-over-quarter
- Profitability path: Clear timeline
- Reference: Pacific Crest Securities

**Market Leadership:**
- NRR: >125%
- Churn: <3% annually
- Customer concentration: No customer >10% revenue
- Reference: SaaS Capital

### Red Flags

**Unit Economics Warning Signs:**
- CAC payback: >18 months
- LTV:CAC ratio: <2:1
- NRR: <100%
- Gross margin: <50%
- Reference: Tomás Tunguz SaaS Analysis

**Growth Problems:**
- Rule of 40 score: <20
- Rapid growth deceleration: >20% QoQ
- Rising CAC with stable growth
- Reference: OpenView (2023)

**Retention Issues:**
- Annual churn: >10%
- NRR declining
- Enterprise customers leaving
- Reference: SaaS Metrics Guide (2023)
