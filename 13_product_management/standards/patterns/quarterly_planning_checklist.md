# Quarterly Planning Checklist
## Comprehensive Framework for Strategic Product Planning

**Version**: 1.0
**Last Updated**: 2025-11-19
**Authority**: Best practices from Google, Stripe, Asana, Notion, and leading PM organizations

---

## Overview

Quarterly planning is the cornerstone of effective product management. This checklist guides teams through a comprehensive 8-week planning cycle that transforms strategic vision into executable quarterly roadmaps.

**Purpose**: Ensure rigorous strategy development, cross-functional alignment, and clear execution roadmaps that drive business results.

**Timeline**: 8 weeks (56 days) prior to quarter start
**Participation**: Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Leadership, Customers
**Success Rate Target**: 90% of plan executed on schedule
**Key Output**: Quarterly roadmap, OKRs, resource allocation, communication plan

## Pre-Planning Phase (Weeks 1-2)
**Owner**: VP Product
**Objective**: Gather intelligence, define planning parameters, and prepare team

### Week 1: Current State Assessment

#### 1.1 Prior Quarter Review
- [ ] **Conduct Previous Quarter Retrospective** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - What shipped vs. plan (% completion rate)
  - What was deprioritized and why
  - Learnings and surprises encountered
  - Team feedback on quarter execution
  - Success Criteria: Retrospective document created
  - Owners: VP Product, PM leads, Engineering lead

- [ ] **Analyze Prior Quarter Metrics** (Owner: Analytics Lead)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Review all OKRs vs. actual performance
  - Analyze impact of shipped features
  - Identify metrics that moved and why
  - Assess health of key business metrics
  - Success Criteria: Metrics analysis shared with team

- [ ] **Customer Impact Assessment** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - NPS score and trend analysis
  - Customer feedback on shipped features
  - Review CSAT and other satisfaction metrics
  - Document unexpected wins or misses
  - Success Criteria: Customer sentiment summary shared

- [ ] **Team Capacity Review** (Owner: Engineering Lead)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Assess team velocity and trends
  - Identify any resource changes coming
  - Document known constraints
  - Review technical debt and maintenance needs
  - Success Criteria: Capacity plan shared with PM

#### 1.2 Strategic Context & Guidance
- [ ] **Review Company Strategy & Goals** (Owner: CEO/COO)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Company-wide OKRs for the quarter
  - Strategic priorities and focus areas
  - Budget allocation and constraints
  - Key business pressures and opportunities
  - Success Criteria: Strategic guidance documented
  - Attendees: VPs, C-suite, Board (if relevant)

- [ ] **Competitive Landscape Update** (Owner: Product Marketing)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - New competitor moves and announcements
  - Industry trend analysis
  - Market opportunity assessment
  - Threat assessment and response planning
  - Success Criteria: Competitive brief distributed

- [ ] **Market & Trend Analysis** (Owner: Product Marketing)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Review analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester)
  - Assess market direction and customer priorities
  - Identify emerging categories or features
  - Monitor customer conference presentations
  - Success Criteria: Market trends analysis document

#### 1.3 Planning Logistics & Setup
- [ ] **Schedule Planning Sessions** (Owner: Product Operations)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Block calendar for all planning activities
  - Arrange meeting spaces (physical or virtual)
  - Send planning agenda and pre-reads
  - Confirm attendance from all required parties
  - Success Criteria: All meetings scheduled and confirmed

- [ ] **Prepare Planning Materials & Templates** (Owner: Product Operations)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Create planning workbooks
  - Set up roadmap template for quarter
  - Prepare stakeholder scorecard templates
  - Set up collaboration spaces (Miro, Figma, Docs)
  - Success Criteria: All templates ready and shared

- [ ] **Communicate Planning Kickoff** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Send company-wide planning announcement
  - Outline planning timeline and cadence
  - Explain process and how to participate
  - Address questions and logistics
  - Success Criteria: Team understands planning timeline

### Week 2: Baseline & Context Building

#### 2.1 Deep Data Analysis
- [ ] **Create Current State Dashboard** (Owner: Analytics)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - All key metrics by segment
  - Trends over last 3 months
  - Cohort analysis of recent features
  - Funnel analysis and conversion rates
  - Success Criteria: Dashboard shared, patterns identified

- [ ] **Identify Metric Opportunities** (Owner: Analytics with PM)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Metrics that are trending negative
  - Low-performing customer segments
  - Feature adoption gaps
  - Revenue leakage opportunities
  - Success Criteria: Opportunities documented

- [ ] **Model Key Business Scenarios** (Owner: Analytics with Finance)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Model impact of strategic initiatives
  - Assess revenue implications
  - Model customer acquisition/retention scenarios
  - Timeline financial impact
  - Success Criteria: Scenario models created

#### 2.2 Customer Intelligence Gathering
- [ ] **Synthesize Customer Research Insights** (Owner: Customer Success/Research)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Themes from customer interviews
  - Top feature requests and feedback
  - Pain points and unmet needs
  - Customer success team insights
  - Success Criteria: Research synthesis document

- [ ] **Review Sales Feedback & Objections** (Owner: Sales Leadership)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Common objections in sales process
  - Competitive losses and feedback
  - Feature requests from prospects
  - Key deal requirements
  - Success Criteria: Sales feedback documented

- [ ] **Analyze Support/Customer Service Data** (Owner: Customer Support)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Most common support issues
  - Product-related tickets trends
  - Bugs vs. feature gaps
  - Customer pain point themes
  - Success Criteria: Support analysis summary

- [ ] **Schedule Customer Advisory Board** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours (setup)
  - Identify 8-12 strategic customers
  - Schedule feedback sessions
  - Prepare discussion guide
  - Plan post-session synthesis
  - Success Criteria: Sessions scheduled for Week 4

#### 2.3 Team Input & Ideation
- [ ] **Facilitate Ideation Session with Product Team** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Brainstorm new initiatives
  - Discuss improvement opportunities
  - Identify technical debt to address
  - Capture innovation ideas
  - Success Criteria: Ideas documented and categorized

- [ ] **Engineering Input on Technical Direction** (Owner: CTO/VP Engineering)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Technical roadmap and investments needed
  - Architecture improvements required
  - Performance and scalability concerns
  - Technology evaluation and POCs
  - Success Criteria: Technical priorities documented

- [ ] **Design Direction & Vision** (Owner: VP Design)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Design system evolution
  - Experience improvements opportunities
  - Accessibility initiatives
  - Design-driven innovation ideas
  - Success Criteria: Design priorities documented

#### 2.4 Final Baseline Creation
- [ ] **Create Planning Primer Document** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Summarize all baseline research
  - Key metrics and health status
  - Prioritized opportunities list
  - Strategic focus areas
  - Key constraints and assumptions
  - Success Criteria: Primer distributed to all participants

## Strategy Development Phase (Weeks 3-4)
**Owner**: VP Product with CEO/Leadership
**Objective**: Define quarterly strategy aligned with company goals

### Week 3: Customer & Market Research Phase

#### 3.1 Customer Feedback Gathering
- [ ] **Conduct Customer Advisory Board Sessions** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 6 hours (3 sessions × 2 hours)
  - Present current direction and get feedback
  - Discuss feature requests and priorities
  - Understand customer strategic goals
  - Identify partnership opportunities
  - Success Criteria: 30+ hours of customer time, insights documented

- [ ] **Conduct Strategic Customer Interviews** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 8 hours
  - 8-10 customer interviews (45 min each)
  - Deep dive on use cases and pain points
  - Future needs and emerging trends
  - Feature validation with customers
  - Success Criteria: Interview notes synthesized into themes

- [ ] **Synthesize Customer Feedback** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Create themes from all feedback
  - Prioritize customer-requested features
  - Identify most valuable opportunities
  - Document customer quotes and stories
  - Success Criteria: Customer feedback synthesis document

- [ ] **Segment-Specific Feedback Review** (Owner: PM with Analytics)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - High-value customers: feedback themes
  - Enterprise customers: requirements
  - SMB customers: pain points
  - Churned customers: exit feedback
  - Success Criteria: Segment-specific priorities identified

#### 3.2 Market & Competitive Intelligence
- [ ] **Conduct Competitive Deep-Dive Analysis** (Owner: PMM)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Major competitor strategy review
  - Feature gap analysis vs. competitors
  - Pricing and positioning comparison
  - Go-to-market strategies of competitors
  - Success Criteria: Competitive landscape document

- [ ] **Analyze Industry Trends** (Owner: PMM)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Emerging technologies relevant to product
  - Industry analyst perspectives
  - Customer expectation shifts
  - Regulatory or compliance changes
  - Success Criteria: Trend analysis document

- [ ] **Market Opportunity Assessment** (Owner: PM with Finance)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - TAM/SAM/SOM analysis
  - Growth opportunities by segment
  - New market entry possibilities
  - Partnership opportunities
  - Success Criteria: Market opportunity assessment

### Week 3-4: Strategic Theme Development

#### 4.1 Strategic Prioritization Workshop
- [ ] **Facilitate Strategy Workshop** (Owner: VP Product with CEO)
  - Timeline: 8 hours (2 full days or 4 half days)
  - Participants: CEO, VP Product, PMs, CTO, PMM, Finance
  - Review all research and baseline data
  - Debate strategic options and tradeoffs
  - Define 2-3 quarterly strategic themes
  - Align on success definition
  - Success Criteria: Strategic themes agreed upon

- [ ] **Develop Strategic Narrative** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Write 2-3 page strategy document
  - Articulate reasoning for theme selection
  - Explain expected outcomes
  - Document key assumptions
  - Success Criteria: Strategy narrative shared and approved

#### 4.2 Strategic Alignment & Validation
- [ ] **Align with Executive Team** (Owner: CEO/VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Present quarterly strategy to leadership
  - Get feedback and approval
  - Discuss resource allocation
  - Identify cross-company dependencies
  - Success Criteria: Executive alignment and approval

- [ ] **Validate with Board (if applicable)** (Owner: CEO)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Board-level strategy review
  - Address any concerns
  - Get board perspective on priorities
  - Success Criteria: Board awareness and alignment

- [ ] **Define Success Metrics/OKRs** (Owner: VP Product with Analytics)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Define 3-5 quarterly OKRs per strategic theme
  - Set quantitative success metrics
  - Identify leading and lagging indicators
  - Establish measurement plan
  - Success Criteria: OKRs defined and finalized

#### 4.3 Risk & Dependency Assessment
- [ ] **Identify Strategic Risks** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Technology risks and challenges
  - Market risks and competitive threats
  - Execution risks and dependencies
  - Resource and budget risks
  - Success Criteria: Risk register created

- [ ] **Define Risk Mitigation Plans** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Response strategy for each risk
  - Contingency plans
  - Escalation criteria
  - Owner assignment for risk monitoring
  - Success Criteria: Mitigation plans documented

## Prioritization & Scoping Phase (Week 5)
**Owner**: PM with VP Product
**Objective**: Translate strategy into prioritized initiatives with clear scope

### Week 5: Initiative Definition & Prioritization

#### 5.1 Opportunity & Initiative Development
- [ ] **Generate Initiative List** (Owner: PMs)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Brainstorm all possible initiatives aligned to themes
  - Outline business cases for each
  - Estimate rough effort
  - Identify dependencies
  - Success Criteria: 20+ initiatives documented

- [ ] **Apply Prioritization Framework** (Owner: VP Product with PMs)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Use RICE, weighted scoring, or other framework
  - Score each initiative on impact and effort
  - Rank initiatives by priority
  - Identify top 10-15 candidates
  - Success Criteria: Initiatives scored and ranked

- [ ] **Detailed Business Case Development** (Owner: PMs)
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Develop detailed cases for top initiatives
  - Define clear success metrics
  - Estimate customer impact
  - Project revenue/business impact
  - Success Criteria: Business cases created

#### 5.2 Scope & Phasing Planning
- [ ] **Define Initiative Scope** (Owner: PM with Engineering)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Detailed requirements for each initiative
  - Scope boundaries and exclusions
  - Phasing approach if multi-phase
  - Dependencies and sequencing
  - Success Criteria: Scope documents created

- [ ] **Conduct Technical Feasibility Assessment** (Owner: CTO/Tech Lead)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Technical complexity review
  - Architecture changes required
  - Technology choices needed
  - Performance implications
  - Success Criteria: Technical feasibility documented

- [ ] **Estimate Effort & Timeline** (Owner: Engineering)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Story point estimation
  - Engineering time allocation
  - Timeline for development
  - Concurrent vs. sequential execution
  - Success Criteria: Effort estimates and timelines

#### 5.3 Final Initiative Selection
- [ ] **Executive Prioritization Review** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Review initiatives with business cases
  - Get CEO/leadership prioritization
  - Confirm resource availability
  - Address any trade-offs
  - Success Criteria: Final priority list approved

- [ ] **Define the Quarterly Roadmap** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Map initiatives to roadmap
  - Assign monthly breakdown
  - Identify must-have vs. nice-to-have
  - Build contingency into plan
  - Success Criteria: Quarterly roadmap created

## Roadmap Development Phase (Week 6)
**Owner**: Product team
**Objective**: Develop detailed, actionable quarterly roadmap

### Week 6: Detailed Roadmap Creation

#### 6.1 Roadmap Artifact Development
- [ ] **Create Executive Roadmap (1-Pager)** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Visual summary of quarterly themes
  - Top 3-5 initiatives per theme
  - Timeline and expected outcomes
  - Format: Visual roadmap suitable for leadership
  - Success Criteria: Executive roadmap approved

- [ ] **Develop Detailed Roadmap** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - All initiatives with timelines
  - Dependencies and sequencing
  - Resource assignments
  - Risk and contingency callouts
  - Success Criteria: Detailed roadmap completed

- [ ] **Create Feature-Level Breakdown** (Owner: PMs)
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Feature-level detail for each initiative
  - User stories or acceptance criteria
  - Design requirements
  - Analytics instrumentation needs
  - Success Criteria: Feature lists completed

#### 6.2 Design & Architecture Planning
- [ ] **Design Direction & Mockups** (Owner: Design Lead)
  - Timeline: 8 hours
  - High-level design direction for initiatives
  - Wireframes or mockups for key features
  - Interaction patterns
  - Design system updates needed
  - Success Criteria: Design artifacts created

- [ ] **Technical Architecture Planning** (Owner: Tech Lead)
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - System architecture changes
  - API design and contracts
  - Database schema changes
  - Technology selections
  - Success Criteria: Architecture docs created

#### 6.3 Resource Allocation & Planning
- [ ] **Detailed Resource Allocation** (Owner: VP Product with CTO)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - PM allocation per initiative
  - Engineering allocation by week
  - Design resource needs
  - Data science or QA needs
  - Success Criteria: Resource plan finalized

- [ ] **Create Detailed Timeline** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Week-by-week delivery plan
  - Milestone definition
  - Launch dates and timing
  - Review gates and checkpoints
  - Success Criteria: Detailed timeline created

#### 6.4 Success Metrics Planning
- [ ] **Define Success Metrics per Initiative** (Owner: PM with Analytics)
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Key metrics to track per initiative
  - Baseline metrics established
  - Target metrics defined
  - Measurement timeline
  - Success Criteria: Metrics framework documented

- [ ] **Create Analytics Instrumentation Plan** (Owner: Analytics)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Events and tracking to implement
  - Dashboard design for monitoring
  - Analysis approach
  - Reporting cadence
  - Success Criteria: Instrumentation plan documented

### Week 7: Alignment Sessions & Buy-In

#### 7.1 Detailed Alignment Reviews

  - Success Criteria: Engineering commitment secured

  - Design review of all initiatives

  - Design system impacts
  - Accessibility planning
  - Success Criteria: Design team aligned and committed

- [ ] **Operations & Customer Success Alignment** (Owner: CS Lead)
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Review impact on customer support
  - Training and documentation needs
  - Sales enablement requirements
  - Customer communication planning
  - Success Criteria: CS and Sales alignment secured

- [ ] **Data & Analytics Alignment** (Owner: Analytics Lead)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Success metrics review
  - Instrumentation plan review
  - Data pipeline requirements
  - Reporting and dashboard needs
  - Success Criteria: Analytics team aligned

#### 7.2 Customer & Market Stakeholder Review
- [ ] **Customer Feedback Validation** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Review proposed roadmap with key customers
  - Validate feature prioritization
  - Get feature timeline feedback
  - Identify any surprises or gaps
  - Success Criteria: Customer validation completed

- [ ] **Market & Competitive Response Plan** (Owner: PMM with PM)
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - How roadmap addresses competitive threats
  - Go-to-market approach
  - Competitive messaging
  - Launch communication strategy
  - Success Criteria: GTM strategy documented

#### 7.3 Leadership & Board Alignment
- [ ] **Executive Leadership Alignment** (Owner: CEO/VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Comprehensive roadmap review with leadership

  - Risk and contingency discussion
  - Success Criteria: Full executive alignment

- [ ] **Board Update (if applicable)** (Owner: CEO)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Board presentation of quarterly plan
  - Strategic rationale

  - Success Criteria: Board awareness and alignment

#### 7.4 Written Feedback & Iteration
- [ ] **Gather Written Feedback** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 3 days
  - Send roadmap for stakeholder feedback
  - Specific feedback request forms
  - Consolidate feedback themes
  - Success Criteria: Feedback consolidated

- [ ] **Incorporate Feedback & Iterate** (Owner: PM with VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Update roadmap based on feedback
  - Document decisions on feedback
  - Communicate changes to stakeholders
  - Finalize roadmap
  - Success Criteria: Final roadmap approved

## Communication & Launch Phase (Week 8)
**Owner**: VP Product with Marketing & Communications
**Objective**: Communicate quarterly plan across organization and externally

### Week 8: Communication & Launch

#### 8.1 Internal Communication
- [ ] **Prepare Communications Materials** (Owner: VP Product with PMM)
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Executive summary document
  - Detailed roadmap presentation
  - Strategic narrative
  - FAQ documentation
  - Success Criteria: All materials prepared

- [ ] **Plan Communication Rollout** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Communication schedule and sequence
  - Identify different audiences
  - Tailor messaging by audience
  - Timeline for rollout
  - Success Criteria: Communication plan finalized

- [ ] **All-Hands Presentation** (Owner: CEO/VP Product)
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours (meeting)
  - Quarterly strategy overview
  - Key themes and priorities
  - Expected impact and outcomes
  - Q&A with team
  - Success Criteria: Team aligned on strategy

- [ ] **Department-Specific Briefs** (Owner: Relevant Leaders)
  - Timeline: 4 hours total
  - Sales team briefing (1 hour)
  - Customer Success briefing (1 hour)
  - Marketing briefing (1 hour)
  - Support/Operations briefing (1 hour)
  - Success Criteria: All teams briefed and aligned

- [ ] **Individual Team Kickoff Meetings** (Owner: PMs)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Dedicated team meeting per product area
  - Roadmap details and sequencing
  - Timeline and milestones
  - Success metrics
  - Success Criteria: All teams kickoff completed

#### 8.2 Documentation & Publishing
- [ ] **Publish Roadmap** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Upload to roadmap tool (Productboard, etc.)
  - Make visible to appropriate stakeholders
  - Set up access and permissions
  - Document how to interpret roadmap
  - Success Criteria: Roadmap live and accessible

- [ ] **Create Roadmap Guide** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - How to read the roadmap
  - What each color/category means
  - How to provide feedback
  - How roadmap may change
  - Success Criteria: Guide document created

- [ ] **Archive Previous Quarterly Materials** (Owner: Product Ops)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Store previous quarter roadmap
  - Store retrospective materials
  - Organize for easy reference
  - Update historical archive
  - Success Criteria: Materials archived

#### 8.3 External Communication (if applicable)
- [ ] **Customer Communication Plan** (Owner: PMM with PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - What to communicate to customers
  - Customer forums/calls to brief
  - Blog or announcement strategy
  - Timeline for external communication
  - Success Criteria: External communication plan

- [ ] **Prepare Customer Communications** (Owner: PMM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Draft customer-facing announcements
  - Prepare for customer calls
  - Create FAQ for customers
  - Plan for demo days or webinars
  - Success Criteria: Materials prepared

- [ ] **Brief Key Customer Accounts** (Owner: Account Executives)
  - Timeline: 2 weeks
  - Scheduled customer briefing calls
  - Personalized messaging per account
  - Gather customer feedback on roadmap
  - Document competitive context
  - Success Criteria: Key accounts briefed

#### 8.4 Kickoff Readiness
- [ ] **Confirm Team Readiness** (Owner: PM with CTO)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Engineering team ready to start
  - Design team ready to execute
  - All prerequisites completed
  - Any blockers resolved
  - Success Criteria: Teams report readiness

- [ ] **Finalize Sprint Planning** (Owner: PM)
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Plan first sprint of quarter
  - Break down first initiatives
  - Allocate initial work items
  - Set first sprint goals
  - Success Criteria: Sprint planning complete

- [ ] **Conduct Planning Retrospective** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Quick feedback on planning process
  - What worked well, what to improve
  - Process improvements for next quarter
  - Celebration of plan completion
  - Success Criteria: Retrospective held

## Success Metrics & Review

### Quarterly Planning Success Criteria

#### Process Success Metrics
- [ ] **Timeline Adherence** (Target: 100%)
  - All planning phases completed on schedule
  - Key decision gates met timeline
  - Communications sent on schedule

- [ ] **Participation & Engagement** (Target: 90%+)
  - Required stakeholders participated
  - Feedback quality and depth
  - Attendance at key sessions

- [ ] **Cross-Functional Alignment** (Target: 90%+)
  - Engineering estimates confidence high
  - Design team aligned on approach
  - Leadership consensus on strategy

#### Output Quality Metrics
- [ ] **Roadmap Completeness** (Target: 95%+)
  - All initiatives have clear success metrics
  - Resource allocation finalized
  - Timeline clear and sequenced
  - Risk mitigation plans documented

- [ ] **Strategic Clarity** (Target: 9/10 rating)
  - Team understands strategic themes
  - Employees can articulate priorities
  - Customers understand direction
  - Competitive response is clear

#### Execution Metrics (Tracked Throughout Quarter)
- [ ] **Plan Adherence** (Target: 80%+)
  - % of planned initiatives started on time
  - % of planned work completed on schedule
  - Major scope changes vs. planned
  - On-time delivery to milestones

- [ ] **Quality of Execution** (Target: 80%+)
  - Features meet quality standards
  - Launch readiness criteria met
  - Customer satisfaction with shipped features
  - Engineering stability metrics

- [ ] **Initiative Impact Assessment** (Owner: PMs)
  - Customer impact of shipped initiatives
  - Revenue impact (if applicable)

#### 8.2 Process Review
- [ ] **Planning Process Retrospective** (Owner: VP Product)
  - What worked well in planning

  - Process improvements for next quarter

- [ ] **Cross-Functional Feedback** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Engineering feedback on prioritization
  - Design feedback on planning process

  - Leadership feedback on strategy

- [ ] **Team Retrospective** (Owner: PM)
  - Product team retrospective

  - Team dynamics feedback
  - Improvements for next quarter

#### 8.3 Learning & Improvement
- [ ] **Document Learnings & Insights** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Key decisions that worked well
  - Decisions to revisit
  - Customer learnings
  - Market/competitive learnings

- [ ] **Create Improvement Plan** (Owner: VP Product)
  - Process improvements identified
  - Planning methodology adjustments
  - Tools or approach changes
  - Training or skill development

## Essential Artifacts & Templates

### Required Deliverables
1. **Strategic Theme Document** (2-3 pages)
   - Quarterly focus areas
   - Strategic rationale
   - Expected outcomes

2. **Quarterly Roadmap** (Multiple formats)
   - Executive summary (1-pager)
   - Detailed roadmap (timeline view)
   - Feature-level breakdown

3. **Business Cases** (Per major initiative)
   - Business objective
   - Success metrics
   - Timeline and resources
   - Expected impact

4. **Risk Register**
   - Identified risks
   - Mitigation strategies
   - Monitoring approach

5. **OKR Document**
   - Quarterly OKRs
