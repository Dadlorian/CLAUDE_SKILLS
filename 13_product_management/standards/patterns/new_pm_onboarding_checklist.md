# New PM Onboarding Checklist: First 90 Days
## Strategic Integration Framework for Product Managers

**Version**: 1.0
**Last Updated**: 2025-11-19
**Authority**: Industry best practices from Reforge, Stripe, Notion, and Figma

---

## Overview

This checklist guides new Product Managers through their critical first 90 days—transforming from onboarding to full autonomy. The 90-day arc is divided into three 30-day phases, each with specific objectives, milestones, and success criteria.

**Purpose**: Ensure rapid competency development while building stakeholder confidence and establishing sustainable working patterns.

**Timeline**: 90 days (13 weeks)
**Success Rate Target**: 85% completion of core activities
**Typical PM Readiness Level**: Advanced independent contributor (Day 91)

## Phase 1: Immersion & Contextualization (Days 1-30)

**Objective**: Understand the business, product, market, and team dynamics. Build foundational relationships.

**Owner**: Hiring Manager + Direct Manager
**Escalation Path**: VP Product

### Week 1: Foundation Setting

#### 1.1 First Day Essentials
- [ ] **Complete IT Setup** (Owner: IT/Admin)
  - All software licenses provisioned
  - VPN, email, calendar access confirmed
  - Hardware configured (laptop, monitor, phone)
  - Slack, Jira, Figma, data warehouse access verified
  - Timeline: Day 1 EOB
  - Success Criteria: Full system access by 9 AM Day 2

- [ ] **Company Orientation** (Owner: People Ops)
  - Company mission, vision, values deep dive
  - Organizational structure walkthrough
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can articulate company vision in own words

- [ ] **Meet Direct Manager** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - 1:1 setup and frequency agreement
  - 90-day plan review
  - Role scope and expectations conversation
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Success Criteria: Written 90-day plan shared

- [ ] **Product Team Introduction** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Product team roster review
  - Individual coffee meetings scheduled (15 min each)
  - Slack introduction message crafted
  - Timeline: Day 1-2
  - Success Criteria: 8+ team members acknowledge introduction

#### 1.2 Product & Market Immersion
- [ ] **Core Product Walkthrough** (Owner: Assigned Mentor PM)
  - End-to-end product demo (2-3 hours)
  - User flows documentation provided
  - Feature inventory overview
  - Current roadmap review
  - Timeline: Days 2-3
  - Success Criteria: Can demo 5 core features independently

- [ ] **Customer Journey Mapping** (Owner: Customer Success)
  - Customer onboarding flow review
  - Common use cases identified
  - Top pain points documented
  - NPS feedback summary reviewed
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can explain top 3 customer segments

- [ ] **Competitive Landscape** (Owner: Marketing)
  - Competitive audit document review
  - Competitor product walkthrough (3-5 competitors)
  - Differentiation summary
  - Market positioning statement
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can articulate 3 competitive advantages

- [ ] **Product Analytics 101** (Owner: Analytics Lead)
  - Data warehouse access verification
  - Key metrics dashboard tour
  - Top 5 metrics deep dive
  - Data dictionary introduction
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can interpret basic funnel analysis

### Week 2: Stakeholder Mapping & Business Context

#### 2.1 Executive Stakeholder Meetings
- [ ] **CEO/Founder 1:1** (Owner: Direct Manager scheduled)
  - Strategic vision and priorities
  - Company growth targets (3-year plan)
  - Recent investor feedback
  - Key business pressures
  - Timeline: 45 minutes
  - Success Criteria: Understand top 3 business priorities

- [ ] **CFO Financial Overview** (Owner: Finance)
  - Unit economics deep dive
  - Revenue model explanation
  - Pricing strategy context
  - Budget allocation process
  - Timeline: 45 minutes
  - Success Criteria: Can explain how product impacts revenue

- [ ] **Head of Sales/Customer Success** (Owner: Sales Leadership)
  - Sales process walkthrough
  - Key customer expansion opportunities
  - Sales team pain points with product
  - Customer health assessment
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Success Criteria: Understand top 5 sales objections

#### 2.2 Cross-Functional Stakeholder Map
- [ ] **Create Stakeholder Influence Matrix** (Owner: New PM)
  - Identify 20-30 key stakeholders
  - Map influence vs. interest levels
  - Note stakeholder goals and concerns
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Matrix shared with direct manager, feedback received

- [ ] **Engineering Leadership Meeting** (Owner: CTO/VP Eng)
  - Technical architecture overview
  - System constraints and debt
  - Development velocity and capacity
  - Tech roadmap alignment
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Understand 3-5 major technical constraints

- [ ] **Design Leadership Meeting** (Owner: Head of Design)
  - Design system overview
  - Design process and review cycles
  - Current design priorities
  - Accessibility standards
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Success Criteria: Can access design system and review board

#### 2.3 Team Dynamics & Process
- [ ] **Individual 1:1s with Team Members** (Owner: New PM)
  - Meet each team member individually
  - Understand individual strengths and goals
  - Identify peer mentors
  - Clarify reporting relationships
  - Timeline: 15 min × team size (typically 5-10 people)
  - Success Criteria: Personal notes on each team member created

- [ ] **Process Documentation Review** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Sprint structure and cadence
  - Roadmap planning process
  - Standup format and frequency
  - Release process documentation
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can explain process to another PM

### Week 3-4: Deep Product Knowledge

#### 3.1 Product Architecture & Capabilities
- [ ] **Technical Architecture Deep Dive** (Owner: Senior Engineer)
  - System architecture diagram review
  - API documentation study
  - Database schema understanding
  - Performance constraints awareness
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can explain architecture to stakeholders

- [ ] **Feature Audit & Documentation** (Owner: Product Manager or PM Mentor)
  - Inventory all product features (50+)
  - Document feature use adoption rates
  - Identify underutilized features
  - Map feature dependencies
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Feature inventory completed and shared

- [ ] **Historical Product Decisions** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Review last 12 months of roadmap items
  - Understand what shipped and why
  - Review project retrospectives
  - Document success/failure learnings
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: 5+ key decision patterns understood

#### 3.2 Metrics & Success Framework
- [ ] **KPI Dashboard Setup** (Owner: Analytics Lead)
  - Understand current KPIs (typically 5-10)
  - Learn metric calculation methodologies
  - Review metric trends over past 12 months
  - Set up personal dashboard access
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can access and interpret all key metrics

- [ ] **Customer Research Review** (Owner: Product Manager)
  - Review last 6 months of user interviews
  - Understand customer sentiment trends
  - Identify recurring pain points
  - Review user research methodology
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Can summarize top 3 customer needs

#### 3.3 First Week Deliverable
- [ ] **Create 90-Day Learning Plan** (Owner: New PM)
  - Document learning gaps identified
  - Prioritize knowledge areas
  - Schedule deep dives and mentoring sessions
  - Identify success metrics for learning
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Plan reviewed and approved by manager

## Phase 2: Execution & Autonomy (Days 31-60)

**Objective**: Lead product work independently, make informed decisions, deepen stakeholder relationships, and demonstrate impact.

**Owner**: Direct Manager
**Escalation Path**: VP Product

### Week 5-6: Operational Ownership

#### 4.1 Roadmap Ownership
- [ ] **Current Roadmap Audit** (Owner: New PM)
  - Review rationale for all current roadmap items
  - Assess stakeholder alignment
  - Evaluate feasibility and timeline
  - Identify roadmap gaps
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Roadmap assessment document created

- [ ] **Lead Sprint Planning Session** (Owner: New PM)
  - Conduct backlog refinement
  - Prioritize sprint work
  - Manage stakeholder expectations
  - Set clear sprint goals
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Sprint planned and team confident in goals

- [ ] **First Roadmap Adjustment Proposal** (Owner: New PM)
  - Identify 2-3 potential optimizations
  - Present business cases for changes
  - Gather stakeholder feedback
  - Implement approved changes
  - Timeline: 5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Adjustment presented to stakeholders with rationale

#### 4.2 Analytics & Data-Driven Decision Making
- [ ] **Conduct Metrics Analysis Project** (Owner: New PM with Analytics)
  - Select 1-2 key metrics to analyze deeply
  - Conduct cohort analysis
  - Identify leading indicators
  - Present findings and recommendations
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Analysis presented to team with actionable insights

- [ ] **Set Up Weekly Metrics Review** (Owner: New PM)
  - Establish weekly metric review cadence
  - Create tracking dashboard
  - Document trends and anomalies
  - Share weekly insights with team
  - Timeline: 2 hours initial, 30 min weekly
  - Success Criteria: Weekly metrics review established

- [ ] **First Experiment Design** (Owner: New PM with Analytics)
  - Identify testable hypothesis
  - Design experiment methodology
  - Define success criteria
  - Plan implementation timeline
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Experiment approved and launched

#### 4.3 Customer Engagement
- [ ] **Conduct Customer Interviews** (Owner: New PM)
  - Interview 5-10 customers or users
  - Prepare interview guide
  - Document learnings
  - Present findings to team
  - Timeline: 8 hours total
  - Success Criteria: Interview notes documented and insights shared

- [ ] **Participate in Sales Call** (Owner: New PM)
  - Observe 2-3 sales demos
  - Participate in 1-2 customer calls
  - Understand objections firsthand
  - Identify product improvements
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Feedback documented and shared

- [ ] **Customer Success Meeting** (Owner: Customer Success Lead)
  - Review customer health metrics
  - Understand churn risks
  - Identify expansion opportunities
  - Discuss product improvements
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Understand top 5 at-risk customers

### Week 7-8: Strategic Contribution

#### 5.1 Strategic Project Leadership
- [ ] **Lead Feature Development Discussion** (Owner: New PM)
  - Facilitate requirements gathering session
  - Work with design on mockups
  - Align with engineering on technical approach
  - Document specifications
  - Timeline: 8 hours
  - Success Criteria: Complete PRD created and approved

- [ ] **Prepare Quarterly Strategy Input** (Owner: New PM)
  - Analyze market trends and opportunities
  - Review customer feedback themes
  - Assess competitive threats
  - Propose strategic initiatives
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Strategy input document created

- [ ] **Lead Cross-Functional Alignment** (Owner: New PM)
  - Facilitate design-engineering sync
  - Manage alignment discussions
  - Document decisions and rationale
  - Ensure all parties understand priorities
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Cross-functional team confident in direction

#### 5.2 Stakeholder Relationship Deepening
- [ ] **Executive Stakeholder Follow-ups** (Owner: New PM)
  - 1:1 check-ins with key stakeholders
  - Share relevant insights and data
  - Gather feedback on product direction
  - Understand their current priorities
  - Timeline: 5 hours (30 min × 10 stakeholders)
  - Success Criteria: Stakeholders feel heard and informed

- [ ] **Present Findings to Leadership** (Owner: New PM)
  - Create presentation of 30-day learnings
  - Share customer insights
  - Present initial recommendations
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Leadership provides constructive feedback

### Week 9: Mid-Point Review & Adjustment

#### 6.1 60-Day Checkpoint
- [ ] **Self-Assessment** (Owner: New PM)
  - Evaluate progress against 90-day plan
  - Identify acceleration opportunities
  - Note any skill gaps
  - Document decisions made and outcomes
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Honest assessment shared with manager

- [ ] **Manager Feedback Session** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Review progress on learning goals
  - Assess decision-making quality
  - Evaluate stakeholder relationships
  - Discuss any concerns or adjustments
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Feedback documented, adjustments made if needed

- [ ] **Team Retrospective** (Owner: New PM)
  - Conduct team retrospective
  - Gather feedback on collaboration
  - Identify working well and improvement areas
  - Adjust processes if needed
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Team input incorporated

## Phase 3: Strategic Ownership & Leadership (Days 61-90)

**Objective**: Demonstrate independent strategic thinking, own product outcomes, mentor others, and solidify leadership presence.

**Owner**: Direct Manager
**Escalation Path**: VP Product

### Week 10-11: Strategic Leadership

#### 7.1 Quarterly Planning Leadership
- [ ] **Lead Quarterly Planning Process** (Owner: New PM with VP Product)
  - Facilitate strategy development sessions
  - Synthesize market, customer, and competitive insights
  - Present quarterly theme and priorities
  - Build business cases for initiatives
  - Timeline: 10 hours
  - Success Criteria: Q plan created and approved by leadership

- [ ] **Communicate Quarterly Strategy** (Owner: New PM)
  - Create quarterly communication deck
  - Present strategy to cross-functional team
  - Hold Q&A sessions
  - Document strategy in accessible formats
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Team confident in quarterly direction

- [ ] **Build Detailed Roadmap** (Owner: New PM)
  - Create 3-month detailed roadmap
  - Add sizing, dependencies, and risks
  - Share with stakeholders for feedback
  - Finalize roadmap artifacts
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Roadmap shared with all stakeholders

#### 7.2 Product Vision Development
- [ ] **Draft Product Vision Statement** (Owner: New PM)
  - Review company vision and strategy
  - Articulate 1-3 year product vision
  - Identify key strategic themes
  - Draft vision narrative and strategy
  - Timeline: 5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Vision statement reviewed and approved

- [ ] **Market Opportunity Assessment** (Owner: New PM)
  - Analyze market trends and TAM
  - Assess competitive positioning
  - Evaluate expansion opportunities
  - Present market analysis
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Market opportunity document created

#### 7.3 Advanced Analytics & Insights
- [ ] **Develop Predictive Metrics** (Owner: New PM with Analytics)
  - Identify leading indicators
  - Build predictive models
  - Create early warning signals
  - Document metric rationale
  - Timeline: 6 hours
  - Success Criteria: Predictive framework documented

- [ ] **Conduct Competitive Intelligence** (Owner: New PM)
  - Deep dive into competitor strategy
  - Analyze competitive feature gaps
  - Identify market positioning opportunities
  - Present competitive analysis
  - Timeline: 5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Competitive intelligence report created

### Week 12: Advanced Customer Insights

#### 8.1 Customer Research Program
- [ ] **Conduct Advanced User Research** (Owner: New PM)
  - Interview 10-15 customers/users
  - Conduct 2-3 contextual inquiries
  - Perform journey mapping exercises
  - Create research synthesis document
  - Timeline: 12 hours
  - Success Criteria: Research findings guide roadmap decisions

- [ ] **Analyze Customer Segmentation** (Owner: New PM with Analytics)
  - Segment customer base by behavior
  - Identify high-value segments
  - Assess segment needs and preferences
  - Present segmentation strategy
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Segment strategy influences prioritization

- [ ] **Partner with Sales/CS for Insights** (Owner: New PM)
  - Conduct sales team interviews
  - Analyze support ticket trends
  - Review customer feedback
  - Create consolidated insights
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Insights incorporated into strategy

### Week 13: Final Review & Future Planning

#### 9.1 90-Day Reflection & Outcomes
- [ ] **Conduct Comprehensive Self-Evaluation** (Owner: New PM)
  - Review 90-day plan vs. actual execution
  - Assess decision quality
  - Document learnings and growth areas
  - Identify next 90-day priorities
  - Timeline: 3 hours
  - Success Criteria: Comprehensive evaluation document created

- [ ] **Gather 360 Feedback** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Collect feedback from 5-7 key stakeholders
  - Synthesize feedback themes
  - Conduct feedback discussion with PM
  - Identify development areas
  - Timeline: 5 hours total
  - Success Criteria: Feedback received and discussed

- [ ] **Manager Review & Beyond 90 Days** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Comprehensive 90-day review meeting
  - Assess readiness for full autonomy
  - Discuss growth and development opportunities
  - Plan next 90 days together
  - Timeline: 1.5 hours
  - Success Criteria: Clear path to full independence

#### 9.2 Leadership Visibility & Presence
- [ ] **Present 90-Day Impact Summary** (Owner: New PM)
  - Create impact presentation
  - Present to leadership team
  - Showcase decisions and outcomes
  - Highlight strategic thinking
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Leadership recognizes strategic contributions

- [ ] **Establish Thought Leadership** (Owner: New PM)
  - Publish 1-2 internal insights
  - Contribute to cross-functional discussions
  - Mentor junior team members
  - Present at team meeting
  - Timeline: 4 hours
  - Success Criteria: Recognized as thought leader

#### 9.3 Future Planning
- [ ] **Create Next 90-Day Development Plan** (Owner: New PM with Manager)
  - Identify advanced skill development areas
  - Plan strategic projects and ownership
  - Set growth and impact targets
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Plan created and shared with manager

## Cross-Phase Activities

### Weekly Activities (All 13 Weeks)

#### A. Ongoing Learning
- [ ] **Weekly 1:1 with Manager** (Owner: Direct Manager)
  - Timeline: 30-60 min
  - Success Criteria: Consistent attendance, action items tracked

- [ ] **Product Team Standup** (Owner: Team)
  - Timeline: 15-30 min
  - Success Criteria: Active participation, insights shared

- [ ] **Self-Directed Learning** (Owner: New PM)
  - Read product management books/articles
  - Take online courses (Reforge recommended)
  - Follow industry trends
  - Timeline: 3-4 hours per week
  - Success Criteria: Learning documented and applied

#### B. Relationship Building
- [ ] **Informal Stakeholder Interactions** (Owner: New PM)
  - Coffee chats with different team members
  - Attend cross-functional meetings
  - Participate in team social events
  - Timeline: 2-3 hours per week
  - Success Criteria: Strong relationships formed

#### C. Process Participation
- [ ] **Attend All Core Meetings** (Owner: New PM)
  - Product planning sessions
  - Cross-functional syncs
  - Customer calls (when relevant)
  - Timeline: 5-10 hours per week
  - Success Criteria: Perfect attendance and participation

### Monthly Activities (Every 30 Days)

#### B. Structured Reflection
- [ ] **Month-End Review** (Owner: New PM)
  - Document month's learnings
  - Assess progress on goals
  - Identify key decisions made
  - Plan next month priorities
  - Timeline: 2 hours
  - Success Criteria: Document created, manager reviewed

- [ ] **Stakeholder Feedback Gathering** (Owner: New PM)
  - Solicit feedback from 3-5 key people
  - Document themes and suggestions
  - Adjust approach based on feedback
  - Timeline: 1 hour
  - Success Criteria: Feedback recorded and actionable

## Success Metrics & Review Checkpoints

### Day 30 Checkpoint: Immersion Phase

**Owner**: Hiring Manager + Direct Manager

#### Evaluation Criteria

1. **Knowledge Foundation** (Target: 80%+)
   - [ ] Can articulate company mission and vision
   - [ ] Understands top 5 KPIs and current performance
   - [ ] Can explain product roadmap and rationale
   - [ ] Familiar with 3+ key competitors
   - [ ] Knows top 3 customer segments
   - **Measurement**: Informal assessment through conversations

2. **Relationship Quality** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Built relationships with 20+ key people
   - [ ] Identified peer mentors and learning resources
   - [ ] Established trust with team members
   - [ ] Regular 1:1 meetings scheduled
   - **Measurement**: Stakeholder feedback survey (5-point scale)

3. **Learning Velocity** (Target: 90%+ completion)
   - [ ] Completed 95% of assigned learning activities
   - [ ] Created relevant documentation and summaries
   - [ ] Identified knowledge gaps and learning plan
   - **Measurement**: Activity completion checklist

4. **Cultural Integration** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Understands team dynamics and culture
   - [ ] Participates actively in team activities
   - [ ] Demonstrates company values in interactions
   - **Measurement**: Manager observation and feedback

#### Review Meeting Agenda (2 hours)
- Discuss learnings and insights gained
- Assess readiness for Phase 2
- Address any concerns or challenges
- Confirm Phase 2 objectives and priorities
- Discuss mentoring and support needs

#### Success Outcome
- Proceed to Phase 2 with increased autonomy
- OR continue Phase 1 activities if gaps identified

---

### Day 60 Checkpoint: Execution Phase

**Owner**: Direct Manager + VP Product

#### Evaluation Criteria

1. **Decision-Making Quality** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Demonstrated sound judgment on 3+ decisions
   - [ ] Decisions aligned with strategy and data
   - [ ] Clear rationale and analysis behind decisions
   - [ ] Stakeholders confident in PM's judgment
   - **Measurement**: Stakeholder feedback on specific decisions

2. **Execution Excellence** (Target: 90% on-time)
   - [ ] Sprint goals consistently met
   - [ ] Roadmap items progressing on schedule
   - [ ] Clear communication on status and blockers
   - [ ] Proactive issue identification and resolution
   - **Measurement**: Project tracking metrics

3. **Stakeholder Trust** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Stakeholders rate PM favorably
   - [ ] Regular communication from stakeholders
   - [ ] Invited to strategic discussions
   - [ ] Recognized as valuable contributor
   - **Measurement**: Stakeholder feedback survey

4. **Customer Impact** (Target: Positive feedback)
   - [ ] Customer insights informing decisions
   - [ ] Feedback integrated into roadmap
   - [ ] Understanding of customer needs demonstrated
   - [ ] CS/Sales confidence in PM
   - **Measurement**: Qualitative feedback, NPS insights

5. **Technical Acumen** (Target: 7/10+)
   - [ ] Understands technical constraints
   - [ ] Can communicate with engineering effectively
   - [ ] Appreciates feasibility considerations
   - **Measurement**: Engineering feedback

#### Review Meeting Agenda (2 hours)
- Review decisions made and outcomes
- Assess stakeholder feedback and relationships
- Discuss customer impact and learning
- Identify acceleration opportunities
- Plan Phase 3 strategic projects
- Discuss any concerns or development needs

#### Success Outcome
- Proceed to Phase 3 with full strategic ownership
- Assign lead responsibility for major initiative
- OR create targeted support plan for specific areas

---

### Day 90 Checkpoint: Strategic Ownership Phase

**Owner**: Direct Manager + VP Product + CEO (optional)

#### Evaluation Criteria

1. **Strategic Thinking** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Articulated clear product vision
   - [ ] Demonstrated market understanding
   - [ ] Connected product strategy to business goals
   - [ ] Identified and prioritized key opportunities
   - [ ] Proposed innovative solutions
   - **Measurement**: Strategy presentation and feedback

2. **Leadership & Influence** (Target: 8/10+)
   - [ ] Led cross-functional teams effectively
   - [ ] Influenced decisions without direct authority
   - [ ] Built trust and credibility with stakeholders
   - [ ] Communicated vision compellingly
   - [ ] Mentored junior team members
   - **Measurement**: 360 feedback assessment

3. **Business Impact** (Target: Measurable progress)
   - [ ] Metrics moving in positive direction
   - [ ] Customer satisfaction maintained/improved
   - [ ] Roadmap items shipping on time
   - [ ] Team productivity and morale high
   - [ ] Revenue/business metrics on track
   - **Measurement**: OKRs, KPI dashboards

4. **Independent Capability** (Target: 9/10+)
   - [ ] Can operate independently on core work
   - [ ] Requires minimal management oversight
   - [ ] Seeks input proactively when appropriate
   - [ ] Makes good decisions with available information
   - [ ] Drives own learning and growth
   - **Measurement**: Manager observation and feedback

5. **Cultural Fit** (Target: 9/10+)
   - [ ] Embodies company values
   - [ ] Strong team relationships
   - [ ] Positive attitude and growth mindset
   - [ ] Contributes to team culture
   - [ ] Aligned with company direction
   - **Measurement**: Team feedback and observation

#### Final Review Meeting Agenda (2.5 hours)
- Comprehensive 90-day review
- Present impact summary and outcomes
- Discuss strategic contributions
- Provide 360 feedback
- Assess readiness for full independence
- Plan career development path
- Discuss next 90-day focus areas
- Celebrate wins and learning

#### Success Outcome
- **Full Autonomy**: PM ready for independent strategic ownership
  - Assign P&L or major product line responsibility
  - Quarterly planning lead role
  - Advanced mentoring opportunities

- **Conditional Readiness**: PM needs targeted development in specific area
  - Create focused improvement plan
  - Assign specific mentoring or training
  - 30-day follow-up checkpoint

- **Escalation Path**: If significant gaps exist
  - Comprehensive development plan created
  - Extended mentoring and support
  - 60-day comprehensive review

## Common Pitfalls & Mitigation

| Pitfall | Impact | Prevention |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Moving too fast without understanding | Misaligned decisions | Structured learning phase in first 30 days |
| Trying to change everything at once | Stakeholder resistance | Demonstrate understanding first, propose improvements later |
| Insufficient customer engagement | Out-of-touch decisions | Require customer interviews in all three phases |
| Technical naiveté | Credibility with engineering | Deep technical education in Phase 1 |
| Weak stakeholder relationships | Poor execution capability | Structured relationship building plan |
| Failure to ask for help | Missed learning opportunities | Explicit mentoring and office hours setup |
| Overconfidence in early decisions | Poor long-term outcomes | Peer review of early decisions and humility |
| Insufficient reflection | Missed learning | Monthly reviews and feedback sessions |

## Acceleration Opportunities

High-performing PMs can accelerate timeline by demonstrating:
- Quick mastery of domain fundamentals
- Strong stakeholder relationships
- Sound decision-making ability
- Customer empathy and insights
- Strategic thinking capability
- Team leadership and influence

**Acceleration Path**: If PM demonstrates exceptional performance by Day 60, consider:
- Assigning larger strategic project by Day 75
- Expanding scope of products/areas owned
- Leading quarterly planning earlier
- Reduced management oversight

**Note**: Quality of onboarding more important than speed. Thorough understanding builds long-term success.

---

## Onboarding Completion Criteria

A PM has successfully completed onboarding when they:

✓ Understand company mission, strategy, and business model
✓ Know product roadmap, strategy, and rationale
✓ Can independently make product decisions
✓ Have strong relationships with key stakeholders
✓ Demonstrate customer empathy and insights
✓ Show strategic thinking ability
✓ Lead cross-functional work effectively
✓ Understand market, competition, and customer landscape
✓ Contribute meaningfully to quarterly planning
✓ Have manager and team confidence
✓ Operate with minimal direct oversight
