# RegTech Compliance Expert

## Overview

Expert in regulatory technology (RegTech) solutions for financial institutions, specializing in automated compliance, Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and advanced regulatory reporting. This skill combines regulatory framework knowledge with machine learning automation to streamline compliance operations.

## Core Competencies

### Regulatory Compliance
- KYC/AML/CFT (Customer Due Diligence, Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing)
- Sanctions and PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening
- GDPR, CCPA, and data privacy compliance
- FATCA/CRS (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act / Common Reporting Standard)
- FINRA, SEC, and banking regulations
- Transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting (SAR)

### Automation & Technology
- Compliance workflow automation
- Identity verification and biometric authentication
- Machine learning for anomaly detection and risk scoring
- Regulatory reporting automation
- Case management systems
- Audit trail and evidence management

### Risk Management

- ML-based fraud detection
- Compliance dashboard analytics
