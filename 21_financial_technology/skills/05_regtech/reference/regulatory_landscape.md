# Global Regulatory Landscape for RegTech

## United States Regulations

### FinCEN (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network)
- Enforces AML/CFT laws and regulations
- Issues guidance and regulations (CFR Title 31)
- Operates OFAC (Office of Foreign Assets Control)
- Processes CTR and SAR filings

**Key Requirements:**
- CIP Rule (31 CFR 1020): Customer identification program
- CDD Rule (31 CFR 1010.230): Customer due diligence requirements
- SAR Filing: $5,000+ suspicious transactions
- CTR Filing: $10,000+ cash transactions

### OFAC Sanctions
- Administers and enforces economic sanctions
- Maintains SDN (Specially Designated Nationals) list
- Country-based sanctions programs
- Sectoral sanctions targeting specific industries
- Penalties: $100,000+ per violation, criminal prosecution possible

### Federal Reserve & OCC
- Banking regulation and supervision
- Capital requirements (Basel III)
- Stress testing and risk management
- Consumer protection and fair lending

### SEC Regulations
- Market manipulation prevention
- Insider trading regulation
- Investment advisory requirements
- Cybersecurity disclosure requirements

### Dodd-Frank Act (2010)
- Response to 2008 financial crisis
- Volcker Rule (proprietary trading restrictions)
- Derivatives regulation and clearing
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)
- Whistleblower programs

## European Union Regulations

### 5th EU Anti-Money Laundering Directive (5AMLD)
- Customer identification and verification
- Beneficial ownership transparency
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk jurisdictions
- Cryptocurrency exchange regulation
- Cross-border reporting requirements

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- Data subject rights (access, deletion, portability)
- Data protection impact assessments
- Data breach notification (72 hours)
- Privacy by design requirements
- Right to be forgotten (with exceptions)
- Data processing agreements for vendors

### MiFID II/IFD (Markets in Financial Instruments)
- Investor protection requirements
- Best execution obligations
- Conflict of interest management
- Algorithmic trading oversight
- Commodity derivatives trading

### PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2)
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA)
- Open banking requirements
- Secure communication protocols
- Transaction fraud prevention

## United Kingdom Regulations

### Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)
- Prudential regulation and supervision
- Senior Managers Regime (SMR)
- Individual Accountability Regime (IAR)
- Consumer Duty requirements
- Post-Brexit regulatory approach

### Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (MLR2017)
- Risk assessment and management
- Customer due diligence requirements
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk
- Beneficial ownership verification
- Regulatory filing requirements

## Asia-Pacific Regulations

### Singapore (Monetary Authority of Singapore - MAS)
- Notice on AML/CFT: Detailed requirements
- 3-tier risk-based approach
- Customer risk rating requirements
- Sanctions screening requirements
- Ongoing transaction monitoring

### Hong Kong (SFC/HKMA)
- Anti-Money Laundering Ordinance (AMLO)
- Customer due diligence requirements
- Beneficial ownership disclosure
- Transaction monitoring and reporting
- Sanctions list compliance

### Australia (AUSTRAC)
- AML/CFT Act 2006
- Reporting Entity Compliance Requirements
- Customer Due Diligence Rules
- Beneficial Ownership Register
- Transaction reporting obligations

### Japan (FSA/NPA)
- Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds Act
- Customer identification requirements
- Beneficial owner verification
- Suspicious transaction reporting
- Enhanced due diligence requirements

## Canada

### FINTRAC (Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre)
- Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) Act (PCMLA)
- Customer identification requirements
- Beneficial ownership reporting
- Suspicious transaction reporting
- Record retention obligations

## Emerging Markets

### India (RBI/FIU)
- Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA)
- Customer Identification Procedure (CIP)
- Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements
- Beneficial Owner identification
- Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR)

### Brazil (COAF/Banco Central)
- AML/CFT regulatory requirements
- Customer due diligence obligations
- Enhanced due diligence for PEPs
- Transaction monitoring and reporting
- Beneficial ownership verification

## International Standards

### FATF (Financial Action Task Force)
40 Recommendations framework:
1. Assessing risks and cooperation
2. National AML/CFT Policies
3. Money laundering offense
4. Terrorist financing offense
5. Predicate offenses
6-27. AML/CFT systems and controls
28-34. Powers and responsibilities
35-40. International cooperation

### Basel Committee on Banking Supervision
- Sound banking practices
- Risk management frameworks
- Capital adequacy standards (Basel III/IV)
- Operational resilience requirements

## Recent Regulatory Trends

### 1. Digital Assets & Cryptocurrencies
- Exchanges classified as financial institutions
- Enhanced due diligence requirements
- Transaction traceability mandates
- Stablecoin oversight proposals
- Travel Rule implementation for crypto

### 2. Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG)
- Climate risk disclosure requirements
- Board diversity mandates
- Modern slavery reporting
- Sustainability-linked derivatives

### 3. Technology & Cybersecurity
- Third-party risk management
- Cloud computing oversight
- Artificial intelligence governance
- Cybersecurity incident reporting

### 4. Beneficial Ownership Transparency
- Public ownership registers (EU)
- Automatic beneficial owner reporting
- Real-time beneficial ownership updates
- Corporate transparency act requirements

### 5. Enhanced PEP Screening
- Expanded PEP definitions
- Family of PEP identification
- Associates and close associates
- Dynamic risk-based approach
