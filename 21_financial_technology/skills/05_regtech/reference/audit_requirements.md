# Audit and Examination Requirements

## Regulatory Examination Framework

### Examination Objectives

```
COMPLIANCE EXAMINATION GOALS:

1. Assess Effectiveness of AML/CFT Program
   ├── Adequate policies and procedures
   ├── Trained and competent staff
   ├── Effective controls and monitoring
   ├── Timely suspicious activity reporting
   └── Adequate record retention

2. Detect and Prevent Financial Crimes
   ├── Money laundering activity
   ├── Terrorist financing
   ├── Sanctions violations
   ├── Fraud and embezzlement
   └── Identity theft

3. Evaluate Customer Risk Management
   ├── Customer due diligence adequacy
   ├── Risk assessment processes
   ├── Ongoing monitoring procedures
   ├── Enhanced due diligence application
   └── Beneficial ownership verification

4. Examine Transaction Monitoring
   ├── Monitoring rule effectiveness
   ├── Alert investigation procedures
   ├── False positive management
   ├── Case disposition appropriateness
   └── Escalation processes

5. Assess Regulatory Reporting
   ├── SAR filing appropriateness
   ├── SAR timeliness
   ├── CTR filing accuracy
   ├── Beneficial ownership reporting
   └── FATCA/CRS compliance
```

### Examination Cycle

```
RISK-BASED EXAMINATION SCHEDULE:

Low-Risk Institution:
├── Examination frequency: Every 3-4 years
├── Scope: Limited targeted review
├── Duration: 1-2 weeks
└── Findings: Typically minor

Medium-Risk Institution:
├── Examination frequency: Every 2-3 years
├── Scope: Comprehensive review
├── Duration: 2-4 weeks
└── Findings: Generally manageable

High-Risk Institution:
├── Examination frequency: Annual
├── Scope: Full comprehensive examination
├── Duration: 4-8 weeks
└── Findings: May include significant concerns

Problem Institution:
├── Examination frequency: Annual or more frequent
├── Scope: Intensive examination
├── Duration: 8+ weeks
└── Findings: Significant compliance gaps
└── Supervision: Enhanced monitoring
```

## Examination Procedures

### Customer Due Diligence Examination

```
CIP (Customer Identification Program) Review:

Sample Test:
├── Random selection of 20-50 customer accounts
├── Verification of identification documents
├── Quality of document verification
├── Timeliness of identity verification
├── Completeness of required information
├── Proper recordkeeping and retention

NIST Compliance Check:
├── Name, address, DOB verification
├── Document number capture (ID, SSN)
├── Document-based address confirmation
├── For non-US persons: Passport or ID
└── Government-issued ID confirmation

Examination Findings:
├── CIP rule violations identified
├── Deficiencies noted
├── Remediation action required
└── Management response expected
```

### CDD (Customer Due Diligence) Testing

```
Enhanced Due Diligence Assessment:

Testing Scope:
├── Risk assessments completed
├── Risk factors documented
├── Source of funds verified
├── Source of wealth investigated
├── Business purpose documented
├── For high-risk: Enhanced procedures applied
└── Updates: Periodic reassessment conducted

Sample Review:
├── 15-25 accounts including high-risk
├── Assessment quality and completeness
├── Documentation adequacy
├── Risk score appropriateness
├── Monitoring procedures adequate
└── EDD procedures properly applied

Red Flags:
├── No risk assessment
├── Cursory risk documentation
├── Inadequate source of funds verification
├── No beneficial owner documentation
├── Inconsistent risk scoring
└── Missing CDD updates
```

### Transaction Monitoring and Reporting

```
Monitoring System Testing:

1. Rule Effectiveness Review
   ├── Rule design appropriateness
   ├── Rule parameters and thresholds
   ├── Rule performance data
   ├── Historical alert analysis
   └── False positive evaluation

2. Alert Investigation Testing
   Sample: 20-30 alerts across time period
   ├── Investigation timeliness
   ├── Investigation depth and quality
   ├── Documentation completeness
   ├── Supervisor review
   ├── Appropriate disposition
   └── Escalation decisions

3. SAR Filing Testing
   Sample: 10-15 SARs filed
   ├── SAR appropriateness determination
   ├── Threshold verification ($5,000+)
   ├── Timeliness (30 days)
   ├── Narrative quality and detail
   ├── Required field completion
   ├── Beneficial ownership disclosure
   ├── Supporting documentation
   └── Retention and tracking

4. CTR Filing Review (if applicable)
   ├── Threshold accuracy ($10,000)
   ├── Timeliness (15 days)
   ├── Information accuracy
   ├── Recordkeeping
   └── Currency tracking

Deficiency Examples:
├── Transactions not screened
├── Alerts not investigated
├── SARs not filed timely
├── Inadequate SAR narratives
├── Missing supporting documentation
├── No monitoring rule effectiveness review
└── Inadequate alert thresholds
```

### Sanctions Compliance Testing

```
OFAC Screening Review:

Testing Scope:
├── Screening frequency (real-time or batch)
├── Screening coverage (100% of transactions)
├── List update frequency
├── Match review process
├── False positive management
├── True positive disposition
└── Escalation procedures

Sample Testing:
├── Review of screening results
├── Testing of OFAC matching algorithms
├── Evaluation of list management
├── Assessment of false positive rates
├── Review of matched transactions
├── Examination of decision documentation
└── Verification of appropriate escalation

OFAC Penalties:
├── Screening failures: $5,000-$50,000 per violation
├── Transactions with sanctioned parties: $20,000-$250,000+
├── Failure to block transaction: Up to $500,000
├── Criminal prosecution possible
└── Reputational damage
```

### Third-Party Vendor Management

```
Vendor Assessment:

1. Third-Party Risk Assessment
   ├── Service provider control adequacy
   ├── Sub-contractor management
   ├── Data security and privacy
   ├── Regulatory compliance
   └── Financial stability

2. Control Review
   ├── SOC 2 Type II certification
   ├── Internal audit reports
   ├── Control test results
   ├── Vulnerability assessments
   └── Business continuity plans

3. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Review
   ├── Performance metrics
   ├── Uptime guarantees
   ├── Incident response procedures
   ├── Data retention policies
   └── Termination procedures

4. Contract Requirements
   ├── Compliance obligations
   ├── Audit rights
   ├── Data security requirements
   ├── Regulatory change adaptation
   ├── Breach notification
   └── Regulatory examination cooperation

Examination Questions:
├── How are vendors screened?
├── How often are vendors audited?
├── What contractual safeguards exist?
├── How is vendor data protected?
├── What happens if vendor fails?
└── How is vendor compliance verified?
```

### Examination Findings Categories

```
MINOR FINDINGS:
├── Examples:
│   ├── Isolated documentation gaps
│   ├── Training timing missed
│   ├── Single transaction not screened
│   └── Minor policy deviation
├── Remediation: Within 30 days
└── Report: Cited in exam report

SIGNIFICANT FINDINGS:
├── Examples:
│   ├── Systemic monitoring gaps
│   ├── Pattern of late SARs
│   ├── Inadequate CDD procedures
│   ├── Failed sanctions screening
│   └── Widespread documentation deficiency
├── Remediation: Within 60-90 days
├── Follow-up: Required
└── Report: Matter of Supervisory Concern

CRITICAL FINDINGS:
├── Examples:
│   ├── No monitoring system
│   ├── SAR filing failures
│   ├── OFAC violations
│   ├── Transactions with sanctioned parties
│   └── No AML/CFT program
├── Remediation: Immediate
├── Follow-up: Frequent
├── Report: Violation listed
└── Potential enforcement action

VIOLATION:
├── Examples:
│   ├── Willful AML violation
│   ├── Knowing sanctions violation
│   ├── Pattern of non-compliance
│   └── Criminal activity assistance
├── Penalty: Civil and/or criminal
├── Follow-up: Enforcement action
└── Potential: License suspension/revocation
```

### Compliance Testing Framework

```
Compliance Test Example:

TRANSACTION MONITORING TESTING:

1. Population Definition
   ├── Time period: Last 30 days
   ├── Transaction count: ~50,000 transactions
   ├── Accounts: All active accounts
   └── Transaction types: All types

2. Sample Selection
   ├── Sample size: 200 transactions
   ├── Stratification: By amount, frequency, geography
   ├── Random selection: Unbiased
   └── Confidence level: 95%

3. Test Procedure
   ├── Verify transaction screened
   ├── Check alert generation (if appropriate)
   ├── Validate alert investigation
   ├── Verify disposition
   ├── Check documentation
   └── Assess supervisor approval

4. Results Analysis
   ├── Compliance rate calculation
   ├── Deficiency identification
   ├── Root cause analysis
   ├── Trend analysis
   └── Recommendations

5. Follow-up
   ├── Management acknowledgment
   ├── Corrective action plan
   ├── Implementation timeline
   ├── Effectiveness testing
   └── Documentation
```

## Best Practices

1. **Proactive Examination Preparation** - Maintain examination-ready documentation
2. **Regular Testing** - Conduct quarterly compliance testing
3. **Management Reporting** - Regular risk reporting to board/audit committee
4. **Third-Party Oversight** - Rigorous vendor management and audits
5. **Training & Awareness** - Comprehensive compliance training program
6. **Documentation** - Complete audit trail of all compliance activities
7. **Regulatory Updates** - Monitor regulatory guidance and rule changes
8. **Continuous Improvement** - Remediate findings and monitor effectiveness
