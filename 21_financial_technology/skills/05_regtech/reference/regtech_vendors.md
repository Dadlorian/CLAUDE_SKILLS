## Recommended Vendor Due Diligence

1. **References Check** - Contact 3-5 references from similar institutions
2. **Security Assessment** - Verify SOC 2, ISO 27001, penetration testing results
3. **Financial Stability** - Verify vendor financial health and viability
4. **Contract Terms** - Have legal review contract and SLAs
5. **Integration Testing** - POC with real data and workflows
6. **Regulatory Alignment** - Verify compliance with required regulations
7. **Support Assessment** - Test support quality and responsiveness
8. **Performance Metrics** - Establish KPIs and monitoring
9. **Exit Strategy** - Document transition procedures and data extraction
10. **Ongoing Assessment** - Quarterly performance reviews and annual RFP process
