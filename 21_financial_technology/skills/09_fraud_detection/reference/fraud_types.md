# Fraud Types Reference

## Financial Fraud Categories

### Payment Card Fraud
- **Card-not-present (CNP) fraud**: Online/phone purchases without physical card
  - Stolen card numbers
  - Account credential compromise
  - Testing transactions with invalid data
  - Skimming from ATMs or POS terminals

- **Card-present fraud**: Physical card usage
  - Counterfeit card fraud
  - Lost/stolen card fraud
  - Fraudulent PIN entry
  - Cloned card transactions

### Account Fraud
- **Account Takeover (ATO)**: Unauthorized access to legitimate accounts
  - Credential stuffing attacks
  - Phishing and social engineering
  - Malware infections
  - Session hijacking

- **New Account Abuse**: Fraudulent application for new accounts
  - Synthetic identity creation
  - Identity theft
  - Fake documentation
  - False information provision

### Refund & Return Fraud
- **Friendly fraud**: Legitimate customer abuse
  - Chargeback abuse
  - Refund without return
  - Quantity inflation
  - Switch fraud (return different item)

- **Policy abuse**: System rule exploitation
  - Return window abuse
  - Price matching exploitation
  - Promotion stacking
  - Coupon fraud

### Merchant Fraud
- **Chargebacks**: Illegitimate chargeback filing
  - Item not received claims
  - Item not as described claims
  - Unauthorized transaction claims
  - Multiple chargeback filing

- **Collusion fraud**: Merchant-customer collaboration
  - Money laundering through transactions
  - Inflated transaction amounts
  - Fake transaction records

### Identity Fraud
- **Synthetic identity**: Fabricated personas
  - Invented SSN/taxpayer IDs
  - Real + fake data combination
  - Account building over time

- **Account takeover with identity theft**
  - Address changes
  - Contact information modification
  - Fraudulent transactions as customer

### E-commerce Fraud
- **Triangulation fraud**: Use of stolen cards to purchase, resell
- **Drop shipping fraud**: Using stolen payment to purchase, ship elsewhere
- **Reselling stolen merchandise**: Liquidating stolen goods
- **Subscription fraud**: Unauthorized recurring charges

### Cryptocurrency Fraud
- **Pump and dump schemes**: Coordinated price manipulation
- **Wash trading**: Self-dealing to inflate volume
- **Rug pulls**: Developers abandoning projects with funds
- **Exchange fraud**: Withdrawal and account access

## Fraud Indicators & Patterns

### Behavioral Red Flags
- Multiple failed authentication attempts
- Unusual time-of-day activity
- Geographic impossibilities (impossible travel)
- Device/browser changes
- Language or currency preference changes
- VPN/proxy usage
- Multiple accounts from same device
- Bulk purchases with new accounts

### Transaction Red Flags
- High transaction velocity (rapid successive transactions)
- Round-number transactions
- Large orders from new customers
- High-value orders deviating from baseline
- Orders to high-risk countries/regions
- Shipping to PO boxes or reshippers
- Free shipping selection on high-value items
- Gift card purchases

### Network Red Flags
- Multiple cards linked to same account
- Multiple accounts from same device/IP
- Email addresses with variations
- Phone numbers linked to multiple accounts
- Accounts sharing physical addresses
- Accounts in fraud rings

## Fraud Ring Characteristics

- **Collusion patterns**: Coordinated activity across accounts
- **Resource sharing**: Same cards, devices, payment methods
- **Time synchronization**: Similar activity timing patterns
- **Geographic clustering**: Localized fraud activity
- **Escalating tactics**: Progression to higher fraud amounts
- **Targeting patterns**: Specific merchant/product focus

## Emerging Fraud Threats

### Advanced Techniques
- **Synthetic media fraud**: Deepfakes, voice spoofing
- **API fraud**: Automated bot-driven attacks
- **Supply chain fraud**: Compromised software/hardware
- **Social engineering**: Advanced phishing, pretexting
- **Biometric spoofing**: Fingerprint/facial recognition bypass

### Evolving Methods
- **Organized fraud**: Professional criminal networks
- **Affiliate fraud**: Marketing partner abuse
- **Influencer fraud**: Fake engagement and followings
- **Loyalty program abuse**: Points/rewards exploitation
- **Cryptocurrency mixing**: Blockchain obfuscation

## Regional & Contextual Fraud

### Geographic Variations
- **US credit card fraud**: Focus on CNP fraud, friendly fraud
- **European fraud**: Stricter liability, more account takeover
- **Asian markets**: High identity fraud, account abuse
- **Developing markets**: Card counterfeiting, testing attacks

### Industry-Specific
- **E-commerce**: Refund fraud, returns abuse
- **Financial services**: Account takeover, money laundering
- **Gaming/Digital**: Account compromise, fraudulent purchases
- **Travel/Hospitality**: Booking fraud, cancellation abuse
- **Cryptocurrency**: Withdrawal fraud, exchange attacks

## Fraud Loss & Impact

### Direct Losses
- Transaction fraud amounts
- Chargeback fees and penalties
- Processing and settlement failures

### Indirect Costs
- Customer acquisition costs (account takeover)
- Investigation and recovery costs
- Compliance and regulatory fines
- Reputational damage
- Customer retention impact
