# Wealth Management and High-Net-Worth Strategies

## Wealth Management Definition

Wealth management integrates financial planning, investment management, tax planning, and estate planning for high-net-worth individuals (HNW). Typically targets investors with $1M+ in investable assets.

## Advanced Tax Strategies

### Charitable Giving Optimization

**Donor-Advised Funds (DAF)**:
- Immediate tax deduction
- Flexible distribution timing
- Professional fund management
- Legacy giving opportunity

**Mechanics**:
```
Donate appreciated securities: $100,000 (cost basis $30,000)
- Avoid capital gains tax: $35,000 tax savings (35% rate)
- Receive immediate deduction: $100,000
- Distribute to charities over time
```

**Charitable Remainder Trusts (CRT)**:
- Donate assets, receive income stream
- Charitable deduction for remainder
- Asset valuation discounts
- Income for life or term of years

**Mechanics**:
```
Donate $500,000 appreciated asset to CRT
- Tax deduction: ~$250,000 (depends on terms)
- Receive 5% annual income: $25,000/year
- Charity receives remainder: ~$250,000
```

**Charitable Lead Trusts (CLT)**:
- Opposite of CRT
- Charity receives income first
- Remainder passes to heirs at discount
- Estate tax savings for next generation

### Business Interest Deductions

**Section 199A Deduction**:
- Up to 20% deduction of qualified business income (QBI)
- Pass-through entities and self-employment
- Income limitations and phase-outs
- Subject to W-2 wage limitations

**Optimization**:
- Entity structure selection (S-Corp vs LLC)
- Income splitting opportunities
- Reasonable compensation strategy
- Rental real estate structuring

### Real Estate Investment Tax Benefits

**Depreciation Strategies**:
- Residential: 27.5-year depreciation
- Commercial: 39-year depreciation
- Cost segregation studies: Accelerated depreciation
- Depreciation recapture tax on sale

**1031 Exchanges**:
- Defer capital gains taxes indefinitely
- Exchange like-kind real property
- Timing requirements (45/180 days)
- Geographic possibilities (remote properties)

**Opportunity Zones**:
- Invest in economically distressed areas
- Tax-deferred growth
- Potential permanent exclusion of gains
- Capital gains redeployment strategy

## Alternative Investment Strategies

### Private Equity Allocations

**Rationale**:
- Higher return potential (10-15% annually)
- Low correlation to public markets
- Illiquidity premium
- Diversification benefits

**Implementation**:
- Direct investments in companies
- Fund of funds for diversification
- Continuation funds for maturity
- Secondary market purchases

**Allocation Levels**:
- Conservative: 5-10% of portfolio
- Moderate: 10-20% of portfolio
- Aggressive: 20-30% of portfolio

### Hedge Fund Integration

**Strategies**:
- Long/short equity
- Market neutral
- Global macro
- Event-driven (M&A, distressed)

**Advantages**:
- Downside protection in bear markets
- Absolute return focus
- Diversification from traditional assets
- Sophisticated risk management

**Disadvantages**:
- High fees (2%+20%)
- Liquidity constraints (lock-ups)
- Manager selection risk
- Complexity and transparency issues

### Real Estate Beyond REITs

**Direct Real Estate Ownership**:
- Rental properties
- Commercial real estate
- Development projects
- Syndication opportunities

**Benefits**:
- Leverage and financing available
- Depreciation tax shields
- Cash flow generation
- Inflation protection
- Control and tangibility

### Cryptocurrency and Digital Assets

**Allocation Considerations**:
- Emerging asset class (10+ years old)
- High volatility (40%+ annual standard deviation)
- Limited correlation to traditional assets
- Regulatory and custody risks

**Positioning**:
- Speculative allocation for HNW clients
- 1-5% of portfolio for aggressive investors
- Bitcoin and Ethereum focus
- Institutional custody solutions

## Concentrated Positions Management

### Stock Concentration Risk

**Challenge**:
Inherited or employee stock options create concentration risk.

**Solutions**:

**1. Systematic Diversification**:
- Sell 5-10% quarterly
- Maintain core position
- Gradual tax-loss harvesting
- Avoid market timing

**2. Hedging Strategies**:
- Collar strategy (buy put, sell call)
- Protective puts on concentrated shares
- Variable forwards
- Exchangeable bonds

**3. Structured Products**:
- Monetize shares without selling
- Tax-deferred structures
- Maintain upside participation
- Complex but flexible

**Example: Collar Strategy**
```
Position: 10,000 shares Apple at $180
Current Value: $1,800,000

Collar Implementation:
- Buy put option at $160 (downside protection)
- Sell call option at $200 (caps upside)
- Cost: Near zero (call premium offsets put cost)

Protected Range: $160-$200
Maximum Loss: $200,000
Maximum Gain: $200,000
```

## Multi-Generational Planning

### Trust Strategies

**Revocable Living Trusts**:
- Control during lifetime
- Avoid probate
- Privacy (not public like wills)
- Easily modified

**Irrevocable Trusts**:
- Permanent estate tax savings
- Asset protection
- Income splitting
- Creditor protection

**Grantor Retained Annuity Trusts (GRAT)**:
- Gift appreciation to next generation
- Receive annuity payments
- Minimal gift tax if structured correctly
- Rolling GRATs for annual gifting

**Qualified Terminable Interest Property (QTIP) Trusts**:
- Provide for surviving spouse
- Transfer appreciation to heirs
- Estate tax deferral
- Flexibility in distribution

### Generation-Skipping Transfer Tax (GSTT)

**Definition**: Tax on gifts/transfers to generation 2+ below donor

**Annual Exclusion**: Each person has GSTT exemption ($13.61M in 2024)

**Planning**:
- Use exemption for large trusts
- Benefits multiple generations
- Permanent tax savings
- Coordination with estate tax planning

### Dynasty Trust Structures

**Features**:
- Perpetual duration in some states
- Multiple generations benefit
- Creditor protection
- Tax-efficient growth

**Typical Distribution Pattern**:
```
Grandparent (Settlor) → Trust
↓
Generation 1 (Children): Income and discretionary distributions
↓
Generation 2 (Grandchildren): Future income and principal
↓
Generation 3+: Perpetual beneficiaries
```

## Insurance Optimization for HNW

### Life Insurance Strategy

**Estate Tax Planning**:
- Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust (ILIT)
- Insurance-owned by trust (not estate)
- Death benefits available tax-free
- Funds available for estate taxes

**Amount Calculation**:
```
Estate Value: $10,000,000
Estate Tax Rate: 40%
Estate Taxes Due: $4,000,000
Recommended Insurance: $4,000,000-$5,000,000
```

### Liability Insurance

**Coverage Types**:
- Umbrella/excess liability: $1M-$10M+
- Professional liability: Business specific
- Directors and officers: Business owners
- Fiduciary liability: Trustees and executors

**Common Coverage Limits**:
- Underlying: $250k-$1M
- Umbrella: $1M-$10M
- Cost: $500-$3,000+ annually

## Business Succession Planning

### Ownership Transition

**Timeline**:
- 7-10 years pre-retirement: Plan structure
- 5-7 years: Begin transition
- 2-3 years: Owner semi-retired
- 1 year: Complete handoff

**Methods**:
1. **Family Succession**: Transfer to children
2. **Sale to Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP)**
3. **Third-party Sale**: Outside buyer
4. **Management Buyout**: Existing managers purchase

### Valuation and Pricing

**Methodologies**:
- Income approach (DCF, capitalization)
- Market approach (comparables)
- Asset approach (balance sheet)
- Blended approach

**Discounts and Premiums**:
- Lack of control discount: 20-35%
- Lack of marketability discount: 20-40%
- Key-person discount: 5-15%
- Premium for synergies: 10-30%
