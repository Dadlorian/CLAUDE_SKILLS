# Robo-Advisory Expert Skill

## Overview
Expert-level guidance on building, managing, and optimizing robo-advisory platforms. This skill provides comprehensive knowledge of automated portfolio management, tax-efficient investing, goal-based financial planning, and algorithmic wealth management for retail and institutional investors. Focus areas include portfolio optimization algorithms, tax loss harvesting, personalized asset allocation, risk profiling, and performance analytics.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Portfolio Management & Optimization
- **Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)**: Efficient frontier calculation, mean-variance optimization
- **Portfolio Construction**: Asset class selection, diversification strategies, correlation analysis
- **Optimization Algorithms**: Lagrange multipliers, quadratic programming, sequential least squares
- **Rebalancing Strategies**: Calendar-based, threshold-based, threshold-corridor methods
- **Risk Modeling**: Standard deviation, Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, CVaR calculation
- **Performance Attribution**: Brinson-Fachler analysis, factor decomposition, style analysis

### 2. Tax Optimization & Compliance
- **Tax Loss Harvesting**: Wash-sale rule compliance, tax-aware rebalancing, optimal harvesting timing
- **Tax-Efficient Investing**: Cost basis tracking, holding period management, dividend reinvestment
- **Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)**: Tax preference items, phase-out calculations
- **Municipal Bonds**: Taxable vs tax-exempt analysis, after-tax return optimization
- **Tax Reporting**: Form 1099, wash-sale notifications, tax-loss carryforwards
- **Regulatory Compliance**: SEC regulations, FINRA rules, suitability standards

### 3. Goal-Based Planning
- **Goal Definition**: Time horizons, return requirements, risk constraints, liquidity needs
- **Monte Carlo Simulation**: Retirement probability analysis, distribution testing, sensitivity analysis
- **Withdrawal Strategies**: Sequence of returns risk, safe withdrawal rates, dynamic strategies
- **Retirement Planning**: Social Security optimization, required minimum distributions (RMDs), estate planning
- **Life-Cycle Planning**: Accumulation vs. distribution phases, glide paths, dynamic allocation
- **Risk Profiling**: Questionnaire design, behavioral finance integration, investor suitability

### 4. Investment Products & Selection
- **Equity Products**: Individual stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, index funds
- **Fixed Income**: Bonds, bond funds, money market funds, treasuries
- **Alternative Investments**: Real estate, commodities, hedge funds, private equity
- **Factor Investing**: Value, growth, momentum, quality, low volatility factors
- **ESG Integration**: Environmental, social, governance criteria, impact investing
- **Currency & Commodities**: Hedging strategies, commodity exposure, currency risk management

### 5. Risk Management & Monitoring
- **Risk Assessment**: Volatility analysis, correlation analysis, stress testing
- **Drawdown Analysis**: Maximum drawdown, recovery periods, drawdown duration
- **Scenario Analysis**: Market stress scenarios, geopolitical risks, regulatory changes
- **Portfolio Monitoring**: Drift analysis, performance tracking, early warning systems
- **Benchmark Selection**: Appropriate benchmarks, blended indices, custom benchmarks
- **Risk Reporting**: Risk dashboards, VaR analysis, sensitivity analysis

### 6. Technology Implementation
- **Portfolio Analytics**: Trade execution, settlement, accounting
- **Client Onboarding**: KYC/AML compliance, suitability assessment, account setup
- **Automated Trading**: Order execution, slippage optimization, transaction cost analysis
- **Performance Reporting**: Customized dashboards, performance attribution, fee analysis
- **Data Integration**: Market data feeds, portfolio data, client data management
- **Compliance Systems**: Regulatory reporting, audit trails, documentation
