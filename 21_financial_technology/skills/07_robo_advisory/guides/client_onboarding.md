# Client Onboarding and Account Setup

## Onboarding Workflow

### Phase 1: Registration (15 minutes)

**Step 1: Account Creation**
- Email collection
- Password setup
- Account activation
- Email verification

**Step 2: Identity Verification (KYC)**
```
Required Information:
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Social Security Number
- Address
- Citizenship
- Employment status
- Account funding source
```

**Step 3: Suitability Assessment**
- Risk profile questionnaire
- Investment objectives
- Time horizons
- Financial constraints
- Knowledge assessment

**Step 4: Agreement Execution**
- Read and acknowledge terms
- Investment advisory agreement
- Privacy policy
- Risk disclosures

### Phase 2: Funding (2-5 business days)

**Step 1: Bank Account Linking**
```
Methods:
1. Micro-deposit verification
   - Two small deposits (2-3 days)
   - Confirm amounts to verify account
   - Secures ACH funding

2. Plaid/Similar Integration
   - Real-time verification
   - Immediate access
   - Bank-level security

3. Wire Transfer
   - Fastest method
   - Higher costs
   - Used for large amounts
```

**Step 2: Initial Deposit**
- Select funding amount
- Enter bank details
- Authorize transfer
- Confirmation receipt

**Step 3: Settlement**
- Wait for deposit to clear
- T+1 to T+3 typical
- Notification upon completion

### Phase 3: Portfolio Setup (1-2 days)

**Step 1: Portfolio Initialization**
```
Inputs:
- Risk profile questionnaire result
- Investment goal(s)
- Time horizon(s)
- Any constraints or preferences

Output:
- Recommended portfolio allocation
- Specific holdings list
- Projected returns and risks
```

**Step 2: Review and Confirmation**
- Show client their allocation
- Explain rationale
- Answer questions
- Get approval

**Step 3: Trade Execution**
- Submit initial purchase orders
- Execute on next trading day
- Monitor until settlement
- Provide confirmation

### Phase 4: Activation (1-3 days)

**Step 1: Settlement Completion**
- All trades settled
- Portfolio now fully invested
- Account active and live

**Step 2: Welcome Communication**
- Welcome email
- Welcome call (optional)
- Educational materials
- Next steps information

**Step 3: Platform Access**
- Account dashboard live
- Holdings and valuation visible
- Performance tracking active
- Reporting available

## Account Types and Structures

### Individual Accounts

**Standard Taxable Account**:
- Single owner
- Standard tax treatment
- 1099-B reporting
- Unlimited contributions

### Joint Accounts

**Joint Tenancy with Rights of Survivorship (JTWROS)**:
- Equal ownership
- Automatic transfer to survivor
- Both owners on statements
- Both responsible for decisions

**Tenancy in Common (TIC)**:
- Separate ownership percentages
- No survivorship
- Separate asset treatment
- Probate required

### Retirement Accounts

**Traditional IRA**:
- Pre-tax contributions
- Tax-deferred growth
- RMDs at age 73
- Deductibility limited by income

**Roth IRA**:
- After-tax contributions
- Tax-free growth
- No RMDs during lifetime
- Income limits on contributions

**SEP-IRA** (Self-employed):
- Up to 25% of self-employment income
- Higher contribution limits
- Employer contributions

**Solo 401(k)** (Self-employed):
- Up to $69,000 annually
- Employee deferrals + employer contributions
- Complex rules but flexible

### Custodial and Education Accounts

**529 Plans**:
- Education savings vehicles
- Tax-free growth for education expenses
- Flexible contribution amounts
- Superfunding provisions

**Coverdell ESA**:
- $2,000 annual limit
- K-12 and college eligible
- Education expenses only

**UTMA/UGMA** (Custodial Accounts):
- Guardian manages for minor
- Transfers to minor at age of majority
- Kiddie tax rules apply

## Compliance and Documentation

### KYC/AML Requirements

**Know Your Customer (KYC)**:
```
Requirement: Verify client identity and understand their profile

Procedures:
1. Collect personal information
   - Name, address, DOB, SSN
   - Employment and income
   - Net worth and assets
   - Investment experience

2. Verify identity
   - Government-issued ID
   - Address verification
   - Third-party databases

3. Document information
   - Complete files with signatures
   - Retain for regulatory requirements
   - Update periodically
```

**Anti-Money Laundering (AML)**:
```
Requirement: Ensure funds not from illegal sources

Procedures:
1. Screening
   - Check against sanctioned person lists
   - Screen country of origin
   - Monitor for suspicious activity

2. Source of Funds
   - Verify legitimate funding source
   - Understand business/income
   - Document for file

3. Ongoing Monitoring
   - Monitor transaction patterns
   - Flag unusual activity
   - Report suspicious transactions (SARs)
```

### Suitability Documentation

**Investment Policy Statement (IPS)**:
```
Key Components:
1. Client Profile
   - Risk tolerance
   - Time horizon
   - Financial goals
   - Constraints

2. Investment Objectives
   - Return targets
   - Income requirements
   - Risk limits

3. Asset Allocation
   - Target percentages
   - Acceptable ranges
   - Rebalancing policy

4. Security Selection
   - Fund choices
   - Benchmark selection
   - ESG preferences

5. Performance Monitoring
   - Measurement standards
   - Review frequency
   - Adjustment triggers
```

## Onboarding Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: Funding Delays

**Solutions**:
- Educate on settlement timelines
- Offer immediate portfolio planning (notional)
- Provide contingency funding options
- Follow up on pending transfers

### Challenge 2: Account Type Confusion

**Solutions**:
- Clear explanation of options
- Recommendation based on situation
- Comparison of tax implications
- Documentation of choice

### Challenge 3: Portfolio Suitability Concerns

**Solutions**:
- Detailed questionnaire review
- Scenario-based testing
- One-on-one consultation option
- Alternative allocations available

### Challenge 4: Technical Issues

**Solutions**:
- Responsive technical support
- Clear instructions with screenshots
- Phone support option
- Patience with unfamiliar users

## Onboarding Best Practices

1. **Streamlined Process**: Minimize steps while ensuring compliance
2. **Clear Communication**: Simple explanations at each stage
3. **Error Prevention**: Verify information at each step
4. **Education**: Help clients understand their investments
5. **Accessibility**: Support for all technical skill levels
6. **Compliance**: Robust KYC/AML procedures
7. **Personalization**: Customized recommendations
8. **Follow-up**: Ensure successful activation
9. **Documentation**: Complete compliance files
10. **Feedback**: Gather input on onboarding experience
