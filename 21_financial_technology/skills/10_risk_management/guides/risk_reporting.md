# Risk Reporting Guide

## Key Reports

### Daily Risk Report
```
Sent: 5:00 PM end of business day

Contents:
- VaR by desk/product
- Limit utilization %
- Greeks summary
- Stress test results
- NP Ls and watch list
- Large exposure changes
- Liquidity position

Recipients: CRO, Traders, Business heads
```

### Weekly Risk Committee Report
```
Sent: Every Monday morning

Contents:
- Market risk summary (VaR, Greeks)
- Credit portfolio quality metrics
- Concentration analysis
- Liquidity position (LCR, NSFR)
- Operational risk events
- Model validation results
- Regulatory updates

Decisions needed: Limit changes, remediation approvals
```

### Monthly Board Report
```
Sent: To Board Risk Committee

Contents:
- Executive summary
- Capital ratios and stress test results
- Material risks identified
- NP L trends and provisions
- Large losses or incidents
- Regulatory feedback
- Strategic recommendations

For: Board oversight and governance
```

## Data Quality
```
Validation:
✓ Numbers reconcile to GL
✓ Exposures match counterparty records
✓ Exceptions explained
✓ Trends reviewed for outliers
✓ Month-end close reconciliation

Standards:
- Reports within 24 hours of period end
- 99%+ data accuracy
- Clear definitions
- Audit trail maintained
```
