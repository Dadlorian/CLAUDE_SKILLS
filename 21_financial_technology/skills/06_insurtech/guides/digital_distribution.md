# Digital Insurance Distribution Guide

## Overview
Guide for building digital distribution channels for insurance sales and customer acquisition.

## Digital Channel Types

### Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)
- Insurer website/app
- Branded digital experience
- Full control of customer experience
- Own customer data
- Higher customer acquisition costs

### Online Aggregators
- Third-party comparison sites
- Lead generation platforms
- Lower CAC
- Limited customer data
- Lower margins

### Mobile-First
- Native mobile apps
- App-based quoting and purchase
- 24/7 availability
- Real-time push notifications
- High engagement

### Social Commerce
- Purchase via social media
- Influencer partnerships
- Social proof integration
- Seamless checkout
- Viral potential

## Direct-to-Consumer Website

### Website Architecture
```
Home Page
├─ Hero section with CTA
├─ Product overview
├─ Value propositions
├─ Customer testimonials
├─ Quick quote button
└─ SEO optimized

Products Page
├─ Product descriptions
├─ Coverage details
├─ Pricing information
├─ Comparison charts
└─ CTA to quote

Quote Flow
├─ Multi-step form
├─ Contextual help
├─ Progress indicator
├─ Auto-save
├─ Quote results
└─ Purchase CTA

Purchase Flow
├─ Policy details review
├─ Payment information
├─ E-signature
├─ Confirmation
└─ Document delivery

Customer Portal
├─ Policy management
├─ Payment management
├─ Documents
├─ Claims portal
└─ Customer support
```

### User Experience Best Practices
1. **Simple Quote Process**: 3-5 minutes max
2. **Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for mobile
3. **Clear Pricing**: No hidden fees
4. **Trust Signals**: Reviews, security badges
5. **Smooth Checkout**: One-page checkout preferred
6. **Accessibility**: WCAG compliance
7. **Load Speed**: <3 seconds load time

### Conversion Optimization
```
Optimization Tactics:
├─ A/B testing of CTAs
├─ Simplified forms
├─ Progress indicators
├─ Trust badges
├─ Social proof
├─ Urgency messaging
├─ Live chat support
└─ Exit-intent offers

Metrics to Track:
├─ Conversion rate
├─ Cart abandonment
├─ Cost per acquisition
├─ Customer lifetime value
└─ ROI by channel
```
