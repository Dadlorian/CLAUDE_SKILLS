## Core Insurance Processes

### Policy Lifecycle Management
- **Policy Design**: Coverage design, terms, conditions, exclusions
- **Quote Generation**: Rating algorithms, pricing calculators
- **Policy Issuance**: Document generation, binding authority
- **Modifications**: Mid-term changes, endorsements, amendments
- **Renewals**: Automatic renewal, retention, lapse management
- **Cancellations**: Voluntary and involuntary cancellations
- **Document Management**: Policy documents, evidence of insurance

- **Payment**: Settlement calculation, payment processing
- **Dispute Resolution**: Appeals and dispute handling
- **Closure**: Final documentation and archival

- **Pricing Models**: Actuarial pricing algorithms
- **Capacity Management**: Limit and quota management
- **Portfolio Management**: Risk aggregation and concentration
- **Reinsurance**: Treaty and facultative reinsurance

### Actuarial Functions
- **Loss Analysis**: Historical loss data analysis

## Performance Metrics

### Operational Metrics
- **Claims Processing Time**: Average time to process claim
- **Claims Approval Rate**: Percentage of claims approved
- **Customer Satisfaction**: NPS and customer satisfaction scores
- **System Uptime**: Platform availability percentage
- **Processing Accuracy**: Error rate in claims processing
- **Cost Per Claim**: Average cost to handle a claim

### Financial Metrics
- **Loss Ratio**: Incurred losses / earned premium
- **Expense Ratio**: Operating expenses / earned premium
- **Combined Ratio**: Loss ratio + expense ratio
- **Premium Volume**: Total premiums written
- **Customer Retention**: Renewal rate percentage
- **Premium per Policy**: Average premium by line

### Product Metrics
- **Claims Rate**: Frequency of claims by product
- **Average Claim Size**: Mean claim amount
- **Settlement Time**: Average days to settle claims
- **Fraud Rate**: Percentage of fraudulent claims
- **Customer Acquisition Cost**: Cost to acquire new customer
