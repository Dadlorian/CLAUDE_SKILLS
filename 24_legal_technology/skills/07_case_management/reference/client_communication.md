## Communication Best Practices

### Regular Status Updates
**Frequency Standards**:
- **Active Litigation**: Weekly or bi-weekly updates
- **Transactional Matters**: Update on each milestone
- **Ongoing Counsel**: Monthly or quarterly updates
- **Inactive Matters**: Quarterly or as needed

**Update Content**:
- Work completed in period
- Next steps and timeline
- Budget and billing status
- Issues or concerns
- Client action items needed

### Client Portal Utilization
**Portal Content**:
- Matter status and progress
- Document repository
- Billing information and invoices
- Time tracking summary
- Task and deadline information
- Team member information

**Portal Benefits**:
- 24/7 client access to information
- Reduces client inquiries
- Transparency about matter progress
- Self-service document access
- Reduces email volume

### Intake Communication
**Initial Contact Communication**:
1. Acknowledgment of intake inquiry
2. Conflict check status
3. Timeline for engagement decision
4. Information needed for engagement
5. Next steps and timeline

**Engagement Letter Communication**:
1. Engagement letter delivery
2. Scope of representation
3. Billing terms and fee arrangements
4. Client responsibilities
5. Signature and return process

### Ongoing Communication

#### Weekly Status Updates
- Time invested in matter
- Work completed
- Next steps and timeline
- Any issues or developments
- Budget status

#### Milestone Communication
- Milestone achievement notification
- Next phase overview
- Updated timeline
- Any required client decisions
- Budget implications

#### Problem Communication
- Issue identification
- Impact on timeline/budget
- Proposed solutions
- Client decision needed
- Escalation if necessary

#### Final Communication
- Matter conclusion notification
- Final deliverables
- Final billing
- Lessons learned
- Follow-up needs

## Common Communication Issues

### Under-Communication
- Infrequent status updates
- Failure to inform of issues
- Client surprises (bad developments, billing)
- Reduced transparency
- Client dissatisfaction
- Increased disputes

### Over-Communication
- Excessive status updates
- Unnecessary contact
- Information overwhelm
- Reduced efficiency
- Higher client costs

### Poor Communication Quality
- Unclear or jargon-heavy language
- Lack of context
- Missing supporting information
- Inconsistent communication
- Professional tone issues

### Communication Timing
- Delayed communication
- Untimely bad news notification
- Slow response to client inquiries
- Last-minute surprises
- Crisis communication

## Best Practices Summary

### Communication Frequency
1. Establish regular update schedule
2. Provide advance notice of major developments
3. Issue bad news promptly
4. Maintain consistency
5. Adjust frequency as needed

### Communication Quality
1. Use clear, non-technical language
2. Provide context and background
3. Explain implications
4. Offer solutions and recommendations
5. Maintain professional tone

### Communication Channels
1. Use secure channels for sensitive communication
2. Offer multiple communication methods
3. Provide 24/7 portal access
4. Maintain email archival
5. Support mobile access

### Documentation
1. Document all important communications
2. Maintain client communication records
3. Preserve engagement terms
4. Create audit trails
5. Comply with retention policies

### Risk Management
1. Clear expectation setting
2. Prompt issue notification
3. Documented decision-making
4. Transparent billing communication
5. Escalation procedures
