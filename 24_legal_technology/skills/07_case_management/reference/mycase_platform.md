# MyCase Practice Management Platform

## Overview

MyCase is a comprehensive, cloud-based legal practice management software designed specifically for small to mid-size law firms. Owned by AffiniPay (which also owns LawPay, CPACharge, and AffiniPay for Associations), MyCase benefits from tight integration with industry-leading payment processing. Known for exceptional customer support and an all-in-one approach, MyCase serves tens of thousands of legal professionals across all practice areas.

## Best Practices

### Setup Recommendations
1. Configure custom fields before importing data
2. Create matter templates for common case types
3. Set up billing rates for all timekeepers before time entry
4. Establish consistent matter numbering system
5. Customize invoice templates with firm branding
6. Configure email integration before go-live
7. Set up task templates for recurring workflows

### Daily Operations
1. Enter time daily (same-day accuracy)
2. File documents and emails to matters promptly
3. Update task statuses regularly
4. Log all client communications
5. Review calendar for upcoming deadlines
6. Apply payments as received

### Monthly Best Practices
1. Generate invoices on consistent schedule
2. Perform trust account three-way reconciliation
3. Review AR aging and follow up on collections
4. Run financial reports for management
5. Audit time entries for completeness
6. Clean up duplicate or inactive records

## Strengths & Considerations

### Key Strengths
- Excellent customer support (highly rated)
- Intuitive, user-friendly interface
- Seamless LawPay payment processing integration
- Strong client portal with mobile access
- Comprehensive feature set for small to mid-size firms
- Regular feature updates and improvements
- Competitive pricing
- Built-in e-signature and forms

### Considerations
- Less extensive integration ecosystem vs. Clio
- Advanced automation limited compared to enterprise systems
- Document assembly basic (merge fields, not full automation)
- Conflict checking basic (manual search)
- Limited advanced workflow capabilities

### Ideal Firm Profile
- Small to mid-size firms (1-50 attorneys)
- General practice or plaintiff-focused firms
- Firms prioritizing ease of use and support
- Firms wanting all-in-one solution
- Firms with straightforward billing needs
- Firms wanting strong client portal

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Clio
- **Support**: MyCase known for superior support
- **Pricing**: Similar pricing, MyCase slightly lower on some tiers
- **Integrations**: Clio has more (250+ vs. 100+)
- **Payment Processing**: MyCase/LawPay integration tighter
- **Features**: Very comparable core features

### vs. PracticePanther
- **UI**: Both modern, PracticePanther considered slightly sleeker
- **Support**: MyCase rated higher for support quality
- **Features**: Very similar feature sets
- **Pricing**: Comparable
- **Forms**: MyCase has stronger built-in forms

### vs. Smokeball
- **Platform**: MyCase cloud-only, Smokeball desktop-focused
- **Automation**: Smokeball stronger automated time tracking
- **Client Portal**: MyCase has superior portal
- **Documents**: Smokeball better document assembly
- **Pricing**: Similar pricing models
