# Calendaring & Deadline Management

## Overview
Effective calendaring and deadline management systems prevent missed deadlines, ensure compliance with procedural rules, and maintain client satisfaction. Integrated calendaring with case management provides automatic deadline tracking and notification.

## Types of Deadlines

### Statutory & Court Deadlines
- **Filing Deadlines**: Specific dates required by court rules
- **Response Deadlines**: Answers, replies, and opposing motions
- **Hearing Dates**: Scheduled hearings and trials
- **Discovery Deadlines**: Interrogatory responses, production dates
- **Statute of Limitations**: Deadline for filing claims

### Internal Deadlines
- **Task Deadlines**: Completion dates for internal work
- **Review Deadlines**: Partner review and approval deadlines
- **Draft Deadlines**: Document drafting and revision deadlines
- **Client Communication**: Status update deadlines
- **Billing Review**: Invoice preparation deadlines

### Client-Specific Deadlines
- **Matter Milestones**: Key dates in matter lifecycle
- **Payment Deadlines**: Invoice payment due dates
- **Response Deadlines**: Client response requirements
- **Decision Dates**: Key decision points in matter
- **Delivery Dates**: Deliverable completion dates

## Calendaring Best Practices

### Rule-Based Calendaring
**Objective**: Automatically generate calendars based on court rules and procedural requirements

#### Rule Setup
- **Jurisdiction Configuration**: Rules for each jurisdiction
- **Court Rules**: Specific rules for different courts
- **State Rules**: State-specific civil procedure rules
- **Administrative Rules**: Agency-specific deadline rules
- **Custom Rules**: Firm-specific deadline rules

#### Rule Components
- **Triggering Event**: Initial event that starts deadline clock
- **Deadline Calculation**: Days before/after triggering event
- **Notice Period**: Buffer time before deadline
- **Business Days**: Calculation method (calendar vs. business days)
- **Holidays**: Court holidays and office holidays excluded
- **Related Events**: Additional events triggered by first deadline

### Deadline Tracking System

#### Calculation Accuracy
- **Triggering Event Documentation**: Clear documentation of starting date
- **Rule Application**: Proper application of calculated days
- **Holiday Accounting**: Correct exclusion of holidays and weekends
- **Time Zones**: Consideration of filing time zones
- **Extension Tracking**: Documentation of deadline extensions
- **Verification**: Verification of calculated deadlines

#### Manual Deadline Entry
**When Used**: When court rules don't apply (contract deadlines, internal deadlines)
**Process**:
1. Document deadline date
2. Calculate notice period
3. Set calendar reminders
4. Assign responsibility
5. Document deadline source

### Calendar Integration

#### Practice Management Integration
- **Automatic Deadline Creation**: Calendar rules automatically create deadlines
- **Task Creation**: Corresponding tasks created for deadline
- **Attorney Assignment**: Automatic assignment to responsible attorney
- **Client Portal**: Deadline visibility through client portal
- **Notification**: Automatic reminders at specified intervals

#### Personal Calendar Integration
- **Outlook Sync**: Calendars sync to Outlook
- **Google Calendar**: Integration with Google Calendar
- **Mobile Devices**: Mobile calendar notifications
- **Cross-Calendar**: Visibility across attorney calendars
- **Conflict Detection**: Identification of scheduling conflicts

### Notification & Reminder System

#### Reminder Schedules
- **5-Day Reminders**: Advance notice for preparation
- **2-Day Reminders**: Final preparation warning
- **1-Day Reminders**: Day-before deadline
- **Same-Day Reminders**: Morning of deadline
- **Custom Schedules**: Firm-specific reminder schedules

#### Notification Channels
- **Email Notifications**: Email reminders to responsible parties
- **In-App Notifications**: Dashboard alerts and notifications
- **Mobile Notifications**: Push notifications on mobile devices
- **Calendar Notifications**: Calendar app notifications
- **Team Notifications**: Team messaging integration (Slack, Teams)

#### Escalation Procedures
- **Initial Notification**: First reminder to responsible party
- **Secondary Notification**: Second reminder to backup attorney
- **Manager Escalation**: Notification to supervising attorney
- **Firm-Wide Alert**: Critical deadline escalation to firm management

## Common Calendaring Mistakes

### Rule Configuration Errors
1. **Incorrect Jurisdiction**: Wrong rules applied to matter
2. **Excluded Holidays**: Missing jurisdiction-specific holidays
3. **Business Day Calculation**: Incorrect calendar vs. business day selection
4. **Rule Sequence**: Incorrect order of deadline calculations
5. **Filing Deadlines**: Not accounting for filing deadlines vs. deadline date

### Tracking Errors
1. **Missed Deadlines**: No notification system or ignored reminders
2. **Extension Documentation**: Failure to document deadline extensions
3. **Related Deadlines**: Missing related deadline modifications
4. **Trial Date Changes**: Calendar not updated when trial date changes
5. **Dismissal/Settlement**: Calendar not cleared when matter closed

### Process Errors
1. **Manual Entry Errors**: Typos in deadline dates
2. **Backup Plan Missing**: No contingency if assigned attorney unavailable
3. **Inadequate Notice**: Insufficient notice period before deadline
4. **Poor Escalation**: Critical deadlines not escalated to management
5. **Client Communication**: Client not informed of critical deadlines

## Deadline Management Best Practices

### Pre-Matter Setup
1. Jurisdiction configuration - ensure correct jurisdictions
2. Local court rules - confirm court-specific rules
3. Judge assignment - load judge-specific scheduling orders
4. Party information - identify all parties and attorneys
5. Client notification - inform client of critical deadlines

### During Matter
1. Deadline tracking - regular review of upcoming deadlines
2. Status updates - update calendar as matter progresses
3. Extension management - document all deadline extensions
4. Change notifications - update calendar when dates change
5. Team coordination - ensure team awareness of deadlines

### Deadline Approaching
1. Advance notice - provide sufficient advance notice
2. Work planning - schedule work before deadline
3. Draft review - allow time for drafting and review
4. Client coordination - confirm client approval/input
5. Final review - QA review before filing/submission

### Post-Deadline
1. Compliance verification - confirm deadline met
2. Follow-up tasks - create follow-up tasks if applicable
3. Related deadlines - update any related deadlines
4. Document retention - maintain deadline documentation
5. Lessons learned - capture improvements for future matters
