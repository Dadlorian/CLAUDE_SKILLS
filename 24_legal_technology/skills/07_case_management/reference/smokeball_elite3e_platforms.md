# Smokeball & Elite 3E Platform Reference

## Smokeball Practice Management

### Overview
Smokeball is a desktop-centric legal practice management software with powerful automation features, particularly known for automated time tracking and comprehensive legal forms libraries. Originally designed for small law firms with strong document assembly needs, Smokeball has evolved into a full-featured practice management solution.

### Key Differentiators

#### Automated Time Tracking
- **Activity Monitoring**: Tracks all computer activities automatically
- **Document Time Capture**: Automatically times document work
- **Email Time Capture**: Tracks email reading and composition
- **Matter Association**: Smart suggestion of matter for activities
- **Automatic Narratives**: Generates time entry descriptions automatically
- **Billable Time Recovery**: Captures time often missed in manual entry
- **Background Tracking**: No timers needed; works automatically

#### Desktop-First Architecture
- **Windows Native Application**: Full-featured desktop software
- **Offline Capability**: Works without internet connection
- **Cloud Sync**: Synchronizes when online
- **Web App**: Limited web access for mobile/remote use
- **Mobile Apps**: iOS and Android apps (more limited than desktop)

#### Legal Forms Library
- **50,000+ Forms**: Jurisdiction-specific legal forms
- **Federal and State**: Forms for all U.S. jurisdictions
- **Practice Area Coverage**: Family law, estate planning, personal injury, real estate, etc.
- **Court Forms**: Official court forms by jurisdiction
- **Automated Population**: Forms pre-fill from matter data
- **Regular Updates**: Forms updated for changes in law and rules

### Core Features

**Matter Management**
- Desktop-centric matter organization
- Matter-specific document folders (Windows folder integration)
- Custom matter types and templates
- Matter status tracking
- Activity stream

**Document Management**
- Windows file system integration
- Version control
- Document templates with automation
- PDF creation and management
- Email integration (Outlook focused)
- Document assembly with conditional logic

**Time & Billing**
- Automated time capture (primary strength)
- Manual time entry
- Flexible billing rates
- Invoice generation
- Pre-bill review
- Payment processing integration

**Calendar & Tasks**
- Calendar management
- Task tracking
- Deadline calculation
- Outlook calendar sync
- Reminders and notifications

**Client Communication**
- Client portal (web-based)
- Secure messaging
- Document sharing
- Invoice delivery and payment

### Pricing
- **Boost**: $39/user/month - Basic features
- **Grow**: $79/user/month - Most popular, includes automation
- **Prosper**: $99/user/month - Full features including forms library

### Ideal For
- Small firms (1-20 attorneys)
- Windows-based offices
- Firms wanting automated time tracking
- Practice areas needing extensive forms (family law, estate planning)
- Firms prioritizing billable time capture

### Limitations
- Desktop-centric (less flexible for remote work)
- Windows-focused (Mac support limited)
- Smaller integration ecosystem
- Less suitable for fully cloud-based operations
- Learning curve for automation features

## Elite 3E Enterprise Legal Management

### Overview
Elite 3E, developed by Thomson Reuters, is the leading enterprise legal management system for large law firms and corporate legal departments. It provides comprehensive financial management, matter management, and business intelligence for firms with complex requirements, multiple offices, and sophisticated financial operations.

### Target Market
- **Large Law Firms**: 100-5,000+ attorneys
- **Global Firms**: Multi-office, multi-currency, multi-jurisdiction
- **Corporate Legal Departments**: Enterprise in-house legal teams
- **Complex Billing**: Sophisticated rate structures, e-billing, LEDES
- **High Transaction Volume**: Thousands of matters, high billing volume

### Implementation Considerations

#### Complexity
- **Implementation Timeline**: 6-24 months typical
- **Resources Required**: Dedicated project team
- **Customization**: Extensive configuration needed
- **Data Migration**: Complex from legacy systems
- **Training**: Comprehensive training program required

#### Costs
- **Licensing**: Per-user licensing (timekeeper and non-timekeeper)
- **Implementation**: Substantial professional services
- **Customization**: Additional configuration costs
- **Infrastructure**: Server, database, network requirements
- **Maintenance**: Annual maintenance fees (15-20% of license)
- **Support**: Enterprise support agreements

#### Staffing Requirements
- **System Administrator**: Dedicated Elite 3E administrator
- **Financial Administrator**: Accounting/finance expertise
- **Report Developer**: BI and reporting specialist
- **IT Support**: Infrastructure and database administration
- **Trainer**: Ongoing user training and support

### Competitive Products

#### Aderant Expert
- Primary competitor for large firms
- Similar feature set and complexity
- Strong time and billing
- Extensive global deployment

#### Intapp Time
- Modern, cloud-native approach
- Strong analytics and AI
- Newer player in enterprise market
- Cloud deployment option

#### Carpe Diem
- Specialized time capture
- Often used alongside Elite 3E
- Advanced automated time tracking
- Activity-based time recording

### When to Choose Elite 3E
- Firm size: 100+ attorneys
- Global operations with multiple offices
- Complex billing requirements
- High transaction volume
- Need for sophisticated financial management
- E-billing and corporate client requirements
- Advanced analytics and BI needs
- Integration with enterprise systems (iManage, InterAction)

### When NOT to Choose Elite 3E
- Small firm (under 50 attorneys) - cost prohibitive
- Limited IT resources
- Simple billing arrangements
- Budget constraints
- Need for quick implementation
- Preference for cloud-only solution
- Simple practice management needs

### Advantages
- **Comprehensive**: All-in-one financial and matter management
- **Scalability**: Handles largest global firms
- **Flexibility**: Highly configurable for complex needs
- **Maturity**: Decades of development and refinement
- **Market Leader**: Industry standard for large firms
- **Integration**: Extensive integration ecosystem
- **Support**: Thomson Reuters enterprise support

### Challenges
- **Complexity**: Steep learning curve, complex to administer
- **Cost**: High licensing and implementation costs
- **Implementation Time**: Long, resource-intensive implementations
- **On-Premise Focus**: Primarily on-premise (cloud version emerging)
- **User Experience**: Interface less modern than cloud-native competitors
- **Customization Required**: Significant configuration needed
