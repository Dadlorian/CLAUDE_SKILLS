# Matter Lifecycle Management

## Overview
The matter lifecycle encompasses the complete journey of a legal matter from initial client contact through closure and post-matter archival. Proper matter lifecycle management ensures consistent client service, accurate billing, and effective knowledge management.

## Matter Lifecycle Stages

### Stage 1: Pre-Matter - Lead & Intake
**Duration**: Days to weeks
**Objectives**: Qualify lead, perform initial conflict check, gather information

#### Key Activities
- **Lead Capture**: Initial client contact and qualification
- **Conflict Check**: Check against existing clients and matters
- **Client Interview**: Gather matter details and client information
- **Initial Assessment**: Determine scope, budget, and timeline
- **Engagement Decision**: Approve or decline representation

#### System Functions
- **Intake Forms**: Automated data collection with conditional logic
- **Conflict Database**: Real-time conflict checking against all records
- **Client Master Record**: Create or update client record
- **Lead Management**: Track inquiries and conversion rates
- **Documentation**: Store intake notes and initial assessment

#### Best Practices
- Standardized intake process
- Automated conflict checking before engagement
- Clear documentation of intake decisions
- Rapid client response times (same day or next business day)
- Organized intake form workflow

#### Common Issues
- Incomplete conflict checks
- Overlooked adverse parties or related matters
- Poor documentation of intake discussions
- Delays in conflict resolution
- Duplicate client records

### Stage 2: Matter Creation & Engagement
**Duration**: Days to weeks
**Objectives**: Establish matter record, issue engagement letter, set up systems

#### Key Activities
- **Matter Creation**: Establish matter record with correct classification
- **Engagement Letter**: Generate and execute engagement letter
- **Rate Setup**: Configure hourly rates, billing rules, fee arrangements
- **Stakeholder Assignment**: Assign responsible attorney, support staff, supervisors
- **Matter Classification**: Set practice area, matter type, client type
- **Budget Establishment**: Set matter budget, budget type, timeline
- **Initial Tasks**: Create standard opening tasks based on matter type

#### System Functions
- **Matter Record Creation**: Master matter record with all key fields
- **Rate Management**: Configure matter-specific rates and billing rules
- **Team Assignment**: Assign attorneys, paralegals, and support staff
- **Template Selection**: Choose task, timeline, and document templates
- **Budget Management**: Set budget type and budget limit
- **Notification Setup**: Configure alerts and reminder rules

#### Best Practices
- Standardized matter naming conventions (e.g., "ClientName - MatterDescription - YYYY")
- Consistent classification system (practice area, matter type)
- Engagement letter templates by practice area
- Clear stakeholder assignment
- Defined escalation procedures

#### Common Issues
- Inconsistent matter naming
- Incorrect rate configuration
- Missing stakeholder information
- Engagement letter delays
- Poor classification standards

### Stage 3: Active Matter Management
**Duration**: Weeks to months/years
**Objectives**: Track work, manage deadlines, maintain client communication, ensure compliance

#### Key Activities
- **Task Management**: Create, track, and complete matter-related tasks
- **Time Tracking**: Record all time spent on matter
- **Calendar Management**: Track critical deadlines and events
- **Client Communication**: Regular updates and document exchange
- **Document Management**: Store and organize matter documents
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor matter status against budget and timeline
- **Escalation**: Manage issues and conflicts as they arise

#### System Functions
- **Task Management**: Create tasks with deadlines, responsibilities, and dependencies
- **Time Entry**: Capture time with proper coding and descriptions
- **Calendar Integration**: Sync calendar events and automatic deadline reminders
- **Client Portal**: Share documents and provide status updates
- **Document Repository**: Organize documents by category and date
- **Alerts & Reminders**: Deadline warnings and task reminders
- **Progress Reports**: Track hours, budget utilization, and milestone completion

#### Best Practices
- Daily time entry (not batch weekly entry)
- Clear task descriptions with matter references
- Regular client communication and updates
- Organized document naming and storage
- Regular progress reviews (weekly for complex matters)
- Escalation protocol for delays or budget concerns

#### Common Issues
- Delayed time entry
- Inaccurate time coding
- Missed deadlines
- Poor client communication
- Disorganized document storage
- Budget overruns without client awareness

### Stage 4: Billing & Invoicing
**Duration**: Monthly/quarterly or upon milestone
**Objectives**: Generate accurate invoices, track collections, manage client relationships

#### Key Activities
- **Invoice Generation**: Create invoices based on time and expenses
- **Billing Review**: Attorney review of time entries and charges
- **Client Adjustments**: Discount approvals or write-off decisions
- **Invoice Delivery**: Provide invoice to client with supporting documentation
- **Payment Tracking**: Monitor payment status and aging
- **Collection**: Follow up on overdue payments
- **Trust Accounting**: Record trust deposits and disbursements

#### System Functions
- **Billing Engine**: Automated invoice generation from time entries
- **Billing Rules**: Configure matter-specific billing rules and adjustments
- **Invoice Templates**: Customize invoices by client preference
- **Payment Processing**: Integrate payment methods (credit card, ACH, check)
- **Aging Reports**: Track outstanding invoices by age
- **Collection Workflow**: Manage payment follow-up and escalation
- **Trust Accounting**: Record trust deposits and disbursements

#### Best Practices
- Timely invoice generation (within 5 business days of billing period)
- Clear invoice descriptions tied to work performed
- Regular billing reviews before invoice issuance
- Prompt payment follow-up within 30 days
- Transparent trust accounting and billing correlation
- Regular reconciliation of trust and operating accounts

#### Common Issues
- Late invoice generation
- Billing disputes due to unclear descriptions
- Poor client communication about billing
- Slow payment follow-up
- Trust account reconciliation errors
- Write-offs not properly documented

### Stage 5: Matter Conclusion & Closeout
**Duration**: Days to weeks
**Objectives**: Complete final work, issue final invoice, close matter record, archive materials

#### Key Activities
- **Final Work**: Complete any remaining matter work
- **Deliverables**: Prepare and deliver final work product
- **Final Billing**: Complete time entries and issue final invoice
- **Trust Account**: Disburse any remaining trust balance
- **Document Review**: Organize final documents for archival
- **File Review**: Ensure all matter work is complete and documented
- **Matter Closure**: Close matter record and archive

#### System Functions
- **Matter Status**: Update matter status to closed
- **Final Billing**: Generate final invoice with all outstanding time/expenses
- **Trust Reconciliation**: Final trust account reconciliation and disbursement
- **Document Organization**: Final document review and organization
- **Closure Checklist**: Verify all closure requirements met
- **Archive**: Move matter to archive status
- **Notifications**: Notify team of matter closure

#### Best Practices
- Comprehensive closure checklist
- Final document review and organization
- Clear client communication about matter conclusion
- Final billing review and trust accounting verification
- Archival of all matter documents
- Documented closure by responsible attorney
- Post-matter client communication

#### Common Issues
- Incomplete closure of matters
- Unreconciled trust accounts
- Outstanding unbilled time after closure
- Disorganized final documents
- Missed closure deadlines
- Inadequate client communication

### Stage 6: Post-Matter - Archives & Knowledge
**Duration**: Indefinite
**Objectives**: Maintain records for future reference, comply with retention requirements, preserve firm knowledge

#### Key Activities
- **Matter Archive**: Maintain closed matter records for reference
- **Knowledge Capture**: Document lessons learned and best practices
- **Retention Compliance**: Comply with record retention requirements
- **Matter History**: Maintain accessible historical data
- **Client History**: Preserve client relationship history
- **Document Archival**: Long-term document storage and retrieval
- **Billing History**: Maintain billing and payment history

#### System Functions
- **Matter Archive**: Archive closed matters while maintaining access
- **Retention Management**: Enforce record retention policies
- **Matter History**: Provide access to historical matter information
- **Client Profile**: Maintain complete client history and past matters
- **Document Archival**: Long-term document storage with search capability
- **Reporting**: Historical reports and analytics
- **Compliance**: Ensure compliance with retention requirements

#### Best Practices
- Comprehensive documentation during active matter phase
- Clear archival standards and procedures
- Regular training on matter management
- Post-matter analysis and knowledge capture
- Compliance with all retention requirements
- Easy retrieval systems for archived matters

#### Common Issues
- Lost matter history due to poor archival
- Compliance violations with retention requirements
- Inaccessible archived documents
- Duplicated work on similar matters
- Inconsistent closure procedures

## Matter Type-Specific Workflows

### Litigation Matters
- **Opening**: Case intake, opposing party identification, jurisdictional analysis
- **Active Phase**: Discovery management, pleading deadlines, evidence organization
- **Closure**: Settlement/trial preparation, final deliverables, damages analysis

### Transactional Matters
- **Opening**: Deal structure analysis, parties identification, deadline documentation
- **Active Phase**: Document drafting, negotiation tracking, condition management
- **Closure**: Closing document preparation, execution coordination, final delivery

### Ongoing Counsel Matters
- **Opening**: Scope definition, retainer agreement, budget establishment
- **Active Phase**: Ongoing advisory, document review, analysis, relationship management
- **Closure**: Retainer termination, final deliverables, ongoing document archive

## Metrics & Monitoring

### Key Performance Indicators
- **Matter creation to engagement letter**: 3-5 days (target)
- **Time to first invoice**: 30 days (target)
- **Billing realization**: 90-95% (target)
- **Matter closure timeline**: 2-4 weeks after final work
- **Client satisfaction**: 4.5+ / 5.0 (target)

### Monitoring & Reporting
- **Matter Status Reports**: Weekly status of active matters
- **Aging Analysis**: Matters approaching deadlines
- **Budget Tracking**: Budget utilization against plan
- **Closure Pipeline**: Matters approaching closure
- **Collection Status**: Aging of outstanding invoices

## Best Practices Summary

1. **Standardization**: Consistent matter naming, classification, and workflows
2. **Automation**: Automated conflict checking, task creation, and notifications
3. **Documentation**: Clear documentation throughout matter lifecycle
4. **Communication**: Regular client updates and transparent billing
5. **Compliance**: Adherence to engagement letter terms and bar rules
6. **Quality**: Regular review and QA of matter setup and billing
7. **Closure**: Systematic approach to matter closing and archival
8. **Knowledge**: Capture lessons learned for future reference
