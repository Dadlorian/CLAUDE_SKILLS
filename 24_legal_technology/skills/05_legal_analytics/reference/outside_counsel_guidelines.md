# Outside Counsel Guidelines

## Overview
Outside counsel guidelines are policies and standards that in-house legal departments establish to govern the engagement, management, and evaluation of external law firms. These guidelines ensure cost control, quality consistency, and strategic alignment while providing clear expectations to counsel partners.

## Core Components

### Rate Standards

#### Billing Rate Caps
- **Partners/Counsel**: $300-$450/hour range
- **Counsel/Senior Attorneys**: $200-$350/hour
- **Associates**: $150-$250/hour
- **Paralegals**: $75-$150/hour
- **Law Clerks/Document Reviewers**: $50-$100/hour
- **Jurisdiction Adjustments**: Higher rates for major markets (NY, SF, LA)

#### Rate Escalation Limits
- Annual rate escalations capped at 3-5% maximum
- Multi-year engagements with fixed escalation provisions
- Performance-based rate modifications
- Seniority-based rate adjustments

#### Special Service Rates
- Expert witness services
- Investigation and litigation support
- Document review and legal research
- Alternative fee arrangements

### Staffing Requirements

#### Staffing Ratios
- **Partner Ratio**: No more than 1 partner to 2-3 associates
- **Senior Attorney Involvement**: Limited to key decisions and strategy
- **Appropriate Seniority**: Use paralegals and junior staff for routine work
- **Leverage Utilization**: Maximize efficient resource allocation

#### Approval Requirements
- Partner or counsel approval for all matters over specified threshold
- Partner approval for any matter expected to exceed budget
- Advance notice for staffing changes or attorney substitutions
- Approval for use of additional counsel or specialists

### Billing Standards

#### Time Entry Requirements
- Time billed in increments of 0.1 hours (6 minutes minimum)
- Clear task descriptions and matter coding
- Prohibition of block billing (combining multiple tasks)
- Requirement for contemporaneous time entries
- No premium billing (e.g., rush fees)

#### Billing Compliance
- Monthly invoices with itemized billing
- Compliance with all approved fee arrangements
- Adherence to outside counsel guidelines
- Proper matter coding and time allocation
- Supporting documentation for expenses

#### Invoice Audit Standards
- Invoices reviewed for completeness and accuracy
- Verification of time entries and billing rates
- Compliance with alternative fee arrangements
- Expense review and substantiation
- Payment holds for non-compliant invoices

### Expense Management

#### Reimbursable Expenses
- Court filing and service fees
- Deposition and transcript costs
- Expert witness fees (pre-approved)
- Travel costs (reasonable and pre-approved)
- Litigation support and document review

#### Non-Reimbursable Expenses
- Internal administrative costs
- Training and professional development
- Office overhead and equipment
- CLE and bar association dues
- Business development and marketing

#### Expense Controls
- Pre-approval required for expenses over specified threshold
- Competitive bidding for major expenses (experts, vendors)
- Reasonableness standard for all expenses
- Detailed receipts and documentation required
- Prohibition of duplicative charges

### Staffing Controls

#### Partner/Senior Attorney Involvement
- Partners limited to strategy sessions, court appearances, negotiations
- Counsel approval required for all partner time
- Documented reasons for partner involvement
- Minimum associate staffing for research and document review

#### Matter Staffing Plan
- Advance staffing plans for matters over specified cost
- Identification of lead attorney and key team members
- Explanation of staffing rationale
- Notification of staffing changes

#### Conflict Management
- Disclosure of potential conflicts upfront
- Compliance with conflict rules and waivers
- Prevention of multiple counsel working same matter without approval
- Rotation policies to avoid over-reliance on specific counsel

## Performance Standards

### Quality Requirements
- Timely and competent legal work
- Compliance with all applicable law and ethical rules
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Client communication and responsiveness
- Progress reporting and milestone achievement

### Cost Control Requirements
- Work within approved budgets or provide early variance notice
- Continuous focus on cost efficiency
- Minimization of duplicative work
- Regular review of time entries for overbilling
- Proposal of cost-saving approaches

### Service Standards
- Response time commitments (24-48 hours typical)
- Regular status updates and communication
- Escalation procedures for issues
- Availability during critical phases
- Post-matter debriefing and feedback

## Matter Engagement Provisions

### Engagement Letter Requirements
All matters should include written engagement letters specifying:
- Scope of work and deliverables
- Fee arrangement (hourly, fixed, contingency, hybrid)
- Rate schedule and rate escalation limits
- Staffing plan and key personnel
- Billing and payment terms
- Expense policy and pre-approval requirements
- Termination and transition provisions
- Conflict and confidentiality provisions
- Dispute resolution procedures

### Retention Strategy
- Regular counsel reviews and evaluations
- Annual rate renegotiation discussions
- Relationship assessment and strategic fit
- Feedback on performance and areas for improvement
- Cross-sell opportunities and scope expansion

### Transition Planning
- Backup counsel for critical practice areas
- Matter transition protocols
- Knowledge transfer and client continuity
- Relationship redundancy (avoid sole-source dependencies)

## Alternative Fee Arrangements

### Flat Fee Engagements
- Clearly defined scope and deliverables
- Risk allocation between firm and counsel
- Payment schedule (upfront, milestone, completion)
- Handle adjustment for material scope changes
- Appropriate for routine or predictable work

### Hybrid Arrangements
- Combination of retainer, hourly, and contingency elements
- Example: Monthly retainer + bonus for successful outcome
- Alignment of incentives with client objectives
- Clear metrics for bonus/penalty triggers

### Risk-Based Fees
- Fee based on case value or complexity
- Success-based contingency component
- Reduced rates in exchange for upside opportunity
- Appropriate for high-value or high-uncertainty matters

### Time-Certain Engagements
- Fixed number of hours or time budget
- Transparent tracking of hours used
- Ability to adjust scope if time budget constrains
- Used for project-based work with defined timelines

## Counsel Evaluation Framework

### Annual Counsel Review
- Assess performance against quality standards
- Evaluate cost control and efficiency
- Review relationship and cultural fit
- Discuss pipeline and staffing levels
- Plan for next year's relationship

### Performance Scorecard Elements
- **Quality (25%)**: Accuracy, responsiveness, competence
- **Cost (35%)**: Budget compliance, efficiency, rate management
- **Service (20%)**: Communication, availability, innovation
- **Strategic Value (20%)**: Alignment, expertise, thought leadership

### Rating System
- **Excellent (A)**: Exceed expectations, priority counsel
- **Good (B)**: Meet expectations, core counsel relationship
- **Satisfactory (C)**: Acceptable but room for improvement
- **Unsatisfactory (D)**: Consider replacement or probation

## Implementation & Governance

### Rollout Process
1. Draft guidelines reflecting organizational priorities
2. Socialize with top counsel partners
3. Incorporate feedback and finalize
4. Publish and formally communicate
5. Implement in new engagement letters
6. Apply to existing relationships through amendments
7. Monitor compliance and measure adoption

### Monitoring & Enforcement
- Regular compliance audits through invoice review
- Monthly spend reports to finance and leadership
- Quarterly business reviews with counsel
- Annual comprehensive performance reviews
- Escalation for non-compliance

### Technology Support
- Use SimpleLegal for automated invoice management
- Billing compliance dashboards
- Spend analytics and trend reporting
- Vendor management workflows
- Document and contract management

### Best Practices
- Communicate guidelines clearly and regularly
- Work collaboratively with counsel partners
- Be willing to adjust expectations based on matter circumstances
- Provide regular feedback and coaching
- Recognize and reward compliant counsel
- Be transparent about cost pressures and constraints
