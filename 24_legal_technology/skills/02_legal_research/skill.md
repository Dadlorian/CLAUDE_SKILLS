## Workflow Methodology

### Research Strategy Development
1. **Issue Identification**: Parse legal questions into searchable components
2. **Jurisdiction Mapping**: Identify applicable jurisdictions and hierarchies
3. **Source Selection**: Choose optimal platforms based on practice area
4. **Query Formulation**: Construct Boolean, natural language, or semantic queries
5. **Results Evaluation**: Assess authority level, treatment, and relevance
6. **Citation Validation**: Verify current good law status
7. **Update Monitoring**: Set alerts for subsequent treatment

### Platform Selection Framework
```
Primary Research: Westlaw Edge or LexisNexis (comprehensive, validated)
Cost-Effective: Fastcase or Casetext (budget-conscious firms)
AI-First: Ross Intelligence or Casetext CARA (cutting-edge)
International: vLex (cross-border, comparative law)
Analytics: Bloomberg Law (litigation insights, dockets)
Specialized: Practice-area specific databases
```
