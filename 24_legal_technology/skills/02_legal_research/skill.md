## Advanced Research Implementation

### Case Law Research Strategy
1. **Jurisdiction Identification**: Determine controlling, persuasive, and analogous authorities
2. **Authority Hierarchy**: Understand court structure and precedent weight
3. **Search Query Development**: Craft Boolean and natural language queries
4. **Database Selection**: Choose appropriate platform (Westlaw, LexisNexis, Casetext, etc.)
5. **Results Analysis**: Evaluate authority level, currency, and relevance
6. **Citation Validation**: Shepardize/KeyCite to verify good law status
7. **Authority Synthesis**: Combine authorities to build legal arguments
8. **Update Monitoring**: Set alerts for subsequent treatment

### Statutory Research Methodology
1. **Statute Location**: Find applicable statutes in relevant jurisdictions
2. **Legislative History**: Research legislative intent and prior versions
3. **Regulatory Implementation**: Find implementing regulations and guidance
4. **Administrative Interpretation**: Locate agency guidance documents
5. **Judicial Interpretation**: Research how courts have interpreted the statute
6. **Amendment Tracking**: Monitor for recent amendments or proposals
7. **Effective Date Analysis**: Determine applicability and temporal scope
8. **Related Authorities**: Find related statutes and regulations

1. **CFR/Registry Access**: Navigate Code of Federal Regulations and state registers
2. **Rule-Making History**: Find proposed rules and public comments
3. **Agency Guidance**: Locate interpretive guidance and policy statements
4. **Compliance Requirements**: Identify specific regulatory requirements
5. **Enforcement Patterns**: Research agency enforcement priorities
6. **Updates & Amendments**: Monitor for regulatory changes
7. **International Regulations**: Research applicable international standards
8. **Compliance Resources**: Find compliance guidance and tools
