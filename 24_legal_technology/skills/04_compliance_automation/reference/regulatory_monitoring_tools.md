## Best Practices for Regulatory Monitoring

### Governance Model
1. **Responsibility Assignment**: Designate regulatory monitoring owner
2. **Escalation Procedures**: Define how changes are escalated
3. **Impact Assessment**: Formal process for assessing regulatory impact
4. **Remediation Workflows**: Defined process for addressing gaps
5. **Reporting Cadence**: Regular reporting of regulatory status

### Implementation Steps
1. **Identify Applicable Regulations**: List all regulations that apply
2. **Set Up Monitoring**: Enable alerts for relevant changes
3. **Create Assessment Process**: Develop impact assessment procedure
4. **Build Compliance Calendar**: Consolidate all compliance deadlines
5. **Establish Workflows**: Document remediation workflows
6. **Assign Ownership**: Clarify departmental responsibilities
7. **Report Regularly**: Generate monthly/quarterly compliance reports

### Key Success Factors
- Executive sponsorship
- Clear ownership and accountability
- Regular communication
- Timely impact assessment
- Efficient remediation processes
- Metrics and reporting
- Continuous process improvement
