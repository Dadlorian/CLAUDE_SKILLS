# Third-Party Risk Assessment Reference

## Vendor Risk Management and Due Diligence Automation

### Overview

Third-party risk assessment is the process of evaluating and monitoring risks posed by vendors, suppliers, service providers, and business partners. Organizations rely heavily on third parties, creating exposure to cybersecurity, compliance, operational, financial, and reputational risks. Automated third-party risk management (TPRM) platforms help organizations assess, monitor, and mitigate these risks efficiently.

### Third-Party Risk Categories

**Cybersecurity and Information Security**
- Data breaches and cyber attacks
- Inadequate security controls
- Access to sensitive data and systems
- Cloud security and infrastructure
- Incident response capabilities

**Regulatory and Compliance**
- Non-compliance with regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, PCI DSS)
- Industry-specific requirements
- Data privacy violations
- Sanctions and anti-corruption compliance
- License and certification requirements

**Operational Risk**
- Service disruptions and outages
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Dependency and concentration risk
- Quality and performance issues
- Geographic and geopolitical risks

**Financial Risk**
- Vendor financial stability and viability
- Pricing and contract terms
- Hidden costs and fees
- Currency and economic risks
- Insurance coverage

**Reputational Risk**
- Vendor misconduct or ethical violations
- Negative publicity and adverse media
- Association with controversial entities
- Environmental, social, governance (ESG) issues
- Labor practices and human rights

**Strategic Risk**
- Vendor lock-in and dependencies
- Intellectual property concerns
- Competitive risks
- Innovation and technology obsolescence
- Mergers, acquisitions, and ownership changes

### Third-Party Risk Management Lifecycle

#### 1. Vendor Identification and Inventory

**Vendor Discovery:**
- Identify all third-party relationships
- Procurement system integration
- Accounts payable analysis
- Contract repository
- Shadow IT discovery

**Vendor Classification:**
- Categorize by type (supplier, service provider, consultant, etc.)
- Classify by criticality (critical, high, medium, low)
- Segment by risk exposure
- Tag by data access and processing

**Vendor Inventory:**
- Centralized vendor repository
- Vendor profile information
- Relationship metadata
- Contract and spend data
- Points of contact

#### 2. Initial Due Diligence and Onboarding

**Due Diligence Assessment:**
- **Tiered Assessment:** Risk-based questionnaires (basic, standard, comprehensive)
- **Security Questionnaire:** IT security and data protection practices
- **Privacy Assessment:** Data privacy and GDPR/CCPA compliance
- **Financial Review:** Financial statements, credit reports, Dun & Bradstreet
- **Legal Review:** Contract terms, liability, indemnification
- **Compliance Screening:** Sanctions, PEP, adverse media, regulatory actions
- **Background Checks:** Ownership, management, corporate history
- **Site Visits:** On-site assessments for critical vendors
- **References:** Customer and partner references

**Document Collection:**
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, etc.
- **Insurance:** Certificates of insurance (cyber, liability, etc.)
- **Policies:** Security, privacy, incident response, business continuity
- **Contracts:** Master service agreements, data processing agreements, SLAs
- **Compliance Evidence:** Audit reports, penetration tests, vulnerability scans

**Risk Scoring:**
- Automated risk calculation based on questionnaire responses
- Weighted scoring by risk category
- Inherent risk (vendor characteristics)
- Residual risk (after controls and mitigations)
- Risk rating (low, medium, high, critical)

**Approval and Onboarding:**
- Risk-based approval workflows
- Escalation for high-risk vendors
- Contract negotiation and execution
- Vendor onboarding tasks
- Add to approved vendor list

#### 3. Continuous Monitoring

**Automated Monitoring:**
- **Cybersecurity Ratings:** SecurityScorecard, BitSight, RiskRecon, UpGuard - external security posture scoring
- **Financial Monitoring:** Credit ratings, financial health indicators, bankruptcy filings
- **Adverse Media Screening:** Negative news, regulatory actions, lawsuits, data breaches
- **Sanctions Screening:** Ongoing sanctions and watchlist checks
- **Dark Web Monitoring:** Leaked credentials, compromised data
- **Certificate Expiration:** SSL, certifications, insurance
- **Website Changes:** Privacy policy, terms of service updates
- **Security Incidents:** Vendor-reported or publicly disclosed incidents

**Periodic Reassessment:**
- Risk-based reassessment frequency (quarterly, annually, biennial)
- Abbreviated or full reassessment
- Updated questionnaires and documentation
- Re-scoring and risk rating updates

**Performance Monitoring:**
- SLA compliance and performance metrics
- Service quality and availability
- Issue and incident tracking
- Customer satisfaction surveys

**Fourth-Party Management:**
- Identify vendor's subcontractors (fourth parties)
- Assess fourth-party risks
- Flow-down contract requirements
- Monitor fourth-party compliance

#### 4. Risk Mitigation and Remediation

**Identifying Gaps:**
- Control deficiencies identified in assessments
- High-risk findings
- Non-compliance with standards
- Missing certifications or policies

**Remediation Plans:**
- Document required corrective actions
- Assign to vendor with deadlines
- Track remediation progress
- Vendor communication and collaboration
- Validate remediation completion

**Risk Treatment Options:**
- **Accept:** Accept residual risk with justification
- **Mitigate:** Implement controls or require vendor remediation
- **Transfer:** Insurance, contractual protections, indemnification
- **Avoid:** Terminate relationship or don't engage vendor

**Compensating Controls:**
- Additional monitoring for high-risk vendors
- Enhanced contractual protections
- Escrow arrangements (source code, data)
- Right-to-audit clauses
- Insurance requirements

#### 5. Vendor Offboarding

**Offboarding Process:**
- Contract termination notification
- Data return or destruction
- Access revocation
- Equipment and asset return
- Final invoicing and payment
- Relationship closure documentation

**Data Management:**
- Ensure data returned in agreed format
- Verify data deletion from vendor systems
- Obtain certificates of destruction
- Remove vendor access to systems

**Post-Termination Obligations:**
- Ongoing confidentiality and non-disclosure
- Survival clauses (indemnification, warranties)
- Transition assistance
- Dispute resolution

### Assessment Frameworks and Standards

**Standardized Questionnaires:**
- **SIG (Standardized Information Gathering) - SHARED Assessments:** 18-section standardized security questionnaire
- **CAIQ (Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire) - CSA:** Cloud security assessment
- **VSA (Vendor Security Alliance) Questionnaire:** Vendor security assessment
- **Custom Questionnaires:** Tailored to organization's risk and requirements

**Security Frameworks:**
- **ISO 27001/27002:** Information security management
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework:** Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
- **NIST 800-53:** Security and privacy controls
- **CIS Controls:** Center for Internet Security controls
- **SOC 2:** Service organization controls (trust services criteria)
- **PCI DSS:** Payment card industry data security

**Privacy Frameworks:**
- **GDPR:** EU data protection regulation
- **ISO 27701:** Privacy information management
- **CCPA/CPRA:** California privacy laws
- **HIPAA:** Health information privacy and security

**Industry Standards:**
- **FFIEC:** Financial institution IT examination
- **FINRA:** Securities industry requirements
- **HITRUST:** Healthcare information security framework

### Best Practices

**1. Inventory All Third Parties**
- Comprehensive vendor discovery
- Ongoing inventory maintenance
- Classification and tiering

**2. Risk-Based Approach**
- Assess based on risk (data access, criticality, inherent risk)
- Tiered due diligence (not one-size-fits-all)
- More scrutiny for critical/high-risk vendors

**3. Standardize Assessments**
- Use industry-standard questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ)
- Leverage shared assessments
- Reduce vendor assessment fatigue

**4. Continuous Monitoring**
- Don't rely on point-in-time assessments
- Automated ongoing monitoring
- Real-time risk visibility

**5. Collaboration**
- Engage procurement, legal, IT, security, compliance
- Vendor collaboration and communication
- Shared responsibility

**6. Lifecycle Management**
- Manage vendors from onboarding to offboarding
- Periodic reassessments
- Contract renewal reviews

**7. Fourth-Party Visibility**
- Understand vendor dependencies
- Flow-down requirements to subcontractors
- Monitor fourth parties

**8. Measurement and Reporting**
- Track KPIs and metrics
- Report to senior management and board
- Continuous improvement
