# Compliance Frameworks Reference

## Overview
Comprehensive guide to major regulatory compliance frameworks and their automation requirements.

## GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

### Key Requirements
- **Scope**: Applies to any organization processing data of EU residents
- **Legal Basis**: One of 6 bases required for data processing
- **Individual Rights**: Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection
- **Accountability**: Organizations must demonstrate compliance

### Automation Opportunities
- **Data Mapping**: Automated discovery and classification of personal data
- **Consent Management**: Digital consent capture and preference management
- **DPIA Workflows**: Privacy impact assessment automation
- **DSAR Management**: Data subject access request fulfillment
- **Breach Notification**: Automated breach detection and notification (72-hour requirement)
- **Retention Management**: Automated data deletion based on policies
- **Third-Party Monitoring**: Vendor GDPR compliance tracking
- **Audit Trails**: Continuous logging of data access and processing

### Key Dates
- **January 31, 2024**: EDPB Guidelines on Online Targeting issued
- **Ongoing**: Regular EDPB decisions and guidance updates

### Implementation Metrics
- DSAR response time (target: <30 days)
- Data inventory completeness (target: >95%)
- Consent capture rate (target: >90%)
- Third-party assessments completed (target: 100%)

## CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

### Key Requirements (CCPA/CPRA)
- **Disclosure**: Clear privacy notice at point of collection
- **Opt-Out Rights**: "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" links
- **Data Access**: Consumer ability to request personal data
- **Deletion Rights**: Consumer request for data deletion
- **Non-Discrimination**: Cannot penalize exercising rights
- **CPRA Expansion**: Sensitive personal information protections, opt-in consent

### Automation Opportunities
- **Privacy Policy Management**: Dynamic policy generation from requirement modules
- **Consumer Request Portal**: Self-service access, deletion, and opt-out functionality
- **Automated Opt-Out Tracking**: Digital preference management
- **Sale of Data Tracking**: Categorized data sharing documentation
- **Verification Workflows**: Consumer identity verification automation
- **Audit Logging**: Complete tracking of consumer interactions
- **Vendor Management**: Third-party compliance verification
- **Risk Assessment**: CPRA readiness and impact assessment

### Key Milestones
- **January 1, 2024**: CPRA enforcement for all categories
- **January 1, 2025**: CCCPA compliance period enforcement deadline
- **January 1, 2026**: Right to correct and limit use effective date

### Implementation Metrics
- Consumer request fulfillment time (target: <45 days)
- Privacy policy update frequency (target: quarterly)
- Vendor assessment completion (target: 100%)
- Opt-out processing time (target: <30 days)

## SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act) Section 404

### Key Requirements
- **Section 404(a)**: Management assessment of internal control effectiveness
- **Section 404(b)**: Auditor attestation of controls (Large Accelerated Filers)
- **Control Design**: Effective controls over financial reporting
- **Testing Cycles**: Regular control testing throughout the year
- **Documentation**: Complete control documentation and evidence

### Automation Opportunities
- **Control Design**: Automated control matrix and documentation
- **Evidence Management**: Centralized repository for control test evidence
- **Testing Automation**: Automated control test execution and results collection
- **Workflow Management**: Testing schedules and assignment automation
- **Key Control Identification**: Risk-based control prioritization
- **Metrics & Monitoring**: Real-time control effectiveness dashboards
- **Audit Trail**: Complete documentation and change tracking
- **Remediation Tracking**: Issue and exception management

### Key Considerations
- **Material Weaknesses**: Controls over revenue, expenditure, inventory, asset safeguarding
- **Significant Deficiencies**: Important but not material control gaps
- **PCAOB Oversight**: Auditor standard AS 1305 compliance

### Implementation Metrics
- Control testing completion rate (target: >90% on schedule)
- Control effectiveness rating (target: >95% effective)
- Evidence collection rate (target: 100%)
- Management assessment accuracy (target: 100%)

## HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)

### Key Requirements
- **Privacy Rule**: Protected Health Information (PHI) handling standards
- **Security Rule**: Technical, administrative, and physical safeguards
- **Breach Notification Rule**: Notification within 60 days of discovery
- **Minimum Necessary**: Only use/disclose minimum necessary PHI
- **Authorization**: Written authorization for non-treatment uses
- **Business Associate Agreements**: Required for third parties

### Automation Opportunities
- **Access Controls**: Automated role-based access management for PHI
- **Audit Controls**: Automated tracking of PHI access and modifications
- **De-identification**: Automated HIPAA-compliant data de-identification
- **Breach Detection**: Automated monitoring for unauthorized PHI access
- **BAA Management**: Automated business associate agreement workflows
- **Consent Management**: Electronic authorization and consent tracking
- **Training Tracking**: Mandatory HIPAA training completion monitoring
- **Risk Analysis**: Automated vulnerability scanning and risk assessment

### Key Metrics
- PHI access audit completion (target: 100% coverage)
- Breach detection time (target: <24 hours)
- BAA coverage (target: 100% of third parties)
- Training completion rate (target: 100% annually)

## PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)

### Key Requirements
- **Version 4.0**: Current requirements effective October 2024
- **Scope**: Organizations handling cardholder data
- **Secure Infrastructure**: Network segmentation and security
- **Vulnerability Management**: Regular security testing
- **Access Control**: Strong access controls and authentication
- **Monitoring**: Continuous activity logging and monitoring
- **Policies**: Security policies and procedures

### Automation Opportunities
- **Vulnerability Scanning**: Automated vulnerability assessment and remediation
- **Access Management**: Automated access provisioning and deprovisioning
- **Change Management**: Automated change approval and tracking
- **Penetration Testing**: Automated and continuous penetration testing
- **Audit Trails**: Automated logging and log management
- **Compliance Reporting**: Automated attestation of compliance (AOC)
- **Vendor Assessment**: Third-party PCI compliance verification
- **Training Management**: PCI compliance training automation

### Key Deadlines
- **October 2024**: PCI DSS 4.0 requirements became effective
- **12-Month Transition**: Compliance deadline depends on implementation schedule

### Implementation Metrics
- Vulnerability remediation time (target: <30 days)
- Access recertification completion (target: quarterly, 100%)
- Audit log review (target: daily)
- Compliance assessment completion (target: annually)

## HIPAA-HITECH Breach Notification

### Notification Requirements
- **Discovery**: Identify and document breaches
- **Notification Timeline**: Notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay, no later than 60 days
- **Notification Content**: Description, breach extent, mitigation steps
- **Media Notification**: If breach affects >500 residents, notify media
- **HHS Notification**: Report breaches >500 to Secretary of HHS

### Automation Opportunities
- **Breach Detection**: AI-powered anomaly detection for PHI access
- **Risk Assessment**: Automated breach risk determination
- **Notification Workflow**: Automated notification generation and distribution
- **Audit Trail**: Complete breach documentation and timeline
- **Victim Notification**: Automated notification letter generation
- **Media Tracking**: Automated media monitoring post-breach
- **Remediation Tracking**: Post-breach mitigation tracking

## Financial Industry Regulations

- **Best Execution**: Demonstrate execution quality
- **Conflict of Interest**: Disclosure and management

- **Product Governance**: Product design and control

### Dodd-Frank Act
- **Consumer Protection**: Fair lending and consumer protections
- **Derivatives Regulation**: Swap execution and transparency
- **Volcker Rule**: Proprietary trading restrictions
- **Compensation**: Executive compensation disclosures

### Basel III
- **Capital Requirements**: Minimum capital ratios
- **Liquidity Standards**: Liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), net stable funding ratio (NSFR)
- **Risk Management**: Operational risk capital
- **Systemic Risk**: G-SIB capital buffers

### Automation Opportunities
- **Trade Surveillance**: Automated monitoring for market abuse
- **Best Execution**: Automated execution quality reporting
- **Compliance Reporting**: Regulatory reporting automation (FINRA, SEC)
- **Sanctions Screening**: Real-time OFAC and watchlist screening
- **Audit Trail**: Trade execution and communication audit trails
- **Risk Monitoring**: Real-time limit monitoring and alerts

## Best Practices for Framework Compliance

1. **Assess Applicability**: Determine which frameworks apply to your organization
2. **Map Requirements**: Document how regulations overlap and differ
3. **Integrate Controls**: Design controls that satisfy multiple frameworks
4. **Centralize Evidence**: Single repository for all compliance evidence
5. **Automate Monitoring**: Continuous rather than point-in-time assessment
6. **Regular Updates**: Stay current with regulatory changes
7. **Third-Party Management**: Extend compliance requirements to vendors
8. **Executive Sponsorship**: Board-level engagement and oversight
