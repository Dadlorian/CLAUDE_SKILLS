# compliance.ai Platform Reference

## AI-Powered Regulatory Intelligence and Compliance Automation

### Overview

compliance.ai is an artificial intelligence-powered regulatory intelligence platform that helps financial institutions and regulated entities monitor regulatory changes, assess compliance obligations, and automate compliance workflows. The platform uses natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning to analyze regulations and provide actionable insights.

### Core Platform Capabilities

#### 1. Regulatory Intelligence

**Regulatory Content Database**
- **Comprehensive Coverage:** Federal, state, and international regulations
- **Financial Services Focus:** Banking, securities, insurance, consumer finance
- **Regulatory Sources:**
  - Federal regulations (CFR, Federal Register)
  - State regulations (all 50 states)
  - Regulatory agencies (CFPB, SEC, FINRA, OCC, FDIC, Fed, NCUA, state banking regulators)
  - International regulators (FCA, EBA, BaFin, etc.)
  - Guidance documents, advisory opinions, interpretive letters
- **Historical Archive:** Access to historical versions of regulations
- **Multi-jurisdictional:** Track regulations across multiple jurisdictions

**Regulatory Change Monitoring**
- **Real-Time Alerts:** Immediate notification of regulatory updates
- **Change Detection:** AI-powered identification of regulatory changes
- **Impact Assessment:** Evaluate significance and relevance of changes
- **Change Summary:** Plain-language summaries of complex changes
- **Comparison Tools:** Side-by-side comparison of regulatory versions
- **Effective Date Tracking:** Monitor when changes take effect
- **Proposed Rules Tracking:** Track rulemaking from proposal to final rule

**Regulatory Horizon Scanning**
- **Predictive Analytics:** Identify emerging regulatory trends
- **Proposed Rulemaking:** Monitor agency rulemaking agendas
- **Legislative Tracking:** Congressional bills affecting regulations
- **International Developments:** Global regulatory trends
- **Regulatory Focus Areas:** Identify regulator priorities

#### 2. Compliance Obligation Management

**Obligation Extraction**
- **AI-Powered Extraction:** Automated identification of compliance obligations
- **Natural Language Processing:** Parse complex regulatory language
- **Obligation Library:** Centralized repository of all obligations
- **Categorization:** Classify obligations by type, topic, regulation
- **Tagging and Metadata:** Rich metadata for searchability
- **Citation Linking:** Direct links to source regulations

**Obligation Mapping**
- **Map to Policies:** Link obligations to internal policies and procedures
- **Map to Controls:** Connect obligations to control activities
- **Map to Products:** Associate obligations with products and services
- **Map to Processes:** Link to business processes
- **Gap Analysis:** Identify unmapped obligations
- **Dependency Tracking:** Understand obligation relationships

**Compliance Program Management**
- **Obligation Assignments:** Assign ownership to compliance teams
- **Workflow Automation:** Route obligations for review and action
- **Due Date Tracking:** Monitor compliance deadlines
- **Attestation:** Collect compliance certifications
- **Evidence Collection:** Gather proof of compliance
- **Issue Tracking:** Document and resolve compliance gaps

- **Topic Modeling:** Categorize regulations by topic and theme
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Assess regulatory tone and intent
- **Question Answering:** Natural language queries of regulations

**Machine Learning Models**
- **Relevance Scoring:** Predict relevance of regulations to organization
- **Change Significance:** Assess materiality of regulatory changes
- **Recommendation Engine:** Suggest related regulations and obligations
- **Anomaly Detection:** Identify unusual regulatory patterns

### Industry-Specific Solutions

#### Banking and Consumer Finance
- **Regulations:** TILA, RESPA, ECOA, FCRA, UDAAP, BSA/AML, Reg B, Reg Z, Reg E
- **Agencies:** CFPB, OCC, Fed, FDIC, NCUA, state banking regulators
- **Focus Areas:** Fair lending, consumer protection, BSA/AML, CRA
- **Products:** Mortgages, credit cards, auto loans, deposits

#### Securities and Investment Management
- **Regulations:** Securities Act, Exchange Act, Investment Advisers Act, Investment Company Act
- **Agencies:** SEC, FINRA, CFTC, state securities regulators
- **Focus Areas:** Fiduciary duty, disclosure, suitability, market conduct
- **Products:** Mutual funds, ETFs, alternative investments, advisory services

#### Insurance
- **Regulations:** State insurance codes, NAIC model laws
- **Agencies:** State insurance departments, NAIC
- **Focus Areas:** Solvency, market conduct, consumer protection
- **Products:** Life, health, property/casualty, annuities

#### Mortgage Servicing
- **Regulations:** RESPA, TILA, FDCPA, state foreclosure laws
- **Agencies:** CFPB, state AG offices, HUD
- **Focus Areas:** Servicing standards, loss mitigation, foreclosure, bankruptcy
- **Processes:** Payment processing, escrow, default management
