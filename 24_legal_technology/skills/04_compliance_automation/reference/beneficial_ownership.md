# Beneficial Ownership Reference

## Overview
Comprehensive guide to beneficial ownership identification, verification, and management.

## Beneficial Ownership Definitions

### Beneficial Owner Criteria

**Financial Interest**
- **Ownership Threshold**: Typically ≥25% of entity ownership (varies by regulation)
- **Indirect Ownership**: Includes ownership through intermediaries
- **Voting Rights**: Control through voting rights or agreement
- **Profit Interest**: Right to profits or economic benefit
- **Asset Ownership**: Control of entity assets

**Control Criteria**
- **Management Authority**: Power to direct management and operations
- **Voting Control**: Ability to control voting and decisions
- **Authority to Direct**: Power to direct financial decisions
- **Executive Role**: Executive officers and board members
- **Contractual Rights**: Control through contracts or agreements

**Ultimate Beneficial Owner**
- **Final Authority**: The natural person who ultimately controls entity
- **Control Chain**: Follow ownership chain to natural person
- **Tracing**: Trace through intermediaries to ultimate controller
- **No Further Ownership**: Cannot trace to another entity with higher control

---

### Regulatory Definitions

**FinCEN Definition (US)**
- Beneficial owner: Any individual who, directly or indirectly, owns 25% or more of entity
- Operative: Managing members, general partners, presidents, CEOs, treasurers, and those with significant control

**EU AML Directive**
- Beneficial owner: Natural person(s) with ultimate control
- Ownership threshold: Generally ≥25% (may vary by member state)
- Control: Direct or indirect control through various means

**UK Beneficial Ownership**
- Beneficial owner: Persons with 25% or more ownership (defined in UK legislation)
- Persons with significant control (PSC): UK company requirement
- Persons of significant control: Defined in Companies House regulations

**FATF Recommendations**
- Beneficial owner: Natural person(s) ultimately owning or controlling entity
- Threshold: 25% or higher (or lower if applicable law requires)
- Control: Ownership or control through voting rights or other means

## Beneficial Ownership Identification

### Ownership Structure Analysis

**Direct Ownership**
- **Individual Shareholders**: Direct natural person ownership
- **Percentage**: Record ownership percentage
- **Nature**: Equity stake, voting rights, profit interest
- **Documentation**: Stock certificates, cap table, shareholder registry

**Indirect Ownership**
- **Intermediate Entities**: Ownership through intermediate entities
- **Layers**: Multiple layers of ownership
- **Tracing**: Trace through intermediate entities to ultimate owner
- **Documentation**: Ownership agreements, articles of incorporation, operating agreements

**Complex Structures**
- **Multi-National**: Entities in multiple jurisdictions
- **Pyramid**: Multiple layers of entities
- **Trusts**: Beneficial ownership in trust structures
- **Partnerships**: Partnership interests in other partnerships
- **Joint Ownership**: Multiple beneficial owners at different levels

### Ownership Verification

**Verification Methods**
- **Document Review**: Review organization documents (cap table, articles, etc.)
- **Third-Party Confirmation**: Verify with corporate registry or third parties
- **Business Registration**: Review government business registration
- **Regulatory Filings**: Review SEC, stock exchange, or regulatory filings
- **Public Records**: Review public records for property, judgments, etc.
- **Bank Information**: Bank account ownership information
- **Interview**: Interview company management for verification

**Verification Challenges**
- **Complex Structures**: Difficulty tracing ownership through complex structures
- **Nominee Ownership**: Nominee shareholders obscuring beneficial ownership
- **Trust Structures**: Difficulty identifying beneficial owners in trust arrangements
- **Cross-Border**: Difficulty identifying foreign beneficial owners
- **Passive Entities**: Difficulty with passive investment entities
- **Domestic Law Variations**: Different requirements and definitions across jurisdictions

- **Frequency**: Annual or periodic review depending on risk
- **Update Tracking**: Track changes in beneficial ownership
- **Documentation**: Document all ownership changes

- **Reorganization**: Changes due to corporate reorganization

- **Timeliness**: Notify of changes within specified timeframe
- **Documentation**: Support changes with documentation
- **Verification**: Verify changes are actual and accurate

### Beneficial Owner Risk Assessment

**Risk Factors**
- **PEP Status**: Is beneficial owner a Politically Exposed Person?
- **Family of PEP**: Is beneficial owner related to PEP?
- **High-Risk Jurisdiction**: Is beneficial owner from high-risk jurisdiction?
- **Sanctions Risk**: Is beneficial owner subject to sanctions?
- **Criminal History**: Does beneficial owner have criminal history?
- **Adverse Media**: Is beneficial owner subject to adverse media?
- **Complex Structure**: Is ownership structure unusually complex?
- **Opaque Structure**: Is structure designed to obscure ownership?
- **Nominee Ownership**: Is nominee likely masking true owner?
- **Trust Structure**: Is trust structure used to obscure ownership?

**Risk Rating**
- **High Risk**: Multiple risk factors present
- **Medium Risk**: Some risk factors present
- **Low Risk**: Minimal risk factors

## Beneficial Ownership Attestation

### Beneficial Owner Certification

**Certification Process**
1. **Information Collection**: Collect beneficial ownership information
2. **Verification**: Verify information accuracy
3. **Certification**: Beneficial owner certifies accuracy of information
4. **Signature**: Obtain authorized signature
5. **Documentation**: Maintain signed certification
6. **Periodic Review**: Review and update periodically

**Certification Statement**
- "I certify that I am the beneficial owner of [Entity Name]"
- "I am aware of no other beneficial owners of this entity"
- "I have provided accurate and complete information"
- "I understand the penalties for false statements"
- Signature and date

**Beneficial Owner Declaration**
- Beneficial owner name and personal information
- Nature and extent of beneficial ownership
- Confirmation of no additional beneficial owners
- Dates of beneficial ownership
- Supporting documentation

## Regulatory Requirements

### FinCEN Requirements (US)

**Legal Entity Beneficial Owner Registry**
- **Effective Date**: January 1, 2024
- **Coverage**: Entities created or registered in the US
- **Beneficial Owners**: Identify and provide information on beneficial owners (≥25%)
- **Information**: Full legal name, date of birth, address, ID document number
- **Ongoing Updates**: Update when information changes
- **Penalties**: Civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation

**Exemptions**
- **Listed entities**: Publicly traded companies exempted
- **Bank/Financial**: Banks and certain financial institutions exempted
- **Insurance**: Insurance companies exempted
- **Registered investment**: Registered investment companies exempted

---

### EU Requirements

**4th and 5th AML Directives**
- **Beneficial Owner**: Identify individuals with 25%+ ownership or control
- **Beneficial Owner Registry**: Member states maintain registries
- **Public Access**: Limited or public access depending on country
- **Verification**: Verify beneficial owner information
- **Updates**: Update when beneficial ownership changes
- **Risk Assessment**: Enhanced due diligence for high-risk beneficial owners

---

### UK Requirements

**Companies House PSC Register**
- **Persons of Significant Control**: Register significant controllers
- **Thresholds**: Ownership or control of ≥25%
- **Register**: Maintain PSC register at Companies House
- **Public Access**: Public access to PSC information
- **Updates**: Update within 14 days of changes
- **Penalties**: Penalties for non-compliance
