## Third-Party Risk Management Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Due Diligence

**Initial Assessment**
- **Business Profile**: Understand vendor business and services
- **Risk Profile**: Initial risk assessment
- **Regulatory Status**: Check regulatory standing
- **Sanctions Screening**: Screen against sanctions lists
- **Background Check**: Verify legitimacy and history

**Due Diligence Questionnaire (DDQ)**
- Business information
- Regulatory compliance
- Data security and privacy
- Business continuity
- Subcontractor management
- Insurance coverage
- References and customers

**Document Review**
- Business certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Insurance policies
- Data processing agreements
- Service agreements
- Compliance documentation
- Audit reports

**Vendor Risk Rating**
- Initial risk assessment: Low, Medium, High, Critical
- Risk scoring based on factors
- Approval authority required
- Monitoring frequency
- Escalation triggers

---

### Phase 2: Contracting

**Contract Development**
- **Terms Negotiation**: Standard terms and conditions
- **Compliance Clauses**: Data protection, security, compliance
- **Insurance Requirements**: Minimum insurance coverage
- **Audit Rights**: Right to audit compliance
- **Data Agreement**: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) if applicable
- **Termination Terms**: Exit strategy and transition plan

**Key Contract Elements**
- Scope of services
- Service levels and SLAs
- Data handling requirements
- Security and compliance requirements
- Audit and monitoring rights
- Breach notification requirements
- Indemnification and liability
- Termination and transition
- Insurance requirements
- Subcontractor management

**Approval Process**
- Risk assessment
- Legal review
- Procurement review
- Compliance sign-off
- Management approval
- Execution

---

### Phase 3: Onboarding

**Onboarding Activities**
- **Systems Access**: Set up system access as needed
- **Training**: Provide required training
- **Documentation**: Collect additional documentation
- **Test Access**: Test service delivery
- **Sign-Off**: Confirm service delivery acceptance

**Transition Management**
- **Transition Plan**: Manage transition from prior vendor
- **Data Transfer**: Securely transfer data
- **Testing**: Validate data accuracy
- **Go-Live**: Cutover to new service
- **Support**: Post-go-live support

---

### Phase 4: Ongoing Monitoring

**Monitoring Activities**
- **Performance Monitoring**: Track service quality
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Monitor ongoing compliance
- **Financial Monitoring**: Monitor financial stability
- **Security Monitoring**: Monitor security posture
- **Incident Monitoring**: Track any incidents or issues

**Monitoring Methods**
- **Service Level Monitoring**: Track SLA performance
- **Audit and Assessment**: Regular on-site audits or assessments
- **Documentation Review**: Review updated certifications and assessments
- **Compliance Testing**: Specific compliance testing
- **Meeting and Reporting**: Regular business reviews

**Monitoring Frequency**
- **High-Risk Vendors**: Quarterly or continuous monitoring
- **Medium-Risk Vendors**: Semi-annual monitoring
- **Low-Risk Vendors**: Annual monitoring
- **Triggered Monitoring**: Event-based (regulatory change, incident, etc.)

---

### Phase 5: Remediation & Termination

**Issue Management**
- **Issue Identification**: Identify compliance or performance issues
- **Root Cause Analysis**: Understand underlying cause
- **Remediation Plan**: Develop corrective action plan
- **Monitoring**: Track remediation progress
- **Verification**: Verify issue resolution

**Escalation Process**
- **Level 1**: Vendor contact with issue details
- **Level 2**: Escalate to vendor management
- **Level 3**: Executive escalation if not resolved
- **Level 4**: Legal action or termination consideration

**Termination Process**
- **Termination Notice**: Provide contractual notice period
- **Transition Plan**: Develop transition plan
- **Data Transfer**: Secure return/destruction of data
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Document procedures and knowledge
- **Final Audit**: Conduct final security and compliance audit
- **Sign-Off**: Confirm full transition

## Risk Assessment Framework

### Risk Categories

**Data Protection Risk**
- **Factor 1**: Level of personal data access
- **Factor 2**: Type of data (PII, PHI, payment card)
- **Factor 3**: Data residency (on-shore vs. off-shore)
- **Factor 4**: Data security controls
- **Risk Score**: High if access to sensitive data + weak controls

**Operational Risk**
- **Factor 1**: Service criticality (critical vs. non-critical)
- **Factor 2**: Backup provider availability
- **Factor 3**: Service track record
- **Factor 4**: Business continuity capability
- **Risk Score**: High if critical service + weak BC capability

**Compliance Risk**
- **Factor 1**: Regulatory requirements
- **Factor 2**: Regulatory standing (no enforcement issues)
- **Factor 3**: Audit history
- **Factor 4**: Compliance certifications
- **Risk Score**: High if regulated service + poor compliance record

**Financial Risk**
- **Factor 1**: Financial stability
- **Factor 2**: Market position and concentration
- **Factor 3**: Insurance coverage
- **Factor 4**: Pricing changes
- **Risk Score**: High if financially unstable + concentrated market

**Cybersecurity Risk**
- **Factor 1**: Security certifications
- **Factor 2**: Incident history
- **Factor 3**: Vulnerability management
- **Factor 4**: Incident response capability
- **Risk Score**: High if poor security posture + incident history

## Third-Party Incident Management

### Incident Notification

**Reporting Process**
1. **Notification**: Vendor reports incident or incident discovered
2. **Assessment**: Assess incident severity and impact
3. **Response**: Implement immediate response measures
4. **Investigation**: Investigate incident cause
5. **Remediation**: Develop and implement remediation
6. **Monitoring**: Monitor effectiveness of remediation
7. **Communication**: Communicate status to stakeholders

**Incident Categories**
- **Data Breach**: Unauthorized personal data access
- **Service Outage**: Unavailability of critical service
- **Security Incident**: Malware, unauthorized access, etc.
- **Compliance Issue**: Regulatory violation
- **Financial Issue**: Financial instability indicators

---

## Remediation & Escalation

### Remediation Process

1. **Issue Identification**: Identify compliance or performance issue
2. **Root Cause Analysis**: Understand underlying cause
3. **Remediation Plan**: Develop corrective action plan
4. **Timeline**: Establish remediation timeline
5. **Monitoring**: Track remediation progress
6. **Verification**: Verify issue resolution
7. **Documentation**: Document remediation completion

### Escalation Levels

**Level 1**: Business unit manager or procurement
**Level 2**: Vendor management function
**Level 3**: Compliance or risk management
**Level 4**: Executive management or legal

## Key Performance Indicators

### Third-Party Risk KPIs

**Assessment KPI**
- % of vendors with current risk assessment
- Target: 100% of high-risk, 95% of medium-risk
- Measurement: Quarterly assessment status

**Monitoring KPI**
- % of high-risk vendors monitored
- Target: 100% continuous or frequent monitoring
- Measurement: Monitoring activity tracking

**Incident Response KPI**
- Vendor incident response time
- Target: <24 hours for critical incidents
- Measurement: Incident response timestamp

**Remediation KPI**
- Vendor remediation closure rate
- Target: 100% closure within agreed timeframe
- Measurement: Remediation tracking
