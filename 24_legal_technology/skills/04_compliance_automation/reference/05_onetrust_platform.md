# OneTrust Platform Reference

## OneTrust Privacy and Compliance Automation Platform

### Overview

OneTrust is a leading privacy, security, and third-party risk technology platform that helps organizations comply with GDPR, CCPA, ISO 27001, and other privacy frameworks. It provides integrated solutions for privacy management, risk management, ethics and compliance, and ESG reporting.

### Platform Components

#### 1. Privacy Management (OneTrust Privacy)

**Data Discovery and Classification**
- **Automated Data Discovery:** Scan databases, file systems, cloud storage, SaaS applications
- **Structured and Unstructured Data:** Identify personal data in all formats
- **AI-Powered Classification:** Machine learning for data categorization
- **Personal Data Inventory:** Comprehensive catalog of all personal data
- **Data Subject Identification:** Detect PII/PHI across systems
- **Data Flow Mapping:** Visualize data movement across organization

**Data Mapping and Records of Processing (RoPA)**
- **Asset Inventory:** Applications, databases, third parties, transfers
- **Processing Activities:** Document all data processing activities
- **Article 30 Compliance:** GDPR-compliant records of processing
- **Legal Basis Tracking:** Document legal basis for each processing activity
- **Automated Questionnaires:** Collect information from data owners
- **Data Flow Diagrams:** Visual representation of data flows
- **Multi-language Support:** Global deployments

**Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIA/PIA)**
- **DPIA Questionnaires:** Template-based or custom assessments
- **Risk Scoring:** Automated risk calculation
- **Workflow and Approvals:** Route for review and sign-off
- **DPO Consultation:** Integrated DPO review process
- **Remediation Tracking:** Manage identified risks
- **Integration with Data Map:** Auto-populate from data inventory
- **Regulatory Templates:** Pre-built templates for GDPR, CCPA, etc.

**Data Subject Rights (DSR/DSAR) Automation**
- **Self-Service Portal:** Consumer-facing request portal
- **Webform Integration:** Embed request forms on website
- **Automated Workflow:** Route requests to data owners
- **Identity Verification:** Configurable verification methods
- **Multi-System Search:** Automated data discovery across systems
- **Data Package Assembly:** Compile responsive data
- **Deadline Tracking:** Monitor 30/45/90 day response requirements
- **Audit Trail:** Complete request history and documentation
- **Supported Request Types:**
  - Access/Right to Know
  - Deletion/Erasure
  - Rectification/Correction
  - Restriction
  - Portability
  - Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing
  - Objection

**Consent and Preference Management**
- **Consent Capture:** Multi-channel consent collection
- **Preference Centers:** Consumer preference management portal
- **Cookie Consent:** Website cookie banner and management
- **Consent Database:** Centralized consent repository
- **Granular Consents:** Purpose-based consent tracking
- **Withdrawal Mechanisms:** Easy opt-out processes
- **Audit Trail:** Complete consent history
- **Consent Analytics:** Reporting and insights
- **Universal Consent Platform:** Share consent across systems

**Cookie Compliance and Website Scanning**
- **Website Scanner:** Automated cookie and tracker detection
- **Cookie Classification:** Categorize cookies (necessary, analytics, marketing, etc.)
- **Cookie Banner:** Customizable consent banners
- **Geo-Targeting:** Different banners for different regions
- **Consent Mode:** Integration with Google Consent Mode
- **Blocking Technology:** Prevent cookies until consent obtained
- **Mobile App SDKs:** Mobile consent management
- **Compliance Reporting:** Cookie compliance status

**Privacy Notice and Policy Management**
- **Template Library:** Pre-built privacy notice templates
- **Policy Generator:** Automated policy creation
- **Version Control:** Track policy changes over time
- **Multi-Language:** Support for global deployments
- **Distribution Tracking:** Monitor policy acknowledgments
- **Update Notifications:** Alert users to policy changes
- **Comparison Tools:** Compare policy versions

- **Response Collection:** Portal for vendor responses
- **Risk Scoring:** Automated vendor risk ratings

- **Remediation Tracking:** Monitor vendor corrective actions

**Vendor Inventory and Lifecycle Management**
- **Vendor Database:** Centralized vendor repository
- **Contract Management:** Store and track vendor contracts
- **Renewal Reminders:** Alert for contract renewals
- **Vendor Onboarding:** Standardized vendor onboarding workflows

- **Fourth-Party Management:** Subcontractor tracking

**Continuous Vendor Monitoring**
- **Automated Reassessments:** Periodic vendor reviews
- **External Threat Intelligence:** Monitor vendor security incidents
- **News and Event Monitoring:** Track vendor-related news
- **Security Ratings:** Integration with security rating services
- **Performance Metrics:** Track vendor performance
- **Compliance Status:** Monitor vendor certifications

**Vendor Collaboration**
- **Vendor Portal:** Self-service vendor access
- **Document Sharing:** Exchange documents securely
- **Remediation Plans:** Collaborative risk mitigation
- **Communication Tracking:** Audit trail of vendor interactions

- **Risk Scoring:** Automated likelihood and impact calculation
- **Risk Heat Maps:** Visualize risk landscape
- **Control Mapping:** Link controls to risks
- **Treatment Plans:** Document risk mitigation strategies
- **Risk Monitoring:** Track risk trends over time

**Internal Audit Management**
- **Audit Planning:** Create and schedule audits
- **Audit Programs:** Define audit scope and procedures
- **Fieldwork Execution:** Conduct testing and collect evidence
- **Finding Management:** Document and track findings
- **Remediation Plans:** Assign and monitor corrective actions
- **Audit Reporting:** Generate audit reports
- **Follow-Up Audits:** Validate remediation effectiveness

**Issue and Incident Management**
- **Issue Logging:** Capture compliance and risk issues
- **Workflow Routing:** Assign and escalate issues
- **Root Cause Analysis:** Document issue causes
- **Remediation Tracking:** Monitor corrective actions
- **Deadline Management:** Track resolution timelines
- **Incident Response:** Security and privacy incident management
- **Breach Management:** GDPR/CCPA breach workflows

**Regulatory Change Management**
- **Regulatory Library:** Database of regulations
- **Change Monitoring:** Track regulatory updates
- **Impact Assessment:** Evaluate effect on organization
- **Obligation Mapping:** Link regulations to policies and controls
- **Compliance Calendar:** Schedule compliance activities
- **Regulatory Horizon Scanning:** Proactive monitoring of upcoming regulations

**Training and Awareness**
- **Training Assignment:** Assign courses to users
- **Content Library:** Compliance training modules

- **Certifications:** Track employee certifications

**Conflicts of Interest**

- **Approval Workflows:** Route for review

- Map current state processes
- Prioritize implementation phases

#### 2. Data Mapping and Discovery

#### 3. Configuration and Customization

#### 4. User Adoption and Training
