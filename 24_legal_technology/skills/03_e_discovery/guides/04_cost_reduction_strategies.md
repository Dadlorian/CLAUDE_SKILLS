# E-Discovery Cost Reduction Strategies

## Overview

E-discovery costs can quickly escalate to hundreds of thousands or millions of dollars. This guide provides proven strategies to reduce costs while maintaining quality and defensibility.

## Early Stage Cost Reduction

### 1. Information Governance
- Implement records retention policies
- Defensible deletion of outdated data
- **Savings**: 20-40% reduction in data volumes

### 2. Early Case Assessment (ECA)
- Assess data before full collection
- Sample for relevance estimation
- Inform settlement discussions
- **Savings**: Avoid unnecessary full collection

### 3. Precise Custodian Identification
- Target key players only
- Avoid over-inclusive custodian lists
- Add custodians incrementally as needed
- **Savings**: $3,000-$10,000 per avoided custodian

## Data Reduction Strategies

### 4. Aggressive Date Filtering
**Example**: Limit to 2-year window vs. 5-year
**Savings**: 60% data reduction

### 5. File Type Filtering
**Exclude**: System files, executables, media files (unless relevant)
**Savings**: 10-30% reduction

### 6. De-duplication
- Global de-duplication
- Near-duplicate detection  
- **Savings**: 30-50% reduction

### 7. Email Threading
- Review only inclusive emails
- **Savings**: 50-80% email review reduction

### 8. De-NIST (System File Removal)
**Savings**: 10-20% reduction

## Review Cost Reduction

### 9. Technology-Assisted Review (TAR)
**Linear Review**: $750,000 (500K docs @ $1.50/doc)
**TAR Review**: $200,000 (prioritized review)
**Savings**: $550,000 (73%)

### 10. Tiered Review Approach
- Junior reviewers: First pass ($50/hour)
- Mid-level: Second pass ($100/hour)
- Senior: Hot documents only ($250/hour)
**Savings**: 40-60% vs. all-senior review

### 11. Offshore Review
- Philippines, India: $25-40/hour
- Near-shore (Canada, Mexico): $40-60/hour
- US contract attorneys: $60-100/hour
**Savings**: 50-70% labor cost reduction
**Considerations**: Security, time zones, complexity

### 12. Prioritized Review
- Review high-value documents first
- Stop when diminishing returns
**Savings**: 30-50% vs. complete review

### 13. Keyword Search Refinement
**Poor keywords**: Review 200K documents
**Refined keywords**: Review 80K documents  
**Savings**: $180,000 (at $1.50/doc)

**Best Practice**: Test keywords on sample, refine iteratively

### 14. Concept Clustering
- Group similar documents
- Review cluster representatives
- Quick-review cluster members
**Savings**: 20-40% review time

### 15. Early Document Promotion
- Analytics identify hot documents early
- Escalate to case team immediately
- Inform strategy and settlement
**Savings**: Avoid unnecessary full review if early settlement

## Production Cost Reduction

### 16. Native Production
**TIFF Production**: $15,000 (imaging + load files)
**Native Production**: $2,000 (organization + metadata)
**Savings**: $13,000 per production

**Best for**: Databases, spreadsheets, electronic-only matters

### 17. Rolling Productions
- Produce in batches as reviewed
- Avoid rush fees
- Better resource planning
**Savings**: 20-30% vs. single large production

### 18. Simplified Production Specs
**Complex**: Multiple formats, extensive QC
**Simple**: Native with basic metadata
**Savings**: Processing and QC time

## Platform and Vendor Strategies

### 19. Platform Selection
**Enterprise (Relativity Server)**: $50K+ setup + hosting
**Cloud (Everlaw, Logikcull)**: Pay-as-you-go, no setup
**Savings**: $30-50K on smaller matters

### 20. Competitive Bidding
- Request quotes from 3+ vendors
- Negotiate volume discounts
- Multi-matter agreements
**Savings**: 15-30% through negotiation

### 21. In-House vs. Outsourced
**Outsourced Processing**: $50-150/GB
**In-House (amortized)**: $20-40/GB
**Consider**: Volume, frequency, expertise

### 22. Self-Service Platforms
**Traditional**: Vendor-managed, higher cost
**Self-Service (Logikcull)**: DIY, lower cost
**Savings**: 40-60% on simple matters

## Process Efficiency

### 23. Batching and Assignment Optimization
- Right-size batches (500-1,000 docs)
- Load balance across reviewers
- Minimize downtime
**Savings**: 10-20% productivity increase

### 24. Reviewer Training
- Comprehensive training upfront
- Reduces errors and rework
- Improves consistency
**Savings**: 15-25% fewer QC overturns

### 25. Automated QC
- Platform-based QC sampling
- Automated overturn tracking
- Statistical validation
**Savings**: Senior attorney QC time

### 26. Privilege Automation
- TAR for privilege screening
- Email domain filtering
- Automated attorney name detection
**Savings**: 30-50% privilege review time

## Strategic Approaches

### 27. Cooperation with Opposing Counsel
- Agree on custodian limits
- Shared ESI protocol
- Reduced motion practice
**Savings**: Significant legal fees and time

### 28. Phased Discovery
- Phase 1: Key custodians and date range
- Phase 2: Expand only if needed
**Savings**: Avoid unnecessary collection

### 29. Sampling and Extrapolation
- Sample representative data set
- Extrapolate results to full collection
- Court-approved methodology
**Savings**: 80-95% review cost

### 30. Settlement Focus
- Use ECA to inform settlement
- Prioritize review of case-critical documents
- Settle before full review
**Savings**: Entire remaining review cost

## Cost-Benefit Decision Framework

**For Each Decision, Ask**:
1. What is the cost?
2. What is the benefit (risk reduction, evidence value)?
3. What are alternatives?
4. Is it proportional to case value?

**Example**:
- **Decision**: Collect 5 additional custodians
- **Cost**: $50,000
- **Benefit**: Marginal (peripheral players)
- **Alternative**: Targeted keyword searches on existing data ($5,000)
- **Decision**: Use alternative

## Metrics to Track

**Cost Per**:
- Cost per GB processed
- Cost per document reviewed
- Cost per custodian collected
- Cost per production

**Efficiency Metrics**:
- Documents per reviewer per day
- TAR reduction percentage
- De-duplication rate
- Review accuracy (QC overturn rate)

**Benchmark Costs** (Industry Averages):
- Collection: $3,000-10,000/custodian
- Processing: $50-150/GB
- Hosting: $20-40/GB/month
- Review: $0.50-3.00/document (varies widely)
- Production: $0.10-0.50/page

## 30-60-90 Day Cost Optimization Plan

**Days 1-30**: Quick Wins
- Aggressive date filtering
- De-NIST and de-duplication
- Refine custodian list
- Negotiate vendor rates

**Days 31-60**: Strategic Optimization
- Implement TAR if appropriate
- Tier review team structure
- Establish QC automation
- Optimize batching

**Days 61-90**: Long-Term Efficiency
- Information governance policies
- Vendor contract renegotiation
- Process documentation
- Technology upgrades

## Case Studies

**Case Study 1: TAR Implementation**
- Matter: Commercial litigation, 2M documents
- Linear review estimate: $3M
- TAR approach: $750K
- Savings: $2.25M (75%)

**Case Study 2: Custodian Reduction**
- Original request: 25 custodians
- Negotiated: 8 key custodians, phased approach
- Savings: $170K in collection and processing

**Case Study 3: Native Production**
- Matter: Contract dispute, spreadsheet-heavy
- TIFF alternative: $45K
- Native production: $8K
- Savings: $37K

## Conclusion

E-discovery cost reduction requires a multi-faceted approach combining technology, process optimization, strategic decision-making, and cooperation. By implementing these strategies and continuously monitoring costs, organizations can achieve significant savings while maintaining quality and defensibility. The key is early action—costs saved in collection and processing multiply through review and production.
