This comprehensive guide covers regulatory requirements, best practices, compliance frameworks, and administrative procedures.

## PAC Fundamentals

### What is a PAC?

A Political Action Committee (PAC) is an organization that pools contributions from members and supporters to make contributions to political candidates and campaigns. Key characteristics include:

- **Legal Entity**: Registered political committee under campaign finance law
- **Contribution Pool**: Pools contributions from individuals
- **Candidate Support**: Makes contributions to candidates and campaigns
- **Independent Operation**: Operates independently from candidates
- **Compliance**: Subject to strict legal and reporting requirements
- **Transparency**: Required to disclose donors and spending
- **Political Purpose**: Exclusively for political purposes

### Types of PACs

#### Connected PACs (Corporate and Trade Association PACs)
- **Sponsor**: Established by corporation, union, or trade association
- **Contribution Limits**: Can receive unlimited corporate/union contributions
- **Spending**: Limited contributions to candidates
- **Independent Spending**: Can make unlimited independent expenditures
- **Regulation**: Heavily regulated by FEC
- **Governance**: Governed by sponsoring organization

#### Non-Connected PACs (Super PACs)
- **Independence**: Independent of any corporation or candidate
- **Funding**: Funded by individuals and organizations
- **Contribution Limits**: Individuals can contribute unlimited amounts
- **Spending**: Can spend unlimited amounts on independent expenditures
- **Restrictions**: Cannot coordinate with candidates
- **Disclosure**: Must disclose major donors
- **Proliferation**: Significant increase after Citizens United decision

#### Unincorporated Associations (527 Organizations)
- **Tax Status**: Tax-exempt under IRC Section 527
- **Funding**: Can raise unlimited funds
- **Spending**: Can engage in unlimited political spending
- **Coordination**: Can coordinate with candidates
- **Regulation**: Less regulated than PACs
- **Disclosure**: Must disclose donors

#### Leadership PACs
- **Sponsor**: Established by elected officials or candidates
- **Purpose**: Support for other candidates (besides themselves)
- **Funding**: Funded by individuals
- **Spending**: Limited contributions to candidates
- **Governance**: Controlled by sponsoring politician
- **Relationship**: Relationship to sponsoring politician

### PAC Roles and Responsibilities

#### PAC Committee Roles
- **Treasurer**: Responsible for financial management and reporting
- **Compliance Officer**: Responsible for legal compliance
- **Chair/Leadership**: Provides strategic direction
- **Board Members**: Govern PAC activities and decisions
- **Executive Director**: Manages day-to-day operations
- **Staff**: Execute PAC activities and programs

#### Key Responsibilities
- **Compliance**: Ensure legal compliance with all regulations
- **Financial Management**: Manage PAC funds responsibly
- **Contribution Solicitation**: Solicit contributions within legal limits
- **Contribution Distribution**: Distribute contributions to candidates
- **Reporting**: Meet all reporting requirements
- **Records**: Maintain comprehensive records
- **Disclosure**: Disclose all required information
- **Governance**: Govern PAC in accordance with bylaws

## Legal and Regulatory Framework

### Federal Campaign Finance Law

#### Legal Basis
- **Federal Election Campaign Act (FECA)**: Primary federal campaign finance law
- **Bipartisan Campaign Reform Act (BCRA)**: Modified FECA in 2002
- **Citizens United v. FEC**: Removed limits on independent spending
- **FEC Regulations**: Detailed FEC regulations implementing the law
- **FEC Advisory Opinions**: FEC guidance on legal requirements
- **Court Decisions**: Judicial interpretation of laws

#### Key Provisions

**Contribution Limits** (federal):
- **Individual to Candidate**: $3,300 per election (2024)
- **Individual to PAC**: $5,000 per year
- **Individual to Party Committee**: $41,300 per year
- **PAC to Candidate**: $5,000 per election
- **PAC to PAC**: Unlimited for non-connected PACs
- **Aggregate Limit**: Previously applied, removed by court decision
- **Corporate/Union Contributions**: Generally prohibited directly to campaigns
- **Federal Contractor**: Cannot contribute

**Contribution Prohibitions**:
- **Corporations**: Direct contributions prohibited
- **Unions**: Direct contributions prohibited
- **Foreign Nationals**: All contributions prohibited
- **Federal Contractors**: Cannot contribute
- **Cash**: Contributions over $100 must be traceable
- **In-Kind**: In-kind contributions also limited
- **Straw Donors**: Cannot use intermediaries to circumvent limits

**Spending Limits**:
- **Candidate Spending**: Limits apply to candidate spending
- **Coordinated Spending**: Spouses can spend unlimited on candidates
- **Independent Spending**: Unlimited independent expenditures allowed
- **Electioneering Communications**: Subject to specific requirements
- **Internal Communications**: Generally not regulated
- **Issue Advocacy**: Can engage in unlimited issue advocacy
- **Hybrid Activities**: Must be careful with mixed activities

#### Reporting Requirements
- **Regular Reports**: File reports on regular schedule
- **Contribution Reports**: Report all contributions received
- **Spending Reports**: Report all expenditures
- **Loan Reports**: Report all loans
- **Debts and Obligations**: Report all outstanding debts
- **Schedule Attachments**: Detailed schedules for major transactions
- **Timeliness**: File reports on time
- **Accuracy**: Reports must be accurate and complete

## Contribution and Spending Regulations

### Contribution Limits and Regulations

#### Federal Contribution Limits (2024)

**Individuals**:
- **To Candidate**: $3,300 per election
- **To PAC**: $5,000 per calendar year
- **To Party Committee**: $41,300 per calendar year
- **Aggregate Limit**: Previously limited, now unlimited
- **Periodic Updates**: Limits adjusted for inflation

**PACs**:
- **To Candidate**: $5,000 per election
- **To PAC**: Unlimited to non-connected PACs, unlimited to connected PACs
- **To Party**: $15,000 per year

**Prohibited Contributors**:
- **Corporations**: Direct contributions prohibited
- **Unions**: Direct contributions prohibited
- **Foreign Nationals**: All contributions prohibited
- **Federal Contractors**: Cannot contribute
- **Contributions in Name of Another**: Prohibited
- **Conduit Contributions**: Prohibited

#### In-Kind Contributions
- **Definition**: Non-monetary contributions of goods or services
- **Valuation**: Contributed at fair market value
- **Limits**: Subject to same limits as cash contributions
- **Reporting**: Must be reported as contributions
- **Examples**: Advertising, printing, postage, consulting, etc.
- **Prohibited In-Kind**: Cannot accept prohibited in-kind contributions
- **Oversight**: Track in-kind contributions carefully

### Spending and Expenditure Regulations

#### Allowable Expenditures
- **Candidate Support**: Contributions to candidates and campaigns
- **Administrative**: Administrative and operational costs
- **Fundraising**: Fundraising activities and costs
- **Issue Advocacy**: Issue advocacy activities
- **Voter Education**: Voter education activities
- **Communications**: Communications with members
- **Travel**: Travel related to PAC activities
- **Legal and Accounting**: Legal and accounting services

#### Prohibited Expenditures
- **Personal Use**: Cannot be used for personal expenses
- **Non-Political**: Cannot be used for non-political activities
- **Illegal Activities**: Cannot fund illegal activities
- **Coordination Violations**: Cannot make improper coordinated expenditures
- **Straw Contributions**: Cannot use funds for straw contributions
- **Foreign Contributions**: Cannot use foreign contributions
- **In-Violation Uses**: Cannot use funds in violation of regulations

#### Contribution Tracking
- **Identification**: Identify contributor for each contribution
- **Limitation**: Track cumulative contributions against limits
- **Return**: Return contributions that exceed limits
- **Records**: Maintain detailed contribution records
- **Verification**: Verify contributor eligibility
- **Refunds**: Process refunds of prohibited contributions
- **Documentation**: Document all tracking activities

### Independent Expenditures

#### Definition and Rules
- **Independence**: Must be made independently from candidates
- **Non-Coordination**: Cannot coordinate with candidates
- **Unlimited**: Can spend unlimited amounts
- **Disclosure**: Must report independent expenditures
- **Identification**: Must identify PAC in communications
- **Timing**: Covered by FEC reporting requirements
- **Super PAC**: Can be made by super PACs
- **Limitations**: Cannot coordinate with candidates

#### Coordination Rules
- **Prohibited Conduct**: Cannot conduct prohibited coordination
- **Communication**: Limits on sharing strategic information
- **Materials**: Limits on sharing materials with candidates
- **Consultants**: Limits on sharing consultants
- **Timing**: Limitations based on timing of independent spending
- **Information**: Some information deemed permissible
- **Firewall**: Maintain firewall between PAC and candidates
- **Legal Review**: Have counsel review for coordination concerns

#### Documentation and Reporting
- **Invoice Documentation**: Document basis for expenditure value
- **Decision Records**: Maintain decision-making records
- **Authorization**: Maintain authorization records
- **Timing**: Document timing of decisions and spending
- **Contractors**: Document contractor relationships

- **Amendments**: Amend reports if needed

## Compliance and Reporting

### Federal Reporting Requirements

#### Report Types and Schedules

**Quarterly Reports** (Year of Federal Election):
- **Timing**: Within 15 days of end of quarter
- **Quarters**: January 1-March 31, April 1-June 30, July 1-September 30
- **Contents**: Contributions received, expenditures made, cash on hand
- **Election Period**: Additional reports in election years

**Monthly Reports** (Off-Election Years):
- **Timing**: Monthly reports (optional)
- **Contents**: Same as quarterly
- **Benefit**: Easier compliance, more frequent updates

**Pre-Election Reports**:
- **Timing**: 12 days before general election (and primary if applicable)
- **Contents**: Itemized contributions and expenditures since last report
- **Detail**: List contributions and expenditures of $1,000+ individually

**Post-Election Reports**:
- **Timing**: Within 30 days of election
- **Contents**: Final activity related to election
- **Debts**: Report any remaining debts

**Year-End Reports**:
- **Timing**: Within 30 days of year-end
- **Contents**: Final report of year's activity
- **Surplus**: Report disposition of any surplus funds

#### Report Contents

**Itemization Thresholds**:
- **Contributions**: Itemize contributions exceeding $200
- **Expenditures**: Itemize expenditures exceeding $200
- **In-Kind**: Itemize in-kind contributions exceeding $200
- **Unitemized**: Combine smaller amounts into unitemized total
- **Supporting Information**: Provide schedules for itemized items
- **Accuracy**: All itemized information must be accurate

**Required Schedules**:
- **Schedule A**: Itemized contributions
- **Schedule B**: Itemized expenditures
- **Schedule D**: Debts and obligations
- **Schedule E**: Electioneering communications
- **Schedule F**: Contributions to candidates
- **Schedule H**: Salaries and expenses
- **Other Schedules**: Additional schedules as required

#### Filing and Submission
- **Electronic Filing**: File electronically through FEC system
- **Deadlines**: Meet all filing deadlines
- **Format**: Use FEC-approved format
- **Certification**: Certify accuracy of reports
- **Amendment**: File amendments if corrections needed
- **Preservation**: Preserve for five years
- **Public Access**: Reports become public
