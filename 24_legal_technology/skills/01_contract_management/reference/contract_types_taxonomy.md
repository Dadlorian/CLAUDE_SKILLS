# Contract Types Taxonomy

## Overview

A well-structured contract type taxonomy enables organizations to classify contracts consistently, apply appropriate workflows and templates, define metadata standards, manage risk effectively, and support analytics and reporting. This guide defines standard contract types across industries and provides guidance on taxonomy development.

## Universal Contract Types

### Procurement & Vendor Contracts

#### Purchase Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for purchase of goods or services
- **Parties**: Buyer (customer), Seller/Vendor (supplier)
- **Key Terms**: Product/service description, quantity, price, delivery
- **Duration**: Often one-time or annual
- **Typical Value**: Variable
- **Legal Risks**: Warranty, delivery, payment, liability
- **Approval Level**: Based on value threshold
- **Key Stakeholders**: Procurement, finance, operations
- **Standard Clauses**: Payment terms, warranties, delivery, liability limitations
- **Associated Documents**: POs, invoices, delivery receipts

#### Service Agreement / Statement of Work
- **Definition**: Agreement for provision of services
- **Parties**: Service Provider, Client
- **Key Terms**: Scope of work, deliverables, schedule, fees
- **Duration**: Project-based or ongoing
- **Typical Value**: $50K - $5M+
- **Legal Risks**: Scope creep, non-delivery, IP ownership, liability
- **Approval Level**: High (escalates by value)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, procurement, finance, department heads
- **Standard Clauses**: Scope, deliverables, payment, SLAs, change management, IP, confidentiality
- **Associated Documents**: Statements of work, specifications, acceptance criteria

#### Software/SaaS Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for use of software or cloud services
- **Parties**: Vendor (licensor), Customer (licensee)
- **Key Terms**: Service levels, fees, users/usage, support
- **Duration**: Annual, multi-year subscriptions
- **Typical Value**: $10K - $10M+ annually
- **Legal Risks**: Data protection, IP licensing, availability, liability
- **Approval Level**: High (escalates by value)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, IT, procurement, business unit sponsors
- **Standard Clauses**: Service levels, data protection, IP licensing, support, termination, liability
- **Associated Documents**: DPA, technical specs, SOWs for implementation

#### Maintenance & Support Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for ongoing support and maintenance
- **Parties**: Vendor, Customer
- **Key Terms**: Service levels, support hours, response times, fees
- **Duration**: Annual, often multi-year
- **Typical Value**: 15-20% of product/service value
- **Legal Risks**: SLA compliance, support quality, response times
- **Approval Level**: Medium (linked to underlying product)
- **Key Stakeholders**: IT/Operations, procurement
- **Standard Clauses**: Service levels, response/resolution times, support hours, escalation
- **Associated Documents**: Service level definitions, support matrix

### Intellectual Property Contracts

#### Software License Agreement
- **Definition**: Grant of rights to use software
- **Parties**: Licensor, Licensee
- **Key Terms**: License scope (exclusive/non-exclusive), field of use, term, fees
- **Duration**: Perpetual or limited term
- **Typical Value**: Broad range
- **Legal Risks**: Scope disputes, unauthorized use, infringement claims
- **Approval Level**: High (IP implications)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, IT, procurement
- **Standard Clauses**: Grant of license, restrictions, IP ownership, termination, warranties
- **Associated Documents**: License schedule, technical documentation

#### Technology Licensing Agreement
- **Definition**: Grant of technology/patent/trademark rights
- **Parties**: Licensor (IP owner), Licensee (user)
- **Key Terms**: Technology scope, exclusive/non-exclusive, royalties, field of use
- **Duration**: Perpetual or term-limited
- **Typical Value**: Royalty-based or upfront fees
- **Legal Risks**: Infringement, sublicense restrictions, territory disputes
- **Approval Level**: Very High (fundamental IP asset)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, R&D, business development
- **Standard Clauses**: Grant, royalties, sublicense restrictions, field of use, termination
- **Associated Documents**: Technology description, patent schedules

#### Development/Invention Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for development of new intellectual property
- **Parties**: Developer/Contractor, Company
- **Key Terms**: Development scope, ownership of IP created, fees, timeline
- **Duration**: Project-based
- **Typical Value**: Development fees + potential IP value
- **Legal Risks**: IP ownership disputes, work-made-for-hire interpretation
- **Approval Level**: High (ownership implications)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, R&D, contracts
- **Standard Clauses**: IP ownership, ownership of pre-existing IP, confidentiality, invention assignment
- **Associated Documents**: Specifications, statement of work

### Relationship Agreements

#### Master Service Agreement / Master Sales Agreement
- **Definition**: Master framework governing multiple transactions
- **Parties**: Typically vendor and customer
- **Key Terms**: General terms, payment terms, governing law, dispute resolution
- **Duration**: Multi-year
- **Typical Value**: Umbrella for multiple transactions
- **Legal Risks**: Scope and applicability disputes, amendment process
- **Approval Level**: High
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, procurement, finance
- **Standard Clauses**: General terms, payment, liability, confidentiality, termination
- **Associated Documents**: Statements of work, exhibits, specific terms for transactions

#### Non-Disclosure Agreement / Confidentiality Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement to protect confidential information
- **Parties**: Disclosing party, Receiving party
- **Key Terms**: Definition of confidential information, permitted uses, duration
- **Duration**: Typically 2-5 years
- **Typical Value**: Non-monetary (protective)
- **Legal Risks**: Carve-out disputes, permitted use enforcement
- **Approval Level**: Medium
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, procurement
- **Standard Clauses**: Definition of confidential info, permitted uses, return/destruction, duration
- **Associated Documents**: Supporting agreement (purchase agreement, etc.)

#### Partnership / Joint Venture Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement between parties to pursue common business objective
- **Parties**: Partners (typically 2-3 parties)
- **Key Terms**: Governance, profit/loss sharing, capital contributions, exit
- **Duration**: Multi-year, often indefinite
- **Typical Value**: Significant (equity/profit sharing)
- **Legal Risks**: Governance disputes, profit sharing disputes, exit complications
- **Approval Level**: Very High (board-level decisions)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, finance, business development, executive team
- **Standard Clauses**: Governance, capital contribution, profit/loss, management, dispute resolution
- **Associated Documents**: Operating agreement, partnership agreement, cap table

#### Reseller / Distribution Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for resale or distribution of products/services
- **Parties**: Manufacturer/Vendor (supplier), Reseller/Distributor (buyer)
- **Key Terms**: Territory, pricing, minimum volumes, support, duration
- **Duration**: Multi-year
- **Typical Value**: Ongoing revenue
- **Legal Risks**: Territory disputes, minimum volume non-compliance, termination disputes
- **Approval Level**: High
- **Key Stakeholders**: Sales, legal, finance
- **Standard Clauses**: Territory, pricing, minimums, support, marketing, termination
- **Associated Documents**: Price lists, support matrix, marketing guidelines

### Employment & Staffing Contracts

#### Employment Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement between employer and employee
- **Parties**: Employer, Employee
- **Key Terms**: Position, compensation, benefits, duties, at-will status
- **Duration**: Ongoing until termination
- **Typical Value**: Salary + benefits
- **Legal Risks**: Severance obligations, restrictive covenants enforceability
- **Approval Level**: HR approval, legal review for senior management
- **Key Stakeholders**: HR, legal, management
- **Standard Clauses**: Confidentiality, IP assignment, non-compete, at-will employment
- **Associated Documents**: Offer letter, equity agreements, non-compete

#### Contractor / Consultant Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for independent contractor services
- **Parties**: Company, Independent Contractor
- **Key Terms**: Scope of work, deliverables, fees, payment schedule
- **Duration**: Project-based or ongoing
- **Typical Value**: Variable based on scope
- **Legal Risks**: Worker classification, IP ownership, indemnification
- **Approval Level**: Medium (depends on scope and cost)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Legal, procurement, department manager
- **Standard Clauses**: Contractor status, work scope, IP ownership, confidentiality, insurance
- **Associated Documents**: Statement of work, specifications, IP assignment

#### Staffing / Temporary Services Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for temporary employees or staffing services
- **Parties**: Staffing Agency, Client Company
- **Key Terms**: Worker qualifications, rates, duration, worker protections
- **Duration**: Project-based or ongoing
- **Typical Value**: Hourly rates x number of workers
- **Legal Risks**: Worker misclassification, liability, insurance coverage
- **Approval Level**: Medium (operational)
- **Key Stakeholders**: HR, procurement, legal, hiring manager
- **Standard Clauses**: Worker qualifications, rates, worker protections, liability, confidentiality
- **Associated Documents**: Job specifications, rate cards, worker agreements

### Real Estate & Property Contracts

#### Lease Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for use of property in exchange for rent
- **Parties**: Lessor (property owner), Lessee (tenant)
- **Key Terms**: Property description, lease term, rent, maintenance responsibilities
- **Duration**: 1-10+ years
- **Typical Value**: Monthly/annual rent obligations
- **Legal Risks**: Lease enforcement, maintenance disputes, default
- **Approval Level**: High (long-term financial obligation)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Procurement, finance, facilities, legal
- **Standard Clauses**: Rent, term, maintenance, insurance, termination, renewal
- **Associated Documents**: Lease exhibits, property description, insurance requirements

#### Facilities/Office Services Agreement
- **Definition**: Agreement for facility management or office services
- **Parties**: Service Provider, Company
- **Key Terms**: Services, locations, service levels, fees
- **Duration**: Multi-year
- **Typical Value**: Monthly service fees
- **Legal Risks**: Service level compliance, liability for facility damage
- **Approval Level**: Medium (operational)
- **Key Stakeholders**: Facilities, procurement
- **Standard Clauses**: Services, service levels, fees, liability, insurance
- **Associated Documents**: Service specifications, locations, pricing schedule

### Industry-Specific Contract Types

#### Healthcare Industry
- **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**: Required by HIPAA for handling PHI
- **Clinical Trial Agreement**: Agreement with research institutions for clinical trials
- **Provider Network Agreement**: Agreement with healthcare providers
- **Pharmaceutical Supply Agreement**: Agreement for pharmaceutical product supply

#### Technology Industry
- **Hardware Purchase Agreement**: For computing equipment and infrastructure
- **Cloud Services Agreement**: For cloud computing and infrastructure
- **API Agreement**: For use of software APIs
- **Data Processing Agreement**: GDPR compliance for data processing

#### Finance & Insurance Industry
- **Securities Agreement**: For securities transactions
- **Loan Agreement**: For lending arrangements
- **Insurance Agreement**: For insurance coverage
- **Banking Agreement**: For banking services

#### Manufacturing & Industrial
- **Equipment Purchase Agreement**: For manufacturing equipment
- **Raw Materials Supply Agreement**: For material supply
- **Manufacturing Agreement**: For contract manufacturing services
- **Equipment Maintenance Agreement**: For equipment support and maintenance

## Taxonomy Design Framework

### Multi-Dimensional Classification

Organizations often classify contracts across multiple dimensions:

#### Dimension 1: Contract Type
- Service Agreement, Purchase Order, Software License, Employment, etc.

#### Dimension 2: Party Type
- Customer, Vendor, Partner, Employee, Contractor, etc.

#### Dimension 3: Contract Value
- Micro (<$10K), Small ($10K-$100K), Medium ($100K-$1M), Large ($1M-$10M), Enterprise ($10M+)

#### Dimension 4: Risk Level
- Low, Medium, High, Critical

#### Dimension 5: Industry
- Technology, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Finance, etc.

#### Dimension 6: Duration
- One-time, Annual, Multi-year, Perpetual

#### Dimension 7: Business Unit
- Procurement, Finance, HR, Legal, Operations, Sales, etc.

```
├── COMMERCIAL CONTRACTS

│   │   ├── Reseller
│   │   └── Distributor
│   ├── Licensing Agreements
│   │   ├── Software License
│   │   ├── Technology License
│   │   └── IP License
│   └── Partnership Agreements

│   ├── NDAs/Confidentiality

└── EMPLOYMENT CONTRACTS
    ├── Employee Agreements
    ├── Contractor Agreements
    └── Staffing Agreements
```

## Taxonomy Governance

1. **Ownership**: Legal/Contracts function owns taxonomy
2. **Update Process**: Quarterly review, documented change process
3. **Communication**: Changes communicated to all users
4. **Training**: Training on new types when added
5. **Documentation**: Detailed type definitions and examples
6. **Standards**: Consistent naming conventions, hierarchies

## Conclusion

A well-designed contract type taxonomy provides the foundation for effective CLM implementation, enabling consistent classification, appropriate workflow routing, effective risk management, and meaningful analytics and reporting. Taxonomy should be regularly reviewed and refined based on organizational needs.
