# MSA Management Best Practices

## Understanding Master Service Agreements

### Definition
A Master Service Agreement (MSA) is an overarching contract that establishes general terms and conditions governing the relationship between parties, with specific work detailed in Statements of Work (SOWs).

### MSA vs. SOW Structure
```
MSA (Framework Agreement)
├── General terms
├── Payment terms
├── IP rights
├── Liability
└── Dispute resolution

SOW 1 (Project-specific)
├── Scope of work
├── Deliverables
├── Timeline
└── Pricing

SOW 2
└── [Additional work]
```

## MSA Governance Framework

### 1. Template Development
- Establish approved MSA templates
- Define variations by vendor type
- Align with risk tolerance
- Regular legal review and updates
- Document all versions

### 2. Negotiation Management
- Define negotiable vs. non-negotiable terms
- Establish escalation procedures
- Track negotiation history
- Document compromises
- Manage counter-offers

### 3. Approval Process
- Multi-level approvals (Legal, Finance, Business)
- Defined authority levels
- Clear rejection reasons
- Expedited approval for standard terms
- Exception management

## SOW Management Under MSA

### SOW Creation Workflow
1. Define scope and deliverables
2. Establish timeline
3. Determine pricing (fixed vs. T&M)
4. Allocate resources
5. Identify risks
6. Obtain approvals
7. Execute SOW

### SOW Linking
- Associate SOW with parent MSA
- Verify consistency with MSA terms
- Reference MSA for general terms
- Document any deviations
- Track inherited obligations

### Change Management
- Document change requests
- Impact assessment
- Cost implications
- Timeline adjustments
- Amendment process

## Best Practices Summary
1. Start with strong, approved MSA templates
2. Clearly separate MSA and SOW responsibilities
3. Implement robust approval workflows
4. Track all amendments and changes
5. Monitor performance against commitments
6. Maintain regular business reviews
7. Plan renewals 90+ days in advance
8. Document all decisions and rationales
