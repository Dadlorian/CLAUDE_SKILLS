# NDA Automation Guide

## Introduction to NDAs
Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are critical legal instruments protecting confidential information during business discussions, partnerships, and transactions. Automating NDA workflows accelerates execution and ensures compliance.

## NDA Types

### Unilateral NDA
- One party discloses; one receives
- Simpler, faster execution
- Common in early-stage discussions
- Template-friendly

### Mutual NDA
- Both parties disclose and receive
- Balanced obligations
- Common in partnerships
- May require negotiation

### Multi-Party NDA
- Three or more parties
- Complex confidentiality relationships
- Used in consortium arrangements
- Requires careful drafting

## Automation Workflow

### Step 1: Request Initiation
- Requester selects NDA type
- Identifies counterparty
- Defines scope
- Sets timeline

### Step 2: Template Selection
- Recommend standard template
- Highlight key terms
- Allow customization
- Legal review if non-standard

### Step 3: Document Generation
- Auto-populate party information
- Insert standard terms
- Generate execution package
- Create tracking record

### Step 4: Routing and Signing
- Send to counterparty
- Set sign deadline
- Enable e-signature
- Track signature status

### Step 5: Execution and Storage
- Verify execution
- Convert to final format
- Store in secure repository
- Create compliance record

### Step 6: Lifecycle Management
- Track expiration dates
- Monitor compliance
- Generate renewal notices
- Archive expired NDAs

## Key NDA Clauses to Automate

### Standard Confidentiality Clauses
- Definition of confidential information
- Permitted use
- Obligation to protect
- Return/destruction of information

### Exclusions and Exceptions
- Public domain information
- Independently developed
- Rightfully received from third party
- Legally compelled disclosure

### Term and Termination
- Agreement duration
- Survival periods
- Termination provisions
- Return of materials

### Enforcement Mechanisms
- Remedies (injunctive relief)
- Governing law
- Dispute resolution
- Attorney's fees
