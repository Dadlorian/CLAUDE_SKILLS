# AI for Due Diligence

## Overview
AI-powered due diligence accelerates M&A contract review by automating initial document triage and risk identification.

## Key Capabilities
- **Document Classification**: Automatically categorize documents by type
- **Clause Extraction**: Pull key provisions from thousands of contracts
- **Risk Flagging**: Identify change of control, assignment restrictions, etc.
- **Obligation Tracking**: Extract deadlines, renewal dates, financial commitments
- **Comparative Analysis**: Compare terms across contract portfolios

## Platforms
- **Kira Systems**: Industry leader for due diligence
- **Luminance**: Unsupervised learning approach
- **eBrevia**: Focus on data extraction
- **ThoughtRiver**: Pre-signature and post-signature review

## Typical Workflow
1. Upload documents to data room (10,000+ contracts)
2. AI categorizes and prioritizes review
3. Extract 100+ data points per contract
4. Flag high-risk provisions
5. Generate diligence report
6. Attorneys review flagged items
7. Result: 60-70% time savings vs. manual review
