# RAG Systems for Legal AI

## Overview
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the most effective architecture for legal AI, combining precise document retrieval with LLM generation capabilities.

## Key Components
1. **Vector Database**: Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, ChromaDB
2. **Embeddings**: OpenAI, Cohere, or legal-specific embeddings
3. **LLM**: GPT-4, Claude, or fine-tuned legal models
4. **Retrieval Strategy**: Hybrid search, re-ranking, contextual compression

## Benefits for Legal
- Grounded in actual legal sources
- Reduceshalluciations
- Provides citations
- Updates easily with new case law
- Maintains attorney-client privilege (on-premise options)

## Implementation Patterns
- Conversational legal research
- Contract Q&A
- Due diligence document review
- Legal memo generation
- Compliance checking
