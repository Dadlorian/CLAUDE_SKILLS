# AI Ethics Frameworks for Legal

## ABA Model Rules
- **Rule 1.1**: Competence includes understanding AI tools
- **Rule 1.3**: Diligence requires verifying AI outputs
- **Rule 1.6**: Confidentiality must be maintained with AI vendors
- **Rule 5.3**: Supervise non-lawyer AI assistants
- **Rule 5.5**: Prevent unauthorized practice of law by AI

## EU AI Act
- **High-Risk Classification**: Legal AI affecting justice
- **Transparency Requirements**: Disclose AI use
- **Human Oversight**: Mandatory for high-risk systems
- **Data Quality**: Training data must be representative
- **Conformity Assessment**: Third-party validation required

## NIST AI RMF (Risk Management Framework)
1. **Govern**: Establish AI governance structure
2. **Map**: Understand AI context and impacts
3. **Measure**: Assess AI risks quantitatively
4. **Manage**: Implement risk controls

## Responsible AI Principles
- **Fairness**: No discriminatory outcomes
- **Transparency**: Explainable decisions
- **Accountability**: Clear human responsibility
- **Privacy**: Protect confidential information
- **Safety**: Robust against misuse
- **Reliability**: Consistent, accurate performance
