# AI for Legal Research

## Overview
AI transforms legal research by providing natural language search, case summarization, and citation analysis.

## Key Technologies
- **RAG Systems**: Combine search with generation
- **Semantic Search**: Understand meaning, not just keywords
- **Case Law Analysis**: Automated reading of judicial opinions
- **Citator AI**: Track case treatment and precedential value
- **Legal Summarization**: Automatic case briefs

## Leading Tools
- **ROSS Intelligence**: Natural language legal research (defunct)
- **Casetext CARA**: AI research assistant
- **vLex Vincent**: AI-powered legal research
- **Fastcase**: Machine learning search
- **Westlaw Precision**: AI-enhanced search
- **Lexis+ AI**: Generative AI for legal research

## Use Cases
- Find relevant case law from natural language query
- Summarize lengthy opinions
- Identify binding vs. persuasive authority
- Track subsequent treatment of cases
- Generate research memos

## Limitations
- May miss edge cases
- Citation accuracy requires verification
- Limited to training data cutoff
- Cannot replace attorney judgment
