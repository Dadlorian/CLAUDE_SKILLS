# Ethical AI Deployment in Legal Practice

## Core Principles

### 1. Maintain Attorney Oversight
- Never deploy fully autonomous legal AI
- All outputs require attorney review
- Human final decision-maker

### 2. Transparency
- Disclose AI use to clients
- Explain how AI assists work
- Provide opt-out option

### 3. Fairness and Bias
- Regular bias audits
- Test across demographics
- Mitigate disparate impact

### 4. Confidentiality
- Client data isolation
- No cross-client training
- Secure deployment

### 5. Competence
- Train attorneys on AI capabilities and limitations
- Understand what AI can and cannot do
- Recognize when to verify outputs

## Deployment Checklist

### Pre-Deployment
☐ Accuracy validation (>90%)
☐ Bias audit (passes 80% rule)
☐ Security review
☐ Attorney training completed
☐ Client disclosure drafted
☐ Error handling protocols

### Deployment
☐ Pilot with small team
☐ Monitor closely
☐ Collect feedback
☐ Measure ROI

### Post-Deployment
☐ Monthly accuracy checks
☐ Quarterly bias audits
☐ User satisfaction surveys
☐ Ongoing training updates

## Risk Mitigation
- Implement human review workflows
- Version control and rollback capability
- Incident response plan
- Professional liability insurance
- Clear documentation of AI involvement
