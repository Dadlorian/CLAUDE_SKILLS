**Core Modules**:
- **AQX**: Core IP management system

  - Trademark portfolio management
  - IP operations workflow automation
  - Global docketing with 200+ jurisdictions
  - Matter management and collaboration

- **PATTSY WAVE**: Innovation and invention management
  - Invention disclosure capture
  - Novelty assessment workflow
  - IP committee review process
  - Innovation metrics and analytics

- **IP Analytics**: Business intelligence for IP portfolios
  - Portfolio health dashboards
  - Cost analysis and forecasting
  - Competitive intelligence
  - Technology landscape analysis

- **Anaqua Insights**: Advanced analytics and reporting
  - Custom report builder
  - Executive dashboards
  - Predictive analytics
  - Portfolio optimization recommendations

**Key Features**:
- Multi-language support (20+ languages)
- Configurable workflow engine
- Integration with major patent offices (USPTO, EPO, WIPO, JPO, CNIPA)
- Outside counsel collaboration portal
- Mobile apps (iOS/Android)
- Document management with versioning
- Email integration and parsing
- Annuity management integration

**Technical Architecture**:
- Cloud-based (AWS) or on-premises deployment
- REST API for integrations
- OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Audit trail and compliance reporting
- ISO 27001 certified

**Typical Users**: Fortune 500 companies, large law firms, universities
**Pricing Model**: Per-user subscription, typically $5,000-$15,000/user/year

## Selection Criteria

### For Large Enterprises
**Recommended**: Anaqua, CPA Global IP Manager
- Need comprehensive features and scalability
- Global operations with multiple jurisdictions
- Complex organizational structures
- Integration with existing enterprise systems
- Dedicated implementation and support teams

### For Law Firms
**Recommended**: CPA Global Foundation IP, AppColl, Inprotech
- Strong docketing and deadline accuracy
- Client matter management
- Billing integration
- Outside counsel collaboration
- High-volume prosecution workflow

### For Innovation-Focused Organizations
**Recommended**: PatSnap, Anaqua with PATTSY WAVE
- R&D and innovation teams
- Technology scouting and competitive intelligence
- Patent landscape analysis
- Invention disclosure management
- Technology transfer activities

### For Small Firms/Solo Practitioners
**Recommended**: Alt Legal, AppColl
- Affordable pricing
- Quick implementation
- Easy to use
- Good USPTO integration
- Minimal IT requirements

### For Patent Analytics/Research
**Recommended**: PatSnap, Clarivate Derwent Innovation
- Advanced search capabilities
- Large patent databases
- Analytics and visualization
- Prior art research
- Competitive intelligence

- User training programs
- Workflow redesign
- Standard operating procedure updates
- Power user identification
- Phased rollout vs. big bang

- System administration
- User access management
- Custom report development
- Integration maintenance
- Version upgrades and testing
