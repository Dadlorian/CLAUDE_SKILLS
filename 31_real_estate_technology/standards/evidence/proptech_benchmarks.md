## Property Management System Performance

**Source:** NMHC Operations Survey 2024, N=350 property management companies

## Smart Building ROI Studies

### HVAC Optimization ROI

**Study:** Johnson Controls "Smart Building ROI Report 2024"
**Sample Size:** 500 commercial buildings, 50M sq ft

**Investment:**
- Average: $2.50/sq ft for BAS upgrade
- Range: $1.80 - $4.20/sq ft

**Energy Savings:**
- **Median:** 23% reduction in HVAC energy consumption
- **Top Quartile:** 32% reduction
- **Bottom Quartile:** 15% reduction

**Payback Period:**
- **Median:** 3.2 years
- **Best Case:** 1.8 years (buildings with very old systems)
- **Worst Case:** 5.5 years (new buildings with recent upgrades)

**Annual Savings per Sq Ft:**
- **Office Buildings:** $0.45 - $0.85/sq ft
- **Multifamily:** $0.30 - $0.60/sq ft
- **Retail:** $0.50 - $0.95/sq ft

**Case Study Example:** 20-story office building, Chicago
- Building: 500,000 sq ft, built 1995
- Investment: $1.2M ($2.40/sq ft)
- Annual Energy Savings: $385,000 (28% reduction)
- Payback: 3.1 years
- 10-year NPV: $2.1M (12% IRR)

### Occupancy Sensing and Space Optimization

**Study:** CBRE "Future of Work and Smart Buildings" 2024
**Sample Size:** 120 office buildings

**Technology:** Occupancy sensors (PIR, CO2, desk sensors, WiFi analytics)

**Findings:**
- **Actual Occupancy:** 42% average desk utilization (pre-pandemic baseline: 65%)
- **Space Optimization:** Companies reduced office footprint by 25% on average
- **Cost Savings:** $15 - $25/sq ft annually through space reduction
- **HVAC Savings:** Additional 12% energy savings from occupancy-based control

**Tenant Satisfaction:**
- 78% of tenants rated smart building features as "important" or "very important"
- 65% willing to pay premium rent (avg. 3-5%) for smart building features
- Mobile access control rated highest satisfaction (4.2/5.0)

## Energy Savings Case Studies

### Case Study 1: LED Lighting Retrofit

**Property:** Multifamily, 300 units, Atlanta
**Source:** NMHC Energy Efficiency Study 2024

**Investment:**
- LED bulbs (common areas + in-unit): $45,000
- Smart lighting controls: $35,000
- **Total:** $80,000

**Results:**
- Lighting energy reduction: 68%
- Annual savings: $42,000
- Payback: 1.9 years
- Maintenance savings: $8,000/year (longer bulb life)

**Additional Benefits:**
- Tenant satisfaction increase: +12% (better lighting quality)
- Reduced maintenance calls: -35%

### Case Study 2: Submetering Implementation

**Property:** 500-unit multifamily portfolio
**Source:** JLL Sustainability Report 2024

**Investment:**
- Water submeters: $150/unit = $75,000
- Electricity submeters: $250/unit = $125,000
- **Total:** $200,000

**Results:**
- Water consumption reduction: 22% (residents more conscious)
- Electricity reduction: 18%
- Annual savings: $156,000
- Payback: 1.3 years

**Behavioral Changes:**
- Residents receiving monthly consumption reports reduced usage 15-25%
- Real-time mobile app feedback drove additional 8% savings

### Case Study 3: Building Envelope Improvements

**Property:** Office building, 200,000 sq ft, Seattle
**Source:** BOMA Energy Performance Study 2024

**Investment:**
- Window film application: $180,000
- Air sealing: $45,000
- **Total:** $225,000

**Results:**
- Heating energy reduction: 18%
- Cooling energy reduction: 35%
- Annual savings: $78,000
- Payback: 2.9 years

## Occupancy Rate Improvements

### PropTech Impact on Leasing Velocity

**Study:** Zillow Research "Technology and Rental Velocity" 2024
**Sample Size:** 50,000 rental units

**Technology Adoption vs. Days on Market:**

| Technology Stack | Avg. Days on Market | Improvement vs. Baseline |
|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| **Baseline** (manual process, email) | 38 days | - |
| **Basic** (listing syndication) | 29 days | -24% |
| **Intermediate** (+ virtual tours, self-scheduling) | 21 days | -45% |
| **Advanced** (+ AI chatbot, self-guided tours, instant application) | 14 days | -63% |

**Revenue Impact:**
- Each day vacant costs $100-150 per unit (depending on rent level)
- 24-day reduction = $2,400 - $3,600 per turn
- For 100-unit building with 33% annual turnover: $79,200 - $118,800 annual gain

### Virtual Tour Effectiveness

**Study:** Apartments.com "Virtual Tour Impact Study" 2024

**Findings:**
- Properties with 3D virtual tours: **45% more inquiries**
- Video tours: **32% more inquiries**
- Professional photos only: Baseline

**Conversion Rates:**
- Virtual tour viewers → Application: 18.5%
- No virtual tour: 12.2%
- **Improvement: +52% conversion rate**

**Cost-Benefit:**
- 3D virtual tour cost: $300-500 per unit
- Increased conversion value: $1,200+ per unit (fewer vacancies)
- **ROI: 240-400%**

| **Financial Reporting** | High | 70% | Automated reconciliation |

### Predictive Maintenance ROI

**Study:** Honeywell "Predictive Maintenance in Buildings" 2024

**Traditional Maintenance:**
- Unplanned downtime: 30-40% of total downtime
- HVAC emergency repairs: $3,500 average cost
- Equipment lifespan: 80% of rated life

**Predictive Maintenance (IoT sensors + AI):**
- Unplanned downtime: 10-15% of total downtime (70% reduction)
- Emergency repairs: $800 average cost (planned prevention)
- Equipment lifespan: 95% of rated life (+15%)

**Financial Impact:**
- Maintenance cost reduction: 25-35%
- Equipment replacement cost reduction: 20% (longer life)
- Energy efficiency improvement: 8-12% (optimized performance)

**Example:** 100-unit multifamily
- Annual maintenance cost: $120,000 (traditional)
- Predictive maintenance investment: $45,000 (sensors + platform)
- Annual savings: $38,000
- Payback: 1.2 years

## Tenant Satisfaction Metrics

### Technology Features Impact on Satisfaction

**Study:** J Turner Research "Tenant Preferences Study 2024"
**Sample Size:** 25,000 multifamily residents

**Most Valued Technology Features:**

| Feature | Satisfaction Rating | Willingness to Pay Premium |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Mobile rent payment** | 4.6/5.0 | 2% higher rent |
| **Package lockers (smart)** | 4.5/5.0 | $15-25/month |
| **Keyless entry** | 4.4/5.0 | $10-20/month |
| **Smart thermostat** | 4.3/5.0 | $5-15/month |
| **Maintenance request app** | 4.2/5.0 | No premium, but reduces churn |
| **Community WiFi** | 4.0/5.0 | $20-40/month |
| **EV charging** | 4.5/5.0 | $30-80/month (usage-based) |

**Renewal Rate Impact:**
- Properties with 4+ smart features: **68% renewal rate**
- Properties with 0-1 smart features: **54% renewal rate**
- **Difference: +14 percentage points**

**Churn Cost Avoidance:**
- Turn cost per unit: $2,000 - $4,000
- Vacancy loss (30 days): $1,500 - $2,500
- For 100-unit building, 14% improvement = 14 fewer turnovers
- **Savings: $49,000 - $91,000 annually**

### Response Time and Tenant Satisfaction

**Study:** AppFolio "Maintenance Response Time Study" 2024

**Correlation: Response Time → Satisfaction**

| Response Time (Emergency) | Satisfaction Rating | Renewal Likelihood |
|--------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **< 2 hours** | 4.7/5.0 | 82% |
| **2-4 hours** | 4.3/5.0 | 71% |
| **4-8 hours** | 3.8/5.0 | 58% |

**Result:** Properties using automated maintenance platforms achieved 2.1-hour average emergency response (vs. 5.8 hours for manual processes)
