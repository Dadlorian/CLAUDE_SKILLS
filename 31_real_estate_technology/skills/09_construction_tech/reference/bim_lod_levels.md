# BIM LOD (Level of Development) Reference

## LOD 100 - Conceptual
- Basic massing
- Approximate size/location
- Symbolic representation

## LOD 200 - Approximate Geometry
- Generic systems
- Approximate quantities
- Early design

## LOD 300 - Precise Geometry
- Specific assemblies
- Accurate quantities
- Construction documents

## LOD 350 - Interface Coordination
- Coordination with other systems
- Clash detection
- Prefabrication

## LOD 400 - Fabrication
- Detailed fabrication/assembly
- Shop drawings
- Installation details

## LOD 500 - As-Built
- Verified field conditions
- As-constructed
- Facility management

## BIM Uses
- Design visualization
- Clash detection
- Quantity takeoff
- 4D scheduling
- 5D cost estimation
- Facility management
