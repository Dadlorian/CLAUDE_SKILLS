# Underwriting Criteria Reference

Comprehensive mortgage underwriting guidelines for conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, and jumbo loans.

## Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)

### Formula
```
DTI = (Total Monthly Debt Payments) / (Gross Monthly Income) × 100

Front-End DTI (Housing Ratio) = (PITI + HOA) / Gross Monthly Income × 100
Back-End DTI (Total Debt) = (PITI + All Monthly Debts) / Gross Monthly Income × 100

Where PITI = Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance
```

### Maximum DTI by Loan Type

| Loan Type | Front-End Max | Back-End Max | With Compensating Factors |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| Conventional | 28% | 36-45% | Up to 50% |
| FHA | 31% | 43% | Up to 57% |
| VA | No limit | 41% | Up to 50% |
| USDA | 29% | 41% | Up to 44% |
| Jumbo | 28-38% | 43-45% | Rarely exceeds 45% |

### Compensating Factors for Higher DTI
- Excellent credit score (760+)
- Significant cash reserves (6+ months)
- Large down payment (20%+)
- Stable employment history (5+ years same field)
- Low loan amount relative to income
- Documented income growth potential
- Minimal credit obligations
- Energy-efficient home (lower utilities)

### DTI Calculation Example
```
Borrower Profile:
Gross Monthly Income: $8,000
Proposed Mortgage Payment (PITI): $2,000
HOA Fees: $200
Car Payment: $400
Student Loan: $300
Credit Card Minimum: $100

Front-End DTI = ($2,000 + $200) / $8,000 = 27.5%
Back-End DTI = ($2,000 + $200 + $400 + $300 + $100) / $8,000 = 37.5%

Result: Qualifies for conventional loan (within 28%/45% limits)
```

### Debts Included in DTI
- Mortgage payment (PITI)
- HOA/condo fees
- Auto loans/leases
- Student loans (actual payment or 1% of balance if in deferment)
- Credit card minimum payments
- Personal loans
- Child support/alimony (court-ordered)
- Co-signed loans
- Tax liens with payment plan
- Business debt (if personally liable)

### Debts Excluded from DTI
- Utilities (electric, gas, water, internet)
- Insurance (health, auto, life)
- Food and groceries
- Transportation costs (gas, parking)
- Cell phone bills
- Medical expenses
- Childcare costs (not court-ordered)
- Debts with <10 months remaining (varies by lender)

### Credit Report Requirements
- **Tri-Merge Report**: Experian, Equifax, TransUnion
- **Middle Score Used**: Median of three scores
- **Two Borrowers**: Lower middle score used
- **Tradelines Required**: 3-4 active tradelines (varies by program)
- **Credit History Length**: 12-24 months minimum
- **Recent Inquiries**: >6 inquiries in 6 months may require explanation
- **Disputes**: Active disputes may need resolution before approval

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) and Down Payment

### LTV Formula
```
LTV = (Loan Amount) / (Lower of Purchase Price or Appraised Value) × 100

Combined LTV (CLTV) = (All Loans) / (Property Value) × 100
  Used when borrower has multiple liens (e.g., first mortgage + HELOC)

Home Equity LTV (HELTV) = (Loan Amount) / (Appraised Value) × 100
  Used for refinances based on current value
```

### Maximum LTV by Loan Program

| Loan Type | Max LTV | Min Down Payment | PMI Required | Notes |
|-----------|---------|------------------|--------------|-------|
| Conventional 97% | 97% | 3% | Yes (if LTV >80%) | First-time buyers, limited income |
| Conventional 95% | 95% | 5% | Yes (if LTV >80%) | HomeReady, Home Possible |
| Conventional Standard | 80% | 20% | No | Best rates, no PMI |
| FHA | 96.5% | 3.5% | Yes (always) | Upfront + annual PMI |
| VA | 100% | 0% | No | Funding fee applies |
| USDA | 100% | 0% | Yes (guarantee fee) | Rural properties only |
| Jumbo | 70-90% | 10-30% | If LTV >80% | Varies by lender |

### Down Payment Source Requirements

**Acceptable Sources:**
- Personal savings (checking, savings, money market)
- Sale of existing property
- Retirement accounts (401k, IRA) - may incur taxes/penalties
- Stocks, bonds, mutual funds (liquid assets)
- Gift funds from family member (with gift letter)
- Down payment assistance programs
- Employer assistance programs
- Grants (government or non-profit)

**Gift Funds Requirements:**
- Gift letter signed by donor (stating no repayment expectation)
- Proof of donor's ability to give (bank statement)
- Paper trail of fund transfer
- Relationship verification (family member for most programs)
- 100% of down payment can be gift for FHA/VA
- Conventional: Borrower must contribute 5% if LTV >80%

**Unacceptable Sources:**
- Unsecured loans (personal loans, credit cards)
- Cash (cannot be verified/sourced)
- Recently deposited funds without documentation
- Cryptocurrency (in most cases)
- Borrowed funds from interested party (seller, real estate agent, loan officer)

### Seasoning Requirements
- **Large Deposits**: Deposits >50% of monthly income must be sourced
- **Seasoning Period**: Funds should be in account 60+ days
- **Documentation**: Bank statements showing 2-3 months history
- **Explanation**: Any unusual deposits require written explanation

## Income Verification

### Employed W-2 Income

**Required Documentation:**
- W-2 forms (most recent 2 years)
- Pay stubs (most recent 30 days, covering 1 full month)
- Verbal/written verification of employment (VOE)
- Year-to-date earnings statement

**Calculation:**
```
Base Salary: Annual salary / 12 = Monthly income
Hourly: (Hourly rate × Hours per week × 52) / 12

Bonus/Commission/Overtime:
- 2-year average if consistent
- Current year only if increasing
- Not used if decreasing or inconsistent
```

**Requirements:**
- 2-year employment history (same field acceptable if job hopping)
- Current employment verification within 10 days of closing
- No unexplained gaps >30 days
- Recent job change acceptable if in same field with equal/higher pay

### Self-Employed Income

**Required Documentation:**
- Personal tax returns (2 years, all schedules)
- Business tax returns (2 years, Form 1120, 1120S, 1065, or Schedule C)
- Year-to-date profit & loss statement
- Year-to-date balance sheet
- CPA letter or business license
- 25%+ ownership requires full business tax returns

**Calculation:**
```
Schedule C (Sole Proprietor):
Net Profit + Depreciation + Depletion + Amortization + Casualty Loss
= Adjusted Income (average 2 years)

K-1 Income (Partnership/S-Corp):
Ordinary Income + Interest + Dividends + Depreciation + Depletion
= Adjusted Income (average 2 years)
```

**Additional Requirements:**
- Declining income trend disqualified or uses current year only
- CPA-prepared returns preferred
- Business must be operational 2+ years
- 25% down payment requirement for some lenders

### Other Income Sources

| Income Type | Documentation | Usage Guidelines |
|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| Social Security | Award letter, 1099-SSA | 100% if continuing 3+ years |
| Pension/Retirement | Award letter, 1099-R | 100% if guaranteed |
| Disability | Award letter, policy docs | 100% if continuing 3+ years |
| Alimony/Child Support | Divorce decree, 6 months cancelled checks | Must continue 3+ years |
| Rental Income | Lease agreement, tax returns | 75% of gross rent (25% maintenance reserve) |
| Investment Income | 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, statements | 2-year average |
| VA Benefits | Award letter | 100% if continuing |
| Military BAH/BAS | LES statements | 100% if active duty |
| Foster Care | State documentation | Case by case |
| Trust Income | Trust document, distribution history | If guaranteed and continuing |
| Unemployment | Not acceptable | -- |

## Asset Verification and Reserves

### Required Assets

**Down Payment + Closing Costs:**
- Must be documented and sourced
- Seasoned in borrower's account
- Liquid and accessible
- Not borrowed (exception: retirement account loan in some cases)

**Reserves (Months of PITI):**

| Loan Type | Primary Residence | Second Home | Investment |
|-----------|------------------|-------------|------------|
| Conventional | 0-2 months | 2-6 months | 6 months |
| FHA | 0 months | N/A | N/A |
| VA | 0 months | N/A | N/A |
| USDA | 0 months | N/A | N/A |
| Jumbo | 6-12 months | 12 months | 12-18 months |

\* Reserves increase for multiple properties, high DTI, lower credit score

### Acceptable Assets

**Liquid Assets (100% of Value):**
- Checking and savings accounts
- Money market accounts
- Certificates of deposit (CD)
- Stocks, bonds, mutual funds
- Cash value of life insurance

**Retirement Assets (60-100% of Vested Value):**
- 401(k), 403(b), 457 plans (60-70%)
- Traditional and Roth IRA (60-70%)
- SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA (60-70%)
- Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) (60-70%)
- Vested pension (case by case)

**Other Assets:**
- Gift funds (with proper documentation)
- Sale of assets (boat, RV, etc.) with proof
- Bridge loan proceeds
- Relocation assistance
- Down payment assistance grants

### Documentation Requirements
- **Bank Statements**: 2 months most recent, all pages
- **Investment Statements**: Most recent quarterly statement
- **Retirement Accounts**: Most recent statement showing vested balance
- **Explanation**: All large deposits, withdrawals, transfers
- **Verification**: Direct verification for balances >$10K (in some cases)

## Appraisal Requirements

### Appraisal Process
1. Lender orders from licensed appraiser (borrower cannot select)
2. Appraiser inspects property and researches comparables
3. Appraisal report delivered to lender (borrower receives copy)
4. Lender review and underwriter approval
5. Additional review if value < purchase price or concerns noted

### Appraisal Value Rules
```
Loan Amount based on LOWER of:
- Purchase Price
- Appraised Value

Example:
Purchase Price: $400,000
Appraised Value: $380,000
Maximum Loan (80% LTV): $380,000 × 0.80 = $304,000
Required Down Payment: $400,000 - $304,000 = $96,000 (24%)
```

### Appraisal Types

| Type | Use Case | Typical Cost | Turnaround |
|------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Full Appraisal | Purchase, refinance | $400-800 | 7-14 days |
| Desktop Appraisal | Refinance, low-risk | $150-300 | 3-5 days |
| Drive-by/Exterior | Refinance, condos | $200-400 | 5-7 days |
| Automated (AVM) | Refinance, conventional | $0-50 | Instant |

### Comparable Sales Criteria
- Similar property type (SFR, condo, townhome)
- Similar size (±20% GLA)
- Similar age (±10-20 years)
- Same neighborhood or competing area
- Sold within 3-6 months (12 months in slow markets)
- Arm's length transactions (not foreclosure, family, etc.)
- Minimum 3 comparables required (ideally 4-6)

### Appraisal Red Flags
- Value significantly below purchase price
- Property in poor condition
- Health/safety issues (mold, foundation, roof)
- Non-permitted additions
- Functional obsolescence
- Declining neighborhood trends
- Insufficient comparable sales
- Subject property is unique/special use

| Property Type | Conventional | FHA | VA | USDA | Jumbo |
|---------------|-------------|-----|-----|------|-------|
| Single-Family | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 2-4 Unit | ✓ | ✓ (owner-occupied) | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ |

| Condo (Non-Warrantable) | Limited | ✗ | Limited | ✗ | Limited |
| Townhome | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Manufactured/Mobile | Limited | ✓ (if on foundation) | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Cooperative (Co-op) | Limited | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | Limited |
| Mixed-Use | Limited | Limited | Limited | ✗ | Limited |
| Rural/Farm | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

### Occupancy Type Impact

| Occupancy | Down Payment | Rates | Reserves | Max LTV |
|-----------|-------------|--------|----------|---------|
| Primary Residence | 3-20% | Best | 0-2 mo | 97% |
| Second Home | 10-20% | +0.25-0.50% | 2-6 mo | 90% |
| Investment | 15-25% | +0.50-1.00% | 6 mo | 75-80% |

### Documentation Levels

**Full Documentation (Full Doc):**
- W-2s, pay stubs, tax returns
- Bank statements, asset verification
- Employment verification
- Best rates and terms

**Alternative Documentation (Alt Doc):**
- Bank statements only (12-24 months)
- 1099 contractors, self-employed
- Higher rates (+1-2%)
- Higher down payment (20-30%)

**No Documentation (No Doc / Stated Income):**
- Not available for owner-occupied (post-2008 regulations)
- Investment properties only
- Hard money lenders
- Much higher rates and fees
