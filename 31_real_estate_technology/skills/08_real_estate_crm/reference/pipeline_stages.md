# Sales Pipeline Stages Reference

## Stage 1: New Lead
- **Entry**: Lead captured
- **Actions**: Immediate response (< 5 min)
- **Exit Criteria**: Initial contact made

## Stage 2: Contacted
- **Entry**: First conversation
- **Actions**: Qualify budget, timeline
- **Exit Criteria**: Qualified or disqualified

## Stage 3: Qualified
- **Entry**: Budget/timeline confirmed
- **Actions**: Property search, showings
- **Exit Criteria**: Showing scheduled

## Stage 4: Showing
- **Entry**: Property tour scheduled
- **Actions**: Follow-up after showing
- **Exit Criteria**: Offer or continue search

## Stage 5: Offer
- **Entry**: Offer submitted
- **Actions**: Negotiation
- **Exit Criteria**: Accepted or rejected

## Stage 6: Under Contract
- **Entry**: Offer accepted
- **Actions**: Inspections, appraisal, financing
- **Exit Criteria**: Clear to close

## Stage 7: Closed
- **Entry**: Closing day
- **Actions**: Hand over keys, follow-up
- **Exit Criteria**: Transaction complete

## Conversion Rates
| Stage | Typical Conversion |
|-------|-------------------|
| New → Contacted | 80% |
| Contacted → Qualified | 40% |
| Qualified → Showing | 60% |
| Showing → Offer | 25% |
| Offer → Contract | 70% |
| Contract → Closed | 90% |

**Overall**: ~5-8% new lead to closed
