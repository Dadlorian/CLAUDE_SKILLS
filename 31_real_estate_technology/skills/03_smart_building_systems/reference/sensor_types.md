# Sensor Types Reference

## Temperature Sensors
- **Thermistor**: Low cost, accurate
- **RTD**: High accuracy, stable  
- **Thermocouple**: Wide range

Range: -40°F to 250°F
Accuracy: ±0.5°F to ±2°F

## Occupancy Sensors
- **PIR**: Motion detection, $20-$100
- **Ultrasonic**: Fine movement, $50-$150
- **CO2-based**: Breathing detection, $200-$500

## Air Quality
- CO2: 400-5000 ppm
- VOC: Volatile organics
- PM2.5/PM10: Particulates
- Humidity: 0-100% RH

## Energy Meters
- Current transformers (CT)
- Smart meters
- Sub-meters for tenant billing

## Water Sensors
- Leak detection
- Flow meters
- Conservation monitoring

## Light Sensors
- Photocells for daylight harvesting
- Automatic dimming
- Energy savings: 30-50%
