# HVAC Basics Reference

## HVAC System Types

### Central Systems
- **VAV (Variable Air Volume)**: Zone control, energy efficient
- **CAV (Constant Air Volume)**: Simple, less efficient
- **Fan Coil Units**: Perimeter zones, hotels

### Packaged Units
- **RTU (Rooftop Unit)**: Common in commercial
- **Split Systems**: Residential, small commercial
- **VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow)**: Multi-zone, efficient

## Control Strategies

### Temperature Control
- **Setpoint**: Target temperature
- **Deadband**: Range before action (e.g., 70-74°F)
- **PID**: Proportional-Integral-Derivative control

### Scheduling
- Occupied: 7 AM - 6 PM weekdays
- Unoccupied: Nights, weekends
- Optimal Start: Pre-cool/heat before occupancy

### Economizer
- Use outdoor air when temp is favorable
- Free cooling saves 20-40% energy

## Energy Optimization

### Setback Strategies
- Occupied: 72°F cooling, 68°F heating
- Unoccupied: 78°F cooling, 62°F heating
- Savings: 10-20% per degree

### Demand Control Ventilation (DCV)
- CO2-based fresh air control
- Reduce over-ventilation
- Savings: 20-30% on ventilation energy

## Common Issues

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Poor comfort | Incorrect setpoint | Adjust schedule |
| High energy | Simultaneous heating/cooling | Fix zone conflicts |
| Equipment failure | Lack of maintenance | Preventive maintenance |
