# Property Management Compliance Checklist

## Fair Housing Compliance

### Protected Classes (Federal)
- ☐ Race
- ☐ Color
- ☐ National Origin
- ☐ Religion
- ☐ Sex (including gender identity and sexual orientation)
- ☐ Familial Status (families with children under 18)
- ☐ Disability (physical or mental)

### Additional State/Local Protected Classes
- ☐ Source of Income (Section 8, other assistance)
- ☐ Age
- ☐ Marital Status
- ☐ Military/Veteran Status
- ☐ Sexual Orientation (explicit in some states)
- ☐ Gender Identity
- ☐ Ancestry

### Fair Housing Requirements
- ☐ Equal Housing Opportunity logo displayed
- ☐ Non-discriminatory advertising language
- ☐ Consistent screening criteria applied to all applicants
- ☐ Reasonable accommodations process for disabilities
- ☐ Reasonable modifications allowed for disabled tenants
- ☐ No discriminatory steering of applicants
- ☐ No discriminatory pricing or terms
- ☐ Staff trained on Fair Housing laws (annually)
- ☐ Documentation of all rental decisions
- ☐ Complaint procedure in place

### Advertising Compliance
**Prohibited Terms**:
- ✗ "Adult community" (unless 55+ certified)
- ✗ "No children"
- ✗ Religious preferences
- ✗ Race, color, or national origin references
- ✗ "Perfect for couples" (familial status)

**Required Disclosures**:
- ✓ Equal Housing Opportunity statement
- ✓ Accessibility features (if applicable)
- ✓ Pet policy clearly stated
- ✓ All fees and deposits listed

## ADA Compliance

### Common Areas (Required)
- ☐ Accessible parking spaces (1 per 25 spaces minimum)
- ☐ Accessible route from parking to units
- ☐ Entrance with no steps or ramp provided
- ☐ Doors at least 32" wide
- ☐ Accessible amenities (pool, gym, clubhouse)
- ☐ Accessible bathroom in common areas
- ☐ Signage with Braille and raised letters
- ☐ Accessible height for controls/outlets

### Units (New Construction/Major Renovation)
- ☐ At least 5% wheelchair accessible units
- ☐ At least 2% sensory accessible units
- ☐ Roll-in showers or transfer tubs
- ☐ Reinforced bathroom walls for grab bars
- ☐ Accessible kitchen counters and appliances
- ☐ Lever-style door handles
- ☐ Accessible light switches and outlets

### Reasonable Accommodations
- ☐ Written policy for accommodation requests
- ☐ Interactive process with tenant
- ☐ Documentation of requests and decisions
- ☐ Timely response (30 days typical)
- ☐ No charge for accommodations
- ☐ Exceptions to pet policy for service/assistance animals

## Lease Documentation

### Required Disclosures
- ☐ Lead-based paint (pre-1978 properties)
  - EPA pamphlet provided
  - Disclosure form signed
  - 10-day inspection period offered
- ☐ Mold information and prevention
- ☐ Bed bug history (required in some states)
- ☐ Asbestos (if present)
- ☐ Flood zone disclosure
- ☐ Proximity to sex offenders (some states)
- ☐ Methamphetamine contamination (if applicable)
- ☐ Shared utility arrangements
- ☐ Manager contact information
- ☐ Notice and entry procedures

### Lease Clauses
- ☐ Names of all parties (landlord and tenants)
- ☐ Property address and unit number
- ☐ Lease term (start and end dates)
- ☐ Rent amount and due date
- ☐ Late fee policy (amount and grace period)
- ☐ Security deposit amount and terms
- ☐ Pet policy and fees
- ☐ Maintenance responsibilities
- ☐ Entry and notice requirements
- ☐ Renewal and termination procedures
- ☐ Utilities responsibility
- ☐ Parking allocation
- ☐ Prohibited activities
- ☐ Landlord and tenant signatures and dates

## Security Deposit Regulations

### Limits by State (Examples)
- California: 2x monthly rent (unfurnished), 3x (furnished)
- New York: 1 month's rent
- Texas: No limit
- Florida: No limit

### Required Practices
- ☐ Deposit held in separate account or bonded
- ☐ Interest paid if required by state/local law
- ☐ Bank account information provided to tenant (some states)
- ☐ Written receipt provided
- ☐ Move-in inspection completed and documented
- ☐ Return timeline followed (15-60 days varies by state)
- ☐ Itemized statement of deductions
- ☐ Receipts/invoices for deductions provided
- ☐ Remaining deposit returned with statement
- ☐ Sent to last known address or forwarding address

## Eviction Procedures

### Notice Requirements (Varies by State)
**Non-Payment of Rent**:
- ☐ 3-day notice (most states)
- ☐ 5-day notice (some states)
- ☐ 10-day notice (few states)
- ☐ Notice must include amount owed and payment instructions

**Lease Violation**:
- ☐ Cure or quit notice (typically 10-30 days)
- ☐ Specific violation(s) listed
- ☐ Opportunity to remedy provided (if curable)

**No Cause / End of Lease**:
- ☐ 30-day notice (month-to-month)
- ☐ 60-90 day notice (tenant over 1 year in some states)
- ☐ 30-60 day notice before lease end (if not renewing)

### Filing and Court Process
- ☐ Notice period expired before filing
- ☐ Complaint filed with correct court
- ☐ Filing fees paid
- ☐ Tenant properly served
- ☐ Court hearing attended
- ☐ Evidence prepared (lease, notices, ledger)
- ☐ Judgment obtained before lockout
- ☐ Writ of possession/restitution obtained
- ☐ Sheriff performs eviction (no self-help evictions)
- ☐ Tenant belongings handled per state law

### Prohibited Actions
- ✗ Self-help eviction (changing locks)
- ✗ Utility shutoff
- ✗ Harassment or intimidation
- ✗ Removing tenant belongings before judgment
- ✗ Physical removal of tenant

## Tenant Privacy and Entry

### Entry Notice Requirements
- ☐ Written notice provided (varies: 24-48 hours typical)
- ☐ Reasonable hours (usually 8am-5pm or 9am-6pm)
- ☐ Valid reason stated (repairs, inspection, showing)
- ☐ Exceptions: Emergency, tenant consent, abandonment

### Inspection Schedule
- ☐ Annual or semi-annual inspections scheduled
- ☐ Notice provided per state law
- ☐ Inspection checklist used
- ☐ Photos taken and stored
- ☐ Follow-up on violations or maintenance needs

## Financial and Tax Compliance

### Trust Accounting
- ☐ Separate account for tenant deposits
- ☐ Commingling prohibited
- ☐ Monthly reconciliation
- ☐ Audit trail maintained
- ☐ Interest calculated and paid (if required)

### Tax Reporting
- ☐ Rental income reported on Schedule E
- ☐ 1099 issued to contractors ($600+ threshold)
- ☐ Depreciation calculated correctly
- ☐ Property tax payments current
- ☐ Sales tax on fees (if applicable by state)

### Owner Reporting
- ☐ Monthly financial statements
- ☐ Quarterly portfolio review
- ☐ Annual budget and variance analysis
- ☐ Distribution calculations and payments
- ☐ Year-end tax documents (1099, K-1)

## Insurance Requirements

### Property Insurance
- ☐ Property insurance current and adequate
- ☐ Liability coverage ($1-2M recommended)
- ☐ Loss of rent coverage
- ☐ Building and contents covered
- ☐ Flood insurance (if in flood zone)
- ☐ Earthquake insurance (if applicable)
- ☐ Umbrella policy (additional liability)

### Tenant Insurance
- ☐ Renters insurance required in lease
- ☐ Minimum coverage specified ($100K typical)
- ☐ Property listed as additional insured
- ☐ Proof of insurance collected annually
- ☐ Coverage verification (certificate of insurance)

### Workers Compensation
- ☐ Coverage for all employees
- ☐ Policy current and compliant
- ☐ Proper classification of employees

## Safety and Habitability

### Building Codes
- ☐ Certificate of occupancy current
- ☐ Building meets local codes
- ☐ Zoning compliance verified
- ☐ Maximum occupancy limits posted

### Life Safety
- ☐ Smoke detectors in all units (tested semi-annually)
- ☐ Carbon monoxide detectors (if required)
- ☐ Fire extinguishers in common areas
- ☐ Exit signs illuminated
- ☐ Emergency lighting functional
- ☐ Sprinkler system inspected (if applicable)
- ☐ Fire alarm system tested annually

### Utilities and Systems
- ☐ Heating system functional (seasonal testing)
- ☐ Air conditioning functional (seasonal testing)
- ☐ Hot water available (120-130°F)
- ☐ Plumbing in good repair
- ☐ Electrical system safe and functional
- ☐ Trash removal service arranged
- ☐ Pest control program in place

### Security
- ☐ Exterior lighting adequate
- ☐ Locks on all doors and windows
- ☐ Broken windows/doors repaired promptly
- ☐ Access control systems functional
- ☐ Security cameras operational (if installed)
- ☐ Emergency contact list posted

## Affordable Housing Compliance (if applicable)

### HUD/Section 8
- ☐ Housing Quality Standards (HQS) inspection passed
- ☐ Annual recertification of tenant income
- ☐ Rent within payment standards
- ☐ Lease addendum for Section 8 tenants
- ☐ HAP contract with housing authority
- ☐ Tenant payments collected on time
- ☐ No discrimination against voucher holders

### LIHTC (Low-Income Housing Tax Credit)
- ☐ Income certifications at move-in
- ☐ Annual recertification
- ☐ Rent limits followed
- ☐ Minimum set-aside maintained
- ☐ Student rule compliance
- ☐ File documentation complete
- ☐ State agency inspections passed

## Data Security and Privacy

### Tenant Data Protection
- ☐ PII encrypted at rest and in transit
- ☐ Access controls and permissions set
- ☐ Background check data secured
- ☐ SSN truncated (last 4 only) where possible
- ☐ Secure document storage
- ☐ Data retention policy followed
- ☐ Secure disposal of old records (shredding)

### Payment Card Industry (PCI) Compliance
- ☐ PCI-DSS certified payment processor used
- ☐ Credit card data never stored
- ☐ Secure payment gateway
- ☐ Annual PCI compliance assessment

### Data Breach Response
- ☐ Incident response plan documented
- ☐ Breach notification procedures defined
- ☐ Cyber liability insurance coverage

## Employment Law Compliance

### Employee Classification
- ☐ Employees vs. contractors correctly classified
- ☐ I-9 forms completed for all employees
- ☐ W-4 forms on file
- ☐ Payroll taxes withheld and remitted

### Wage and Hour
- ☐ Minimum wage requirements met
- ☐ Overtime calculated correctly (1.5x over 40 hours)
- ☐ Exempt vs. non-exempt properly classified
- ☐ Time tracking system in place
- ☐ Meal and rest breaks provided (state-specific)

### Workplace Posters
- ☐ Fair Labor Standards Act
- ☐ Equal Employment Opportunity
- ☐ OSHA safety posters
- ☐ State labor law posters
- ☐ Workers' compensation
- ☐ Family and Medical Leave Act (if 50+ employees)

## Annual Compliance Calendar

**January**:
- ☐ Issue 1099s to contractors
- ☐ W-2s to employees
- ☐ Send annual owner tax documents

**February**:
- ☐ Review insurance policies (renewal season)
- ☐ Update emergency procedures

**March**:
- ☐ Fair Housing training for staff
- ☐ Review leases expiring Q2

**April**:
- ☐ Spring property inspections
- ☐ Review and adjust rents

**May**:
- ☐ Test HVAC systems before summer
- ☐ Pool opening and safety check

**June**:
- ☐ Mid-year financial review
- ☐ Update budgets if needed

**July**:
- ☐ Review leases expiring Q4
- ☐ Safety inspection (fire alarms, exits)

**August**:
- ☐ Prepare for fall leasing season
- ☐ Student housing turnover

**September**:
- ☐ Annual property inspections
- ☐ Renters insurance renewal reminders

**October**:
- ☐ Test heating systems before winter
- ☐ Pool closing procedures
- ☐ Budget preparation for next year

**November**:
- ☐ Review leases expiring Q1 next year
- ☐ Holiday scheduling and coverage

**December**:
- ☐ Year-end financial close
- ☐ Finalize next year's budget
- ☐ Annual staff performance reviews

## Record Retention Schedule

**7+ Years**:
- Tax returns and supporting documents
- Financial statements
- Eviction records
- Discrimination complaints

**3-7 Years**:
- Lease agreements (after expiration)
- Security deposit records
- Maintenance records
- Insurance policies

**1-3 Years**:
- Rental applications (declined)
- Vendor contracts (after expiration)
- Utility bills

**Permanent**:
- Property deeds and titles
- Major capital improvements
- Environmental assessments

Compliance is an ongoing process requiring regular review and updates based on changing laws and regulations.
