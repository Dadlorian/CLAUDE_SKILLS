# Property Management Terminology Glossary

## A

**Abandonment**: When a tenant vacates the property before lease end without notice and with no intention to return.

**Absorption Rate**: Speed at which available rental units are leased in a specific market during a given timeframe.

**ACH (Automated Clearing House)**: Electronic network for financial transactions, commonly used for rent payments.

**Actual Rent**: Current rent being collected from a tenant, may differ from market rent.

**Addendum**: An addition or amendment to a lease agreement.

**Amenity**: Feature or service provided to tenants (pool, gym, parking, etc.).

**AMI (Area Median Income)**: Median household income for a specific geographic area, used in affordable housing calculations.

**Applicant**: Person applying to rent a property.

**ARM (Accredited Residential Manager)**: Professional certification from IREM.

**As-Is**: Property condition accepted without repairs or improvements.

**Assignment**: Transfer of lease rights from original tenant to a new tenant.

## B

**BACnet**: Building Automation and Control network protocol for smart building systems.

**Balloon Payment**: Large final payment in a lease or loan structure.

**Base Rent**: Primary rent amount before additional fees or charges.

**Blacklist**: Illegal practice of maintaining a list of undesirable tenants (use "do not rent list" instead).

**Breach of Contract**: Violation of lease terms by either party.

**Buildium**: Popular property management software platform.

**Build-to-Suit**: Custom construction for specific tenant needs.

## C

**CAM (Common Area Maintenance)**: Expenses for shared spaces in commercial properties, often passed to tenants.

**Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)**: NOI divided by property value, expressing rate of return.

**Cash Flow**: Net income after all expenses and debt service.

**CFBT (Cash Flow Before Taxes)**: Income after operating expenses and debt service, before income taxes.

**Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)**: Government certification that building is safe for occupancy.

**Charge Code**: Category identifier for ledger transactions (RENT, LATE, PET, etc.).

**Class A/B/C Properties**:
- **Class A**: Newest, highest quality, best locations, premium rents
- **Class B**: Mid-range age and quality, moderate rents
- **Class C**: Older properties, lower rents, may need updates

**CoC (Cash-on-Cash Return)**: Annual return on actual cash invested.

**Co-Tenant**: Additional adult tenant on the same lease.

**Concession**: Financial incentive offered to attract tenants (free rent, reduced deposits).

**Condo (Condominium)**: Individual ownership of units within a larger building or complex.

**Co-Signer (Guarantor)**: Person who guarantees lease obligations if tenant defaults.

**CPM (Certified Property Manager)**: Professional designation from IREM.

**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: Software for managing leads and customer interactions.

## D

**Days on Market (DOM)**: Number of days a unit has been vacant and available.

**Delinquency**: Outstanding rent or fees past due date.

**Deposit**: Money held as security against damages or unpaid rent.

**Depreciation**: Tax deduction for property value reduction over time.

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)**: NOI divided by annual debt payments.

**Due Diligence**: Investigation and analysis before property acquisition.

**Dwelling Unit**: Living space intended for occupancy by one household.

## E

**Economic Occupancy**: Percentage of potential rent being collected.

**EGI (Effective Gross Income)**: Total income after vacancy loss and concessions.

**Estoppel Certificate**: Document signed by tenant confirming lease terms and obligations.

**Eviction**: Legal process to remove a tenant for non-payment or lease violations.

**Expense Ratio**: Operating expenses as percentage of gross income.

## F

**Fair Housing Act**: Federal law prohibiting discrimination in housing.

**Fair Market Rent (FMR)**: Typical rent for similar properties in the same market.

**FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act)**: Law governing use of consumer credit information.

**Fixed-Term Lease**: Lease with specific start and end dates.

**Floorplan**: Unit layout showing number of bedrooms/bathrooms and configuration.

**Force Majeure**: Clause excusing performance due to extraordinary events.

**Foreclosure**: Legal process where lender takes property due to loan default.

## G

**GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles)**: Standard accounting framework.

**Gross Lease**: Lease where landlord pays most or all operating expenses.

**Gross Potential Rent (GPR)**: Maximum rent if all units occupied at market rate.

**Gross Scheduled Rent (GSR)**: Total rent based on actual lease agreements.

**Guarantor**: See Co-Signer.

## H

**Habitability**: Minimum standards for safe and livable conditions.

**HAP (Housing Assistance Payment)**: Government payment to landlord in Section 8 program.

**Holding Deposit**: Money paid to secure a unit, applied to move-in costs.

**HOA (Homeowners Association)**: Organization governing a community or condo complex.

**Holdover Tenant**: Tenant remaining after lease expiration without new agreement.

**HQS (Housing Quality Standards)**: Inspection standards for Section 8 properties.

**HUD (Housing and Urban Development)**: Federal agency overseeing housing programs.

**HVAC**: Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems.

## I

**Indemnification**: Agreement to compensate for losses or damages.

**In-Place Rent**: Current rent being charged to existing tenants.

**IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: Time-weighted return on investment.

**IoT (Internet of Things)**: Connected smart devices in buildings.

## J

**Joint and Several Liability**: Each co-tenant is fully responsible for total rent.

**Judgment**: Court order requiring payment or action, often from eviction.

## K

**Key Money**: Illegal payment demanded for unit access (prohibited in most jurisdictions).

## L

**Late Fee**: Charge assessed when rent is paid after grace period.

**Lead-Based Paint Disclosure**: Required disclosure for pre-1978 properties.

**Lease**: Legal contract between landlord and tenant for property rental.

**Lease Break**: Tenant terminating lease before expiration.

**Lease Renewal**: Extension of existing lease for additional term.

**Ledger**: Financial record of all charges and payments for a lease.

**LIHTC (Low-Income Housing Tax Credit)**: Federal program incentivizing affordable housing.

**Loss-to-Lease**: Difference between market rent and in-place rent.

**LTV (Loan-to-Value)**: Loan amount as percentage of property value.

## M

**Make-Ready**: Process of preparing unit for new tenant after move-out.

**Market Rent**: Competitive rent for similar units in the area.

**Matterport**: 3D virtual tour technology.

**MLS (Multiple Listing Service)**: Database of property listings.

**Month-to-Month Lease**: Lease with no fixed end date, typically 30-day notice to terminate.

**Move-In/Move-Out Inspection**: Documented unit condition at lease start/end.

## N

**NACHA**: Organization governing ACH network and payments.

**Net Lease**: Tenant pays some or all property expenses beyond base rent.

**Net Operating Income (NOI)**: Income after operating expenses, before debt service.

**NNN (Triple Net) Lease**: Tenant pays property taxes, insurance, and CAM in addition to rent.

**Non-Refundable Fee**: Payment that will not be returned (pet fee, application fee).

**Notice to Vacate**: Formal notification of intent to move out.

**NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds)**: Failed payment due to insufficient account balance.

## O

**Occupancy Rate**: Percentage of units occupied.

**OER (Operating Expense Ratio)**: Operating expenses as percentage of income.

**On-Site Manager**: Property manager who lives at or works regularly on-site.

**Origination Fee**: Fee for processing loan or lease application.

## P

**Pay or Quit Notice**: Legal notice demanding rent payment or vacate premises.

**PCI-DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard for processing credit cards.

**Pet Deposit**: Refundable deposit for tenants with pets.

**Pet Rent**: Monthly fee for pet ownership (non-refundable).

**Physical Occupancy**: Percentage of units with tenants (vs. vacant).

**PII (Personally Identifiable Information)**: Private data requiring protection.

**PMS (Property Management System)**: Software for managing properties.

**Portfolio**: Collection of properties under single ownership or management.

**Proration**: Calculation of rent for partial month.

**Prospect**: Potential tenant who has shown interest but not applied.

## Q

**Quiet Enjoyment**: Tenant's right to use property without interference.

## R

**Recertification**: Annual income verification for affordable housing programs.

**Referral Fee**: Payment for referring new tenants.

**REIT (Real Estate Investment Trust)**: Company owning income-producing real estate.

**Rent Concession**: Reduced rent or free rent to attract tenants.

**Rent Control**: Government regulation limiting rent increases.

**Rent Roll**: Report listing all units, tenants, rents, and lease dates.

**Renters Insurance**: Tenant's policy covering personal property and liability.

**RESO (Real Estate Standards Organization)**: Industry data standards body.

**RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room)**: Total revenue divided by total units.

**Right of Entry**: Landlord's right to enter unit with proper notice.

**ROI (Return on Investment)**: Total return including appreciation and cash flow.

**RUBS (Ratio Utility Billing System)**: Method to allocate shared utilities to tenants.

## S

**SCRA (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)**: Protections for military members including lease termination rights.

**Section 8**: Federal housing assistance program (Housing Choice Voucher).

**Security Deposit**: Refundable deposit held for damages or unpaid rent.

**Self-Showing**: Allowing prospects to tour units independently using smart locks.

**Service Animal**: Animal providing assistance to person with disability (not a pet, no fees allowed).

**Showing**: Scheduled property tour for prospective tenant.

**Soft Pull**: Credit check that doesn't affect credit score.

**Sublet (Sublease)**: Tenant renting to another party while remaining on lease.

**Syndication**: Distributing property listings to multiple advertising platforms.

## T

**Tenant**: Person or entity renting a property.

**Tenant Portal**: Online platform for tenants to pay rent, submit requests, etc.

**Tenant Screening**: Background, credit, and rental history check.

**Term**: Duration of lease agreement.

**TIN (Tax Identification Number)**: EIN or SSN used for tax purposes.

**Turn/Turnover**: Process and cost of preparing unit between tenants.

**Turnkey**: Property ready for immediate occupancy, no work needed.

## U

**Underwriting**: Financial analysis to assess risk and feasibility.

**Unit Mix**: Distribution of unit types in a property (studios, 1BR, 2BR, etc.).

**Usable Square Feet**: Interior space available for tenant use.

**Utilities**: Services like water, electric, gas, trash, sewer.

## V

**Vacancy Loss**: Revenue lost from unoccupied units.

**Vacancy Rate**: Percentage of units currently vacant.

**Variable Rent**: Rent that changes based on defined factors (percentage lease).

**Vendor**: Service provider or contractor (plumber, landscaper, etc.).

**Verification**: Confirmation of applicant information (employment, income, rental history).

## W

**Walk-Through**: Inspection of property with tenant.

**Wear and Tear (Normal)**: Expected deterioration from regular use, not chargeable to tenant.

**Work Order**: Request for maintenance or repair.

**Wrap-Around Mortgage**: Loan including existing mortgage balance.

## Y

**Yardi**: Leading property management software platform.

**Year-over-Year (YoY)**: Comparison of metric to same period previous year.

**Yield**: Return on investment, usually expressed as percentage.

**YieldStar**: RealPage's revenue management and pricing software.

## Z

**Zoning**: Government regulations on property use (residential, commercial, mixed-use).

**Zoning Variance**: Exception to zoning regulations.

This glossary covers essential terminology for property management professionals and systems.
