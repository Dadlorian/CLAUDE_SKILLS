# Lease Lifecycle Reference

## Overview
Complete reference for the lease lifecycle from application to move-out, including all stages, workflows, and best practices.

## Lifecycle Stages

### 1. Pre-Lease (Application & Screening)

#### Guest Card / Initial Inquiry
- **Duration**: Day 0
- **Actions**:
  - Prospect information collection
  - Tour scheduling
  - Initial qualification (income, move-in date)
- **Systems**: CRM, showing software
- **Data Collected**: Name, contact info, desired move-in date, budget

#### Property Tour
- **Duration**: Days 1-7
- **Types**:
  - In-person tour with leasing agent
  - Self-guided tour (smart locks)
  - Virtual tour (video call, 3D walkthrough)
- **Actions**:
  - Show available units
  - Explain amenities and lease terms
  - Answer questions
  - Provide application instructions
- **Follow-up**: Within 24 hours

#### Application Submission
- **Duration**: Day 7-14
- **Required Information**:
  - Personal: Full name, DOB, SSN, ID
  - Contact: Email, phone, current address
  - Employment: Employer, income, tenure
  - Rental History: Previous landlords (2-3 years)
  - References: Personal and professional
  - Emergency Contact
- **Application Fee**: $35-$75 (covers screening costs)
- **Co-applicants**: All adults 18+ must apply

#### Screening Process
- **Duration**: 24-48 hours
- **Components**:

  **Credit Check**:
  - Minimum score requirements: 600-650 typical
  - Review: payment history, collections, bankruptcies
  - Age of credit history

  **Criminal Background Check**:
  - National and county-level searches
  - Review: felonies, sex offender registry
  - Fair Housing considerations

  **Eviction History**:
  - Court records search (7-year lookback)
  - Previous landlord verification

  **Income Verification**:
  - Requirement: 2.5x-3x monthly rent
  - Documentation: Pay stubs, bank statements, tax returns
  - Employment verification

  **Rental History**:
  - Contact previous landlords
  - Verify: payment history, lease compliance, condition
  - Recommendation for re-rental

#### Decision
- **Timeline**: 2-3 business days after complete application
- **Outcomes**:

  **Approved**:
  - Standard approval with deposit
  - Conditional approval (co-signer, higher deposit)

  **Denied**:
  - Adverse action letter required (FCRA)
  - Must state reason (credit, income, rental history)
  - Provide credit bureau contact information

  **Waitlist**:
  - No current availability
  - Hold application for future openings

#### Lease Preparation
- **Duration**: 3-5 days
- **Tasks**:
  - Generate lease document
  - Prepare addendums and disclosures
  - Calculate pro-rated rent (if applicable)
  - Create move-in cost breakdown
  - Schedule lease signing appointment

### 2. Lease Execution

#### Lease Signing
- **Methods**:
  - In-person with wet signatures
  - E-signature (DocuSign, Adobe Sign)
  - Remote notarization (if required)

- **Documents to Sign**:
  - Lease agreement
  - Pet addendum (if applicable)
  - Parking agreement
  - Community rules and regulations
  - Lead paint disclosure (pre-1978 properties)
  - Smoke detector / carbon monoxide acknowledgment
  - Mold/asbestos disclosure
  - Bed bug addendum
  - Renters insurance requirement acknowledgment

- **Move-in Costs Due**:
  - First month's rent (or pro-rated)
  - Security deposit (1-2 months' rent)
  - Pet deposit ($200-$500 per pet)
  - Parking fee (if separate)
  - Application fee (if not paid earlier)
  - Administrative/lease prep fee ($100-$300)

#### Pre-Move-In Inspection
- **Timing**: Before keys handoff
- **Process**:
  - Walk through unit with tenant
  - Document existing condition (photos/video)
  - Complete move-in inspection form
  - Note any pre-existing damages
  - Both parties sign inspection report
- **Purpose**: Baseline for security deposit return

#### Key/Access Card Distribution
- **Items Provided**:
  - Unit keys (2-3 sets)
  - Mailbox key
  - Access cards/fobs for common areas
  - Gate/parking access
  - Storage unit key (if applicable)
- **Documentation**: Key receipt signed by tenant

#### Move-In Day
- **Date**: Typically first day of lease term
- **Tenant Responsibilities**:
  - Utilities transfer to tenant name
  - Renters insurance activation (proof required)
  - Update USPS forwarding address
  - Set up rent payment method (ACH, autopay)
- **Property Responsibilities**:
  - Unit ready and clean
  - All maintenance issues resolved
  - Appliances functional
  - Access systems activated

### 3. Active Lease

#### Rent Collection
- **Due Date**: Typically 1st of month
- **Grace Period**: 3-5 days (varies by state/lease)
- **Payment Methods**:
  - ACH (preferred, lowest cost)
  - Credit/debit card (2-3% processing fee)
  - Money order or cashier's check
  - Personal check (if allowed)
  - Cash (rarely accepted, security concerns)
- **Autopay**: Encouraged for on-time payments

**Late Rent Process**:
```
Day 1-3: Grace period (no fee)
Day 4: Late fee applied ($50-$100 or 5-10% of rent)
Day 5: First reminder notice (email/text/call)
Day 10: Second notice (certified mail)
Day 14: Pay or quit notice / 3-day notice
Day 21: File eviction (if unpaid)
```

#### Ongoing Maintenance
- **Tenant Responsibilities**:
  - Minor repairs (light bulbs, HVAC filters)
  - Keep unit clean and sanitary
  - Report maintenance issues promptly
  - Allow entry for repairs with proper notice

- **Property Responsibilities**:
  - Major repairs (appliances, plumbing, HVAC)
  - Structural maintenance
  - Common area upkeep
  - Emergency repairs (24/7)
  - Preventive maintenance

**Work Order Process**:
1. Tenant submits request (portal, email, phone)
2. Categorize and prioritize
3. Assign to maintenance staff or vendor
4. Schedule entry with tenant
5. Complete repair
6. Close work order, tenant confirmation

#### Lease Compliance
- **Property Inspections**:
  - Annual or semi-annual unit inspections
  - Notice required (typically 24-48 hours)
  - Document condition, lease violations

- **Common Violations**:
  - Unauthorized occupants
  - Unapproved pets
  - Smoking (in non-smoking units)
  - Noise complaints
  - Damage beyond normal wear and tear
  - Illegal activities

**Violation Process**:
1. Document violation
2. Issue warning notice
3. Opportunity to cure (5-10 days)
4. Second notice if uncured
5. Lease termination / eviction if severe

#### Lease Amendments
- **Common Changes**:
  - Add/remove occupant
  - Add pet
  - Add parking space
  - Change payment method
  - Modify lease terms
- **Process**: Written addendum, both parties sign

#### Rent Increases
- **Annual Increases** (month-to-month or renewal):
  - Typical: 2-5% for renewals
  - Market-based: 5-15% in hot markets
  - Notice required: 30-60 days (varies by state)

- **Restrictions**:
  - Rent control jurisdictions
  - Affordable housing programs (HUD, LIHTC)
  - Lease term (no mid-lease increases without clause)

### 4. Lease Renewal

#### Renewal Timeline
```
120 days before expiration: Review market rents, set renewal strategy
90 days before: Renewal offer sent to tenant
60 days before: Follow up if no response
45 days before: Final reminder, market unit if no renewal
30 days before: Confirm renewal or prepare for move-out
```

#### Renewal Offer
- **Components**:
  - New lease term (6, 12, 24 months)
  - New rent amount
  - Updated pet fees/parking (if applicable)
  - Expiration date of offer
  - Incentives (if needed to retain tenant)

**Incentive Examples**:
- $0 rent increase for 12+ month term
- Free month of parking
- Upgraded appliances/features
- One-time rent credit

#### Tenant Decision
- **Outcomes**:

  **Renew**: Sign new lease or amendment
  - Update system with new dates and rent
  - Communicate move-in cost changes (if any)
  - E-sign or in-person signing

  **Decline to Renew**: Provide notice to vacate
  - Typically 30-60 days required
  - Begin marketing for new tenant
  - Schedule move-out inspection

  **No Response**: Follow up and assume non-renewal
  - May convert to month-to-month (if allowed)
  - Or require move-out per lease terms

### 5. Lease Termination

#### Notice to Vacate
- **Notice Period**: 30-60 days (per lease/state law)
- **Required Information**:
  - Move-out date
  - Forwarding address
  - Reason for leaving (optional)
- **Acknowledgment**: Confirm receipt and review move-out process

#### Move-Out Process Communication
- **Move-Out Packet**:
  - Move-out checklist
  - Cleaning requirements
  - Repair expectations
  - Inspection scheduling
  - Deposit return timeline
  - Final utility reading info

#### Pre-Move-Out Inspection
- **Timing**: 2 weeks before move-out
- **Purpose**:
  - Identify issues tenant can address
  - Estimate potential deductions
  - Give tenant opportunity to make repairs
- **Optional**: Not all jurisdictions allow/require

#### Move-Out Inspection
- **Timing**: Move-out date or within 24-48 hours
- **Process**:
  - Walk through unit (with or without tenant)
  - Compare to move-in condition
  - Document all damages/issues (photos/video)
  - Complete inspection checklist
  - Identify normal wear vs. damage

**Normal Wear and Tear**:
- Faded paint
- Minor carpet wear
- Small nail holes
- Worn door handles
- Caulking deterioration

**Tenant Damage** (chargeable):
- Holes in walls (large)
- Broken fixtures/appliances
- Pet damage
- Excessive dirt/uncleanliness
- Missing items
- Cigarette burns/smoke damage

#### Key Return
- All keys and access cards returned
- Change locks if keys not returned
- Charge for unreturned keys

#### Security Deposit Accounting
- **Timeline**: 15-60 days (varies by state law)
- **Deductions**:
  - Unpaid rent and fees
  - Cleaning (if not left in acceptable condition)
  - Repairs for damages beyond normal wear
  - Carpet replacement (if damaged, pro-rated)
  - Missing items
  - Utility charges

**Deposit Return Statement**:
```
Original Security Deposit:        $2,000.00
Deductions:
  - Carpet cleaning                  ($150.00)
  - Wall repair (holes)              ($200.00)
  - Cleaning (excessive)             ($100.00)
  - Outstanding utilities             ($75.00)
                                   -----------
Total Deductions:                   ($525.00)
Amount Returned to Tenant:        $1,475.00
```

- **Documentation**: Itemized statement + receipts/invoices
- **Delivery**: Mail to forwarding address with check

#### Final Ledger Closeout
- Post final charges
- Apply deposit to balance
- Collect any remaining balance due
- Refund overpayment or deposit remainder
- Close lease in system

### 6. Post-Lease

#### Turnover / Make-Ready
- **Timeline**: 3-10 days typical
- **Tasks**:
  - Professional cleaning
  - Paint (full or touch-up)
  - Carpet cleaning or replacement
  - Repair any damages
  - Replace HVAC filters
  - Light bulb replacement
  - Appliance check/service
  - Lock change
  - Final inspection

**Turnover Checklist**:

#### Unit Ready for Re-Lease
- Update unit status to "vacant"
- Set availability date
- Upload photos to listing
- Syndicate to listing sites
- Schedule showings

#### Record Keeping
- **Retention Requirements** (varies by jurisdiction):
  - Lease documents: 3-7 years
  - Financial records: 7 years
  - Maintenance records: 3-5 years
  - Deposit documentation: 3-7 years
  - Eviction records: 7 years

## Special Situations

### Early Termination
- **Tenant Request**: Early termination fee (1-2 months' rent)
- **Military Clause**: SCRA allows military personnel to break lease
- **Habitability Issues**: If property fails to maintain, tenant may terminate
- **Domestic Violence**: Some states allow DV victims to break lease

### Eviction
- **Grounds**: Non-payment, lease violations, illegal activity
- **Process**: Notice → Filing → Court hearing → Judgment → Sheriff eviction
- **Timeline**: 30-90 days typical (varies by state)
- **Record**: Remains on tenant's record for 7 years

### Lease Transfer/Assignment
- **Sublease**: Tenant remains responsible, sublets to another party
- **Assignment**: Tenant transfers all rights to new tenant
- **Landlord Approval**: Typically required, cannot unreasonably deny
- **Process**: Application and screening of new tenant, addendum to lease

### Death of Tenant
- **Process**: Work with estate executor or family
- **Lease Termination**: Varies by state law and lease terms
- **Deposit Return**: To estate or designated beneficiary
- **Belongings**: Follow state abandonment laws

## Automation Opportunities

### E-Signatures
- Lease documents, addendums, notices
- Faster execution, better experience
- Legal and enforceable in all 50 states

### Autopay
- 80%+ adoption reduces collection efforts
- Reduces late payments and fees
- Improves cash flow predictability

### Automated Reminders
- Rent due reminders (3 days before)
- Late rent notifications
- Lease expiration alerts
- Renewal offer reminders
- Work order updates

### Self-Service Portal
- Tenants can: Pay rent, submit work orders, renew lease, update info
- Reduces staff workload
- Improves tenant satisfaction

### Chatbots
- Answer common questions 24/7
- Schedule tours
- Provide application status
- Reduce staff phone calls

This comprehensive lifecycle management ensures compliant, efficient, and tenant-friendly operations.
