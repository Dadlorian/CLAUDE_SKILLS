# Lease Types Reference

## Residential Leases
- **Standard**: 12-month term, fixed rent
- **Month-to-Month**: No fixed term
- **Rent-to-Own**: Lease with purchase option

## Commercial Leases
- **Gross Lease**: Landlord pays all expenses
- **Net Lease**: Tenant pays some expenses
- **Double Net (NN)**: Tenant pays taxes + insurance
- **Triple Net (NNN)**: Tenant pays taxes, insurance, maintenance
- **Modified Gross**: Negotiated expense split
- **Percentage Lease**: Base + % of sales

## Lease Clauses
- Rent amount and due date
- Security deposit
- Lease term and renewal
- Maintenance responsibilities
- Pet policy
- Subletting rules
- Early termination
- Late fees
