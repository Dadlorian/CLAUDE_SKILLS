# Creating 3D Tours Guide

## Equipment Needed
- 3D camera (Matterport, Ricoh Theta)
- Tripod
- Tablet/phone for control
- Good lighting

## Capture Process
1. **Prepare property**
   - Declutter and stage
   - Turn on all lights
   - Open blinds for natural light

2. **Plan scan positions**
   - Every 8-10 feet
   - Cover all rooms
   - Include outdoor spaces

3. **Capture scans**
   - Level camera
   - Wait for scan completion (30-60 sec)
   - Ensure overlap between scans

4. **Upload & Process**
   - Upload to platform
   - Processing time: 1-4 hours
   - Review for completeness
