**BRAND**: [Company Legal Name]
Address: [Full Address]
("Company" or "Brand")

**INFLUENCER**: [Influencer Legal Name]
Social Handle: [@handle]
Address: [Full Address]
("Influencer" or "Creator")

## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

The Influencer agrees to create and publish the following content ("Deliverables"):

| Deliverable | Platform | Format | Posting Date |
|-------------|----------|--------|--------------|
| 1 x Feed Post | Instagram | Photo/Carousel | [Date] |
| 3 x Stories | Instagram | Story Slides | [Date] |
| 1 x Reel | Instagram | Short Video | [Date] |

**Content Requirements**:
- Feature [Product/Service Name] prominently
- Include brand handle tag: [@brandhandle]
- Include branded hashtag: #[CampaignHashtag]
- Include affiliate link/promo code: [CODE] in bio or swipe-up
- Caption minimum 100 words highlighting [key benefits]

**Content Approval**:
- Influencer must submit draft content 48 hours before posting date
- Brand will provide feedback within 24 hours
- Maximum 2 rounds of revisions included

## 2. COMPENSATION

**Payment Terms**:
- Flat Fee: $[AMOUNT] USD
- Payment Method: [Bank transfer / PayPal / Platform]
- Payment Schedule:
  - 50% ($[AMOUNT]) upon signing
  - 50% ($[AMOUNT]) upon content approval and posting

**Alternative**: Commission-Based
- Commission: [X]% of sales via promo code [CODE]
- Payment: Net-30 days after month end
- Tracking: Via [Platform/System]

---

## 3. USAGE RIGHTS AND LICENSE

**Grant of Rights**:
The Influencer grants the Brand a non-exclusive, worldwide license to:
- Repost content on Brand's owned social media channels
- Use content in paid social media advertising
- Feature content on Brand website and marketing materials
- Use content for [DURATION: 12 months / perpetuity] from posting date

**Exclusions**:
- Brand may not alter content without Influencer approval
- Brand must credit Influencer when reposting: [@influencerhandle]
- Influencer retains ownership of original content

**Whitelisting** (if applicable):
- Brand may run paid ads from Influencer's account for [X] days
- Budget cap: $[AMOUNT]
- Influencer approval required before ads go live

---

## 4. FTC DISCLOSURE & COMPLIANCE

**Influencer must**:
- Clearly disclose the partnership per FTC guidelines
- Use #ad or #sponsored prominently (within first 3 lines or first slide)
- Include verbal disclosure if video content
- Not use misleading claims about the product/service

**Disclosure Examples**:
✅ "Excited to partner with @brand on this post! #ad"
✅ "This post is sponsored by @brand. #sponsored"
✅ "Thanks to @brand for sponsoring this video! #ad"

**Platform-Specific**:
- Instagram: Use "Paid Partnership" tag
- YouTube: Check "Includes paid promotion" box
- TikTok: Toggle "Branded Content" disclosure

---

**Campaign Exclusivity**:
During the campaign period [START DATE] to [END DATE], Influencer agrees not to:
- Post about direct competitor brands: [List competitors]
- Accept competing brand partnerships in [category]

**Post-Campaign Exclusivity**:
For [X] days after campaign end, Influencer will not post about competing brands.

**Exceptions**: [Any exceptions to exclusivity]

---

## 6. CREATIVE FREEDOM & BRAND GUIDELINES

**Creative Control**:
- Influencer retains creative freedom in content style and voice
- Content must align with Influencer's typical content aesthetic
- Brand will provide guidelines (not scripts) for key messaging

**Brand Guidelines** (Attached as Appendix A):
- Product usage and demonstration guidelines
- Brand tone and messaging guardrails
- Visual standards (if applicable)
- What NOT to say (prohibited claims)

---

## 7. PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS

**No Guaranteed Results**: This is a brand awareness campaign. Influencer does not guarantee:
- Specific engagement rates
- Specific number of sales/conversions
- Specific follower growth for Brand

**Good Faith Effort**: Influencer commits to:
- Posting at optimal times for their audience
- Engaging with comments on sponsored posts
- Promoting content via Stories (if included in deliverables)

**Metrics Reporting** (if applicable):
- Influencer will provide screenshot of insights within 7 days post-campaign
- Metrics: Reach, impressions, engagement rate, link clicks

---

## 8. TERMINATION

**Termination for Cause**:
Either party may terminate if:
- Other party breaches material term and fails to cure within 7 days
- Influencer engages in behavior that harms Brand reputation
- Brand fails to pay per payment schedule

**Compensation Upon Termination**:
- If Brand terminates without cause: Full payment due
- If Influencer terminates without cause: Return deposit, no further payment
- If content already posted: Compensation remains earned

---

## 9. REPRESENTATIONS & WARRANTIES

**Influencer Represents**:
- Influencer owns or has rights to all content created
- Follower count is genuine (not artificially inflated with bots)
- Will comply with all platform terms of service
- Is not bound by conflicting agreements

**Brand Represents**:
- Has rights to the product/service being promoted
- Will provide product/service as described
- Marketing claims are truthful and not misleading

---

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

Each party agrees to indemnify the other from claims arising from:
- Breach of this Agreement
- Violation of third-party rights (IP, privacy, etc.)
- Violation of laws or regulations

---

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep confidential:
- Terms of this Agreement (compensation amount)
- Proprietary information shared during collaboration
- Unpublished product information (if applicable)

**Exceptions**: Information that becomes public through no fault of receiving party.

---

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Governing Law**: [State/Country]

**Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement and supersedes prior discussions.

**Amendments**: Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

**Independent Contractor**: Influencer is an independent contractor, not an employee.

**Assignment**: Neither party may assign without written consent of the other.
