# Content Marketing Strategy Guide
## How to Build a Content Marketing Program from Scratch

### Step 1: Audience Research (Week 1-2)
**Activities**:
- Interview 10-15 customers about pain points, goals, content preferences
- Analyze customer support tickets for common questions
- Review sales call recordings for objections and interests
- Survey email list about content topics they want
- Analyze competitor content (what resonates?)

**Deliverables**:
- 2-3 detailed buyer personas
- Jobs to be Done framework
- Content topic priorities

### Step 2: Content Audit & Gap Analysis (Week 2-3)
- Inventory existing content
- Identify high-performers (traffic, engagement, conversions)
- Map content to buyer journey stages
- Identify gaps in coverage
- Determine content to update, consolidate, or remove

### Step 3: Topic Cluster Strategy (Week 3-4)
**Structure**:
- **Pillar Pages**: Comprehensive guides (3,000-5,000 words)
- **Cluster Content**: Specific subtopics linking to pillar
- **Internal Linking**: Connect all related content

**Example Cluster**:
- Pillar: "Complete Guide to Email Marketing"
- Clusters: "Email Automation Best Practices", "How to Improve Open Rates", "Email Deliverability Guide"

### Step 4: Editorial Calendar (Week 4-5)
**Components**:
- Publishing frequency (2-4x per week recommended)
- Content formats (blog, video, podcast, infographic mix)
- Topic assignments and deadlines
- SEO keywords for each piece
- Distribution channels and promotion plan

### Step 5: Content Creation Process (Ongoing)
1. **Research**: Keyword research, competitor analysis, original data
2. **Outline**: Structure with H2/H3 subheadings
3. **Draft**: Write with SEO and reader value in mind
4. **Edit**: Clarity, grammar, brand voice alignment
5. **Optimize**: SEO elements, images, internal links
6. **Publish**: CMS upload, formatting, meta data
7. **Promote**: Social, email, communities, paid amplification

### Step 6: Distribution Strategy
**Owned Channels**: Blog, email list, social profiles
**Earned Channels**: PR, guest posts, influencer shares, backlinks
**Paid Channels**: Native ads, promoted posts, content syndication

### Step 7: Measurement & Optimization
**Track**:
- Traffic (organic, referral, social)
- Engagement (time on page, scroll depth, bounce rate)
- Conversions (email signups, demo requests, purchases)
- SEO (rankings, backlinks, domain authority)
- Revenue attribution

**Optimize**: Double down on winners, iterate on underperformers, kill what doesn't work
