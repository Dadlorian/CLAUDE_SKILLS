# Email Automation Workflows
## Pre-Built Email Sequences Ready to Deploy

## Welcome Series (5 emails over 2 weeks)

**Email 1: Immediate Welcome** (Send immediately)
Subject: Welcome to [Brand]! Here's your [gift/discount] 🎁

**Email 2: Brand Story** (Day 2)
Subject: How [Brand] started (and why we're different)

**Email 3: Social Proof** (Day 5)
Subject: See what [10,000 customers] are saying about us

**Email 4: Education** (Day 9)
Subject: [Customer name], here's how to get the most value from [product]

**Email 5: Conversion** (Day 14)
Subject: Ready to [benefit]? Here's [special offer]

---

## Abandoned Cart Recovery (3 emails)

**Email 1: Reminder** (1 hour after abandonment)
Subject: Did you forget something?

**Email 2: Incentive** (24 hours after)
Subject: Still thinking it over? Here's 10% off

**Email 3: Last Chance** (72 hours after)
Subject: Your cart expires soon + Free shipping offer

---

## Trial User Activation (7 emails over 14 days)

**Day 0**: Welcome to your trial + Setup guide
**Day 1**: Quick win - Complete [key action] in 5 minutes
**Day 3**: Feature highlight - [Top feature] walkthrough
**Day 5**: Case study - How [customer] achieved [result]
**Day 7**: Midpoint check-in + Offer help
**Day 10**: Upgrade offer - Special discount for converting now
**Day 13**: Trial ending soon - Last chance to convert

---

## Re-engagement (4 emails over 6 weeks)

**Email 1: We miss you** (After 30 days inactive)
Subject: We noticed you've been gone...

**Email 2: What's new** (Week 2)
Subject: You've missed [new features/content]

**Email 3: Special offer** (Week 4)
Subject: Come back: [Exclusive discount/offer]

**Email 4: Final farewell** (Week 6)
Subject: Is it time to say goodbye? [Unsubscribe or reactivate]

---

## Post-Purchase Nurture (6 emails over 3 months)

**Day 1**: Thank you + Order confirmation
**Day 7**: How are you liking [product]? Quick survey
**Day 14**: Tips to get more value from [product]
**Day 30**: Customer spotlight + Community invite
**Day 60**: Cross-sell - You might also like [related product]
**Day 90**: Refer a friend - Give $20, Get $20
