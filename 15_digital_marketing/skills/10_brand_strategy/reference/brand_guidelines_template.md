## 6. BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Brand Personality

We are: **Friendly, Knowledgeable, Empowering**

**Friendly**
- ✅ Conversational and approachable
- ✅ Use "you" and "we"
- ❌ Not overly casual or use excessive slang

**Knowledgeable**
- ✅ Back claims with data and examples
- ✅ Educate, don't just sell
- ❌ Not condescending or jargon-heavy

**Empowering**
- ✅ Focus on what the customer can achieve
- ✅ Provide actionable advice
- ❌ Not creating dependency or fear

### Voice Guidelines

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Conversational | Overly formal or corporate |
| Clear and simple | Jargon-filled or complex |
| Optimistic | Hyperbolic or unrealistic |
| Helpful | Pushy or sales-y |

### Tone Adaptation

While our voice stays consistent, tone adapts:

**Celebratory** (product launch):
"We're thrilled to introduce [feature]! 🎉"

**Educational** (blog post):
"Here's how to [achieve outcome] in 3 steps."

**Supportive** (customer service):
"We're here to help. Let's get this sorted out."

**Apologetic** (service issue):
"We're sorry for the inconvenience. Here's what happened and how we're fixing it."

### Writing Examples

❌ **Off-Brand**:
"Leverage our synergistic platform to actualize your strategic imperatives."

✅ **On-Brand**:
"Use our platform to achieve your business goals faster."

---

❌ **Off-Brand**:
"Click here for more info."

✅ **On-Brand**:

## 7. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Simplicity
Less is more. Remove elements until the design breaks.

### Consistency
Use established patterns. Don't reinvent for every project.

### Hierarchy
Guide the eye with size, color, and spacing.

### White Space
Give elements room to breathe. Don't fear empty space.

### Accessibility
Design for everyone. WCAG 2.2 AA minimum.
