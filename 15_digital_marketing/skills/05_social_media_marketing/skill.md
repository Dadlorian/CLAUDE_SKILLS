## Advanced Paid Social Strategies

### Meta Ads Campaign Structure

**Campaign Level** (Objective):
- Awareness (reach, impressions)
- Engagement (page likes, reactions, comments)
- Traffic (website clicks)
- Conversions (leads, sales)
- App installs (mobile app focused)
- Video views (video consumption)

**Ad Set Level** (Audience & Budget):
- Audience selection (targeting)
- Bidding strategy (automatic, manual)
- Budget allocation (daily, lifetime)
- Schedule timing
- Placements (feed, stories, reels, video)

**Ad Level** (Creative)**:
- Ad copy (headline, primary text)
- Images/videos
- Call-to-action button
- Landing page

### Audience Targeting Strategies

**Layered Targeting Approach**:
1. **Core audience**: Lookalike audiences (similar to best customers)
2. **Saved audiences**: Interest-based, demographic-based
3. **Custom audiences**: Website visitors, email list, app users
4. **Exclusions**: Existing customers (to avoid waste), competitors

**Audience Segments to Create**:
- **Cold**: Never heard of brand
- **Warm**: Visited website, engaged with content
- **Hot**: Abandoned cart, engaged with ads multiple times
- **VIP**: Existing customers, high spenders

### Creative Optimization

**Ad Format Strategies**:
- **Static images**: 1.2:1 aspect ratio, clear headline
- **Carousels**: Show multiple products/features
- **Video**: 1:1 (square) or 9:16 (vertical) for mobile
- **Collections**: Showcase product catalog
- **Instant Experience**: Mobile-optimized full-screen ads

**Creative Testing Framework**:
- **Variable 1**: Hook/headline variations
- **Variable 2**: Visual (product shot, lifestyle, testimonial)
- **Variable 3**: Copy angle (benefit, curiosity, social proof)
- **Winning creative**: Double or triple down on winners

**Video Creative Best Practices**:
- First 3 seconds are critical (show your hook)
- Captions on (many watch muted)
- Vertical video for feed, square for stories/reels
- 15-30 seconds optimal length
- High production value = better CTR

### Bid Strategy Selection

**Automated Bidding Strategies**:
- **Lowest Cost**: Get most volume for budget (learning curve)
- **Target Cost**: Specify cost-per-result (e.g., $5 CPA)
- **Target ROAS**: Specify return multiple (e.g., 3:1)

**Manual Bidding**:
- **Cost Per Click (CPC)**: Pay for each click
- **Cost Per Impression (CPM)**: Pay per 1,000 impressions
- **Less common**: Use when you want strict control

**Choosing the Right Strategy**:
- New ads: Start with Lowest Cost (needs learning data)
- Mature campaigns: Target Cost or ROAS (more predictable)
- Awareness goal: CPM or LowestCost (impressions matter)

### Remarketing Sequences

**Website Visitor Remarketing**:
- **3-day campaign**: Anyone who visited in last 3 days
- **7-day campaign**: Abandoned product page viewers
- **14-day campaign**: Visited but didn't convert
- **30+ day campaign**: Keep brand top-of-mind

**Shopping Cart Abandoners**:
- Show product they abandoned
- Discount offer (10-20%)
- Show alternative products
- Final push (urgency, stock limited)

**Cross-Sell/Upsell Campaigns**:
- Target customers who bought X
- Show complementary product Y
- Bundle offer
- "Frequently bought together"

## Social Media Analytics & Measurement

### Organic Social Metrics
- **Reach**: Unique accounts who saw content
- **Impressions**: Total times content was shown
- **Engagement rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Reach
- **Engagement types**: Understand what drives engagement
- **Click-through rate**: Clicks to website
- **Share of voice**: Your mentions vs. competitors

### Paid Social Metrics
- **Impressions, Reach**: Campaign visibility
- **Click-through rate (CTR)**: Quality indicator
- **Cost per click (CPC)**: Efficiency metric
- **Cost per engagement (CPE)**: Platform cost
- **Conversion rate**: % who completed desired action
- **Cost per acquisition (CPA)**: Cost to acquire customer
- **Return on ad spend (ROAS)**: Revenue / Ad spend

### Attribution & Revenue Tracking
- **UTM parameters**: Track traffic source
- **Platform pixels**: Facebook, LinkedIn pixels on website
- **Conversion API**: Server-side tracking (iOS 14+ reliable)
- **Revenue attribution**: Direct and assisted conversions
- **Customer lifetime value**: Long-term platform ROI

### Reporting Dashboard
- Key metrics by platform
- Campaign performance vs. goals
- Best/worst performing content
- Audience growth trends
- Engagement trends
- Conversion/revenue attribution

## Social Media Crisis Management

### Prevention
- Monitoring setup (alerts for brand mentions)
- Response protocols prepared
- Team training on response
- Clear approval process
- Decision-making authority

### Response Playbook

1. **Monitor & Assess**
   - Assess severity (viral, negative volume)
   - Understand root cause
   - Gather facts before responding

2. **Internal Communication**
   - Alert leadership
   - Brief communications team
   - Align on key message

3. **External Response** (within 1-2 hours for major issues)
   - Acknowledge issue
   - Show understanding
   - Commit to action
   - Provide timeline for resolution

4. **Ongoing Communication**
   - Regular updates
   - Show progress
   - Transparency
   - Follow-through

5. **Post-Crisis**
   - Analysis of what happened
   - Process improvements
   - Team learnings
   - Documentation

### Crisis Examples & Responses
- **Negative customer review**: Respond privately, offer resolution
- **Company controversy**: Public statement, concrete actions
- **Social media hack**: Notify followers, reset password, security update
- **Misguided marketing**: Apology, explanation, correction
