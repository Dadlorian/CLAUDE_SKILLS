# Social Media Content Calendar Template
## 30-Day Plan with Post Examples

### Week 1: Brand Awareness

**Monday**: Educational post
"5 mistakes people make with [topic] (and how to avoid them)"

**Wednesday**: Behind-the-scenes
"Meet [team member]: The person who [role/achievement]"

**Friday**: User-generated content
Repost customer success story with permission

---

### Week 2: Engagement

**Monday**: Question/Poll
"Which feature should we build next? A, B, or C?"

**Wednesday**: Industry news
"Hot take: [Trending topic in your industry]"

**Friday**: Tip/Tutorial
"How to [achieve result] in under 5 minutes"

---

### Week 3: Community Building

**Monday**: Customer spotlight
"How [Customer Name] achieved [impressive result]"

**Wednesday**: Company culture
"Our team tradition: [Fun company ritual]"

**Friday**: Ask Me Anything
"Drop your [industry] questions below 👇"

---

### Week 4: Conversion

**Monday**: Product benefit
"The #1 reason customers choose us: [Key differentiator]"

**Wednesday**: Social proof
"Just hit [milestone]! Thanks to customers like you 🎉"

**Friday**: Limited offer
"Weekend special: [Discount/Offer] ends Sunday"

---

## Content Mix (80/20 Rule)
- 80% Value (education, entertainment, inspiration)
- 20% Promotional (product, offers, sales)

## Posting Frequency by Platform
- LinkedIn: 3-5x per week
- Instagram: 4-7x per week (+ daily Stories)
- Facebook: 1-2x per day
- Twitter: 3-5x per day
- TikTok: 1-4x per day
